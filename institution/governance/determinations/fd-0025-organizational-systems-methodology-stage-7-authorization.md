## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Stage 6 acceptance and its limits

- Status: "ACCEPTED AND CLOSED."
- **Principal finding accepted:** "Two separate authoring contexts applying the
  same frozen Draft v3 to the same HGP baseline independently reached materially
  equivalent advancement refusal under the method's own minimum-evidence rules."
- **Implications accepted:** "The advancement logic is not solely dependent on
  the original author's judgment." · "The records-only constraint is
  reproducible." · "The method can constrain diagnosis toward refusal rather
  than completion when evidence is insufficient."
- **Non-implications:** "External validity is not established." · "Evaluator
  independence is not established." · "Model-independent reproducibility is not
  established."

### 2.2 The reproducibility distinction, accepted as a finding

- "Subject-diagnosis outputs were materially more reproducible than
  method-deficiency outputs."
- Interpretation: "Treat this as evidence that Draft v3 is presently more mature
  at guiding organizational diagnosis than at guiding self-critique of the
  diagnostic method."
- **Rule against over-reading it:** "Do not turn weak reproducibility of
  deficiency discovery into an assumption that all first-author or second-author
  deficiency findings are invalid. **Adjudicate each deficiency against its
  underlying evidence.**"

### 2.3 The corpus, fixed

- "The required corpus consists of exactly: D-1 through D-8 and MO-01 through
  MO-22." Count: **30**.
- **Critical rule:** "Inventory and adjudicate all thirty records individually.
  **Do not begin by collapsing the corpus into themes.**"
- If the count is not thirty: "Stop and reconcile the source inventory before
  substantive disposition. Do not silently drop, renumber, merge, or invent
  records to reach thirty."
- Sixteen required fields per record, from the identifier through remaining
  uncertainty, including whether the deficiency was independently reproduced,
  contradicted, weakened by later clarification, or strengthened by later
  evidence.

### 2.4 The disposition vocabulary

- **ACCEPT** — "the deficiency remains supported and should produce a future
  methodology change substantially as proposed."
- **ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION** — "the underlying problem is supported but the
  original proposed remedy, framing, scope, or mechanism should change."
- **DEFER** — "the issue may be legitimate but current evidence is insufficient
  to justify method change or requires validation unavailable in Generation
  One."
- **DECLINE** — "accumulated evidence no longer supports the deficiency, later
  clarification defeats it, it reflects author error rather than method defect,
  or the proposed change would worsen the method."
- **DUPLICATE / SUBSUMED** — "only when another specifically identified
  deficiency **fully** captures the same required method change… **Do not use
  duplicate/subsumed merely to reduce the corpus cosmetically.**"
- **Critical rule:** "Do not use 'resolved' as a Stage 7 disposition merely
  because a future Draft v4 could address an issue."

### 2.5 The analytical order

Six sequences: method ambiguities and internal inconsistencies; D-8 and
independence; evidence-access and replication design; advancement, closure and
evidentiary sufficiency; instrument and reporting defects; usability and
presentation risks. "This order governs analytical sequencing only. It does not
assign severity, priority, or institutional importance."

### 2.6 Method-ambiguity review

Seven ambiguities to review at minimum, including "difference between evidence
state and comparison classification"; "difference between representable and
validated behavior"; "meaning of diagnostic closure versus Stage completion";
"relationship between evidence sufficiency and instrument completion";
"relationship between author behaviour and method defect".

Rule: "Where existing methodology already answers an ambiguity, cite and
preserve it. Where evidence supports a future change, include it in the Draft v4
change set. **Where Founder Office authority is required to settle methodology
semantics, identify the exact determination needed instead of silently resolving
it.**"

### 2.7 D-8, preserved and not weakened

- Status: **CONTINUING EVIDENCE QUALIFICATION.**
- Stage 6 "improved internal evidence but did not establish true independence
  because: both authors were within the same model family; the second author
  received injected memory context; some subject/program facts leaked through
  that context; the harness placed the author within the subject rather than
  requiring independent subject discovery; both applications occurred inside
  Huerta Group's own environment."
- "Replication does not equal independent external validation." · "**Do not
  weaken, remove, or rewrite D-8 merely because Stage 6 produced materially
  similar outputs.**"
- "Treat same-model-family agreement as **correlated-error risk**."

### 2.8 The replication-design defect, determined

- Accepted finding: "Stage 6 replication design created a conflict between
  author isolation and equal evidence boundaries."
- Effect: "Withholding `institution/` improved author isolation but also
  withheld FD-V1 evidence available to the first author, materially affecting
  authority findings."
- Classification: **REPLICATION-DESIGN DEFICIENCY.**
- **Rule:** "Do not reinterpret the resulting authority divergence as pure
  methodology disagreement."
- Any future protocol must define in advance: identical permitted evidence;
  identical unavailable evidence; prohibited prior-results exposure; and "the
  mechanism used to prevent leakage without changing subject access."

### 2.9 The closure defect, determined

- Accepted finding: A17 rule 7 required the custody disposition before closure,
  "but Stage 5 closed without that agreement."
- Classification: **METHOD-APPLICATION DEFECT.**
- "Do not retroactively fabricate the missing custody disposition."
- Task: "Determine the narrowest future rule that prevents false closure while
  still allowing an application or validation stage to end truthfully,"
  distinguishing application-stage completion, subject-diagnosis completion,
  advancement permission, and an unresolved closure condition. "Do not design a
  universal project-management closure state."

### 2.10 One-sided evidence, and the populated-output risk

- One-sided evidence: determine whether the accumulated evidence supports making
  it "a required methodological distinction," preferring "a descriptive
  evidence-state layer that can coexist with comparison classification." It must
  never infer "credibility; speaker intent; knowledge state; truth; reliability
  merely from silence." **A four-option answer is required.**
- Populated output: accepted that a diagnostic artifact "can be highly populated
  while evidence remains insufficient for advancement." The benefit — the method
  "does not collapse into empty output" — is recorded alongside the risk.
  **Critical distinction:** "Instrument population is not evidence sufficiency.
  Diagnostic completeness is not the same thing as data-entry completeness."
- **Prohibited in both:** percentage complete; diagnostic score; maturity score;
  traffic-light maturity scoring unless separately governed; numerical
  confidence model; weighted evidence index.

### 2.11 Author behaviour versus method defect

Determine for each conduct-related deficiency whether Draft v3 "lacked
guidance," "contained ambiguous guidance," the author "failed to follow clear
guidance," "the execution harness compromised the protocol," or evidence is
insufficient to tell.

- "Do not amend methodology merely to compensate for every implementation
  mistake where the method already provided sufficiently clear instruction."
- "Conversely, **repeated author deviation may itself indicate that the method's
  instruction is not operationally clear enough**."

### 2.12 The Draft v4 boundary

- **"Do not create Draft v4."**
- Identify only "the minimum coherent set of methodology changes that would
  justify creation of a future Draft v4," arising **only** from ACCEPT and
  ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION dispositions plus necessary coherence changes —
  excluding deferred and declined issues, speculative improvements not grounded
  in the corpus, and "external-validation features not yet evidenced."
- Coherence requirement: "implementable as one internally coherent future
  revision rather than a collection of disconnected edits."
- Replication-protocol mechanics are to be preserved separately: "Do not
  automatically embed replication-protocol mechanics inside the client-facing
  Organizational Diagnostic if they belong instead to methodology validation
  procedure."

### 2.13 Maturity, the external gap, and the further-application question

- Ceiling: "Stage 7 does not authorize a maturity designation beyond:
  INTERNALLY TESTED PROVISIONAL DIAGNOSTIC."
- External gap: **OPEN.** Stage 7 does not close or materially prove external
  validity, client validity, independence from Huerta Group,
  **different-model-family reproducibility**, multi-person organizational
  validity, contested-authority validity, general applicability, Method Library
  readiness, or commercial readiness.
- A required assessment, with a three-option answer, of whether another internal
  application "would provide materially new evidence at this point" — and the
  boundary: "**Do not conduct that application under Stage 7.**"

### 2.14 Continued exclusions

Third diagnostic application; Draft v4; external client application; Method
Library admission; methodology adoption; standardization; diagnostic software;
assessment automation; AI diagnostic authority; scoring; maturity models;
commercial packaging; pricing; public methodology claims; HGP changes;
Corporate Command Center changes.

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — the adjudicator is the first author.** Twenty-two of the thirty
records were written by the second author and eight by the first; **the first
author adjudicates all thirty, including its own.** This is the same
independence defect recorded at `d8-evidence-base-qualification.md` §5.4 for the
Stage 6 comparison, and no procedure in Stage 7 cures it. It bears hardest
wherever a first-author deficiency is accepted and a second-author deficiency
declined.

**Deficiency 3 — the second author cannot be consulted on its own records.**
Its context ended with Stage 6. Where a MO record's meaning is ambiguous, the
adjudication reads its preserved text and says so, rather than asking.
