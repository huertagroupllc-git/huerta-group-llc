## What this record preserves, and what it does not

This record preserves **Founder Office acts stated in the instrument**:
the adoption of the Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation — Version 1,
the policy determinations accompanying it, the restated OSPA authority
boundary, and the execution authorization and its boundary.

It does **not** preserve, and the repository does **not** hold, the text
of either authoritative source the instrument directed be preserved.
Neither source was transmitted. See Deficiencies 1 and 2.

**Critical boundary — the instrument's abstract is not the Foundation.**
The invariants and semantic tests transcribed below are the *instrument's*
wording of what the adopted Foundation requires. They are not the
Foundation's text, not its §31, and not a substitute for it. Their
section numbering, definitions, scope conditions, and the whole of §§1–30
and §32 are unknown to the implementing office. This record may be cited
as evidence that the Foundation was adopted and as evidence of the policy
determinations. It may **not** be used as the Foundation — not for
architecture, not for schema, not for definition, and not as a basis for
inferring what the Foundation says. The instrument itself forbids the
substitution: "Do not reconstruct from abbreviated repository records,
summaries, prior execution packages, model memory, or technical-assessment
material."

### 2. Binding invariants

Ten invariants, as stated in the instrument:

1. "State, Change, and Attention are distinct."
2. "Material Change is consequence-based rather than activity-based."
3. "Dependency does not imply Blocker."
4. "A Handoff transfers expected action, not institutional authority."
5. "Corporate Operations owns Headquarters operational representation, not
   underlying source-domain truth."
6. "Operational Instance and Operational Record remain distinct."
7. "No universal lifecycle may be imposed merely for implementation
   convenience."
8. "Founder Attention is an exception layer, not an activity feed or
   generic inbox."
9. "Surviving OSPA multidimensional-state doctrine must remain preserved."
10. "Technical convenience does not authorize semantic collapse."

### 3. Semantic tests

**Requirement:** "All ten semantic tests in Foundation §31 must remain
answerable YES under the technical architecture."

The instrument enumerates them as:

1. "Can State change without creating Attention?"
2. "Can Attention arise without a State change?"
3. "Can a Dependency exist without being a Blocker?"
4. "Can a Blocker be removed while the underlying matter remains
   incomplete?"
5. "Can a Handoff complete while a different Dependency remains?"
6. "Can an authoritative fact remain owned elsewhere while Corporate
   Operations owns its Headquarters representation?"
7. "Can a Renewal exist before it creates an approaching Deadline?"
8. "Can a Follow-Up exist without a Deadline?"
9. "Can a routine change remain invisible at Headquarters because it is
   immaterial?"
10. "Can the same operational matter have multiple simultaneous state
    dimensions without collapsing into one generic status?"

The enumeration above is the instrument's; the Foundation's own §31
wording has not been seen by the implementing office.

### 4. Founder Office policy determinations

**Attention entry.** "Functions and ventures may nominate or escalate a
matter as requiring Founder Attention. Corporate Operations & Command
Management owns admission to and semantic consistency of the Headquarters
Founder Attention representation. Corporate Operations may not suppress an
escalation that independently requires Founder authority under governing
institutional authority."

**Attention exit.** "An Attention Item leaves Founder Attention once
required Founder awareness, judgment, decision, authorization, or
intervention has occurred and Founder involvement is no longer required.
The underlying Operational Instance may remain active, incomplete, blocked
for another reason, or dependent upon downstream execution."

**Materiality disputes.** "Corporate Operations ordinarily determines
Headquarters materiality. A source function may escalate a disputed
classification to Founder Office where exclusion would materially impair
corporate awareness, authority, risk management, or execution."

**Representation freshness.** "No universal numeric SLA is established. A
Headquarters representation must be updated promptly enough after a known
consequential source-domain change that continued reliance on the prior
representation would not mislead responsible corporate action."

**Attention priority.** Originating functions may propose **Immediate**,
**Near-Term**, or **Normal**. "Corporate Operations may normalize. Founder
Office may override. No numerical scoring, weighted prioritization, or
algorithmic severity model is authorized." (Consistent with `fd-0010` and
`fd-0011`.)

## Deficiencies recorded

### Deficiency 1 — the Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation V1 source was not transmitted; preservation is incomplete

The instrument directs preservation "exactly from the complete
retransmitted source supplied in the immediately preceding Founder Office
package," and prohibits reconstruction "from abbreviated repository
records, summaries, prior execution packages, model memory, or
technical-assessment material."

**No such source accompanied the instrument, and no preceding Founder
Office package containing it reached the implementing office.** What
arrived is the execution package alone — a 36,183-byte instrument opening
`<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?><claude_execution_package>` and
closing `</claude_execution_package>`, containing directives about the
Foundation and no Foundation text. Its own references to "§31" and "§32"
are references to sections it does not carry.

Verification performed before recording this deficiency: the working tree,
all gitignored paths, and the complete git object database including
unreachable objects; the sibling repositories `huerta-group-publishing`,
`curbside-spa`, `gotrade-official` and `nest & quill`; the local filesystem
under `GitHub`, `Documents`, `Desktop`, `Downloads`, iCloud Drive and the
session scratchpad; the Founder's Google Drive by title and full text; the
Founder's Gmail; and the implementing office's own transmission cache and
prior session records. The discriminating negative: the phrase "semantic
test" occurs in exactly one artifact on the machine outside this
instrument's own delivery — the instrument that asks for the document. A
thirty-one-section doctrine whose §31 is ten semantic tests does not exist
in a corpus where that phrase occurs nowhere else.

**Preservation is therefore left incomplete by design**, per the
instrument's own prohibition. The Foundation was **not** reconstructed
from the instrument's invariant list, from the semantic-definition
package's questions, or from any other derived material. Its reserved
location at `institution/foundations/operations/` remains empty.

### Consequential limitations

1. **Phase A validation cannot pass.** Two of its ten checks — "Verbatim
   byte/text fidelity of Source 1" and "Verbatim byte/text fidelity of
   Source 2" — have no subject.
2. **Phase B may not be entered.** Its entry condition is "Both complete
   authoritative sources are durably preserved and Phase A validation
   passes."
3. **Phase C may not be entered.** Its entry condition is "Phases A and B
   complete successfully." Two of its ten governing sources are the
   unavailable documents themselves, and a third — the Founder Office
   "§32 policy resolutions" — is available only as this instrument's
   restatement of sections it does not carry. The blocker is substantive,
   not merely procedural: the Requirements Report is the
   sole authority for what Generation One must do, and no invariant list
   substitutes for it. Designing the bounded system boundary, the
   source-of-truth matrix, or the venture, relationship, financial,
   administration and governance seams against requirements never seen
   would manufacture requirements evidence — the defect `fd-0010`
   execution principle 5 and canonical-source-preservation §7 exist to
   prevent.
4. **The semantic-definition package remains provisional.** Its concept
   inventory was to be re-tested against the report; it still cannot be.
