## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The governing readiness question

*"Could Huerta Group accept Client #1 after this is built without improvising
authority, scope, confidentiality, custody, provenance, or institutional
rules?"*

### 2.2 The nine required distinctions

**Organization ≠ Engagement · Person ≠ Organization · Opportunity ≠ Engagement ·
Diagnostic ≠ Engagement · Evidence ≠ Finding · Recommendation ≠ Authorization ·
Client Question ≠ Client Decision · Work Product ≠ Deliverable · Client-specific
learning ≠ Huerta Group institutional knowledge.**

**And the narrowness rule that governs how they are held:** *"Do not create a
database table or software entity merely because an operating concept exists.
Determine the narrowest durable representation appropriate to the actual
repository/runtime architecture."*

### 2.3 The Opportunity → Engagement acceptance gate

**Critical rule:** *"Opportunity acceptance does not itself create an active
engagement."*

An opportunity may become an active engagement **only** where durable written
authorization establishes, at minimum: **parties; authorized body of work or
incorporated scope; material exclusions/boundaries; person/authority acting for
the client; commercial basis or authoritative reference; authorization for
Huerta Group to perform the agreed work; applicable confidentiality/access
obligations or authoritative reference; evidence that authorization was
accepted/executed.**

**Stop behaviour:** *"If any mandatory acceptance prerequisite is absent, the
system must stop truthfully and preserve why the engagement cannot become
active."*

**Administrative dependency:** *"Development must model this gate without
inventing contract doctrine. Administration & Compliance remains responsible for
establishing the appropriate agreement/SOW/contract mechanism. Until that
dependency is satisfied, readiness may not be declared complete on this limb."*

### 2.4 Authority

**Client authority:** *"Do not infer client authority merely from
participation."* The architecture must represent who may authorize the
engagement, provide access, make relevant organizational decisions, approve
changes, receive and approve deliverables, and authorize implementation.

**Huerta Group authority:** the Founder may presently occupy multiple
responsibilities — *"Represent that as current assignment. Do not hard-code a
permanent one-person model."*

### 2.5 Hybrid custody

**Founder determination: hybrid custody is authorized.** Five classes:
`HG_ENGAGEMENT_RECORD` · `CLIENT_SOURCE` · `INTERNAL_WORKING` · `DELIVERABLE` ·
`LEARNING_CANDIDATE`.

*"Governed structured engagement records may use repository-native custody where
appropriate. **Raw confidential client source material must not be placed
indiscriminately into the ordinary institutional corpus.**"* Raw material goes
to *"an organization-controlled, private, client-isolated storage boundary"*
using *"the narrowest technically adequate existing mechanism"*, and **"Do not
build a generalized document-management system."**

### 2.6 Client isolation, and its stop rule

Production multitenancy **NOT REQUIRED**. Required guarantees: attribution to
the correct organization and engagement; **private by default**; explicit access
boundary; **no cross-client retrieval by default**; **operator access scopable
rather than universally granted**; **no automatic inclusion in institution-wide
AI/search/context systems**; **no automatic admission into institutional
knowledge or methodology.**

**The stop rule:** *"If the guarantees cannot be achieved without authenticated
application-level authorization, **stop that affected implementation and return
the specific deficiency.** Do not construct multitenant SaaS infrastructure
merely for future hypothetical scale."*

### 2.7 Seams

**Headquarters** receives **bounded executive representation only**, and
expressly not raw client evidence, stakeholder testimony, detailed findings,
client decisions as source records, confidential content, or detailed work
history. **"Headquarters representation ≠ client-delivery source of truth."**

**QuickBooks remains authoritative for accounting.** Bounded manual references
only; **no invoicing, payments, project accounting, ledger duplication, or
transaction ingestion.**

**Communications:** an engagement-record mechanism for authoritative reference
to material communications. **"Do not build email."** Official domain-based
business email is an Administration & Compliance readiness dependency.

### 2.8 Implementation discretion

**Repository-first:** where governed repository-backed manual operation can
satisfy identity, authority, history, provenance, isolation and custody,
*"prefer it over unnecessary software."*

**Software threshold:** runtime, database or software capability is added *"only
where structured transactional behavior, access enforcement, referential
integrity, or operational reliability genuinely requires it."*

**"Do not infer permission for production client hosting merely from P0
construction authority."**

### 2.9 Verification boundary

Hypothetical mechanical fixtures may represent a placeholder organization,
person, opportunity, disposition mechanics, and the presence or absence of
acceptance prerequisites.

**Never fabricated:** *"stakeholder testimony; client authority; executed
contractual acceptance; confidential source material; diagnostic evidence;
findings; recommendations; client decisions; deliverables; substantive client
facts."*

**Readiness truth test:** *"The system must demonstrate truthful stopping
behavior whenever a mandatory prerequisite is absent."*

### 2.10 The readiness standard

*"Do not declare READY merely because the software works."* READY requires that
Huerta Group could truthfully accept Client #1 without improvising any of
fifteen enumerated things.

**"If an external administrative dependency remains unresolved, report NOT READY
even if the technical P0 backbone itself is complete."**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern); both digest forms are in the affected-artifacts record below.

**Deficiency 2 — a named controlling source does not exist, and construction
proceeded without it.** The instrument's `<governing_sources>` requires
retrieval of the **"Generation One Client Engagement Requirements Report."**
**No such document exists in the repository**, and no identifier `FO-CE-02`
— cited in the instrument's own administrative-dependency clause — appears
anywhere in it. The only `generation-one-requirements-report.md` in the corpus
is the **Internal Operations & Command Platform** requirements report
(`fd-0010`), which contains **zero** occurrences of client engagement,
opportunity, engagement scope, or client authority, and is a different program's
artifact. Construction proceeded on the instrument's own specification, which is
detailed and self-contained. **The consequence is that the required
"requirements-report conformance matrix" could not be produced**, and that
conformance is asserted against the instrument rather than against a
requirements report Development has never seen.

**Deficiency 3 — the construction sequence has no P0-C4.** The instrument
enumerates P0-C1, P0-C2, P0-C3 and P0-C5, and its required-return sections
mirror that gap. Whether P0-C4 was withdrawn, deferred, or omitted in error is
not determinable from the instrument, and Development did not invent one.

**Deficiency 4 — the client custody store was not built, on repository
authority.** `adr-0004` states the current Supabase project **"owns inquiry
records only"**, that it is **"not the universal Company database"**, that
**"no future system stores data in it by default"**, and that **future data
topology is unresolved until the second data-owning system is authorized** — a
decision that must weigh **"sensitivity isolation (client-confidential and
identity records demand the strongest isolation)"**. A client custody store is
that second data-owning system and requires **its own ADR**. Building it under
P0 authority would have improvised the very topology `adr-0004` reserves.

**Deficiency 5 — one isolation guarantee is presently unachievable, and the
instrument's stop rule was applied.** `fd-0037` §2.6 requires operator access
**"scopable rather than universally granted."** `adr-0006` introduces
authentication **"only when the first real internal or client capability
requires it"**, and none exists; access to any store is therefore by shared
credential and **cannot be scoped per operator**. Per the instrument's own
rule — *"stop that affected implementation and return the specific
deficiency"* — that implementation was stopped and the deficiency returned.
