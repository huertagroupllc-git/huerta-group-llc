# fd-0038 — Founder Office Authorization: Institutional Evolution Log Generation One

**Classification: Institutional Record** — determination record (evidentiary,
non-governing). The record evidences the determinations; authority resides with
the Founder Office and the artifacts the determinations govern.

- **Instrument:** 2027 Organizational Readiness Buildout — Institutional
  Evolution Log Generation One, execution package, Founder Office →
  Institutional Platform Development, status **"AUTHORIZED — REPOSITORY-NATIVE
  GOVERNANCE OPERATING SYSTEM"**, priority **Task One**.
- **Received:** 2026-09-09. **Recorded:** 2026-09-09.
- **Starting baseline:** `1a9e3aa`.

## What is authorized

Completion of the Institutional Evolution Log as a durable repository-native
operating system for recording material institutional evolution: canonical
location and format, qualification doctrine, entry schema, maintenance
workflow, an AI-assisted maintenance procedure, an initial evidence-supported
reconciliation, and verification that the repository can serve as durable
institutional memory.

## The determinations

**FD-EL-1 — The Phase Zero architecture stands.** The Evolution Log
established as Phase Zero under `fd-0003` (FO-6) — `institution/evolution/`,
its records, INDEX, TEMPLATE, registry, schema and validator — is the canonical
system. It is to be **completed, not replaced**. No parallel governance
architecture is created.

**FD-EL-2 — Retrospective population is expressly authorized, bounded.**
`institution/evolution/README.md` reserved retrospective records to express
Founder Office authorization. This instrument is that authorization, and it is
bounded: only **material institutional evolution supported by authoritative
repository evidence** may be recorded. A smaller set of strong entries is
preferred to broad speculative history. Records so added carry
`retrospective: true` and cite this determination as their
`retrospectiveAuthorization`.

**FD-EL-3 — Fabricated or inferred history remains prohibited.** Conversation
memory is not evidence. Founder approval is never inferred from
implementation. Where evidence is missing or contradictory, no entry is made
and the gap is surfaced.

**FD-EL-4 — A qualification doctrine is required.** The log must state, in the
repository, what materially qualifies and what is excluded by default, so that
the threshold is applied consistently by operators and by AI assistance rather
than judged afresh each time.

**FD-EL-5 — An AI-assisted maintenance procedure is required.** A durable,
repository-native procedure that future AI assistance follows after a material
determination: inspect repository authority, apply the threshold, draft only
when qualified, cite evidence, and stop on missing or conflicting evidence.

**FD-EL-6 — The authority boundary is unchanged.** The Evolution Log records
that institutional state changed and points at the authorities that changed
it. It is not a second source of truth for the substance of decisions governed
by the Constitution, ADRs, Foundations, Standards, Operational Assets or the
Decision History.

**FD-EL-7 — The log is not a changelog, decision database, project tracker or
transcript archive.** Individual commits, bug fixes, editorial and formatting
corrections, ordinary operations, one-off client records, transient
discussion, and unratified proposals are excluded by default.

**FD-EL-8 — Repository-native only.** No database, Supabase system, CMS, web
interface or runtime service is created for the Evolution Log.

## What is not authorized

Design System work · public-site redesign · Constitution Article III ·
Headquarters feature development · unrelated platform development · broad
reconciliation of the governance repository beyond the Evolution Log · a
duplicate decision database · reconstruction of history from conversation.

## Deficiencies recorded

**D1 — The retrospective rule had no authorizing instrument.** The evolution
schema requires `retrospectiveAuthorization` on any retrospective record, and
no determination had issued it. Recorded here; this determination is that
instrument.

**D2 — The qualification threshold was implicit.** The Phase Zero README said
"significant institutional evolution" without stating the test or the
exclusions. Recorded here; FD-EL-4 requires it stated.

**D3 — No AI-assisted maintenance procedure existed.** Neither `AGENTS.md`,
`CLAUDE.md` nor the log itself instructed AI assistance on qualification,
evidence or stopping. Recorded here; FD-EL-5 requires it.

## Relationship to prior determinations

Refines `fd-0003` (FO-6), which established the log as Phase Zero and withheld
retrospective population. Does not supersede it: the architecture it
established stands unchanged.
