## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The nature of the stage

- "Stage 11 is a disposition stage only. Do not modify Draft v5. Do not apply
  corrections. Do not perform another validation application."
- **Objective:** "to leave the Stage 10 evidence, findings, correction
  proposals, and remaining methodology questions **more determinate than they
  were before Stage 11**."
- **Repository first:** "Do not reconstruct the corpus from this execution
  package, conversational memory, or summaries. Repository-native Stage 10
  records control."

### 2.2 The corpus-integrity gate

Before disposition, verify that authoritative Stage 10 records agree on: finding
count; finding identifiers; finding classifications; proposed-correction count;
proposal identifiers; finding-to-proposal relationships; participant-question
count and identity; PQ-6 status; exercise-matrix classifications.

- **If disagreement exists:** "STOP before substantive disposition. Return:
  **Stage 10 Authoritative Corpus Discrepancy** — including the conflicting
  records; the exact disagreement; **which disposition work cannot proceed
  safely.** Do not choose one version by preference."

### 2.3 The four classification definitions, and the reassessment rules

- **EXERCISED** — "The treatment encountered the authentic condition necessary to
  test it."
- **PARTIALLY EXERCISED** — "Some, but not all, material conditions necessary to
  test the treatment authentically occurred."
- **NOT EXERCISED** — "The treatment could in principle be exercised within the
  internal environment, but its required authentic condition did not occur in
  this application."
- **NOT EXERCISABLE INTERNALLY** — "The required condition cannot authentically
  be produced within the presently available internal environment without
  manufacturing the condition or changing the validation boundary."

**Critical rules:** do not retain *not exercisable internally* merely because
Stage 9 predicted it; **reassess every Stage 10 classification against what
actually occurred**; do not manufacture conditions to change a classification;
**preserve the original Stage 10 matrix historically**; and where classifications
change, "create an **additive** Stage 11 reconciliation rather than rewriting
Stage 10."

### 2.4 The A2 constraint, and its stop rule

- **Boundary:** "Stage 11 does not authorize a general expansion of A2 because
  Stage 10 exposed a recurrence-related deficiency." **A2's non-ordering
  architecture is preserved.**
- **Strict prohibitions.** No proposed treatment may introduce: "evidence
  strength; reliability ranking; credibility ranking; quality ranking; numerical
  weighting; preferential evidence classes; ordering among evidence kinds;
  implicit hierarchy through terminology."
- **Disposition rule:** "Disposition the recurrence deficiency at the narrowest
  semantic level actually supported by Stage 10 evidence."
- **Stop rule:** if it "cannot be addressed without changing A2's non-ordering
  architecture: do not devise drafting language. Return the exact conflict as a
  Founder Office determination requirement."

### 2.5 The Part B non-compliance

- **Status:** "Treat the Stage 10 Part B deviation as **substantive validation
  evidence**."
- **Six required questions:** what Draft v5 literally requires; what the applier
  actually did; why literal compliance was judged operationally defective;
  whether the requirement duplicates information held canonically elsewhere;
  whether the objective can be met by reference, inheritance, exception-only
  reporting or another non-duplicative mechanism; and whether any proposed
  treatment "preserve[s] visibility of access limitations at the point where
  those limitations materially affect an instrument."
- **Governing principle:** "Optimize for **conspicuous and attributable evidence
  limitations, not merely fewer lines**."
- **Anti-duplication principle:** "Mechanical duplication should not bury the
  very access limitation or exception that differs."
- **And:** "Do not assume the Stage 10 applier's workaround is automatically the
  correct methodology correction. Disposition the finding and the proposed
  correction separately."

### 2.6 Finding disposition

Every authoritative finding dispositioned **individually**, using **only the
repository's existing governed disposition vocabulary** — "Do not create a Stage
11-specific disposition class." Fourteen fields are required per finding,
including the Stage 10 limitation affecting interpretation, the affected
provision, related findings, the category, whether accepted treatment would
change methodology semantics, whether correction would require revalidation, and
whether Founder Office authority is required before drafting.

- **Critical distinction:** "**Finding accepted ≠ proposed correction
  accepted.**"

### 2.7 Proposal disposition

Every proposal dispositioned **individually and separately from its finding**,
with twelve required fields including whether it conflicts with A2 non-ordering
or another determination.

- **Rule:** "Where the finding is valid but the proposal is broader than the
  evidence supports, **preserve the finding and narrow/modify/decline the
  proposal** as warranted."

### 2.8 The participant-facing gap

- "**Do not cure the participant-facing validation gap through methodology text
  changes** merely because participant-dependent provisions remain unexercised."
- **Critical distinction:** "**METHOD DEFECT ≠ ABSENCE OF VALIDATION
  EVIDENCE.**"
- "An unexercised provision is **not defective** solely because Stage 10 did not
  encounter the authentic condition required to test it."

### 2.9 Semantics and revalidation

Each accepted correction is tested for whether it clarifies semantics, changes
semantics, changes instrument structure only, changes reporting only, changes
closure or advancement behaviour, or changes validation protocol rather than the
method. **Authority rule:** anything altering methodology semantics beyond
existing determinations is "flag[ged] for Founder Office determination before
recommending drafting."

Revalidation is classified as **NO FRESH VALIDATION · TARGETED REVALIDATION ·
FULL BOUNDED INTERNAL REAPPLICATION · EXTERNAL VALIDATION REQUIRED TO TEST** —
and "Do not use these labels as maturity scores."

### 2.10 Participant questions and PQ-6

The eight questions and PQ-6 are retrieved and preserved **exactly**. Do not
answer them from repository evidence unless Draft v5 explicitly permits that
source for the question; do not answer them from prior Founder conversations; do
not infer likely answers; **do not collapse them into methodology findings.**
Determine only what their unanswered status means for validation completeness,
subject-diagnosis closure, participant-dependent evidence, and future testing.

### 2.11 The consolidated change set

Assembled **only after** all individual dispositions are complete, carrying
source, accepted treatment, affected provision, semantic effect, revalidation
consequence, and whether Founder Office authority remains required.

- "Do not draft corrected methodology wording unless needed minimally to
  describe the nature of the future change. **Stage 11 is not Draft v6
  construction.**"

### 2.12 Maturity and the next stage

- "Stage 11 disposition **does not itself validate Draft v5**." State the exact
  post-disposition maturity; "do not elevate maturity merely because findings
  have now been adjudicated." Prohibited: validated through use; externally
  validated; independently validated; established methodology; Method Library
  ready; standardized.
- **Next stage:** recommend whether bounded Draft v6 construction is warranted —
  "Do not begin it." **If unresolved semantics remain**, "Return those exact
  Founder Office questions first. Do not recommend drafting as though the method
  were determinate." **If the main gap is unexercised participant validation**,
  "Preserve that as a validation-evidence gap. **Do not disguise it as a drafting
  problem.**"

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0030` §3. This
instrument is the program's **second adjudication stage**, and the first to
adjudicate findings produced by an applier other than their adjudicator.
Preserving it verbatim is what allows a later reader to check the dispositions
against the constraints they were made under — particularly the A2 prohibition
list and the rule that a finding accepted is not a proposal accepted, which
together account for five of the seven proposals being narrowed. **The reading
remains the implementing office's and remains reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern), and received without a trailing newline as the four before it were;
both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the corpus this stage adjudicates contains a defect Development
introduced.** The Stage 10 report's §27 exercise-matrix table sums to sixteen
under a heading reading "seventeen-item," and omits CS-17 — reproducing an error
in the Stage 10 applier's summary sentence and adding Development's own failure
to add up a column before returning it. The integrity gate at §2.2 caught it;
**it should have been caught before the Stage 10 return.** Recorded at
`stage-11-exercise-matrix-reconciliation.md` §1.

**Deficiency 3 — the same office adjudicates findings about a method it wrote,
for the second time.** Stage 7 adjudicated deficiencies in a method Development
drafted; Stage 11 adjudicates findings about a version Development drafted,
produced by an applier Development briefed. `fd-0030` §4 Deficiency 4 recorded
the harness dependence at Stage 10; **adjudication inherits it.** The
dispositions are checkable — every one names its evidence and its affected
provision — but they are not independent, and the five narrowings in particular
are Development declining changes to its own text.
