## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The nature of the version

- **Objective:** "Produce Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One Draft v4 as
  the bounded consolidation version of the methodology developed through
  Stages 1–8."
- Draft v4 may incorporate **only** "the seventeen Stage 7 Draft v4 change-set
  items; their controlling Stage 7 dispositions; the six Founder Office
  determinations preserved in fd-0026; strictly necessary internal-consistency
  edits required to make those already-authorized changes coherent."
- **Boundary:** "Do not introduce new methodology substance merely because a new
  version is being written. Draft v4 is a consolidation revision, not a
  methodology redesign."
- **Repository first:** "Do not reconstruct the change set, dispositions, Draft
  v3 wording, or fd-0026 from memory or this execution package when
  repository-native sources exist. Repository evidence controls."

### 2.2 Change scope and the eighteenth-change prohibition

- **Authorized sources, in priority order:** `fd-0026`; the seventeen items and
  their dispositions; **Draft v3 as the substantive baseline** for everything
  not changed; strictly necessary consistency edits. "No lower-priority source
  may override a higher-priority source." "Preserve all Draft v3 substance not
  actually displaced."
- **Critical rule:** "No accepted consolidation item may disappear into
  generalized prose without traceability."
- **Prohibition:** "Do not introduce an eighteenth substantive methodology
  change."
- **Prohibited changes, enumerated:** new diagnostic stages; new instruments not
  directly required by an accepted item; new evidence classes beyond authorized
  treatment; new organizational scoring; maturity models; new automation
  concepts; new software assumptions; new external-client defaults; new
  commercial framing; "methodology simplification not traceable to an authorized
  item"; "methodology expansion for completeness or elegance."

### 2.3 `fd-0026` precedence

- **General rule:** "For CS-01, CS-03, and CS-14, draft from fd-0026 rather than
  from any narrower Stage 7 wording."
- **CS-01** — the source-kind attribute answers only "What kind of thing
  supplied this claim?"; it must distinguish at minimum person/stakeholder
  account, organizational or institutional record, supplied institutional
  source, systematic verification/validation record, and other genuinely
  evidenced kinds. **Strict prohibitions:** "No source-kind ordering. No
  source-kind ranking. No source-kind weighting. No source-kind scoring. No
  implied hierarchy. No conversion of source kind into evidence strength."
  Evidence quality remains separate and expressible only through limitations,
  contradictions, provenance, freshness, coverage, contextual explanation.
  "Preserve A2's non-ordering rule."
- **CS-03** — "'Diagnostic team' means the actual institutional or individual
  actor materially performing the diagnostic." Both institutional and
  individual/agent prior involvement are disclosed where relevant. **Non-binary
  rule:** "Do not implement 'ever interacted with subject = non-independent.'"
  Where material, record who or what participated, capacity, subject area,
  whether independence is limited in that area, and the resulting limitation.
  Institutional dependence is the structural default where the diagnosing
  institution shaped the subject; "a different individual or agent does not
  automatically remove that dependence."
- **CS-14** — A17 rule 5 must "state the minimum binding handling principle;
  cite the governing Huerta Group confidentiality doctrine where available;
  record whether that governing doctrine was actually held; establish the
  conservative default where the doctrine is unavailable or cannot govern the
  engagement." **Do not copy the full parent doctrine.** The conservative
  default is stated in the instrument, as is the claim-preservation rule
  ("that evidence exists; its provenance; the diagnostic conclusion supported
  within the authorized boundary; necessary limitations") and the
  beyond-default rule requiring confidentiality terms or client authority
  before proceeding.
- **CS-02** — "A1 preserves the subject's terminology. A2 governs the
  diagnostic's own evidence classification." A subject's own evidence taxonomy
  is preserved faithfully as subject terminology; it may not "replace or mutate
  A2 during the diagnostic"; a stronger subject scheme "may become later
  methodology-evolution evidence but is not automatically incorporated."
  Consistency check: "Ensure no Draft v4 wording causes A1 and A2 to compete for
  authority."
- **CS-07** — identity-of-record is a required output, defined, produced by
  **A4**, supported but not produced by A3, and "remains required even where
  authoritative identity evidence is absent or conflicted; it must report that
  truth rather than disappear." Instrument 10 remains a required output produced
  throughout; "returning unresolved questions to client authority is an
  advancement action/outcome, not the same object as Instrument 10. Use distinct
  terminology." Exclusions are produced "where relevant diagnostic domains
  discover them"; A18 "may aggregate them in final output." A18 must require
  "source-of-truth boundaries sufficiently identified for diagnosis, including
  unresolved or contested authority where that is the truthful result," and must
  not require the diagnostic "to institutionally resolve every source authority
  before it can identify the source-of-truth boundary."
- **CS-12** — Draft v4 must distinguish "application/stage work completed;
  subject diagnosis complete; advancement permitted; closure condition
  unresolved." Where a mandatory closure condition is unsatisfied, work may
  stop, be preserved and returned, but "subject diagnosis may not be represented
  as unqualified complete," and a conspicuous unresolved-closure statement is
  required. "Unresolved closure is not a sixth diagnostic outcome." "Do not
  generalize this into a Huerta Group-wide project lifecycle."

### 2.4 The historical-version gate

- **Before drafting:** locate the canonical path and recorded digest for each of
  Drafts v1, v2 and v3; "verify each draft byte-identically against its
  established digest"; "record the verification results before creating Draft
  v4."
- **If any digest fails:** "STOP before Draft v4 construction. Return the exact
  historical-version integrity discrepancy. Do not repair, normalize,
  regenerate, or overwrite the historical draft."
- **Preservation rule:** "Draft v1, Draft v2, and Draft v3 remain immutable
  historical/tested versions."
- **Path rule:** "Draft v4 must be created at a new governed path using the
  existing methodology versioning convention."
- **After construction:** recalculate and verify all three again — "All three
  must remain byte-identical."

### 2.5 A10's ambiguity

- **Status:** "OPEN LIMITATION — NOT A DRAFTING DISCRETION."
- **Requirements:** retain A10's existing substance except where another
  authorized Stage 9 change directly requires modification; "do not resolve the
  ambiguity through wording preference"; "do not normalize the ambiguity away";
  "do not infer Founder intent"; "identify the ambiguity explicitly in Draft
  v4's limitations/open-questions treatment"; "preserve provenance to the
  applicable Stage 7/8 records."
- **Posture:** "The ambiguity does not block Draft v4 construction."

### 2.6 Evidence inheritance

Four classifications, fixed by the instrument:
**UNCHANGED_WITH_PRIOR_INTERNAL_EVIDENCE** — "materially unchanged methodology
behavior already exercised in earlier internal testing";
**MODIFIED_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION** — "prior evidence/rationale informs the
change but the method behavior or requirement changed materially";
**NEW_STRUCTURAL_TREATMENT_REQUIRES_VALIDATION** — "a new structural treatment
not directly exercised in prior tested versions";
**UNRESOLVED_LIMITATION** — "where Stage 9 expressly preserves an ambiguity or
unvalidated condition."

- **Rule:** "Do not claim changed elements are tested merely because their
  predecessor existed in Draft v3."
- **Rule:** "Do not erase prior evidence. Distinguish inherited rationale from
  inherited validation."

### 2.7 The bounded consistency review

Performed "after Draft v4 text and instruments are complete," it must confirm
**twenty-seven** specified matters, from A1/A2 precedence through the
separation of source kind from evidence class, identity-of-record production,
outcome-versus-instrument distinction, the four closure states, confidentiality
operability without the parent doctrine, both prior-involvement layers, the
sufficiency presentation, one-sided evidence, the exclusion of declined and
deferred items, the exclusion of replication mechanics, and A10's ambiguity
remaining visible.

- **Scope:** "This is a consistency review only. Do not treat it as behavioral
  validation of Draft v4."

### 2.8 Maturity posture

- **Draft v4 classification:** "PROVISIONAL DRAFT — UNTESTED AS A VERSION."
- "Prior internal testing remains valid evidence for materially unchanged
  methodology elements."
- "Modified elements inherit rationale/provenance but require fresh validation
  before they can inherit behavioral validation claims."
- "Founder Office-directed structural treatments require validation even where
  they correct known deficiencies."
- **Ceiling:** "Do not elevate the Organizational Diagnostic beyond its current
  internally tested provisional methodology posture through Stage 9 alone."
- **And:** "Do not label Draft v4 tested from repository consistency checks."

### 2.9 The new-semantic-question stop rule

Where implementing an adopted item "exposes a genuinely new methodology-semantic
question" not determinately answered by Draft v3, the Stage 7 disposition,
`fd-0026`, or existing Methodology Foundation authority, "stop only the affected
change" and record the affected item, the exact question, why existing authority
cannot resolve it, the Draft v4 section affected, and whether the remainder can
be completed without prejudicing the issue. "Do not resolve it through editorial
preference."

### 2.10 The validation-stage recommendation

"Recommend only the smallest meaningful next validation stage justified by the
actual changed elements in Draft v4." "Do not automatically recommend external
application merely because Draft v4 exists. Determine the minimum validation
needed to establish that the consolidation behaves as intended."
**Boundary:** "Recommendation is not authorization."

### 2.11 Repository execution

- **Authorized changes:** the Draft v4 file; its integrity/version record; the
  seventeen-item traceability matrix; applicable methodology index, manifest and
  version references; the Stage 9 completion record; "strictly necessary
  repository metadata directly required to register the new draft version."
- **Not authorized:** "historical draft edits; unrelated methodology
  restructuring; new governance outside the Stage 7/8 change set; new validation
  application records."
- **CI:** "Do not create unrelated modifications merely to manufacture a workflow
  run. If CI runs normally against the Stage 9 commit, report its actual
  result."

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0026` §3. This
instrument authorizes the **first version of the method with no application
behind it**, and it does so while forbidding every route by which such a version
could be mistaken for a tested one. Preserving it verbatim is what makes the
boundary auditable: a later reader can check what Stage 9 was permitted to build
against what Draft v4 actually claims. **The reading remains the implementing
office's and remains reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — the instrument was received without a trailing newline.**
Every earlier instrument ended with one. The preserved fenced block adds the
byte the form requires, so the preservation record carries **two** digests: the
one produced by its own stated extraction method, and the one of the instrument
exactly as received. Recorded rather than smoothed over, because a preservation
record that quietly normalizes bytes is worth less than one that says which byte
it added.

**Deficiency 3 — the determination that authorizes this stage also supplies the
review that checks it.** The twenty-seven-point consistency review is the
instrument's own list, run by the office that did the drafting, on its own
output. That is the same structural dependence Stage 6 was built to test and
`fd-0025` §2.8 recorded, and it is not cured by the review being thorough. It is
recorded here so that the review's results are read as **the drafter's own
account of its own coherence**, which is what they are.
