## Determination (verbatim from the instrument)

**FD-V2 — Venture Register Topology.** The approved architecture:

- *Institutional plane:* "Huerta Group LLC controls venture standing,
  charter, grants, reserved authority, lifecycle conditions,
  parent/venture relationships, and other institution-wide
  determinations."
- *Operational plane:* "Each admitted venture may govern and operate
  itself within the authority actually delegated to it."
- *Core rule:* "Repository location does not determine authority. The
  governing plane of the fact does."
- *Authority rule:* "Institutional authority remains singular.
  Operational governance may be distributed. Crossing from operational
  authority into institutional authority requires an explicit
  institutional-plane act."

The determination's accompanying doctrine — the five existing-records
rules for Huerta Group Publishing, the three institutional-register
rules, the five identity-and-reference rules, and the three
synchronization rules — is preserved verbatim and completely in the
canonical instrument; this record does not duplicate it.

## Resolution of the reserved matter

FD-V2 resolves the register-topology question reserved by the
Corporate Venture Governance — Phase 1 Blueprint (§28, FD-V2) and
identified but not resolved in FD-V1 Charter §10.1: the parent
determinations register **remains the single institutional-plane
determination register**, and venture repositories hold **defined
standing** — operational decision histories, standards, validation
systems, blueprints, as-built records, and other governance assets
that are authoritative within their delegated plane without becoming
parallel institutional authority. The interim rule the Charter stated
("until resolved, parent-level acts are recorded only in the parent
determinations register") is confirmed as the permanent rule.

## Historical rule (preserved distinction)

Huerta Group Publishing's pre-existing operational governance records
— including records whose language refers to "Founder Office" — are
preserved as written and are not rewritten. Their authority context is
determined by FD-V2's planes: they governed, and govern, matters
within delegated venture authority; they never created and cannot
create or modify institutional-plane authority. Governance records
authority; it does not create history.

## Effect and boundaries

This determination effects register topology only. It creates no new
institutional authority, admits no venture, makes no capability grant,
changes no charter, and authorizes no cross-repository
synchronization infrastructure, no application or website change, and
no public representation. Per the instrument: FD-V3 (name-use
policy), FD-V4 (parent-platform representation), and FD-V5–FD-V8
remain open Founder Office matters and are not begun by this record;
FD-V4 must establish the public-truth boundary before any
corresponding website implementation occurs.
