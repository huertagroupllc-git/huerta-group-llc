## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 CS-01 — Source kind versus evidence quality

- **Decision:** "APPROVE a descriptive source-kind attribute. The source-kind
  attribute answers only: 'What kind of thing supplied this claim?' It does not
  answer: 'How good, reliable, strong, credible, or persuasive is this
  evidence?'"
- **Permitted structure:** values must remain "categorical; descriptive;
  organization-neutral; non-ordinal."
- **Minimum capability:** Draft v4 must distinguish at minimum "person /
  stakeholder account; organizational or institutional record; supplied
  institutional source; systematic verification / validation record; other
  source kinds genuinely required by application evidence."
- **Normalization authority:** "Development may normalize exact labels during
  future Draft v4 construction provided the categories remain descriptive and
  non-hierarchical."
- **Critical boundary:** "A systematic verification record may be distinguished
  from a stale narrative record because they are **different evidence-artifact
  kinds**, not because one is methodologically ranked above the other."
- **Evidence quality** remains separate and may be expressed only through
  limitations, contradictions, provenance, freshness, coverage, and contextual
  explanation. "Do not encode evidence quality through the source-kind field."
- "A2's existing non-ordering rule remains controlling."

### 2.2 CS-02 — A1 terminology versus A2 evidence classification

- **Decision:** "A2 governs the diagnostic's own evidence classification. A1
  governs the subject's terminology."
- Where the subject has its own evidence taxonomy, validation vocabulary or
  evidence-strength scheme, "record it faithfully as part of the subject's own
  terminology and governance."
- **Diagnostic rule:** "Use the subject's scheme when describing how the subject
  itself classifies evidence. Do not allow the subject's scheme to replace,
  redefine, or silently modify A2 for purposes of the diagnostic's
  cross-application evidence model."
- **Reason:** "The diagnostic requires a stable cross-subject
  evidence-classification framework while still representing the subject's own
  evidence language accurately."
- **Future methodology evolution:** where a subject's framework "appears
  materially stronger, more precise, or more useful than A2, preserve that
  observation as evidence for later methodology evolution. Do not substitute
  that scheme during the current engagement merely because it appears superior."
- **Governing distinction:** "A1 preserves the subject's language. A2 preserves
  the diagnostic's evidence semantics. **Neither erases the other.**"

### 2.3 CS-03 — Prior involvement and the meaning of "diagnostic team"

- **Decision:** "'Diagnostic team' means the actual institutional or individual
  actor materially performing the diagnostic."
- **Disclosure rule:** the method must disclose **both** "the institutional
  relationship" and "the relevant human/agent relationship where they differ."
- **Material prior involvement** — where the diagnosing actor previously
  participated in work "that could reasonably shape interpretation of the
  subject", including designing, constructing, governing, implementing, or
  materially evaluating the subject's systems; "authoring substantial portions
  of evidence later diagnosed"; or "repeatedly advising on the same operational
  structures now under diagnosis."
- **Non-material exposure:** "Mere incidental familiarity, one-off exposure, or
  ordinary awareness does not automatically make a diagnosis non-independent."
- **Required disclosure** where material: who or what participated; in what
  capacity; what portion of the subject; "whether the current diagnosis
  therefore lacks independence in that area"; and "what limitation that
  creates."
- **Institutional default:** institutional prior involvement is the default
  structural reading "because replacing an individual or agent does not
  eliminate prior involvement by the institution that designed, constructed, or
  governed the subject."
- **Individual layer:** individual or agent authorship "must also be disclosed
  where relevant because it may create an additional dependence layer."
- **Scope boundary:** "This does not establish a universal independence doctrine
  for all Huerta Group consulting work. It governs disclosure within the
  Organizational Diagnostic."

### 2.4 CS-07 — Identity-of-record, and A18 internal consistency

- **Identity-of-record: RETAINED and defined.** "A sourced statement of what the
  organization's own authoritative or governing evidence establishes about: what
  the organization is; what it exists to do; the obligations or purposes that
  define it" — together with explicit notation where "no authoritative identity
  record exists; authoritative identity sources conflict; identity authority
  remains unresolved."
- **What it is not:** "a consultant-written mission statement; public website
  copy treated as identity by default; a summary invented from observed
  activity; a judgement about what the organization ought to become."
- **Producing stage:** "Purpose and Identity discovery / A4." Supporting
  evidence may come from A3, "but A4 owns production of the identity-of-record
  output."
- **Absence rule:** where no authoritative identity source is held or identity
  authority is unresolved, "identity-of-record remains a required output and
  must state that truthfully rather than being omitted."
- **A18 consistency:** Instrument 10 remains a required output produced
  throughout; "'Return unresolved questions to client authority' is an
  advancement outcome/action and is **not the same object** as Instrument 10.
  Future Draft v4 terminology must keep those distinct."
- **Explicit exclusions:** "produced where the relevant diagnostic domain
  discovers them. Do not artificially assign them to one universal stage. A18
  may aggregate them as a required final output."
- **Source-of-truth requirement:** "A18 should ask whether source-of-truth
  boundaries are **sufficiently identified** for diagnosis. Do not require all
  source authorities to be institutionally established before diagnosis may
  proceed." Minimum-evidence condition: "Source-of-truth boundaries sufficiently
  identified, **including unresolved or contested authority where that is the
  truthful finding**."

### 2.5 CS-12 — Closure with A17 rule 7 unsatisfied

- **Decision:** "A subject diagnosis **may close as an application record** with
  an unresolved mandatory closure condition, but it **may not be represented as
  an unqualified completed diagnosis**."
- **Required distinctions:** "application/stage work completed; subject
  diagnosis complete; advancement permitted; closure condition unresolved."
- Where a mandatory closure condition remains unsatisfied: "the application may
  stop; its work may be preserved; its outcome may be returned; the diagnosis
  must carry a conspicuous unresolved-closure statement."
- **The statement must identify:** the unsatisfied condition; why it remains
  unsatisfied; who or what must satisfy it, if known; "what consequence the
  unresolved condition has for use of the diagnosis."
- **Classification boundary:** "This is **not a sixth diagnostic outcome**. It
  is a condition on the status and interpretation of the diagnostic record."
- **Language rule:** "Do not use 'closed', 'complete', or equivalent unqualified
  language for the subject diagnosis when a mandatory closure condition remains
  unsatisfied."
- **Scope boundary:** diagnostic-method specific; "does not establish a general
  Huerta Group project-management lifecycle."

### 2.6 CS-14 — The confidentiality-doctrine dependency

- **Decision:** "Methodology Foundation v1 §7.5 **does not travel by
  implication** merely because the Organizational Diagnostic cites it."
- **Client-facing requirement:** such a diagnostic "may not depend on an
  inaccessible parent governance document for a rule necessary to handle
  confidential material safely."
- **No full duplication:** "Draft v4 should not duplicate the full
  confidentiality doctrine because doing so would create a second governed
  source and drift risk."
- **A17 rule 5 future requirement:** "state the minimum binding handling
  principle necessary for safe diagnostic operation; cite the governing Huerta
  Group confidentiality doctrine where available; record whether that governing
  doctrine was actually held by the diagnostic team; require a conservative
  default where the governing doctrine is not held or cannot govern the
  engagement."
- **The conservative default, stated:** "Do not reproduce, transfer, abstract
  into another institutional corpus, or disclose client-specific/confidential
  material beyond what is necessary to perform and evidence the authorized
  diagnostic."
- **Claim preservation:** where a material claim can be preserved without
  reproducing confidential substance, preserve "the existence of the evidence;
  its provenance; the diagnostic conclusion it supports within the permitted
  boundary; any necessary limitation — without copying the confidential
  substance itself."
- **Beyond the default:** "appropriate confidentiality terms or client authority
  must be established before proceeding."

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — three determinations expand their Stage 7 change-set items.**
CS-01's determination supplies a five-value minimum capability where the change
set proposed four categories; CS-03's requires disclosure of **both** the
institutional and individual layers where the change set offered them as
alternatives ("the institution or the individual"); CS-14's requires A17 rule 5
to **state a minimum binding handling principle**, which the change set had
expressly avoided proposing. Each expansion is the Founder Office's to make and
is recorded here so that a later draft's scope is measured against the
determination rather than against the narrower change-set text.

**Deficiency 3 — one Stage 7 ambiguity is settled by a determination that did
not name it.** CS-07's source-of-truth requirement resolves the returned
ambiguity on whether A18 element 4 asks for boundaries *identified* or
*established* — it asks for *sufficiently identified*. The instrument does not
say it is doing this. The reconciliation records it explicitly so the ambiguity
is not left standing in the record by oversight.
