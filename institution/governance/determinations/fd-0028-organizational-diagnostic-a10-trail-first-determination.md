## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The reading adopted

- **Selected reading:** "READING A".
- **Governing semantics:** "The four-item A10 list identifies trail-source
  categories to be pursued where they exist and are reasonably accessible. It
  is **not a conjunctive four-part completion gate**."
- **Trail-first, defined:** "Work must be discovered from evidence of the last
  actual instance **before** a described or documented process is accepted as
  the representation of how the work occurs."
- **The four trail-source categories:** artifacts produced; records and
  timestamps; correspondence around the work; a stakeholder walkthrough of the
  last actual instance.
- **Critical boundary:** "These are categories of trail evidence. They are not
  four mandatory limbs that must all exist and all be obtained before A10 may
  be considered performed."

### 2.2 The "as available" rule, and what it may not become

- **Prohibition:** "'As available' must not be implemented as permission to use
  whichever evidence source is easiest while ignoring the rest."
- **For each materially relevant category**, the team must determine and record:
  "whether the source appears to exist; whether it is reasonably accessible
  within the authorized diagnostic boundary; whether it was actually obtained;
  if not obtained, why it was not obtained."
- **Silent-omission rule:** "A materially relevant source that appears to exist
  may not be silently omitted merely because another source already produced a
  plausible trail."
- **Prior familiarity:** "Prior familiarity with the workflow does not satisfy
  trail-first evidence acquisition."

### 2.3 When the procedure is performed

Five conditions, all stated by the instrument:

1. "The diagnostic team obtains and examines the materially relevant trail
   evidence reasonably available within the authorized boundary."
2. "Unobtained, unavailable, nonexistent, or inaccessible trail-source
   categories are explicitly recorded."
3. "The actual-work trail is recorded before a described or documented process
   is accepted as the workflow representation."
4. "The described/documented process is then recorded separately."
5. "The actual trail and the described/documented process are compared."

- **Evidentiary-gap rule:** "The A10 procedure may be performed even where
  evidentiary gaps remain."

### 2.4 What a missing source does

- **Rule:** "An unobtained listed source does not automatically mean A10 was not
  performed."
- **It instead affects:** "evidentiary sufficiency; confidence/context expressed
  through the existing non-scored evidence framework; what findings may
  legitimately be made; Instrument 5's sufficiency marker; potentially A18
  advancement."
- **Visibility:** "The missing source and reason must remain visible."
- **Anti-inference:** "Missing evidence must never be converted into an
  assumption that the missing source would have agreed with the evidence
  actually obtained."

### 2.5 Instrument 5's sufficiency states

- **Critical distinction:** "A10 procedure performed ≠ evidence sufficient."
- **"completed"** — where materially relevant categories were obtained and no
  material evidentiary gap remains.
- **"completed with named gaps"** — where A10 was performed, one or more
  materially relevant sources were unavailable or unobtained, and "the remaining
  trail is still sufficient to construct a meaningful workflow representation."
- **"blocked by insufficient evidence"** — where "available trail evidence is
  too incomplete to support a meaningful workflow representation."
- **Rule:** "Do not mark every artifact-derived or partial trail as completed
  automatically."

### 2.6 A10 versus A18

- "A10 does not independently determine advancement."
- A18 "remains responsible for determining whether workflows, handoffs,
  dependencies, and related operating conditions are sufficiently understood to
  proceed."
- **Permitted condition, stated expressly:** "A10 performed + evidence gaps +
  A18 not satisfied."
- "Completion of the A10 procedure does not establish that the workflow is
  sufficiently understood."

### 2.7 Stakeholder evidence and observation

- "This A10 determination does not diminish existing stakeholder-evidence
  requirements elsewhere in the diagnostic."
- "A10 does not make a stakeholder walkthrough a universal per-workflow
  completion gate."
- **Load-bearing workflows:** where a walkthrough "is materially available and
  relevant," the team should "obtain it; or explicitly record why it was not
  obtained."
- "Where stakeholder evidence is independently required by another methodology
  provision, failure to obtain it remains consequential under that provision."
- **Observation:** the preference for observing load-bearing work as performed
  is preserved, and "does not become a mandatory fifth A10 trail limb."

### 2.8 The two historical applications

- "Stage 5 applied what is now the adopted Reading A."
- "Stage 6 applied what was at the time a faithful Reading B."
- "Do not rewrite Stage 5 or Stage 6." "Do not retroactively characterize either
  author as having applied the method incorrectly."
- **Historical value:** "Preserve the divergence as methodology-development
  evidence showing that A10 was genuinely ambiguous before Founder Office
  determination."

### 2.9 The reconciliation, its bounds, and its stop rule

- **Authorization:** "Make only the minimum Draft v4 wording reconciliation
  required to encode the Founder Office determination."
- **Prohibited expansion:** no new instrument; no new evidence class unless
  already authorized elsewhere in Draft v4; no evidence hierarchy; no scoring;
  no workflow maturity concepts; "do not expand A10 beyond what the
  determination requires."
- **Status effect:** "Remove A10 from the set of unresolved semantic ambiguities
  after successful reconciliation. Preserve its historical provenance and prior
  ambiguity record."
- **Chronology to preserve:** "Draft v4 creation with unresolved A10 ambiguity;
  Founder Office A10 determination afterward; bounded A10 reconciliation
  afterward."
- **The versioning stop rule, verbatim:** "Use the repository's existing
  methodology/versioning rules to determine whether this bounded Founder Office
  reconciliation remains Draft v4 at a new recorded digest/revision state or
  requires another repository-native representation. **Do not create Draft v5.
  If existing governance cannot represent an authorized same-version semantic
  reconciliation truthfully without creating Draft v5, stop before choosing and
  return that repository-governance issue.**"

### 2.10 Validation package, and its boundary

- **Purpose:** "Identify the smallest meaningful validation package required to
  test Draft v4 as a version after the A10 semantic prerequisite is resolved."
  **"Do not perform the validation."**
- **Direction:** "one bounded internal application conducted by an author who
  did not draft Draft v4, against an internal subject capable of exercising:
  participant evidence; confidential-material handling; subject-governed
  terminology; materially relevant trail-first workflow discovery where
  possible."
- **Independence boundary:** "Do not claim evaluator independence unless the
  actual conditions support it. The objective is **bounded non-authoring
  validation, not false independence**."
- **Subject selection:** "Do not select a subject merely because it is
  convenient if it cannot exercise the changed Draft v4 features."
  Validation "must remain diagnostic/read-only unless separately authorized."
- **No scoring:** "Do not propose numerical scoring as the validation method."
- **The package must not:** "authorize the application; pre-answer expected
  diagnostic findings; seed expected subject conclusions; change Draft v4;
  create Draft v5; establish external validity; create new methodology."
- **Maturity posture:** Draft v4 remains a provisional draft untested as a
  version, because "resolving A10 removes the semantic ambiguity but does not
  constitute behavioral application evidence." Not to be claimed: tested
  baseline; validated through use; externally validated; independently
  validated; standardized; Method Library ready.

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0027` §3. This
instrument is the **first Founder Office answer to a methodology question that
the method's own replication exposed** — an ambiguity no author invented, that
two faithful readings of one paragraph produced, and that changed an element
answer and an instrument's completeness. Preserving it verbatim is what lets a
later reader check the adopted semantics against what a later draft claims they
were. **The reading remains the implementing office's and remains reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern). As with the Stage 9 instrument, this one was received **without a
trailing newline**; both digest forms are recorded in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the determination and the reconciliation authorization arrived
in one instrument.** The office that returned the A10 question is the office
that now implements the answer, in the same execution, against wording it
drafted. That is not a defect in the determination — the answer is the Founder
Office's — but it means **no independent party checked that the reconciled
wording says what the determination says**. The reconciliation record therefore
sets the prepared wording beside the determination clause by clause, so the
comparison is available to a reader who did not write either.

**Deficiency 3 — the instrument's own stop rule was triggered, and this record
was written anyway.** `fd-0028` records a determination whose Draft v4
implementation is **pending a separate governance decision** (§5). A
determination record that describes an adopted reading not yet present in the
draft it governs is a divided state, and it is recorded as divided rather than
being made to look settled. Until the versioning question is answered, **A10 is
semantically determined and textually unreconciled**, and no record in this
corpus may state otherwise.

## 5. The versioning question returned, not answered

The reconciliation was prepared and **not applied**. Methodology Foundation v1
§6.4 provides that a substantive refinement — one changing "meaning," "scope,"
"requirements," "methodological logic," "decision criteria," "required
evidence," "approved application," "limitations," or "disclosure boundaries" —
**"require[s] a new version and approval appropriate to the change."** The A10
reconciliation changes meaning, requirements, required evidence and limitations,
and appears on none of §6.4's non-substantive list. The instrument forbids
Draft v5. Development therefore stopped **before choosing** a versioning
treatment, as §2.9 requires, and returned the issue.

The full statement of the issue, the three candidate representations and their
governance consequences, and the prepared wording are recorded at
`a10-determination-reconciliation-record.md`. **Development does not choose
among them.**
