## The determinations

**FD-G2-1 — Materiality is determined.** The establishment, authentic-use
validation, acceptance and freezing of Headquarters Client Engagement
Generation Two **constitutes material institutional evolution** under the
Evolution Log qualification doctrine. This determination supplies the
authoritative institutional evidence that the doctrine requires and that the
Institutional Governance Repository did not previously hold.

**FD-G2-2 — Exactly one entry.** One Evolution Log entry records the
institutional transition from the prior client-engagement state to the accepted
Generation Two operating baseline. No implementation event inside the
Generation Two programme — remediation increments, interface work, migrations,
probes, report exercises — is elevated into an entry of its own.

**FD-G2-3 — Generation Two is the governed client-engagement operating
baseline.** It is Huerta Group's operating model for client-engagement work
within Headquarters. It does **not** replace repository governance authority,
Corporate Operations custody, QuickBooks accounting authority, or the governed
domain model beneath Headquarters.

**FD-G2-4 — Validation was synthetic.** The client-engagement lifecycle was
exercised end to end under governed synthetic validation. No authentic client
information has been admitted.

**FD-G2-5 — The accepted and frozen baseline is `e728a18`.**

**FD-G2-6 — Discretionary development is frozen.** Further Generation Two
change requires concrete operational evidence, bounded launch hardening, or
another independently governed institutional authorization. Routine fixes and
bounded launch hardening do not themselves become Evolution Log entries.

**FD-G2-7 — The universal classification validator is withheld.** The
observation returned under `fd-0038` — that every future `fd-NNNN` might be
required to declare an Evolution classification — is **not** to be
implemented. The Founder Office withholds the mechanism unless authentic
maintenance use later demonstrates a need.

## What is not authorized

Evolution Log redesign · qualification doctrine change · schema or template
change · governance automation · Headquarters development · launch hardening ·
Design System work · public-site work · Constitution work · any other
institutional-platform increment.
