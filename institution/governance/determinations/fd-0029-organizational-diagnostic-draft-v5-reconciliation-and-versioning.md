## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The versioning determination

- **Founder Office decision:** "The A10 reconciliation is a substantive
  methodology refinement under Methodology Foundation v1 §6.4."
- **Consequence:** "Draft v4 must not be modified in place. Create Draft v5 as
  a new version."
- **The version's whole reason:** "Draft v4 consolidation + Founder-determined
  A10 Reading A reconciliation." "Do not reopen the seventeen-item Stage 7
  consolidation. Do not introduce any unrelated methodology revision. Do not
  validate Draft v5 during this execution."

### 2.2 Draft v4's preserved status

- Draft v4 remains **"PROVISIONAL DRAFT — UNTESTED AS A VERSION."**
- **Its historical meaning, stated by the instrument:** "Draft v4 is the
  historical Stage 9 consolidation version whose text still contains the A10
  ambiguity later resolved by fd-0028."
- **Prohibition:** "Do not rewrite Draft v4 to make it retrospectively agree
  with fd-0028."
- **Pre-construction gate:** verify Drafts v1, v2, v3 and v4 against their
  established digests; on any failure, "Stop before Draft v5 construction.
  Return the exact integrity discrepancy. Do not repair or normalize historical
  drafts."

### 2.3 Draft v5's scope, and its hard boundary

- **Baseline:** "Draft v4 is the direct textual baseline for Draft v5."
- **Permitted change locations, exhaustively:** A10; Instrument 5; A20; Part D;
  version/status header; "strictly necessary cross-reference text"; "strictly
  necessary traceability/version-history text."
- **Hard boundary:** "No other Stage 9 methodology element may be revised."
- **Not to be reopened:** the seventeen Stage 7 change-set items; their
  dispositions; the `fd-0026` determinations; other Draft v4 terminology;
  instrument architecture; method progression; method maturity rules;
  external-validation doctrine.
- **A20 and Part D specifically:** changed "only to the extent strictly
  required to keep advancement, reporting, limitations, or procedural
  interpretation internally consistent with fd-0028," never "as an opportunity
  to revise unrelated methodology substance," and "every A20 or Part D change
  must cite its direct necessity to the A10 determination."

### 2.4 The prepared reconciliation as the implementation basis

- "Use the already-prepared clause-by-clause A10 reconciliation as the
  implementation basis."
- "Do not reopen the semantics settled by fd-0028."
- "Do not editorially redesign A10 merely because a new version is being
  created."
- **If applying it exposes a genuinely new semantic conflict outside the
  bounded A10 consequence:** stop the affected edit, identify the conflict, the
  Draft v5 location affected and the governing sources in tension, and return
  the question. "Do not expand Draft v5 to solve it."

The instrument restates `fd-0028`'s substance in full — the governing meaning
of trail-first; the four source categories and the rule that they are "not four
mandatory conjunctive limbs"; the "as available" four-fact standard; the
no-convenience-omission rule; prior familiarity as no substitute; the five
conditions under which the procedure is performed; the gap rule; the
anti-inference rule; Instrument 5's three sufficiency values; A18's continuing
advancement authority and the permitted state *A10 performed + evidence gaps +
A18 not satisfied*; and the stakeholder-walkthrough and observation boundaries.

### 2.5 Draft v5's evidence status

- **Unchanged elements:** "may retain their existing historical internal
  evidence qualification."
- **The reconciled A10 treatment carries:** "Founder Office semantic authority;
  historical rationale; Stage 5/6 development evidence; **but no fresh Draft v5
  behavioral validation.**"
- **Rule:** "Do not describe Draft v5 as tested merely because the semantic
  question is now resolved."
- **Header must state that Draft v5 is not:** "a new discovery generation; a
  reopening of Stage 7; a validation result; an established method; an
  externally validated method."

### 2.6 Stage 5 and Stage 6

- **Stage 5:** "Preserve exactly as an application conducted under the
  then-ambiguous A10 wording and consistent with what later became Reading A."
- **Stage 6:** "Preserve exactly as an application conducted under the

- "Their divergence remains methodology-development evidence explaining why

### 2.7 The validation package

- **Purpose of the reassessment:** "Determine whether the previously prepared
  Draft v4 validation package remains valid when applied to Draft v5." **"Do
  not begin validation."**
- **Six assessment questions:** whether the non-authoring requirement remains

  adequately exercises the now-determinate A10 Reading A behaviour; whether any

- **Expected disposition:** "Prefer reuse of the existing validation package if
  it remains substantively valid. Make only proposal-level terminology/
  version-reference updates necessary to target Draft v5. **Do not redesign
  validation unless the version change actually requires it.**"

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0028` §3. This
instrument settles a **repository-governance** question rather than a
methodology-semantic one, and it is the first to do so: it decides how an
authorized methodology change is represented in the version chain. Preserving it
verbatim is what makes the resulting version identity auditable — a later reader
can check why Draft v5 exists against what was actually authorized, rather than
inferring it from the draft's own header. **The reading remains the
implementing office's and remains reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern). As with the two instruments before it, this one arrived **without a
trailing newline**; both digest forms are recorded in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the drafting office implemented its own prepared wording.**
The prepared reconciliation was written by Development at the previous
execution and applied by Development at this one, against a determination
Development also transcribed. `fd-0028` §4 Deficiency 2 recorded the same
structural dependence; **it is not cured by the wording having been prepared in
advance, only made checkable.** The clause-by-clause conformance table at
`a10-determination-reconciliation-record.md` §4 and the location-by-location
record at `draft-v5-a10-reconciliation-traceability.md` exist so a reader who
wrote neither can perform the comparison.

**Deficiency 3 — one addition beyond the prepared wording, recorded rather than
absorbed.** Instrument 5's recording rules gained, in addition to the prepared
sufficiency-marker text, a requirement that **the trail row record the four
per-category facts** (`fd-0028` §2.2). The prepared reconciliation placed those
four facts in A10 only. Development judged the addition strictly necessary —
Instrument 5 is the form on which workflow evidence is actually recorded, so a
recording requirement that appears only in A10 has no place to land — and made
it in the recording rules rather than the minimum-field list, which is the more
conservative of the two available placements. **It is nonetheless an addition
beyond the prepared text and is flagged here so the Founder Office can strike
it.** No minimum field was added to any instrument.
