## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The objective, and its limits

- "Execute one bounded internal validation application of frozen Organizational
  Diagnostic — Generation One Draft v5 against Huerta Group Publishing."
- **The purpose is not** "to perform a fresh HGP diagnosis for operational
  action." It is "to determine whether the materially changed and newly
  introduced Draft v5 treatments **can be applied coherently under authentic
  internal conditions**."
- "Draft v5 must remain frozen throughout the application. Do not repair, edit,
  or revise the method during validation."

### 2.2 What Stage 10 does not test — stated by the instrument, not inferred

"HGP is a previously diagnosed internal subject. Stage 10 therefore does not
test: **fresh-subject discovery; external validity; client validity; evaluator
independence; general applicability.**"

This is recorded here because it is the instrument's own framing of its result
**before** any result exists, and no later reading of Stage 10 may exceed it.

### 2.3 The subject

- **HGP is authorized** as the Stage 10 internal validation subject, selected as
  presently evidenced to exercise in one bounded application: a real
  participant; a governed subject vocabulary; subject-specific evidence
  classification and verification records; confidential material;
  actual-instance workflow evidence; multiple records of truth; and
  representation/source-boundary questions.
- **READ ONLY.** No change to HGP governance, operational records, application
  state, databases, workflows, publication state, product behaviour or
  software — and expressly **no correction of HGP "merely because diagnostic
  evidence identifies an issue."**

### 2.4 The validating author

- **Hard requirement:** "Stage 10 must be performed by an authoring context that
  did not draft Draft v5."
- An independence and prior-involvement statement is **created and frozen before
  substantive HGP inspection**, covering institutional involvement,
  individual/agent involvement, prior construction, governance, evaluation and
  authorship involving HGP, what prior-result material is withheld, unavoidable
  contextual leakage, and the resulting limitation.
- **Institutional independence rule:** "A different agent instance does not make
  the validator institutionally independent from Huerta Group."
- **Truth rule:** "Describe only the independence actually achieved. Do not
  claim blind, fresh, independent, or external validation unless the actual
  conditions satisfy those terms."

### 2.5 Prior-result isolation

- Withhold from the validating author, to the extent technically practicable:
  completed Stage 5 and Stage 6 instruments and their substantive findings and
  comparisons; **D-1 through D-8**; **MO-01 through MO-22**; "prior narrative
  summaries telling the validator what to find"; and "first-author expected
  conclusions."
- **"Do not withhold legitimate HGP subject evidence merely to improve author
  isolation."**
- **The Stage 6 lesson, restated by the instrument:** "changing the permitted
  evidence boundary to improve isolation can invalidate comparison."
- **If context leaks:** "Measure and disclose the leakage. Do not pretend
  isolation exists where it does not."

### 2.6 Participant evidence

- Required "where Draft v5 requires it or where necessary to exercise materially
  changed treatments." Founder participation may be used in the relevant HGP
  capacity, captured as participant/stakeholder evidence under Draft v5.
- **Critical rule:** Development "must not answer the participant questions on
  behalf of" the Founder, an author, a venture steward, an imprint, Publishing
  Operations, or another HGP authority.
- Questions are limited to what Draft v5, the bounded observables, or an
  exposed evidence insufficiency genuinely require, and must not embed the
  expected answer.
- **Do not manufacture** disagreement, contested authority, false uncertainty,
  or artificial stakeholder complexity.
- Where a live response is required and unavailable, "stop only at the point the
  response becomes necessary and return the minimum neutral participant
  questions required. **Do not infer answers.**"

### 2.7 The seventeen items, and the anti-manufacture rule

- Four permitted classifications only: **EXERCISED · PARTIALLY EXERCISED · NOT
  EXERCISED — AUTHENTIC CONDITION DID NOT OCCUR · NOT EXERCISABLE INTERNALLY.**
- "Every classification must identify observable application evidence supporting
  it."
- **"Do not manufacture conditions, disagreement, missing evidence, authority
  conflict, confidentiality problems, or workflow defects merely to move an item
  into EXERCISED."**
- The four items previously identified as not honestly exercisable internally
  "may remain unexercised if authentic Stage 10 conditions still do not exercise
  them."

The instrument additionally fixes the substantive targets: A10 Reading A with
its six per-category fields and five existence states; *procedure performed ≠
evidence sufficient*; the permitted state *A10 performed + named gaps + A18 not
satisfied*; Instrument 5's three sufficiency values with an express prohibition
on any numeric measure; the **Stage 6 visually-complete-output risk test**;
CS-12 closure semantics with no sixth outcome; CS-07 identity-of-record produced
by A4; one-sided evidence only where authentic evidence produces it; CS-03 at
both layers with incidental awareness excluded; CS-01 source kinds without
hierarchy; the A1/A2 precedence rule with a prohibition on substituting the
subject's scheme; CS-14 confidentiality with the **meta-rule that "the
validation must obey the confidentiality rule it is testing"**; and
source-of-truth boundaries identifiable while authority remains unresolved.

### 2.8 Freeze and correction discipline

- "During Stage 10, Draft v5 remains immutable."
- On any defect: record the evidence, the affected section or instrument, the
  classification, and the consequence; **prepare any proposed correction
  separately and do not apply it.**
- "Proposed corrections are evidence for Founder Office review. They do not
  constitute Draft v6 authority."
- **Descriptive finding types only** — held under authentic application · held
  with limitation · ambiguous · failed to produce required representation ·
  contradicted another method rule · not exercised · not exercisable internally.
  "Do not turn these into scores or maturity levels."

### 2.9 Maturity afterwards

- Starting status: **PROVISIONAL DRAFT — UNTESTED AS A VERSION.**
- "Assess only what Stage 10 evidence actually supports." Nothing pre-declared:
  not validated through use, established methodology, Method Library ready,
  externally validated, independently validated, or standardized.
- **"State the narrowest exact claim supported by Stage 10 evidence. Separately
  state what remains unsupported."**
- **The external-validation gap must remain explicit**: even a strong Stage 10
  result establishes no fresh-subject validity, external-client validity,
  organizational diversity, true evaluator independence, general applicability,
  commercial readiness, or Method Library maturity.

### 2.10 The next-stage boundary

"Recommend exactly one bounded next stage based on actual Stage 10 evidence" —
correction disposition if defects were exposed; further bounded internal
validation if a critical treatment remained materially untested; or Founder
Office consideration of a carefully bounded external stage if internal
validation is sufficiently complete. **"Do not assume external application is
next merely because Stage 10 completes. Recommendation is not authorization."**

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0029` §3. This
instrument authorizes the **first application of any version of this method

Preserving it verbatim is what allows a later reader to measure whatever Stage

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern), and received without a trailing newline as the three before it were;
both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the subject has not moved since the prior applications.** HGP
is at baseline `e28f12ad8a5e5531bed9fca20225a967669ab2de`, **the same commit
Stage 5 and Stage 6 both used**. The corpus the validator reads is therefore
the corpus two prior applications read. This is not a defect in the
authorization — the instrument selected HGP knowingly — but it means Stage 10
tests the method against **the same evidence**, not merely the same
organization, and any resemblance between its findings and the withheld prior
findings is at least partly attributable to that rather than to reproducibility.
Recorded before the application began.

**Deficiency 3 — prior-result withholding is instructed, not enforced.** The
validating author operates in the same filesystem as the withheld records.
Isolation rests on instruction plus the author's own disclosure of what it read,
exactly as at Stage 6. **It cannot be verified from outside**, and the Stage 10
report states what was withheld and what the author reported reading rather than
asserting that isolation held.

**Deficiency 4 — the office that drafted Draft v5 organizes its validation.**
Development wrote the method, selected what to withhold, wrote the validator's
briefing, and will write the report about the result. The validating author is
non-authoring; **the harness around it is not.** `fd-0025` §2.8 recorded the
same structural exposure at Stage 6, where the harness introduced the largest
divergence in the comparison. It is recorded again here because it has not been
cured, only made visible.
