## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 MD-1 — the A15 terminating threshold applies to both substantive limbs

**ACCEPTED METHODOLOGY DEFECT.** The shortcut "may fire only when BOTH
conditions are established through Observed evidence under existing A2
semantics": that "the capability already exists — built, standby, or dormant",
and that "the capability is actually awaiting an organizational-authority
decision rather than additional construction."

**A Client-Stated assertion concerning either limb remains valid Client-Stated
evidence. It is not false, downgraded, or rejected. It simply does not establish
the terminating condition.** Where either limb is not Observed, "continue
through the ordinary Technology-Worthiness Test."

**A2 boundary:** "a bounded threshold for this terminating question only. Do not
create: Observed-over-Client-Stated hierarchy; evidence ranking; weighting;
strength; quality scoring; credibility ordering."

### 2.2 MD-2 — dormant capability and corroboration

**ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.** "Do not require observation of a capability
operating when the proposition being tested is that the capability is dormant,
standby, or otherwise not presently operating." Such a capability "may be
established through direct observation of: the capability itself; its
implementation; configuration; deployment state; or other directly examinable
material evidence establishing its actual state."

"Lack of operational observation remains a limitation concerning actual use and
performance. **It is not evidence that the dormant capability does not exist.**"

**Prohibition:** "Do not manufacture operation merely to corroborate dormancy.
Do not add: new A2 evidence class; strength axis; recurrence score; hierarchy;
weighting."

### 2.3 MD-3 — existing capability awaiting authority decision

**ACCEPTED METHODOLOGY DEFECT.** "Add one distinct permitted
Technology-Worthiness outcome whose semantic meaning is: **Existing capability
awaits organizational authority decision.**" Exact wording may be normalized
"provided the meaning remains narrow and unambiguous."

It means: construction is not presently the unresolved issue; the next material
issue is an organizational-authority decision concerning activation, adoption,
release, use or equivalent disposition; the decision is routed to the competent
authority; and the outcome "authorizes neither activation nor further
construction", implies **no suitability, no validation, no approval, no
production readiness**, and "must not be collapsed into *existing system should
be improved rather than replaced* when improvement is not the unresolved
matter."

**Instrument 10 "may carry the associated authority question" but "is not a
substitute for recording the correct A15 Technology-Worthiness outcome."**

### 2.4 MD-4 — the aggregate impression that Client-Stated evidence is weak

**NO STRUCTURAL EVIDENCE-MODEL CHANGE AUTHORIZED.** MD-4 is preserved "as
validated behavioral risk/disclosure." Draft v7 "may add bounded
limitation/guidance language" making clear that "different methodological
questions legitimately require different kinds of evidence", and that "a local
requirement for Observed evidence must not be generalized into a global claim
that Client-Stated evidence is weaker, inferior, less credible, or lower
ranked."

**Evidence to be preserved:** that some conditions legitimately cannot be
established from participant assertion alone; that **"Founder Client-Stated
evidence materially changed fourteen reconciled items"**; and that
**"Client-Stated clarification eliminated a false disagreement."**

**Strict prohibition:** "Do not add: evidence ranking; weighting; quality;
independence scoring; maturity scoring; new evidence axis; preferential evidence
classes."

### 2.5 MD-5 — multiple outcome-bearing states over time

**ACCEPTED METHODOLOGY DEFECT.** "A18 must not represent an application as
though it has one timeless outcome." Where an application is suspended,
resumed, clarified after an earlier close, or otherwise reaches multiple
outcome-bearing states, **each state must retain** its date and order, evidence
basis, reason, the constraint operating at that time, and its relationship to
the later state.

"A later state may supplement or supersede the operational effect of an earlier
state where appropriate. **It must not erase the earlier state.**"

**Minimum distinctions:** application-stage status; diagnostic outcome at that
point in time; advancement permission; unresolved closure conditions.

**Boundary:** "Do not create a universal project-management lifecycle or generic
status system. This is chronology and diagnostic semantics only."

### 2.6 RQ-G — the custody disposition

**CUSTODY DISPOSITION ESTABLISHED**, as a **program-specific determination** for
institutionally significant methodology records produced through this program:
Huerta Group LLC is institutional owner; the governed Huerta Group LLC
repository is the durable institutional record location; GitHub is the present
organization-controlled technical custody mechanism for that repository; records
admitted through existing institutional/repository governance are official
institutional records; **"ChatGPT Projects, Claude sessions, conversations,
contributor memory, and transient working environments are not authoritative
merely because work occurred there"**; and material institutional outcomes must
enter the governed record through existing admission and preservation
mechanisms.

**Methodology boundary — binding:** **"Do not encode GitHub, a repository, or
Huerta Group's technical mechanism as a universal client requirement."** Where
Draft v7 must reference custody it retains **organization-neutral** semantics,
and "the organization's own authorized custody arrangement must establish the
disposition."

**Application consequence:** reconcile the Draft v6 closure record and
"determine whether A17 rule 7 is now discharged for that application", while
preserving all earlier unresolved closure states historically. **Separate
records:** methodology text and application-validation closure are updated "in
its proper governed location rather than collapsing them."

### 2.7 Scope control, and the closed authority list

The permitted authority sources are enumerated and closed: the MD-1 … MD-5
determinations and RQ-G. **"No proposed Draft v7 change lacking one of those
authorities may proceed unless it is a strictly necessary internal-consistency
or cross-reference consequence of one of them."**

Not authorized: "general rewrite for elegance; new diagnostic stages; new
evidence ontology; new scoring; new maturity system; new client lifecycle; new
software concepts; new external-validation doctrine; new commercialization
language; reopening settled Draft v6 provisions."

**Preserved and not reopened:** the FU-1 … FU-5 reconciliations and **RQ-H
Alternative C**.

### 2.8 Draft v7's status on creation

"Classify Draft v7 truthfully as a new provisional version. **Do not describe it
as validated merely because its changes derive from validated findings or
Founder Office determinations.**" Four things are distinguished: unchanged
elements with prior internal evidence; changed elements carrying prior
evidentiary rationale but not yet validated in v7 form; elements that can be
structurally or mechanically checked; and elements requiring future behavioural
validation.

### 2.9 Validation readiness instead of validation

**"Do not begin a new validation application."** Every change is instead
classified under five readiness categories — `MECHANICAL_STRUCTURAL`,
`PRESERVED_EVIDENCE_REASSESSMENT`, `PARTICIPANT_BEHAVIOR_REQUIRED`,
`NOT_INTERNALLY_PRODUCIBLE`, `EXTERNAL_APPLICATION_REQUIRED` — each with the
evidence available now, what may legitimately be concluded, what may not, and
whether another internal application could yield genuinely new evidence.

**Historical validation integrity:** "Do not rewrite prior Draft v5/v6
applications as though they used Draft v7… **State explicitly whenever an
assessment is retrospective and not a Draft v7 application.**"

### 2.10 The next-step boundary

"The recommendation must be evidence-driven. **Do not automatically recommend
another internal application.**" If the changes are sufficiently addressed
structurally and by preserved evidence, say so; if a narrow fresh internal test
could produce genuinely new evidence for a specific provision, identify that
provision precisely **without executing the test**; if the remaining meaningful
evidence can only come from external application, state that directly.
**"Recommendation is not authorization."**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern); both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — Draft v7 was constructed by the office whose method it is, on
determinations answering defects that office's own harness surfaced.** Draft v6
was drafted by Institutional Platform Development, validated by a non-authoring
context inside Development's harness, reconciled by Development, and is now
succeeded by a version Development drafted. **The only independence in the chain
was at the application step, and it does not extend here.** `fd-0036` imposes no
independence requirement and none is claimed. Recorded, not cured.

**Deficiency 3 — the determinations were made by the participant whose evidence
produced the findings.** The Founder Office supplied the participant evidence
that exposed MD-1 … MD-5, wrote the instrument determining them, and determined
RQ-G — a question about the custody of records concerning itself. This is the
fourth consecutive instrument carrying a form of this exposure (`fd-0033` §4
Deficiency 4, `fd-0034` §4 Deficiency 2, `fd-0035` §4 Deficiency 3). It is
recorded again because it has not been cured, only made visible.

**Deficiency 4 — MD-2 was implemented without amending the provision whose
wording created it.** MD-2 arose because A2 rule 4's corroboration route names
*observation of operation*, which a dormant capability cannot satisfy. The
implementation states the resolution **in A15** and leaves A2 byte-identical, on
the reading that rule 4 governs assertions *about* a condition rather than
first-hand examination of the artifact constituting it. **That reading is
Development's**, it is recorded at `draft-v7-traceability.md` §2 rather than
left implicit, and the Founder Office may reject it — in which case the correct
locus for MD-2's treatment is a question this record does not settle.
