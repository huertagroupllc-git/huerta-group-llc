## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 The application and its purpose

- "Execute the authorized internal participant-validation application of frozen
  Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One Draft v6 against Huerta Group LLC."
- **Intended standing:** "This is intended to be the **final substantial
  internal methodology-validation application** unless authentic use exposes a
  material semantic defect requiring correction before external use."
- **Purpose:** "to test Draft v6 behavior under authentic participant-inclusive
  internal conditions."
- **And its limit, stated in the objective itself:** "This application does not
  establish external applicability, client validity, or evaluator
  independence."

### 2.2 The subject, and its stated limitations

Huerta Group LLC, an internal organizational subject, which the instrument
records as: **"Founder-led; familiar to the institution conducting the
diagnostic; single-participant in present operation; not an external client;
not a fresh organizational environment."**

**Why it was chosen:** authentic evidence exists for organizational identity;
existing built and operating capabilities; **A15 shortcut conditions**;
workflows; decision rights; responsibility concentration; institutional memory;
source roles; tooling; and **participant evidence**.

### 2.3 The validator

- **Hard requirement:** "Use an authoring context that did not construct Draft
  v6."
- A prior-involvement statement is **created and frozen before subject
  inspection**, identifying institutional involvement, agent-level involvement,
  prior methodology-stage participation, **prior knowledge exposed through the
  execution environment**, accessible and withheld prior results, unavoidable
  leakage, and the resulting limits on evaluator independence.
- **Critical rule:** "A different agent instance is **not** institutionally
  independent from Huerta Group merely because the authoring context changed."
- **Isolation, and its limit:** withhold completed prior diagnostic outputs and
  validation conclusions "to the extent technically practical" — but **"do not
  withhold legitimate Huerta Group LLC subject evidence merely to manufacture
  artificial independence."**

### 2.4 Participant evidence

- **Required**, from the Founder, "in the actual Huerta Group LLC role/capacity
  relevant to the question."
- **"Development must not answer participant questions on the Founder's
  behalf."**
- **Permitted responses**, each of which is evidence: a direct answer; a partial
  answer; *"I don't know"*; **declining to answer**.
- **Question rule:** only what is materially required. **Do not manufacture**
  additional stakeholders, contested authority, delegation, disagreement, or
  organizational complexity "that does not authentically exist."
- **Interactive stop rule:** where a response is required and unavailable, "stop
  only the affected application progression and return the minimum neutral
  participant question set required. **Do not infer or reconstruct answers from
  prior Founder conversations**" unless Draft v6 expressly permits the prior
  record as the source being used.
- **Anti-fabrication, stated separately and in terms:** "**do not fabricate a
  completed report if participant responses have not yet been obtained.**"

### 2.5 A15 / RQ-1 — the highest-priority target

- **Purpose:** "Determine whether the RQ-1 A15 threshold behaves as Founder
  Office determined **without causing drift in A2**."
- The validator identifies **at least one genuine** condition involving an
  existing built, standby, dormant or already-operating capability, and
  **determines independently** whether its existence is Observed under A2.
- **Not sufficient by itself, however credible:** "Founder assertion; repository
  prose; roadmap; README; technical access; reference to an existing
  capability."
- **If Observed:** record the affirmative result, the supporting evidence class
  explicitly, and the actual evidence.
- **If only Client-Stated:** preserve the assertion, record that the terminating
  condition is not established affirmatively, and **continue through the ordinary
  Technology-Worthiness Test.**
- **Critical rule:** "Failure to fire the shortcut does not mean the capability
  does not exist and does not imply a new build."

### 2.6 A2 anti-ranking — the validation looks for damage the determination may have done

- **Target:** whether the A15-specific Observed threshold "causes the validator
  to treat Observed as **globally superior evidence elsewhere**."
- **Inspect both the completed outputs and the validator's own reasoning
  language**, looking for ranking, hierarchy, preference, weighting, evidentiary
  strength, credibility ordering, quality ordering, or "silent privileging of
  Observed outside A15."
- **Governing rule:** "A2 remains unordered."
- **If drift occurs:** "Record it as behavioral-validation evidence **against**
  Draft v6. Do not repair the draft or reinterpret A2 during the application."

### 2.7 A6, A7, A9, A10 — exercised, never simulated

- **A6.** Concentration in the Founder is **"an observed current condition, not
  a universal or permanent governance rule."** Where distributed or contested
  authority does not exist, "classify those conditions as authentically
  unexercised rather than inventing them."
- **A7.** **"Do not infer habit-held responsibility merely because the Founder is
  currently the only person."** Habit-held work requires participant evidence
  and/or evidence from actual work, recorded separately from formal assignment,
  actual performer, durability of assignment, and role-compression effect.
- **A9.** Founder Office conversation "may authentically function as sensemaking,
  clarification, routing. **It does not become authoritative merely because a
  matter begins there.**" And the converse: **"Do not erase the genuine
  operational role of conversation simply because durable authority resides
  elsewhere."**
- **A10.** A real, recent workflow instance; **the Founder's own step-by-step
  walkthrough of the last actual instance**, not rewritten into an idealized
  process before comparison; all four trail-source categories assessed with the
  five existence states kept distinct; trail recorded before any described
  process; and the confirmation that **procedure performed ≠ evidence
  sufficient**, with *A10 performed + named gaps + A18 not satisfied* permitted.

### 2.8 Counting

- **"Matrix totals must be derived mechanically from the authoritative item
  table"**, and reconciled: the sum of the classification counts equals the
  total authoritative V6 items.
- **"Do not manually type summary counts without deriving/checking them from the
  authoritative matrix."**
- **The program-level boundary:** repeated Stage 7 and Stage 10 summary-count
  defects "remain a program-level validation matter. **Do not turn count
  reconciliation into client-facing methodology substance.**"

### 2.9 Defect discipline

Four categories, **"Do not collapse these"**: **method defect** (Draft v6's
meaning, requirement, instrument design or internal inconsistency);
**application defect** (the validator's failure to follow a determinate method);
**validation gap** (an authentic condition did not arise, so a provision is
untested); **limitation** (evidence, access or independence conditions
constraining the claim).

Draft v6 is **immutable throughout**; findings are recorded and **corrections
proposed remain unapplied**; **no Draft v7.**

### 2.10 External validity, and maturity

- **Stated prominently by the instrument, before any result exists:**
  "**Regardless of outcome, [this application] does not narrow the
  external-validation gap**" — because it remains Founder-led, internal,
  familiar, single-participant, non-client, and inside Huerta Group's own
  evidentiary environment.
- **Its value:** "closure of internal behavioral-validation gaps."
- **Maturity:** reassess "only from actual evidence"; nothing pre-declared —
  not validated through use, externally validated, independently validated,
  established methodology, Method Library ready, or standardized. **"State the
  narrowest exact proposition supported by the internal evidence after this
  application."**

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0032` §3. This
instrument authorizes the **first application in this program's history to
require authentic participant evidence**, and therefore the first that can reach
A10's walkthrough limb, A7's habit test, and A9's first three source roles —
the account-dependent half of the method, unexercised in every version since the
program began. It is also the first to require a validation to **look for damage
its own preceding determination may have done.** Preserving it verbatim is what
allows a later reader to check the result against the standard set before the
work, including the instrument's own statement that the external-validation gap
will not move. **The reading remains the implementing office's and remains
reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern), and received without a trailing newline as the six before it were;
both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — subject and diagnosing institution are the same entity.** Every
prior application in this program diagnosed either the parent or a venture of
it; this one diagnoses **the institution that wrote the method, operates the
validator, and authored the evidence**. That is a stronger dependence than any
prior stage carried, and it is not cured by the validator being non-authoring.

**Deficiency 3 — the harness is Development's, again.** Development wrote the
briefing, chose what to withhold, and will assemble the report — the same
structural exposure recorded at `fd-0030` §4 Deficiency 4 and `fd-0031` §4
Deficiency 3. **The validator is non-authoring; the harness around it is not.**

**Deficiency 4 — the participant is the authority who commissioned the
validation.** The Founder Office is simultaneously the subject's accountable
authority, the participant supplying evidence, and the office that authorized
this application and answered RQ-1. **A2 rule 6's shared-source condition
therefore applies at maximum strength**: an account from this participant and a
record this participant approved are not independent sources. Recorded before
any evidence was taken.
