### 2.4 Records-derived evidence rules

- "Use the repository and already-authorized institutional evidence sources

  "Preserve provenance for every material comparison." · "Where records are
  unavailable or insufficient, classify the condition honestly instead of
  inferring an answer."

### 2.5 Comparison classifications and no default preference

- Seven classifications, defined in the instrument: **Corroboration ·
  Contradiction · Difference in Terminology · Missing Records · Unsupported

  Unresolved — Further Evidence Required.**
- "Do not automatically prefer repository records merely because they are
  documented." · "Do not automatically prefer Founder Office stakeholder
  testimony merely because the stakeholder is the Founder." · "Founder status
  does not convert stakeholder testimony into independently corroborated
  evidence for purposes of this diagnostic test." · "Institutional authority
  and evidentiary classification are separate questions."
- Per material claim, eleven fields are required, and: "A single answer may
  contain multiple claims with different classifications. Split them where
  necessary rather than forcing one classification across an entire answer."

### 2.6 Contradiction handling and non-elevation

- "Preserve contradictions explicitly." · "Do not resolve a contradiction
  merely because the stakeholder is the Founder."
- **Non-elevation:** "If a current Founder statement appears to constitute a
  new authoritative determination rather than merely stakeholder evidence, do

- **Stale records:** "If records appear stale but no governing correction has
  yet occurred, classify the record as potentially stale or

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

This instrument was not named by `fd-0020`; it arrived after it. The
implementing office preserved it under the standing determination at
`fd-0020` §2.4 because it materially establishes authority (it authorizes
Stage 3 completion), determinations (§§2.2–2.9 above), execution boundaries
(the continued exclusions and the recommendation boundary), and substantive
reasoning (the fifteen priority validation questions), and because it carries
the only copy of the Founder Office's own Q1–Q30 answers — the evidence the
whole comparison rests on. Preserving it is the same act that `fd-0020` §3
recorded for the earlier program instruments, and it directly answers the
institutional-memory deficiency this program keeps finding: instruments that
existed only in a transmission context.

**This reading is the implementing office's and is reviewable.** If the
Founder Office intended no determination record for an instrument that
authorizes completion rather than a new stage, `fd-0021` may be narrowed or
withdrawn; nothing else in Stage 3 depends on it, and the preserved instrument
would remain preserved under `fd-0020` §2.4 either way.

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** The instrument was preserved from the text as transmitted to the
implementing office's execution context; the Founder Office's own copy was not
available for byte comparison. Identity rests on the transmitted text itself
(`fd-0019` Deficiency 2 / `fd-0020` §4 Deficiency 1 pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — the stakeholder session was single-pass and asynchronous.**
The instrument supplied thirty answers and no channel for the
clarification-only follow-ups the stakeholder-evidence procedure §4 permits.
Several answers are under-determined and were recorded as such rather than
interpreted; see the completion report §21 and §31.
