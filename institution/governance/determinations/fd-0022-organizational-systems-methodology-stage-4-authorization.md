### 2.2 The four Founder clarifications (Client-Stated evidence)

- **Q3** — original response "Process." Clarified: "'Process' is the Founder's
  natural catch-all word for how things are worked through. The intended
  meaning is broad: essentially everything done in life can be understood as a
  process." Rule: "Do not narrow this into a specialized Huerta Group
  governance or methodology term." Evidence status: "Preserve as clarified
  Client-Stated evidence."
- **Q14** — original response "All of them came from me and are on here in
  ChatGPT projects." Clarified scope: "accounting / finance; administration /
  compliance; organizational systems / methodology; development / technology;
  publishing / HGP; other existing Huerta Group functional areas represented
  in the current institutional structure." Evidence status: "Client-Stated
  evidence concerning how the Founder understands the areas presently
  operating." Authority boundary: "This clarification does not independently
  create, amend, or supersede the formal institutional function roster."
- **Q18** — original response "Founder Office conversation." Clarified:
  "Founder Office conversation is not asserted to be the final authoritative
  institutional record." In current practice it is an environment where "an
  issue or idea begins; the Founder thinks through it conversationally; the
  matter is clarified and broken down; Founder Office determines or identifies
  the appropriate institutional function / room through which the matter should
  proceed." · "Where durable authority or a definitive institutional record is
  required, the appropriate governed function / record remains the
  destination." Required characterization: "Treat Founder Office conversation
  as a sensemaking, clarification, and routing environment in current
  practice." **Chronology rule:** preserve the initial answer, the Stage 3
  interpretation, the Stage 3 conflict classification, the clarification, and
  the revised Stage 4 classification. **Prohibition:** "Do not erase or rewrite
  the fact that the initial answer was ambiguous."
- **Q29** — original response "All of them." Clarified: "All current major
  directional decisions are presently reserved to the Founder because Huerta
  Group is currently a one-person organization." Not meaning: "This does not
  mean every possible organizational decision is inherently or permanently
  Founder-reserved." Future posture: "Future delegation and decision-rights
  distribution remain to be determined as the organization grows." Diagnostic
  principle: "Current authority concentration is an observed organizational
  condition, not a universal governance principle."

### 2.6 Sensemaking versus authority; authority concentration

- Evidence: "Huerta Group currently demonstrates that where people think
  through a matter and where institutional authority ultimately resides may be
  different environments."
- The diagnostic should distinguish: "Where does sensemaking occur? Where is a
  matter clarified? Where is it routed? Who has authority to decide it? Where
  does the durable authoritative record ultimately reside?"
- **Critical rule:** "Do not generalize 'Founder Office' as the mechanism.
  Generalize only the distinction between sensemaking/clarification/routing and
  authoritative decision/record."
- Draft v2 test: inspect whether Draft v2 and its instruments cleanly allow
  those locations, roles or mechanisms to differ; if not, treat the deficiency
  "through one of the ten correction dispositions or a narrowly derived Stage 4
  clarification correction where authorized."
- Authority concentration: Draft v3 must represent, "without privileging any
  one state," one-person concentration, distributed, delegated, contested and
  unresolved authority. "Current concentration is an observed condition, not the
  diagnostic's default governance model." · "Do not encode a single apex
  individual as structurally required."

clarifications were supplied and no channel for further clarification
accompanied them. Where a clarification is itself under-determined, it is

and Q29 clarifications bear on comparisons `C-07`, `C-35` and `C-70`, which are
therefore **left at their Stage 3 classifications** with the clarification
recorded beside them. Whether those comparisons should be re-run is returned to
the Founder Office (Stage 4 report §28), not decided here.
