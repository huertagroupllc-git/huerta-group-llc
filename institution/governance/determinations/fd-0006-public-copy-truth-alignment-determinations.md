## Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

- **FO-01:** "Phase 1 is Public Copy Truth Alignment only. No
  redesign. No information-architecture changes. No Design System
  work. No new capabilities."
- **FO-02:** "Huerta Group Publishing is excluded from Phase 1
  parent-platform repositioning. Huerta Group Publishing has matured
  into an operating institution and requires a separate Founder Office
  positioning program. Do not casually insert, expand, or reposition
  Huerta Group Publishing throughout the Huerta Group LLC website
  during this phase."
- **FO-03:** "The parent/venture relationship is an unresolved
  institutional-governance matter. Do not invent or formalize
  parent/venture governance through public copy. Preserve this issue
  for the future Corporate Venture Governance / Enterprise Structure
  Architecture program."
- **FO-04:** "Public wording shall not imply an established client
  portfolio unless repository evidence supports that claim. Replace or
  refine unsupported language such as 'organizations we serve' where
  necessary. Use truthful forward-looking or capability-oriented
  language supported by repository evidence. […] Select wording
  according to page context and repository truth rather than applying
  one phrase mechanically everywhere."
- **FO-05:** "Education and Managed Services must remain clearly
  distinguished between architecturally envisioned capability and
  operationally existing capability. Do not present future or
  conceptual architecture as an active operating service."
- **FO-06:** "The institution is evolving beyond a conventional
  company website toward a possible future institutional public
  platform. This is an observation, not Phase 1 implementation
  authority. Do not restructure the website around this future state
  during this assignment."
