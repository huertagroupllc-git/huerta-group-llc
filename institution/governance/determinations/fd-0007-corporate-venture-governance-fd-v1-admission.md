# fd-0007 — Founder Office Determinations: Corporate Venture Governance — FD-V1 Admission, FD-V9, FD-V10

**Classification: Institutional Record** — determination record

## Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

- **FD-V1:** "Huerta Group Publishing is admitted as an operational
  venture of Huerta Group LLC under the Corporate Venture Governance
  architecture."
- **FD-V9:** "Venture Graduation Principle. A venture may later be
  recognized through organizational stages such as Internal Initiative,
  Admitted Venture, Mature Venture, Strategic Business Unit, or
  Independent Subsidiary where appropriate. Graduation is never
  automatic and requires explicit Founder Office determination."
- **FD-V10:** "Venture Success Principle. A venture's purpose is not to
  become independent. A venture exists to fulfill its charter. Whether
  it remains inside Huerta Group LLC, becomes a Strategic Business
  Unit, or later becomes a subsidiary is a governance decision rather
  than a venture objective. Institutional success is measured by
  fulfillment of purpose, not organizational form."

## Approved principles (verbatim from the instrument's program context)

"Huerta Group Publishing is the first formally chartered venture of
Huerta Group LLC." · "Huerta Group Publishing operates under delegated
authority." · "Huerta Group Publishing is not an independent legal
entity, subsidiary, or separate company." · "Capability sharing occurs
only through explicit institutional grant." · "Venture graduation is
never automatic." · "Institutional success is measured by fulfillment
of chartered purpose, not organizational shape."

## Historical rule (preserved distinction)

This record preserves, without rewriting, the distinction the
instrument requires: Huerta Group Publishing **operated before formal
chartering** (its repository history begins 2026-07-02, under Founder
authorization evidenced in its own records) and was **formally
admitted under FD-V1 effective with this instrument's approval**
(2026-08-12). Governance records authority; it does not create
history. Nothing in this admission represents formal venture
governance as having existed earlier than it did.

## Effect and boundaries

The admission effects venture standing and charter authority only.
Per the approved Charter and Blueprint (non-propagation): it
authorizes no commercial activity, no public launch, no distribution,
no new capability grants beyond the Charter's exhaustive grant
register (publisher-of-record presentation; venture name usage), and
no progression expectation under FD-V9/FD-V10. FD-V2 through FD-V8
remain open Founder Office matters and are not begun by this record.
