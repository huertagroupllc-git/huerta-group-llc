## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Scope of the construction

Draft v6 implements **only** "V6-01 through V6-07 as already dispositioned in
Stage 11; the Founder Office RQ-1 determination governing A15 and Instrument 9;
and strictly necessary internal-consistency edits directly caused by those
authorized changes."

- "Do not reopen Draft v5 generally. Do not introduce unrelated methodology
  substance. Do not perform a new validation application."
- **Not to be reopened:** Stage 7 change-set adjudication; `fd-0026`
  determinations; A10 semantics; A2 overall architecture; source-kind doctrine;
  RQ-2; participant-validation gaps; method maturity claims;
  external-validation doctrine.

### 2.2 RQ-1 — the determination

- **Selected standard:** **ALTERNATIVE B.**
- **Governing rule:** "A15's preceding question may be answered affirmatively
  only when the existence of the capability is supported as **Observed**
  evidence under the methodology's existing A2 evidence model."
- **Reason:** "An affirmative answer can terminate the Technology-Worthiness Test
  before its substantive questions determine the outcome. Therefore the
  condition relied upon by that shortcut must be **established as a condition
  rather than merely asserted**."
- **Meaning, for A15 only:** *"already exists"* means "established to exist under
  the existing A2 Observed classification."
- **What it does not mean:** "described as existing; claimed to exist; planned;
  referenced in a record; believed by a participant to exist; technically
  reachable."

### 2.3 A2 preservation, and the bounded exception

- **Critical rule:** "**Do not redefine Observed for A15.**"
- **Record semantics:** where a supplied organizational record states a
  capability exists — "the existence of the record may be Observed; the contents
  of the record may be Observed as contents; **the capability condition asserted
  by the record does not automatically become Observed** merely because the
  statement appears in an institutional record."
- Existing A2 corroboration and classification rules **remain controlling**.
- **Non-ordering.** The threshold "must not create: Observed > Client-Stated;
  evidence hierarchy; reliability ranking; credibility ranking; quality scale;
  weighting; global preference for Observed evidence."
- **Bounded exception:** "The higher threshold applies **only** because this
  specific A15 question can terminate further inquiry."

### 2.4 Client-Stated assertions, and records-only diagnoses

Where a Client-Stated assertion claims a capability exists: record the
assertion, its provenance, and its A2 classification; record that "the A15
terminating condition is **not established affirmatively** unless the condition
is Observed"; and "continue through the ordinary Technology-Worthiness Test."

- "**Failure to satisfy the terminating shortcut does not mean the assertion is
  false.**"
- "**Failure to satisfy the terminating shortcut does not imply a new capability
  should be built.**"
- The full test "may still conclude *existing system should be improved rather
  than replaced* where the developed evidence supports that outcome."
- **Records-only:** "A records-only diagnosis may be unable to use the A15
  shortcut. **That does not block Instrument 9.**" The assessor "should proceed
  through the ordinary Technology-Worthiness Test using the evidence actually
  available."

### 2.5 Access is not existence — Alternative C declined

- **Declined alternative:** "**Access does not establish the capability
  condition.**"
- "Technical or practical access to a system does not by itself prove that the
  claimed capability exists." A reachable system may "contain no such capability;
  contain an obsolete capability; contain a differently scoped capability;
  contain only records asserting that a capability exists."
- **Access remains independently relevant** to evidence limitations, coverage,
  advancement, and what may be verified.

### 2.6 Instrument 9, and V6-07

- Instrument 9's preceding-question rule is modified so that "an affirmative
  terminating answer requires the capability's existence to be Observed under
  A2."
- **V6-07 is preserved**, and the instrument gives the reason the Founder Office
  wants it kept even though an affirmative answer will always be Observed: the
  field "makes the evidentiary basis explicit and supports treatment of negative
  answers; not-established answers; Client-Stated assertions that do not satisfy
  the shortcut."
- **Not to be changed:** "the five permitted Technology-Worthiness outcomes; the
  rule that need authorizes nothing; the substantive ordinary
  Technology-Worthiness Test beyond changes already authorized by V6-01…V6-07."

### 2.7 Stage 10's history

## 3. Application of `fd-0020` §2.4 to this instrument — recorded reading

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0031` §3. This
instrument is the **first to answer a reserved methodology question and
authorize its implementation in the same act**, and the first to adopt a
threshold on an evidence class anywhere in this method. Preserving it verbatim
is what allows a later reader to check that the threshold stayed where the
Founder Office put it — on one question — rather than spreading into A2, which
is the risk the instrument itself spends most of its length guarding against.
**The reading remains the implementing office's and remains reviewable.**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern), and received without a trailing newline as the five before it were;
both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the office that drafted the question drafted the answer's
implementation.** Development framed RQ-1's three alternatives, and Development
has now written the text implementing the one chosen. The framing bounded the
choice: a Founder Office presented with three alternatives chose among three.
**Whether a fourth existed that Development did not see is not establishable from
this record**, and the RQ-1 return's own statement that "no fourth alternative is
supported" is Development's assessment of its own framing.

**Deficiency 3 — the determination's principal risk is one this construction
cannot test.** `fd-0032` §2.3 forbids the threshold creating any general
preference for Observed evidence. Draft v6 states that guard in three places.
**Whether an applier reading A15's Observed requirement nonetheless comes to
treat Observed as better evidence elsewhere is a behavioural question**, and no
consistency review reaches it. It is named here as the thing the next validation
should look for.
