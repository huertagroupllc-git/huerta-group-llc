## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Stage 5 acceptance, and the distinction upheld

- Status: "ACCEPTED AND CLOSED."
- "Stage 5 validation is complete. HGP diagnosis remains intentionally
  incomplete because the method correctly refused advancement without
  stakeholder evidence and without access to the operational record the venture
  itself identifies as authoritative."
- Method status: "INTERNALLY TESTED PROVISIONAL DIAGNOSTIC." Not designated:
  validated through use; externally validated; standardized; established;
  Method Library ready.

### 2.2 Objective — reproducibility, and what it is not

- "Execute the … replication of the frozen Organizational Diagnostic Draft v3
  against the exact Huerta Group Publishing baseline examined in Stage 5."
- "The replication must be conducted by a second authoring context that is
  isolated from Stage 5 conclusions to the greatest extent technically
  practical."
- Purpose: "to determine whether materially similar diagnostic outputs arise
  from the frozen method and the same available subject evidence, **rather than
  primarily from the first author's prior knowledge, tacit interpretation, or
  authorship history**."
- "This is reproducibility testing. It is not external validation, methodology
  adoption, Method Library admission, Draft v4 development, or HGP
  remediation."

### 2.3 The two gates

- **Draft v3:** verify against the Stage 4/5 digest **before creating the
  second-author package**; if verification fails, "Stop before replication.
  Return the exact integrity discrepancy. Do not regenerate, repair, or
  normalize Draft v3." Draft v3 "must not be modified before, during, or after"
  the replication.
- **HGP baseline `e28f12a`:** verify; do not update, repair, write to, or
  "inspect a later revision as substitute subject evidence." If the exact
  baseline is unavailable, "Stop before substantive replication and return the
  precise baseline-access problem."

### 2.4 D-8 — determined

- **Founder Office determination:** "Previous Generation One methodology
  applications were performed by a Development function that materially
  participated in construction of: the methodology; Huerta Group systems and
  governance; and/or prior work involving the diagnostic subject. **Those
  applications provide internal testing evidence but not evaluator
  independence.**"
- Historical treatment: "**Do not rewrite prior reports.** Record D-8 as a
  later qualification on the Generation One evidence base."
- Draft boundary: "**Do not amend Draft v3 merely because D-8 exists.** Stage 6
  must independently determine whether replication evidence supports a later
  method correction."
- Later assessment required of what D-8 means for the evidence status of
  Stage 2, Stage 3, Stage 5 and Stage 6, under two rules: "Do not
  retroactively invalidate prior evidence merely because it lacks evaluator
  independence," and "internal testing may still be substantive even where
  evaluator independence is absent."

### 2.5 The Stage 5 sequencing deviation — disposed

- Preserved: "Stage 5 should have recorded its prior-familiarity limitation
  before venture evidence was inspected."
- Disposition: the defect "does not invalidate Stage 5 because: the limitation
  actually governed the work; no evidence indicates it was altered after
  inspection; **the deviation was disclosed rather than concealed**."
- **New standing rule:** "Stage 6 must create and freeze the second-author
  independence statement **before** the second author receives substantive HGP
  evidence."

### 2.6 Second-author isolation

- "The Stage 6 diagnostic must be performed by a different agent instance /
  authoring context from the one that produced the Stage 5 diagnostic," with
  "no inherited Stage 5 working context."
- **Thirteen classes of forbidden material**, including every Stage 5 finding,
  completed instrument, comparison result, deficiency, report, working note,
  and every expected outcome — advancement, contradictions, terminology,
  source-of-truth, governance, and method deficiencies.
- **Permitted:** frozen Draft v3 and its instruments; a neutral execution
  derivative containing no Stage 5 findings; the baseline identifier; the
  evidence-access boundary; procedural instructions; confidentiality and
  read-only boundaries.
- **Technical isolation limitation:** where total isolation is impossible,
  "identify exactly what Stage 5 material was technically visible or
  discoverable; identify whether it was actually accessed; record the
  limitation before comparison; **do not overstate second-author
  independence**."
- **No hidden coaching:** twelve classes of expectation must not be seeded,
  including "the fact that Stage 5 stopped at a particular evidentiary boundary
  as an expected answer." · "The second author must independently discover
  whatever the method and permitted evidence support."

### 2.7 Single-source reporting — observed, not required

- The Stage 5 treatments "remain available reporting compositions… **They are
  not newly adopted mandatory methodology doctrine.**"
- "Do not instruct the second author that it must use these phrases." Observe
  whether it independently identifies the same evidentiary condition, uses the
  treatment, uses materially equivalent wording, "or experiences ambiguity
  because Draft v3 lacks sufficient reporting guidance."

### 2.8 Evidence boundary, stakeholder boundary, sealing

- "Do not broaden the evidence-access boundary merely to produce a complete
  diagnosis." Where the hosted record is outside the boundary, record that it is
  institutionally identified, that it remains outside authorized access, and the
  diagnostic consequence — "**Do not infer its contents.**"
- "Do not request the ten outstanding Stage 5 HGP stakeholder answers merely to
  complete the prior diagnosis"; they "remain evidence of Stage 5's refusal
  boundary." If the second author independently reaches such a boundary it stops
  there, derives its own minimum neutral questions, and does not supply answers.
- **Sealing before comparison:** complete, record, freeze and fingerprint the
  second-author result **before** any Stage 5 comparison. "Do not expose
  Stage 5 findings to the second author before sealing."

### 2.9 Comparison, and the prohibition on scoring

- Seventeen minimum comparison dimensions, from evidence selected through method
  deficiencies identified.
- Eight difference classifications: **SUBSTANTIVELY REPRODUCED · MATERIALLY
  EQUIVALENT — DIFFERENT WORDING · EVIDENCE-SELECTION DIFFERENCE ·
  INTERPRETATION DIFFERENCE · METHOD AMBIGUITY · AUTHOR-SPECIFIC INFERENCE ·
  ACCESS-BOUNDARY EFFECT · UNRESOLVED.**
- **"Do not create: numerical reproducibility score; percentage agreement
  score; weighted consistency index; method maturity score."**
- "Perfect textual agreement is not expected. Material agreement or
  disagreement on substantive organizational findings is the relevant
  evidence."
- Author-specific inference: "Do not automatically decide which author is
  correct. Determine whether the difference reflects evidence, method
  ambiguity, access boundary, or author-specific inference."
- Method ambiguity: "Record ambiguity separately. **Do not create Draft v4.**"

### 2.10 Ceiling, exclusions, and the failure modes

- **Stage 6 ceiling:** "Stage 6 can establish, at most, stronger internal
  reproducibility evidence that more than one authoring context can produce
  materially similar findings from the same frozen method and subject
  evidence."
- Successful replication does not establish external validity, client validity,
  independence from Huerta Group, multi-person organizational validity,
  contested-authority validity, general applicability, Method Library
  readiness, or commercial readiness.
- Exclusions: Draft v4; methodology adoption; external client application;
  Method Library admission; standardization; diagnostic software; assessment
  automation; AI diagnostic authority; scoring systems; maturity models;
  commercial packaging; pricing; public methodology claims; HGP modification;
  Corporate Command Center modification.
- **Failure modes:** if no technically meaningful second authoring context can
  be established without exposing Stage 5 findings — "**do not perform a fake
  replication**; document the isolation limitation; return the exact dependency
  to Founder Office." Draft-integrity or baseline failure: stop before
  replication.

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — second-author isolation is partial, and was measured rather
than assumed.** The second author's harness injected a user-level memory index
into its context before any instruction could prevent it. Rather than estimate
the exposure, the second author was required to quote it verbatim before
beginning; it did, and the record is at
`stage-6-second-author-independence-statement.md` §3–§4. Stage 5's findings,
deficiencies and advancement outcome did **not** leak; subject product-status
claims and Stage 3-era program facts did.

**Deficiency 3 — both authors are the same model family.** Raised by the second
author, not by the first: correlated errors between them will resemble
reproducibility. This qualifies every agreement Stage 6 records and cannot be
removed by any internal arrangement.
