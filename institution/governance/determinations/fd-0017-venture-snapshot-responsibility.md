## 1. What this record disposes of

GEN1-REQ §19 Q5 asked whether each venture executive function should provide its
Headquarters snapshot, or whether Corporate Operations should derive it from
governed venture reporting. The question was raised at requirements discovery
(2026-08-18), deferred through `fd-0014`, and returned as the live decision by
the Stage 5 report once the parent-level view existed and showed five of twelve
HGP fields honestly absent.

The Founder Office has answered it.

## 2. The determination

**Venture authority supplies venture truth; Corporate Operations owns
Headquarters representation.**

1. **Venture responsibility.** The authoritative venture function is responsible
   for supplying the substantive venture condition from which parent-level
   oversight is established.
2. **Corporate Operations responsibility.** Corporate Operations is responsible
   for admitting, maintaining and presenting the Headquarters representation of
   that condition.
3. **Corporate Operations may not independently invent, infer or reinterpret**
   substantive venture state merely to populate the snapshot.
4. **The venture does not thereby own or write the Corporate Operations
   register.** Supplying the underlying truth confers no write authority over
   the Headquarters representation of it.
5. **Absence rule.** Where an authoritative venture source does not establish a
   required parent-level fact, the Headquarters representation remains absent.
6. **Provenance rule.** Provenance sufficient to establish which venture
   authority supplied the underlying condition must be preserved.

**Not authorized:** automated synchronization; direct venture writes into the
Corporate Operations register; duplication of venture operational systems.

## 3. What this resolves, and what it does not

It resolves *who is responsible*. It does not resolve *what HGP's absent fields
contain* — that is a matter for the venture to supply and for Corporate
Operations to admit, and neither is an act Development may perform on their
behalf.

It creates no schema, no interface and no automation. The Generation One
architecture already supports both paths and continues to; the determination
settles which path is correct rather than changing what is technically possible.
