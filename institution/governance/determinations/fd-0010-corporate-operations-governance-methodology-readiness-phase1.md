# fd-0010 — Founder Office Determinations: Corporate Operations & Command Platform — Governance & Methodology Readiness Phase 1

**Classification: Institutional Record** — determination record
(evidentiary, non-governing). The record evidences the determinations;
authority resides with the Founder Office and the artifacts the
determinations govern.

- **Instrument:** Corporate Operations & Command Platform — Governance &
  Methodology Readiness, Phase 1: Preservation & Institutionalization
  execution package, Founder Office → Institutional Platform
  Development.
- **Received:** 2026-08-18, as transmitted to the implementing office.
- **Recorded:** 2026-08-18.
- **Software implementation authorization:** None.

## Controlling status determined by the instrument

The Internal Operations & Command Platform "remains paused before
technical architecture and implementation." The instrument determines
that "No Corporate Operations Register, database, file-based register,
application module, authentication system, ADR implementation, schema,
migration, integration, or production behavior is authorized."

## Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

Execution principles determined by the instrument:

1. "Repository evidence and existing institutional authority control
   execution."
2. "Preservation does not create substantive authority."
3. "Repository admission and institutional authority must remain
   distinguishable."
4. "Absence from the governed repository is not evidence that an
   institutionally established artifact or function does not exist."
5. "Permanent source loss does not authorize reconstruction."
6. "Development may identify semantic gaps but may not fill them by
   invention."
7. "Requirements evidence does not become software implementation
   authority merely through repository preservation."
8. "Technical metadata must accurately represent institutional status
   rather than silently change it."

Status determinations concerning the Generation One Requirements Report:

- Requirements discovery: **"Accepted"**
- Generation One concept: **"Accepted subject to Governance & Methodology
  Readiness"**
- Technical architecture: **"Paused"**
- Technical implementation: **"Not Authorized"**

Concerning genuine semantic gaps, the instrument determines: "Permanent
OSPA loss does not authorize reconstruction. Where surviving authority
leaves a genuine gap, the institution may prospectively adopt new
operational doctrine. Any such doctrine must: be clearly identified as
new authority; be adopted through competent institutional authority;
never be represented as recovered OSPA." Drafting that doctrine is
expressly outside Phase 1 absent separate authorization.

Concerning FD-V7, the instrument determines: "FD-V7 is reserved to
Founder Office. Platform & Systems Development shall not make, imply, or
mechanically encode the reserved determination before Founder Office
acts."

## Deficiencies recorded

Three deficiencies are recorded rather than resolved by inference
(canonical-source-preservation §3; fd-0004 precedent).

**Deficiency 1 — conflict with fd-0001 determination 1 (reserved to the
Founder Office).** `fd-0001` determination 1 states verbatim: "OSPA
reconstruction working documents remain untracked working material and
shall not be committed." This instrument directs custody preservation of
the surviving recovered OSPA source bytes under repository control,
requires verification of "byte identity between the workbench source and
repository-preserved custody copy," and assigns that work **Immediate**
priority. The two cannot both be satisfied for the same artifacts.

Platform & Systems Development executed the later, specific instruction
because it is explicit on this exact point, issues from the same
authority as `fd-0001`, and addresses a risk the instrument characterizes
as compounding and permanent. **Platform & Systems Development has not
determined that `fd-0001` determination 1 is superseded, and does not
assert supersession.** The relationship between this instrument and
`fd-0001` determination 1 requires formal Founder Office disposition —
supersession, amendment, or scoped exception. Until that disposition is
made, the custody exception is recorded in `workbench/README.md` rule 2
as standing against `fd-0001` determination 1, and the registry
relationship between `fd-0010` and `fd-0001` is recorded as `related`,
not `supersedes`.

**Deficiency 2 — Generation One Requirements Report text not
transmitted.** The instrument accepts the report and directs its faithful
preservation, but the report's text was not transmitted to the
implementing office and is not present in this repository or any
repository accessible to it. The report is therefore recorded by
**status, provenance, authority boundary, and relationships only**; **the
report's own text** is not reproduced, summarized, paraphrased, or
reconstructed. Characterizations transmitted by the instrument itself —
notably the trigger-reassessment evidence categories above — are recorded
and attributed to the instrument, never to the report.
This follows the `fd-0009` / FD-V11 precedent, where transmitted
substance was recorded and untransmitted text was left unreconstructed.
Faithful preservation of the report requires its transmission.

**Deficiency 3 — Corporate Operations & Command Management establishing
instrument not transmitted.** The function is recorded as established by
the Founder Office in the Corporate Headquarters project. The
establishing instrument itself was not transmitted and is not present in
this repository. The institutionalization therefore preserves **only the
authority substance enumerated by this instrument**, and records the
absence of the underlying establishing instrument as a provenance
deficiency. Nothing beyond the enumerated substance has been inferred.

   item 22).

   semantic gaps — which must be clearly identified as new authority,
   adopted through competent institutional authority, and never
   represented as recovered OSPA.
