## 1. What this record disposes of

The Authentic-Use Stale Obligation Disposition report returned a semantic
deficiency: three stale obligations stood on the HGP trade-name matter, and only
the Follow-Up had a governed exit. Renewal and Deadline vocabularies describe an
obligation *progressing* — actionable, satisfied, reached, passed — and none of
those values could say an obligation was **never established** without recording
a false institutional fact.

The Founder Office has supplied the missing meaning, and two further
determinations the same case surfaced.

## 2. Determination one — NOT ESTABLISHED

**Applies to Renewal and Deadline only.**

> NOT ESTABLISHED applies when a Headquarters operational representation
> previously carried an obligation, but subsequent reconciliation with the
> authoritative source establishes that the underlying obligation is not
> presently established by that authority.

What it means, and what it carefully does not:

- The obligation must no longer be represented as current.
- Its historical representation remains preserved.
- **No assertion** that the obligation was satisfied.
- **No assertion** that it lapsed.
- **No assertion** that its deadline was reached or passed.
- **No assertion** that the authoritative source itself previously established
  the obligation.

**The critical boundary:** this corrects Headquarters *representation*. It does
not retroactively alter authoritative source history, and it does not imply the
source ever said what Headquarters recorded.

**Follow-Up is excluded.** `moot` already exists within Follow-Up semantics and
is sufficient; no new Follow-Up disposition is created.

## 3. Determination two — supersession does not cascade

Superseding an Operational Record **does not** automatically dispose of attached
Renewals, Deadlines, Follow-Ups, Dependencies, Blockers, Founder Attention
Items, or any other operational concept. Each retains its own governed
disposition semantics.

Record supersession may *coordinate* with individual dispositions; it does not
substitute for them. **No automatic disposition cascade is authorized.**

This closes the gap the report raised as §24.3, and closes it in the direction
that preserves each concept's independence rather than collapsing them under the
Record.

## 4. Determination three — expiration metadata is not a Deadline

The HGP trade-name registration expires 2031-06-15. That date **may not** become
a current Deadline merely so the register can hold it. Expiration metadata and
an actionable Deadline are not equivalent.

Where the operational model has no truthful descriptive location for expiration
metadata, the date remains in authoritative-source and provenance context. A
future Renewal or Deadline may be represented only when Administration &
Compliance establishes an actual continuation requirement or consequential
filing boundary.

## 7. Authority boundary

This record evidences determinations; it creates none. It does not amend the
Constitution, the Vision Foundation, the Corporate Operations Semantics
Foundation, the Generation One Requirements Report, any ADR, or any prior
determination. NOT ESTABLISHED is a **prospective addition to two
concept-specific vocabularies** and is expressly not a generic cancellation
state, a universal task lifecycle, or a shared disposition model. ADR-0017 is
unchanged; no access class gains a capability; no production hosting or
recurring cost is authorized.
