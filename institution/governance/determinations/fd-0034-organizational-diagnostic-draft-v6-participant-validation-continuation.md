## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Resumption, not restart

- "Resume the existing Draft v6 participant-validation application from the
  participant-evidence gate."
- **"Do not restart the diagnostic. Do not alter the existing validation
  boundary. Do not modify Draft v6."**
- The instrument's `<continuation_rule>` requires the **existing validator
  context** to be reused, so that the frozen prior-involvement statement, the
  subject baseline, the evidence-access boundary, the partially completed
  instruments, the V6 matrix and the chronology all carry forward. **"Do not
  create a second validation application merely because participant evidence
  arrived later."**

**This is the first suspended-and-resumed application in the program's
history**, and it is the condition that produced method defect MD-5.

### 2.2 The participant evidence

- Capture PQ-V6-1 through PQ-V6-8 **"exactly as Client-Stated evidence."**
- **"Preserve the Founder's wording, meaning, uncertainty, and lack of certainty
  where expressed. Do not silently strengthen, normalize, professionalize, or
  reconcile the responses."**
- Preserve uncertainty **"including 'No idea,' 'idk,' and 'I believe.'"**
- **"Do not infer answers the Founder did not provide."**

### 2.3 Inferences expressly forbidden — enumerated by the instrument

Recorded in full because the enumeration is the instrument's operative
discipline, and because each prohibition changed a result:

- From PQ-V6-6: **"Do not infer: a storage system; repository location;
  custodian; retention rule; transfer mechanism; formal custody policy."** And:
  **"Do not mark it satisfied merely because the Founder expressed a general
  preference to preserve the material."**
- From PQ-V6-5: **"Do not convert the latter language into a legal or
  institutional non-existence determination if repository authority establishes
  Huerta Group LLC as an existing entity/institution."**
- From PQ-V6-7: **"Do not treat tools as organizational actors merely because
  they are named in the organizational-boundary response."**
- From PQ-V6-4: **"Do not treat 'website hasn't gone live' as automatically
  answering whether a built, standby, dormant, or operating capability
  exists."**
- From PQ-V6-1: **"Do not invent an instance."** And: "If no qualifying actual
  instance exists, classify the affected A10 validation target according to the
  existing exercise-classification semantics rather than manufacturing a
  walkthrough."
- From PQ-V6-3: **"Do not infer habit-held responsibility solely from this
  response."**
- From PQ-V6-8: **"Do not convert the assertion into Observed evidence merely
  because the Founder stated it."**
- Globally: do not fill gaps using **"Development memory; prior Founder
  conversations outside the authorized evidence basis; likely institutional
  intent; nearby repository prose that does not establish the proposition at
  issue."**

### 2.4 Comparison rules

Preserved on the reading recorded at `fd-0021` §3 through `fd-0033` §3. This

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern); both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — the participant is the authorizing office.** The Founder Office
wrote this instrument, supplied the eight responses inside it, wrote the
handling rules governing how its own responses must be treated, and will review
the result. `fd-0033` §4 Deficiency 4 recorded the same structural exposure for
the validation; it is sharper here, because the authority now also **is** the
evidence. It is recorded, not cured.

**Deficiency 3 — three count errors in the returned report were found by
Development and are not corrected in it.** The close commit count, the
participant-limb arithmetic, and the validation-gap total. None affects a
classification, finding, defect, matrix value, or the advancement state. This is
the **third** count-defect episode in the program (Stage 7, Stage 10, and this).
Full detail at
`institution/governance/methodology/draft-v6-participant-validation-count-reconciliation.md`.

**Deficiency 4 — the diagnostic altered the corpus it measured.** `73ff08a`
admitted this application's own partial record into the governed corpus during
the suspension, moving the validators from 257/257 to 259/259 between the
application's open and its close. Recorded at completion report §5 as a
limitation on any later reading of the evidence base, and it is recorded here
because it is a property of how this program executes, not of Draft v6.
