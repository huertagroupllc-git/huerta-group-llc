## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Additive resumption, not a new application

- "Resume the existing Draft v6 participant-validation record using: FU-1
  through FU-5 as new dated Client-Stated participant evidence; and the Founder
  Office RQ-H determination."
- **"Preserve all prior answers, findings, classifications, and chronology
  additively."**
- **"Do not rewrite earlier evidence as though the clarifications had existed
  at the time."**
- The required history pattern, for every affected item: **original participant
  response · original validation interpretation/classification · later
  clarification · additive reconciliation result.**
- On integrity failure: **"Stop and return the exact discrepancy. Do not repair
  historical evidence or Draft v6."** The gate passed; the five prior records
  were verified unchanged by diff against `096682f`.

### 2.2 Classification of the clarification evidence

- **"FU-1 through FU-5 are Client-Stated evidence."**
- Preserve qualifiers — **"I don't know exactly"**, broad wording such as
  **"Everything"**, and **"the distinction between intended meaning and
  independently established institutional fact."**
- **"Clarification may change interpretation of earlier Client-Stated evidence
  without retroactively changing what the earlier response originally said."**

### 2.3 Per-response handling, with the forbidden inferences enumerated

- **FU-1** is *"clarification of referent"*, distinguishing present
  institution-building from actual external/business operations. **"Do not
  infer that no organizational activity has occurred."**
- **FU-2** — *"Preserve the breadth of the response."* **"Do not normalize this
  into a precise methodology taxonomy unless independent evidence warrants that
  taxonomy."**
- **FU-3** — preserve durable preservation *"comparable to keeping something in
  a library"* and *"for the record"* as official institutional standing.
  **"This clarification does NOT satisfy A17 rule 7."** Still missing: *"specific
  custodian; storage location/mechanism; retention rule; specific custody
  disposition."* **"Preserve the closure condition as unresolved."**
- **FU-4** — *"Preserve the uncertainty. Do not transform this response into a
  complete sequence."* Permitted use is limited to three explicitly supplied
  facts. **"Do not manufacture missing sequence steps from repository
  chronology. Do not convert nearby commits into participant-described actions.
  Do not treat repository facts as though the Founder personally supplied them
  in the walkthrough."**
- **FU-5** — Client-Stated evidence for the A10 correspondence/source-location
  fact. **"ChatGPT Projects are not authoritative merely because work is
  discussed there."** **"Do not infer that every relevant conversation exists,
  is accessible, or was obtained merely because the Founder named ChatGPT
  Projects."**

### 2.4 Comparison rules

- **"Do not automatically prefer participant evidence. Do not automatically
  prefer records."**
- **"Do not fill gaps using: Development memory; prior Founder conversations
  outside the authorized evidence basis; likely institutional intent; nearby
  repository prose that does not establish the proposition at issue."**

### 2.5 RQ-H — RESOLVED

- **Governing alternative: ALTERNATIVE C. Alternative B is declined.**
- "The organizational boundary remains whatever the organization states under
  A3 step 11."
- **"Where a Client-Stated boundary answer does not name a category posed by
  the method, the omitted category remains unnamed in the evidence."**
- Silence establishes **"neither exclusion from the organization; nor inclusion
  in the organization; nor an unresolved institutional fact."**
- "The prior omission of Huerta Group institutional functions or HGP from the
  Founder's boundary response is a fact about that participant-evidence set
  only."
- **"No new A3 boundary state is authorized."** **"No substantive Draft v7
  change or revalidation is authorized from RQ-H alone."**

### 2.6 Bounded reassessment

- "Re-evaluate only the existing validation findings, instrument sufficiency
  states, A18 advancement elements, and A10 trail facts actually reached by
  this new evidence."
- Instrument 4 and Instrument 6 reassessed **only where the clarification
  materially supplies missing evidence**; Instrument 6 expressly **not**
  broadened "into a complete memory-risk inventory."
- Instrument 5: **"Do not mark completed merely because the Founder named
  ChatGPT Projects. Apply Draft v6's actual sufficiency rules."**
- A18: **"Do not rerun the full diagnostic or reinterpret unaffected A18
  elements."**
- MD-1 … MD-5 assessed for whether the new evidence **changes the evidentiary
  basis, narrows, expands, leaves unchanged, or exhausts** each. **"Do not
  convert a clarification into methodology repair automatically."**

### 2.7 A17 rule 7

**"A17 rule 7 remains unsatisfied unless the authoritative existing validation
record independently contains the specific custody disposition required by
Draft v6."** And, as a Founder Office direction rather than a finding for
Development to reach: **"FU-3 alone does not satisfy it."** The required output
is stated in the instrument itself, including that **"subject diagnosis
therefore may not be represented as unqualified complete."**

### 2.8 The new-semantic-question standard

A new question exists **only if** all three hold: "Draft v6 contains two
materially different plausible readings"; "those readings would cause competent
assessors to behave differently"; and "existing Founder Office/Methodology
authority does not resolve the choice."

**Never elevated into a methodology semantic question:** *"subject uncertainty;
missing evidence; unexercised conditions; application mistakes."*

### 2.9 Remaining gaps

- "State the exact remaining internal-validation gaps after reconciliation."
- **"Another full internal application is not presently warranted."**
- Gap types preserved where still applicable: distributed participants,
  contested authority, participant refusal, organizational scale, genuine
  external client context, and other conditions not authentically producible
  inside current Huerta Group operation.
- **"Do not invent internal subjects or scenarios to close these gaps."**

### 2.10 The versioning test, with its own anti-inflation rule

"Another version is justified only if the evidence supports a coherent
methodology correction requiring text/instrument change. **It is not justified
merely because: participant answers became clearer; subject findings remain
unresolved; external validation is still absent; some authentic conditions were
not exercisable internally.**"

One of three answers must be given, **evidence basis only**, and — **"Do not
construct the version."**

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2
pattern); both digest forms are in the preservation record.

**Deficiency 2 — this reconciliation has less independence than the
application it reconciles, and that is a regression.** `fd-0033` §2.3 required
the application to be performed by an authoring context that did not construct
Draft v6, and it was. **This reconciliation was performed by Institutional
Platform Development — the office that constructed Draft v6** — reassessing
that application's findings, its instrument markers, its advancement state and
its method defects. `fd-0035` imposes no independence requirement, so nothing
was violated; **but the independence achieved is lower than at any point since
Stage 9**, and it is recorded rather than left for a reader to notice. It is
disclosed at reconciliation report §1 rather than in a footnote.

**Deficiency 3 — the participant is the authorizing office, and now also
determines the reserved question about his own evidence.** The Founder Office
wrote this instrument, supplied the five answers inside it, wrote the handling
rules governing how its own answers must be treated, **resolved RQ-H — a
question about how its own boundary answer's silence is read** — and will
review the result. `fd-0033` §4 Deficiency 4 and `fd-0034` §4 Deficiency 2
recorded the earlier forms of this exposure. It is sharper again here and is
recorded, not cured.

**Deficiency 4 — the instrument offers a state Draft v6 does not have.**
`fd-0035` §2.4 directs a determination of whether the walkthrough category is
*"obtained, partially obtained, or still materially incomplete."* **Draft v6's
A10 provides five states and none is partial.** Draft v6 was applied as the
method under reconciliation and no state was invented; the divergence is
recorded at reconciliation report §17 and §28, on the same handling
`fd-0033` §2.7 versus A7 received at completion report §61 L-10.
