## Truth boundary (verbatim from the instrument)

The approved public facts governing parent representation of Huerta
Group Publishing:

- "Huerta Group Publishing is Huerta Group LLC's first formally
  chartered venture."
- "Huerta Group Publishing operates under delegated authority within
  Huerta Group LLC."
- "Huerta Group Publishing is building and operating publishing
  infrastructure centered on authorship, editorial development,
  production, and durable publication records."
- "Huerta Group Publishing operates software supporting its publishing
  operation."
- "The Author Operating System is a platform operated by Huerta Group
  Publishing."

Prohibited implications (verbatim in substance): commercial launch;
open public enrollment or self-service access; operational public
billing; fully automated distribution; independent-company or
subsidiary status; the Author Operating System as a Huerta Group LLC
corporate platform; additional ventures publicly operating. Maturity
ceiling: "Chartered does not mean launched. Internally operational
does not mean commercially available."

## Representation before linking

Huerta Group LLC may acknowledge Huerta Group Publishing publicly
before the venture's official public launch; a direct parent-to-HGP
public link remains gated until launch authority or a separate Founder
Office determination authorizes pre-launch linking. At recording, no
repository authority records the launch gate as satisfied — the
default (no parent-to-HGP public link) governs.
