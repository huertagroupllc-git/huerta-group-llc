## 2. Determinations (verbatim from the instrument)

### 2.1 Stage and objectives

- Stage: "Stage 5 — Internal Application of Frozen Draft v3 to Huerta Group
  Publishing"; status "AUTHORIZED".
- Four bounded objectives: preserve and verify Draft v3 as the frozen Stage 4
  baseline; complete the Q3, Q14 and Q29 clarification comparisons; "resolve or
  formally propose the narrowest truthful reporting treatment for one-sided
  evidence"; and "apply frozen Draft v3 once to Huerta Group Publishing as an
  internal subordinate organizational subject and evaluate the method without
  modifying HGP or silently modifying the diagnostic."
- Boundary: "This is methodology validation work. It is not authorization to
  repair HGP, redesign HGP, construct software, establish external validity, or
  adopt the Organizational Diagnostic as established Huerta Group methodology."
- Repository-first rule: "Work from the actual repositories and governed
  artifacts… Do not reconstruct Stage 4, Draft v3, HGP architecture, HGP
  terminology, HGP operational history, diagnostic instruments, evidence
  classifications, or methodology rules from this execution package when the
  authoritative repository artifacts exist."

### 2.2 The Draft v3 freeze gate

- "Before HGP diagnostic application begins, verify Draft v3 against the
  Stage 4 digest/integrity record."
- If not verified: "STOP before HGP application. Return the integrity
  discrepancy. Do not normalize, regenerate, or silently repair Draft v3."
- "Do not modify Draft v3 during Stage 5 application."
- Finding rule: any method issue exposed is recorded separately as observation,
  comparison finding, method deficiency, or proposed correction.
- **Critical rule:** "No correction may be silently folded into Draft v3 while
  the test is running."

### 2.3 Stage 4 findings accepted; representable versus demonstrated

- Accepted: sensemaking versus clarification versus routing versus source
  authority; authority concentration as observed condition rather than
  governance default; participant authorship disclosure; limb-level evidence
  capture; session-by-session access statements; stated-to-exist versus
  actually-obtained evidence; account-internal contradiction preservation;
  unopposed evidence not being treated as corroborated.
- **Representable versus demonstrated:** "Preserve the distinction between a
  condition the diagnostic can represent and a condition actually demonstrated
  through validation evidence. In particular, contested authority may remain
  representable in Draft v3, but Huerta Group evidence has not validated that
  behavior."

### 2.4 One-sided evidence reporting

- The deficiency: evidence on only one side; no contradictory second source;
  "and the absence of another source is itself known."
- Must not be classified as: "corroborated merely because it is unopposed";
  "contradicted"; or "unresolved merely because the current reporting
  vocabulary lacks a better fit."
- Instruction: develop "the narrowest truthful treatment that expresses the
  evidentiary state without inferring the speaker's credibility, intent,
  certainty, honesty, or state of mind." Preference: "a descriptive
  evidence-state/reporting treatment rather than a new inferential
  classification."
- Governance boundary: determine whether it is introducible as
  reporting/composition under existing authority or would amend a fixed
  governed comparison vocabulary; "If it would require changing governed
  methodology semantics beyond Stage 5 authority, do not silently amend them.
  Preserve the proposed treatment and return the exact methodology-governance
  decision required."
- Eight required record fields, from the deficiency through "whether Founder
  Office authority is required before formal adoption."

### 2.5 Clarification comparison completion

- "Re-run only the affected Stage 3 comparison rows for Q3, Q14, and Q29 using
  the later Founder clarifications already preserved as Client-Stated
  evidence." · "Do not alter the original answers." · "Preserve chronology."
- Eight required fields per question, from the original response through the
  remaining limitation.
- Aggregates: "Recalculate aggregate comparison counts only if the frozen
  method actually requires them. Do not create aggregate metrics merely for
  report completeness."

### 2.6 The HGP application

- Subject: Huerta Group Publishing. Classification: "INTERNAL SUBORDINATE
  ORGANIZATIONAL SUBJECT."
- Purpose: exercise frozen Draft v3 "against a governed subject that is
  structurally distinct from Huerta Group LLC parent-level institutional
  operation and possesses its own terminology, records, operational history,
  workflows, state transitions, corrections, and systems."
- Not validated: "This application does not establish evaluator independence or
  external organizational applicability."
- **Prior-familiarity limitation, compulsory and recorded before examination:**
  Development has previously read HGP records, supported its architecture and
  implementation, evaluated its design and validation evidence, and used
  admitted HGP evidence. Consequence: "Do not characterize this as: blind
  validation; independent validation; external validation; unfamiliar-subject
  validation." Evidence rule: "Prior familiarity is a methodological
  limitation, not admissible Stage 5 diagnostic evidence by itself."
- Terminology rule: "Use HGP's own terminology first. Do not translate HGP
  terminology into Huerta Group parent/corporate terminology merely for
  convenience."
- Twelve specific validation targets, including whether the method can
  "distinguish multiple types of records of truth without prematurely
  collapsing them into one universal source," "represent deliberate absence
  without manufacturing a deficiency," and "preserve diagnostic findings that
  resemble product defects without crossing authority boundaries and repairing
  them."

### 2.7 Venture boundary and non-intervention

- "HGP remains its own governed venture." · "HGP operational records remain
  HGP-authoritative." · "Diagnosing HGP does not authorize changing HGP."
- Not authorized: HGP correction; workflow change; governance amendment;
  product change; schema change; database write; software modification.
- Confidentiality: author-confidential, manuscript-specific, deliberative, or
  product-specific material "unnecessary to the diagnostic" is not reproduced
  into the parent methodology corpus; only the organizational evidence
  legitimately required is recorded or abstracted.
- **Critical rule:** "A diagnostic finding is not implementation authority."
  Where a finding warrants future HGP attention, "identify the responsible HGP
  authority/function without executing the correction."

### 2.8 Evidence discipline, advancement, maturity, and the external gap

- Six evidence states preserved distinctly: directly observed repository/system
  evidence; HGP-stated institutional records; Founder/venture-steward
  Client-Stated evidence if actually supplied; inference; inaccessible
  evidence; "evidence known or stated to exist but not actually obtained."
- "Do not treat Development's prior knowledge as obtained evidence." · "Missing
  access must remain visible as a limitation. Do not silently substitute prior
  familiarity for unavailable evidence."
- Stakeholder gate: "Do not require a Founder/venture-steward interview merely
  to mirror the Huerta Group LLC Stage 3 test." Where the method requires
  stakeholder evidence, "STOP the affected advancement at that point," prepare
  only the minimum neutral questions, and "Do not fabricate answers merely to
  complete the Stage 5 report."
- Advancement: "Apply Draft v3's actual advancement logic exactly." · "A useful
  diagnosis does not need to produce remediation." · "Do not force advancement
  merely to demonstrate the method."
- Maturity ceiling: Stage 5 does not authorize designation as validated through
  use, externally validated, standardized, established methodology, or Method
  Library method.
- External gap: "HGP is a Huerta Group venture. Therefore successful Stage 5
  application remains internal evidence," establishing no external
  applicability, client validity, multi-organization validation, "true
  evaluator independence," standardization, commercial readiness, or Method
  Library maturity.

### 2.9 Continued exclusions and repository execution

- Exclusions: external client engagement; HGP remediation; HGP schema changes;
  HGP software changes; HGP database writes; Method Library admission; method
  standardization; diagnostic software; assessment automation; AI diagnostic
  authority; scoring; maturity models; pricing; packaging; marketing claims;
  Corporate Command Center changes.
- Repository: use existing methodology/governance conventions; "Do not create a
  parallel evidence repository"; "Do not copy unnecessary confidential HGP
  substance into parent records"; "Preserve provenance sufficient to
  reconstruct why each diagnostic finding exists"; bounded commits; gates before
  finalizing; "Do not modify the HGP repository merely to complete the
  diagnostic"; "Do not push HGP changes because Stage 5 authorizes no HGP
  changes."

## 4. Deficiencies recorded

**Deficiency 1 — no independent copy of the preserved instrument was
compared.** Preserved from the text as transmitted; the Founder Office's own
copy was not available for byte comparison (`fd-0019` Deficiency 2 pattern).

**Deficiency 2 — the venture-steward account was not obtained.** The
instrument forbids requiring an interview merely to mirror Stage 3, and
Development did not seek one. The consequence is that the HGP diagnosis is a
**records-only** diagnosis under Draft v3 A3 step 8, which constrains its
outcome by rule; the minimum neutral question set is returned instead
(Stage 5 report §28).

**Deficiency 3 — evaluator independence does not exist and cannot be
manufactured.** Development authored or supported much of the subject's
architecture and previously used its evidence. The limitation is recorded
before the examination and carried into every finding.
