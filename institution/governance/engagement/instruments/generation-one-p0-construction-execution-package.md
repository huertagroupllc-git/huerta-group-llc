# Generation One — Client Engagement P0 Construction & First-Client Readiness — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0037`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it.

**This instrument opens a new program.** It is the first Founder Office
instrument outside the Organizational Systems Methodology program, and the
first to concern **outside clients**. It authorizes the minimum governed
operating backbone required to accept Client #1 **without improvising
authority, scope, confidentiality, custody, provenance, or institutional
rules**, and it fixes nine distinctions the architecture must hold
structurally. Its governing discipline is **narrowness**: *"Do not create a
database table or software entity merely because an operating concept
exists,"* and *"prefer governed repository-backed manual operation"* over
software wherever integrity permits. It carries its own truth standard —
**READY may not be declared merely because the software works** — and requires
**NOT READY** while any external administrative dependency remains open. It
authorizes **no production client portal, no CRM, no Draft v8, and no
methodology iteration.**
