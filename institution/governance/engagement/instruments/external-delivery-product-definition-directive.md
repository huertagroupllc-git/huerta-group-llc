Source: Founder Office
Receiving Function: Institutional Platform Development
Subject: External Delivery — Generation One Headquarters Product Definition

Founder Office accepts the Corporate Operations & Command Management operating requirement for the External Delivery Capability Network & Implementation Scoping / Sourcing model.

Define the bounded Headquarters product capability necessary to operationalize that accepted model.

Product objective

Enable Huerta Group Headquarters to institutionalize external delivery capability across three related layers:

1. Capability Network
Durable knowledge and evidence concerning individuals and organizations that may provide external delivery capability.

2. Implementation Scoping & Sourcing
Opportunity/engagement-specific determination of what contemplated implementation requires, how it should be delivered, what external capability/cost/risk is involved, and whether Huerta Group possesses sufficient information to make a commercial offer.

3. External Delivery Assignment
Governed connection of an appropriately selected external resource to a bounded component of authorized Huerta Group client work, including execution state, Huerta Group acceptance and resulting provider-performance evidence.

The product must support Founder Office’s preference to progressively reduce unnecessary dependence on Founder-performed execution while preserving Huerta Group discretion over delivery method.

External execution is a normal delivery option, not an exception and not a mandate.

Accepted conceptual records

Product definition should establish the minimum required representation for:

* External Provider;
* Provider Capability;
* Provider Evidence;
* Provider Evaluation;
* Implementation Scoping & Sourcing;
* Implementation Requirement / Required Capability;
* candidate resource consideration;
* Delivery Method;
* External Delivery Assignment;
* Provider Performance Record.

Determine which require independent persisted primitives and which can responsibly be represented through relationships or derived state.

Do not mechanically translate every conceptual noun into a database table.

Provider model

Support:

* individual freelancers/independent specialists;
* businesses;
* agencies;
* professional firms;
* other legitimate external delivery organizations.

Provider type must not establish quality or preference.

Incomplete records are legitimate.

Unknown must remain representable.

Provider discovery source must be preserved without treating source as qualification.

Tucson Digital Deals participation, marketplace presence, personal relationship, referral or similar provenance must remain distinct from Huerta Group qualification.

Capability-specific evidence and maturity

Preserve the accepted maturity concept:

Discovered → Evaluated → Tested → Proven

Maturity is evidence-based, contextual and reversible.

Product architecture must not assume that a provider is universally Proven.

Evidence and maturity may differ materially by capability.

If a provider-level summary/posture is useful, it must not overwrite or obscure capability-specific evidence.

Do not create public scores, star ratings, approved-vendor badges or universal numerical rankings.

Network development

Headquarters must permit authentic network development immediately, including:

Discover → Record → Characterize Capability → Gather Evidence → Evaluate → Optional Test → Actual Use → Learn

The product should allow a provider to be useful before every field is known.

Historical evidence must remain durable even when current sourcing posture changes.

Implementation Scoping & Sourcing

Define a bounded workflow supporting:

Contemplated Implementation
→ Define Outcome
→ Decompose Requirements
→ Identify Required Capabilities
→ Determine Candidate Delivery Methods
→ Resolve Material Unknowns
→ Source/Evaluate Resources where necessary
→ Discover Costs/Dependencies/Risks
→ Determine Delivery Architecture
→ Determine Commercial Readiness
→ Founder Commercial Decision / Commercial Proposal path or Do Not Pursue

OSA must not be required as the originating path.

Do not create a generalized sales pipeline.

No standardized sourcing/scoping turnaround exists.

The product should preserve sufficient timestamps/state to allow Huerta Group to learn actual elapsed time from authentic use.

Founder external-execution preference

Delivery Method must permit at minimum:

* Founder/internal;
* AI/software-assisted internal;
* external subcontractor;
* hybrid;
* client-retained provider coordination;
* independently retained licensed/specialized professional;
* unresolved.

Product design should make external sourcing an ordinary first-class path rather than a buried exception.

However, do not create automatic outsourcing or provider-selection logic.

The operating question is whether qualified external capability can perform the requirement to Huerta Group’s required standard under acceptable economics, control, confidentiality, risk and accountability.

Proposal economics

Headquarters may preserve provider quotations, expected external cost, software/direct costs and other sourcing economics necessary for commercial decision-making.

These are operational/commercial planning facts.

They are not accounting transactions.

QuickBooks remains authoritative for actual bills, expenses, liabilities, payments, invoicing and other accounting truth.

Do not duplicate QuickBooks.

Ordinary proposal work versus paid scoping

Preserve the accepted substance test.

Ordinary proposal development may include reasonable research, straightforward provider quotations, software-price checking and similar work principally necessary to price understandable implementation.

When reliable pricing requires material professional analysis necessary to determine what the solution itself should be, Headquarters must support disposition toward separately authorized paid scoping rather than allowing unlimited free proposal development.

Do not establish a fixed hour or fee threshold.

External assignment and acceptance

External assignment must connect to actual authorized Huerta Group work rather than independently creating client authority.

Preserve:

Provider completion ≠ Huerta Group acceptance ≠ Client Deliverable / acknowledgement

The product must permit a proportionate acceptance basis and Huerta Group disposition such as acceptance, rework required, rejection/escalation, or equivalent semantics.

Accepted external output may subsequently participate in the existing governed Work Product / Deliverable chain.

Do not duplicate those client-engagement primitives.

Provider-performance learning

Actual use must feed durable provider evidence.

After meaningful assignments, Headquarters should be able to preserve relevant observations including requirement satisfaction, quality/rework, timeliness, communication, security/access compliance, integration burden, problems, acceptance outcome and whether Huerta Group would consider the provider again.

Avoid permanent simplistic ratings.

Current sourcing posture may change while historical evidence remains.

Pre-first-client-execution gates

The product definition must preserve visible readiness boundaries for actual externally executed client work.

Actual external client execution remains gated by sufficient:

* underlying client authorization;
* client-side authority to use the delivery arrangement;
* provider-side contractual basis;
* confidentiality/data handling;
* IP/work-product chain;
* security/system/physical access controls where applicable;
* licensing/professional restrictions where applicable;
* assignment-specific scope;
* acceptance basis.

Do not invent legal doctrine.

Do not imply that Headquarters software itself establishes legal sufficiency.

Existing-system integration

Integrate by reference with existing authoritative Headquarters concepts including, where applicable:

* Organization;
* Person;
* Possible Work;
* Engagement;
* Client Decision;
* Authorized Work;
* Work Product;
* Deliverable;
* Commercial Proposal.

Do not duplicate them.

Maintain existing authority boundaries with Client Engagement, Corporate Operations, Administration & Compliance, Accounting/QuickBooks and counsel.

Generation One restraint

Do not design:

* enterprise procurement;
* public provider directory;
* provider marketplace;
* referral network;
* TDD integration;
* RFQ/RFP platform;
* purchase-order system;
* accounts payable;
* provider invoicing/payment processing;
* generalized contract-management system;
* generalized project-management system;
* workforce scheduling;
* time tracking;
* utilization planning;
* automatic provider selection;
* AI provider matching;
* provider portal;
* Client Platform changes;
* public website changes;
* OSA changes.

Authentic-use orientation

Product definition should support the Corporate Operations validation scenarios, particularly:

* recording a newly discovered freelancer;
* recording a Tucson business independently from its TDD relationship;
* conducting a bounded non-client test;
* straightforward implementation sourcing;
* detecting the paid-scoping threshold;
* hybrid delivery;
* provider completion requiring rework;
* successful external client assignment producing reusable performance evidence.

Do not create an elaborate synthetic procurement environment.
