## 2. Seven proposed corrections — none applied

- **PC-1 (MF-1).** Add the evidence class *Client-Stated* to A0's enumeration of
  retained uses of "client," as a third retained fixed name, noting it is a
  class label rather than prose. **No doctrine change; a completion of the CS-16
  settlement.**
- **PC-2 (MF-2).** **Do not add a strength or recurrence axis to A2** — the
  author states the correct disposition may be to decline. What is proposed is
  narrower: record in A20, or in a note to A2 rule 6, that A2 carries no
  recurrence or observer-independence axis and that this is a known and
  deliberate limit. *"A2's freedom from ordering is a feature that this
  application confirmed under pressure, and it should not be traded away."*
- **PC-3 (MF-3).** Extend A18 item 3 to require, where the count is zero, a
  statement of **why** — distinguishing *no shared-source corroboration
  occurred* from *no accounts existed, so the condition could not arise*.
  Optionally extend the mark to record-versus-record sharing.
- **PC-4 (MF-4).** Rename Instrument 1's *opposing account* to *opposing
  source*, or add a parallel field for record-versus-record opposition.
  **Retain the register-wide-statement mechanism unchanged** — it prevented a
  real burial here.
- **PC-5 (MF-5).** Split Part B's header requirement: the **advancement-state
  declaration and the sufficiency marker with its reason** at the head of every
  instrument in full, always; the **access statement** in full only where it
  differs between instruments, and otherwise once, as a named dated
  statement-in-force each header cites by name with the determinations material
  to that instrument. *"This is the deviation I actually made, offered for
  adoption or rejection rather than presented as compliance."*
- **PC-6 (MF-6).** Add to A20's non-selective limitations list that **A10's
  correspondence category is participant-dependent at its first fact**, joining
  A6, A7 and A9. Optionally note in A10 that where its existence cannot be
  determined, *existence unknown* is the correct entry.
- **PC-7 (MF-7).** State the evidentiary standard for A15's preceding question —
  whether Client-Stated evidence of a dormant capability suffices to fire it,
  and if so, that the termination records the class of the evidence that fired
  it.

## 3. Two matters Development flags before any disposition

**Neither is a disposition. Both are things a later adjudication should not have
to rediscover.**

**(a) MF-5 is a recorded non-compliance, not only a proposal.** The validating
author **did not follow Part B's header requirement literally** and says so in
its own words rather than presenting its practice as compliance. Two
consequences follow. First, **every instrument header in the Stage 10
application is a deviation from Draft v5 as written** — the application's outputs
should be read knowing that. Second, PC-5 is unlike the other six: adopting it
would ratify what was already done, and declining it would leave the method with
a requirement that its first non-authoring applier could not meet. **That is a
sharper choice than the other proposals present, and it should not be
dispositioned as though it were routine.**

**(b) MF-2 and PC-2 come with the author's own recommendation to decline the
larger change.** The finding is real and the author's evidence for it is strong —
A2 rule 6's own wording reaches for an axis it lacks — but the author expressly
warns against adding an axis to A2 and proposes only a disclosure. **A later
adjudication that reads the finding without the proposal could easily
over-correct**, and A2's non-ordering is the property `fd-0026` §2.1 protected
most carefully and that this application confirmed under pressure at CS-01.
