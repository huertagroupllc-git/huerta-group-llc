## 7. MD-1 implementation

**Where:** A15 — *Preceding question* rewritten; *Where either limb rests on a
Client-Stated assertion* rewritten. Instrument 9 — preceding-question rule
rewritten.

**What the text now requires.** The preceding question is stated as having **two
limbs**, and a "yes" requires **both** to be **Observed**: that the capability
already exists — built, standby, or dormant — **and** that it is actually
awaiting an organizational-authority decision rather than further construction.
The stated reason is carried through: an affirmative answer can stop the test,
so **every condition the shortcut rests on** is established rather than
asserted.

**Client-Stated evidence is protected in terms.** Where either limb rests on an
assertion, it is recorded with its provenance and class and is **"not false, not
downgraded, and not rejected"** — it simply does not establish the terminating
condition, and the ordinary test proceeds.

**The defect is named in the text rather than quietly fixed.** A15 records that
*"an earlier version placed the requirement on existence alone, which allowed
the shortcut to fire while the second limb — the one that makes it consequential
— rested on assertion."* A reader of Draft v7 can see what changed and why
without leaving the document.

**A2 boundary held.** No hierarchy, ranking, weighting, strength, quality
scoring or credibility ordering was introduced; §19 records the verification.

## 8. MD-2 implementation

**Where:** A15, a new treatment inserted immediately before *Access is not
existence*.

**What the text now says.** Where the proposition under test is that a
capability exists but is **standby, dormant, or otherwise not presently
operating**, observation of it operating is **not required and is not demanded**
— *"a dormant capability does not operate, and requiring operation would make
the proposition unestablishable in principle."* Its existence and material state
may be established by **direct examination of the capability itself** — its
implementation, configuration, deployment state, or other directly examinable
material evidence.

**Absence of operational observation is bounded to what it actually limits:**
*"what may be said about its actual use and performance"*, recorded as a
limitation. It is **not evidence that the capability does not exist**, and
**operation is never manufactured to corroborate dormancy**.

**A2 was not touched, and the text says why.** The treatment states that this is
first-hand evidence under **A2's own definition of *Observed***, and that it is
**not** A2 rule 4's corroboration route — *"rule 4 governs when an assertion
*about* a condition becomes Observed, and direct examination of the artifact
that constitutes the capability is not an assertion about it."*

**This reading is Development's and is flagged as rejectable.** MD-2 arose from
A2 rule 4's wording; the implementation resolves it in A15 and leaves A2
byte-identical. **If the Founder Office does not accept that reading, the
correct locus for MD-2's treatment is an open question** — recorded at `fd-0036`
§4 Deficiency 4 and `draft-v7-traceability.md` §2, not buried.

**Coherence with MD-1, as `fd-0036` §2.2 requires.** They operate on different
things: MD-1 fixes **how many limbs** must be Observed; MD-2 fixes **how one of
them may be established** where the capability is dormant. Neither weakens the
other — a dormant capability's existence may be established by examination, and
the second limb still must be Observed independently.

## 9. MD-3 implementation

**Where:** A15 — routing paragraph rewritten, new outcome semantics added.
Instrument 9 — permitted outcomes and recording rules rewritten.

**The outcome, as worded:** ***existing capability awaits an
organizational-authority decision***.

**Its meaning, fixed narrowly in A15:** construction is **not the presently
unresolved issue**; the next material issue is an organizational-authority
decision about **activation, adoption, release, use, or an equivalent
disposition**, routed to the competent authority. It **authorizes neither
activation nor construction** and implies **no suitability, no validation, no
approval, and no production readiness**. It is **not collapsed into *existing
system should be improved rather than replaced*** where improvement is not the
unresolved matter — and the text records that *"an earlier version had no
outcome for this condition and forced exactly that misdescription."*

**Instrument 10's relationship is stated in both places:** it **carries the
authority question** and **is not a substitute for recording the correct outcome
here**; both are produced.

**Instrument 9's recording rules were updated to match**: the outcome is one of
the **six** permitted outcomes and nothing else, the new outcome is **never used
as a softer form** of the improvement outcome, and it **authorizes nothing**.

## 10. MD-4 limitation/guidance implementation

**Where:** A20 — two paragraphs added after the observation-dependent-tests
disclosure, which is where `fd-0031` PC-2 established that this class of
disclosure belongs.

**What it says.** A15's threshold, A6's role-compression test and A7's habit
test each require Observed evidence for their own reasons, and an application
meeting all three in succession **"can come away with the impression that
accounts are the weak evidence. That impression is wrong, and this list says so
rather than leaving it to be inferred."** Different methodological questions
legitimately require different kinds of evidence; a local Observed requirement
**"establishes nothing whatever about Client-Stated evidence in general"** and
must not be generalized into a claim that it is weaker, inferior, less credible
or lower ranked. **A2 remains unordered** (A2 rule 5).

**The evidence for both halves is carried, as `fd-0036` §2.4 requires.** Some
conditions genuinely cannot be established from a participant assertion alone.
And in the one application with participant evidence, **Client-Stated evidence
materially changed fourteen reconciled items**, and a **Client-Stated
clarification eliminated a disagreement that would otherwise have stood as a
finding**. The text concludes: *"An applier who reads the three tests as a
verdict on accounts has reached a conclusion the evidence contradicts."*

**Disclosure only.** The paragraph states expressly that it **"adds no ranking,
weighting, quality measure, independence score, maturity score, evidence axis,
or preferential class."** Verified at §19.

## 11. MD-5 implementation

**Where:** A18, a block added after the four-states and language-rule
paragraphs.

**What the text now requires.** Where an application is **suspended, resumed,
clarified after an earlier close, or otherwise reaches more than one
outcome-bearing state**, it is not represented as though it had a single
timeless outcome. **Every outcome-bearing state is retained**, each recording
its **date and order**, **evidence basis**, **reason**, the **constraint
operating at that time** including any since lifted, and its **relationship to
any later state**.

**History rule, in terms:** *"A later state may supplement or supersede the
operational effect of an earlier one. It never erases it."*

**The four minimum distinctions are stated and made independent:**
application-stage status; the diagnostic outcome **at that point in time**;
advancement permission; and any unresolved closure condition — with the
consequence spelled out: *"a closure condition may be discharged while
advancement remains refused, and the same outcome may be reached twice without
the diagnosis becoming complete."* That case is not hypothetical; it is what
this package produced (§13).

**Boundary observed:** *"This is chronology and diagnostic semantics only. It
defines no project lifecycle, no generic status system, and no workflow."*
