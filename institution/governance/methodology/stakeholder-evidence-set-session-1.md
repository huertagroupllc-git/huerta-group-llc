## 1. Capture rules actually applied

1. **Verbatim.** §2 reproduces the thirty responses exactly as transmitted,
   including the stakeholder's punctuation and typographic apostrophes. No
   wording was altered, completed, tidied, or ordered.
2. **No strengthening.** Where a response is short, hedged, or general, the
   row records it short, hedged, or general. "I feel like," "I'd say," "I'm
   sure," "I don't know," "Nothing yet," and "All of them" are preserved as
   the stakeholder's own force of claim.
3. **No translation.** No neutral or Huerta term was substituted for a
   stakeholder term during capture (`fd-0020` §2.11). The stakeholder's words
   are §4's organization's-terms rows.
4. **Splitting, not merging.** Where one answer carries several distinct
   claims, it becomes several rows (§3) so that the comparison can classify
   them separately.
5. **Limbs.** Several questions asked more than one thing. Limbs the response
   does not reach are recorded in §5 as *not supplied* rows — never filled
   from records, prior determinations, conversation history, or Development's
   knowledge, all of which would have supplied many of them.
6. **Conflicts kept.** Where two responses conflict with each other, both are
   kept and cross-referenced (§6). The stakeholder was not asked to choose.
7. **Nothing added.** No row contains anything the team knows and the
   stakeholder did not say.
