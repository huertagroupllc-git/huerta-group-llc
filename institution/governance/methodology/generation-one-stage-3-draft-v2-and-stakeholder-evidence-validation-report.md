## 7. Material differences between Draft v1 and Draft v2

1. **Terminology discovery precedes translation** (A1): the organization's
   words are recorded before any neutral term; every neutral term is
   conditional; a distributed authority structure is now as much a
   non-assumption as a single apex.
2. **Access is a recorded fact** (A3 step 10; Part B header): full /
   partial / none per corpus, system, and person, carried into every
   instrument and the A18 outcome.
3. **Stakeholder access is planned and its absence labelled** (A3 step 8;
   A18): "records-only" diagnoses cannot proceed or conclude no
   intervention.
4. **Prior knowledge is inadmissible until obtained** (A17 rule 6;
   Instrument 1 "obtained via").
5. **Existence, confidence, and authority are three facts** (A9; Instrument
   4), never collapsed; document existence and asserted condition are two
   rows (A2 rule 4).
6. **Work is discovered trail-first** (A10; Instrument 5 trail row), from
   artifacts and the last actual instance, before any description.
7. **One matter, N representations** (A9, A14; Instruments 4 and 7).
8. **Role compression is evidenced, never selected** (A6; Instrument 2).
9. **Routine acts with consequences** are asked about (A13; A10).
10. **Implementer-side custody**, including the team's own material, is
    inventoried and dispositioned (A12; A17 rule 7; Instrument 6).
11. **The advancement judgement is evidence-classed** and discloses
    author-of-records (A18).
12. Smaller: "evidenced by," "retrievable by the organization," "owner of
    the next expected action," "places recorded," the Instrument 9
    preceding question, consumption classification, cadence, the
    people-and-roles inventory.

Unchanged: purpose (A0, one clause added); A4, A11, A16, A19; Instruments
8 and 10; the five permitted Instrument 9 outcomes; the global instrument
rules; the mechanism-neutral memory principle.

## 33. New assumptions exposed by stakeholder validation

Exposed by *preparing* the validation (no validation occurred):

1. **Question authorship is itself a leading act.** Four of thirty
   questions had to be rewritten because their first drafts named concepts
   from A1; the method has no rule for reviewing its own questions for
   leading, only for its findings.
2. **The team's records set can be sealed, the team's memory cannot.** The
   sealed records-derived set protects the comparison from adjustment, but
   nothing protects the capture of answers from the team's familiarity
   except the capture rules — an assumption of discipline.
3. **A stakeholder may be the author of the records too.** Here the
   accountable authority authored or approved most records; "author-of-
   records" was written for the team, and Draft v2 has no equivalent
   disclosure for a stakeholder judging records they wrote.
4. **"Records-only" has a mirror the method lacks:** an "account-only"
   diagnosis (an organization with people and no records) has no label or
   constraint.

These are observations for the post-test correction package, not changes to
frozen Draft v2.
