### What disagrees

**The derived tallies contradict the table they are derived from, in two
records:**

| Record | What it says | What the table says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `stage-10-draft-v5-validation-application.md` §8, summary sentence | *"**twelve** EXERCISED, three PARTIALLY EXERCISED (CS-03, CS-06, CS-09), one EXERCISED on one branch (CS-14)"* — **12 + 3 + 1 = 16** | **Thirteen** rows read plain EXERCISED |
| `stage-10-draft-v5-internal-validation-report.md` §27 | EXERCISED **12**, naming CS-01, 02, 04, 05, 07, 08, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16 — and the column sums to **16** under a heading reading *"Seventeen-item exercise matrix"* | **CS-17 is EXERCISED in the table and appears in neither list** |

**The exact disagreement: CS-17 is classified EXERCISED in the authoritative
table and is omitted from both derived tallies, which therefore account for
sixteen of seventeen items.**

**Development's own error is the larger half.** The applier's summary sentence
was wrong by one; Development then reproduced the same omission in the Stage 10
report's §27 table and again in its §28 prose (*"the twelve above"*), and
returned it to the Founder Office without the column being added up. **A table
headed "seventeen-item" whose counts sum to sixteen should not have survived
the return.** It is recorded here rather than quietly corrected.

### Which disposition work this blocks, and which it does not

`fd-0031` §2.2 requires this stated rather than assumed.

- **It blocks nothing in the finding or proposal dispositions.** Those depend on
  MF and PC identifiers and relationships, which agree exactly.
- **It does not block the matrix reconciliation either**, because the disputed
  quantity is a *derived tally*, not a classification. The per-item table is
  complete, unambiguous, and evidenced, and `fd-0031` §2.3 requires every
  classification reassessed against what actually occurred rather than carried
  forward — so Stage 11 rebuilds the matrix from the per-item evidence in any
  case.
- **Development is not choosing one version by preference.** The table is not
  preferred over the summary; the summary is *derived from* the table and
  contradicts its own source, and the source carries the evidence while the
  tally carries none.

**The Founder Office may disagree with that judgement**, and the judgement is
therefore stated rather than buried: Development assessed this discrepancy as
**non-blocking** and proceeded. If the Founder Office assesses it as blocking,
the Stage 11 dispositions below stand on evidence that is unaffected by it.

### The second count defect in this program, and worth noting as a pattern

`fd-0026` §2.7 records the first: Stage 7's summary prose named five reserved
determinations while its item-level flags marked six, and the Founder Office
directed it **preserved rather than repaired**. This is the same failure mode —
a derived count diverging from the item-level record that generates it — in a
different stage, by a different author, and again caught only downstream.
**Two instances is a pattern in how this program summarizes itself, not two
accidents**, and it is recorded here so that a later stage can decide whether
the corpus needs a counting discipline rather than another correction.

## 2. The four classification definitions applied

Verbatim from `fd-0031` §2.3, which fixed them:

- **EXERCISED** — "The treatment encountered the authentic condition necessary
  to test it."
- **PARTIALLY EXERCISED** — "Some, but not all, material conditions necessary to
  test the treatment authentically occurred."
- **NOT EXERCISED** — "The treatment could in principle be exercised within the
  internal environment, but its required authentic condition did not occur in
  this application."
- **NOT EXERCISABLE INTERNALLY** — "The required condition cannot authentically
  be produced within the presently available internal environment without
  manufacturing the condition or changing the validation boundary."

**One reading is recorded because it decides three rows.** Participant evidence
is **internally available** — the Founder Office exists, is reachable, and is
HGP's own accountable authority; Stage 10 lacked it because the engagement
instructed the team not to obtain it, which is a per-application scope decision
rather than a property of the internal environment. **Participant-dependent
conditions are therefore internally reachable**, and their absence at Stage 10
yields *partially exercised* or *not exercised* — **never** *not exercisable
internally*. Reading it the other way would imply the remedy is external
application, which `fd-0031` §2.10 expressly forbids assuming, and would
contradict the Stage 10 report's own finding that the condition worth seeking
next is participant access.

## 4. The two substantive reclassifications

### 4.1 CS-07 — identity-of-record: EXERCISED → PARTIALLY EXERCISED

**Reason.** `fd-0026` §2.4 made the **absence rule** part of the treatment, not
an optional extra: where no authoritative identity source is held or identity
authority is unresolved, "identity-of-record remains a required output and must
state that truthfully rather than being omitted." That is a distinct material
condition of the treatment, and **it did not occur** — HGP has a settled,
sourced, approved answer.

**The applier said so itself**, and this reclassification takes it at its word
rather than overriding it: *"The 'still required when absent' limb was not
exercised — HGP has a settled answer — and that is reported rather than dressed
up."* Stage 10 nonetheless recorded the item as EXERCISED because the production
limb worked. Under `fd-0031` §2.3's definition — *some, but not all,* material
conditions — **partially exercised is the correct value.**

**The unexercised limb classifies as NOT EXERCISED, not NOT EXERCISABLE
INTERNALLY**: a subject whose identity authority is absent, conflicted or
unresolved could in principle exist internally. **Both present internal subjects
happen to have governing charters**, which is why the condition did not occur —
a fact about the two subjects available, not about the environment. Recorded so
a later stage does not assume the limb is unreachable.

### 4.2 CS-14 — confidentiality: EXERCISED (one branch) → PARTIALLY EXERCISED

**Reason, first: Stage 10's label was not one of the four permitted values.**
*"EXERCISED (one branch; the other did not arise)"* describes the situation
accurately and classifies it in a vocabulary that does not exist. Under the four
definitions it is unambiguously **partially exercised**.

**And the unexercised branch is the load-bearing one.** `fd-0026` §2.6 required
A17 rule 5 to state a minimum binding principle **precisely so the method works
when the parent doctrine does not travel with it** — the defect MO-14 recorded
was that the method cited a document it might not hold. Stage 10 exercised the
branch where the doctrine **was** held. **The branch that motivated the entire
change was not tested.**

**That branch classifies NOT EXERCISABLE INTERNALLY, and this is the one place
in the matrix where that value is correct at the limb level.** Producing it
requires withholding Methodology Foundation v1 from a validator that could
otherwise read it — and the Stage 10 evidence-access record states in terms that
withholding it "would have manufactured the answer." `fd-0031` §2.3's definition
excludes exactly that: a condition that cannot be produced "without manufacturing
the condition."

**Consequence, recorded plainly:** the conservative-default limb of CS-14
**cannot be honestly validated inside this institution's present environment.**
It is not a defect, it is not a drafting problem, and no correction addresses
it. It is a validation-evidence gap that only an engagement genuinely lacking the
parent doctrine could close.

## 6. What the reconciled matrix means for the method's evidence position

**Five of seventeen items are partially exercised, and four of the five are
partial for one reason: no participant.** CS-14's is partial for a different and
harder reason — its unexercised branch cannot be produced internally without
manufacturing it.

**None of this is a method defect** (`fd-0031` §2.8), and the disposition record
keeps the two apart deliberately: an unexercised provision lacks evidence, it is
not thereby wrong.
