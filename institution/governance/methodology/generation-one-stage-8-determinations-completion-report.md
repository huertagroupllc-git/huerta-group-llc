## 5. CS-01 reconciliation

**Determination received — dependency resolved.**

The source-kind attribute is approved as **descriptive and non-ordinal**: it
answers *"What kind of thing supplied this claim?"* and never *"How good…is
this evidence?"* Evidence quality is expressed only through limitations,
contradictions, provenance, freshness, coverage and contextual explanation, and
**A2's non-ordering rule remains controlling**.

**Draft v4 requirement:** a source-kind attribute on Client-Stated rows carrying
at least five values — person/stakeholder account, organizational or
institutional record, supplied institutional source, systematic
verification/validation record, and other kinds genuinely required by
application evidence — as an Instrument 1 field.

**Scope note:** **broader than the Stage 7 item**, which proposed four
categories. **Remaining ambiguity:** exact labels — future drafting jurisdiction
under the determination's express normalization authority. **Founder Office
authority still required: no.**

## 7. CS-03 reconciliation

**Determination received — dependency resolved.**

"'Diagnostic team' means the actual institutional or individual actor materially
performing the diagnostic," and **both** the institutional and the human/agent
relationship are disclosed where they differ. Material prior involvement is
enumerated across seven forms; **incidental familiarity, one-off exposure and
ordinary awareness are expressly excluded**. Institutional involvement is the
default structural reading "because replacing an individual or agent does not
eliminate prior involvement by the institution that designed, constructed, or
governed the subject." Scope is disclosure within this diagnostic only — **not a
universal independence doctrine for Huerta Group consulting**.

**Draft v4 requirement:** a single A18 disclosure block defining the term,
requiring both layers, applying the material/non-material test, and stating the
five required disclosure elements — beside the two existing disclosures, before
any finding.

**Scope note:** **an expansion.** The Stage 7 item offered the denotation as an
either/or; the determination requires both. **Remaining ambiguity:** layout
within one block — drafting jurisdiction. **Founder Office authority still
required: no.**

## 8. CS-07 reconciliation

**Determination received — dependency resolved.**

Identity-of-record is **retained and defined** as "a sourced statement of what
the organization's own authoritative or governing evidence establishes" about
what the organization is, what it exists to do, and the obligations or purposes
that define it — with explicit notation where no authoritative record exists,
sources conflict, or identity authority is unresolved. It is **expressly not** a
consultant-written mission statement, public copy by default, a summary invented
from activity, or a judgement about what the organization ought to become.
**Producing stage: A4**, supported by A3. Where absent, it "must state that
truthfully rather than being omitted."

A18's internal inconsistencies are settled: Instrument 10 and the advancement
action that returns questions are **different objects** and must stay distinct;
explicit exclusions are produced where the relevant domain discovers them and
aggregated by A18; and element 4 asks whether boundaries are **sufficiently
identified**, "including unresolved or contested authority where that is the
truthful finding."

**Both Stage 7 open points are answered** — the exclusion limbs are distributed
with A18 aggregation, and neither the outcome nor the output is removed.

**Ambiguity settled without being named:** the element-4 standard resolves the
returned question of *identified* versus *established*. Recorded at `fd-0026`
§4 Deficiency 3 so it is not left standing by oversight. **Founder Office
authority still required: no.**

## 9. CS-12 reconciliation

**Determination received — dependency resolved.**

**Qualified yes.** "A subject diagnosis may close as an application record with
an unresolved mandatory closure condition, but it may not be represented as an
unqualified completed diagnosis." Four states are distinguished —
application/stage work completed · subject diagnosis complete · advancement
permitted · closure condition unresolved. A conspicuous **unresolved-closure
statement** must identify the condition, why it is unsatisfied, who must satisfy
it, and "what consequence the unresolved condition has for use of the
diagnosis." It is **not a sixth outcome**.

**One addition beyond the Stage 7 item:** a **language rule** — "closed",
"complete" and equivalents may not be used unqualified. It bears directly on the
Stage 5 defect: a diagnosis that closed with custody unagreed and was described
without qualification.

**Remaining ambiguity:** placement relative to the CS-17 sufficiency declaration
— drafting jurisdiction. **Founder Office authority still required: no.**

## 10. CS-14 reconciliation

**Determination received — dependency resolved.**

Methodology Foundation v1 §7.5 **does not travel by implication**. A
client-facing diagnostic may not depend on an inaccessible parent document for a
rule necessary to handle confidential material safely — **and** may not
duplicate the full doctrine. A17 rule 5 must therefore state the **minimum
binding handling principle**, cite the governing doctrine where available,
record whether it was actually held, and require a **conservative default**
where it is not. The default is specified, together with a claim-preservation
rule: preserve the existence of the evidence, its provenance, the conclusion it
supports and any limitation, "without copying the confidential substance
itself."

**Scope note — the largest expansion of the six.** The Stage 7 item was
**wording-only** and expressly declined to propose stating any principle inside
the method; the determination requires one. **CS-14's drafting scope is
therefore structural, not wording-only.**

**Also settled:** the Stage 7 open point on what the conservative default should
be. **Remaining ambiguity:** the exact wording of the minimum principle —
sufficient for safe operation without duplicating the doctrine. Drafting
jurisdiction, and the narrowest of the six. **Founder Office authority still
required: no.**
