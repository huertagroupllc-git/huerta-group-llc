## 2. What this application is, and is not

- **Environment:** Huerta Group LLC internal validation.
- **Stakeholder:** the Founder Office, as the accountable-authority
  stakeholder of the organization being diagnosed.
- **Purpose:** to test whether Draft v2 can *obtain* and *represent*
  stakeholder evidence without relying on the diagnostic team's hidden prior
  knowledge — specifically the ten capabilities in `fd-0020` §2.11.
- **Not the purpose:** to obtain the supposedly correct Huerta Group answer
  from the Founder. There is no right answer; there is only what the
  stakeholder says, recorded faithfully, and what the records say, recorded
  separately.
- **Evidence class of every response:** **Client-Stated** unless
  independently corroborated (`fd-0020` §2.11). Corroboration, if any, is
  recorded in the comparison, never assumed.
- **Not external validation.** The stakeholder is internal; the maximum
  claim remains *internal provisional methodology draft with self-application
  and first structured internal stakeholder-evidence validation*, and only
  once evidence has genuinely been received.

## 3. The diagnostic team's declared position

Under Draft v2 A17 rule 6 and A18:

- **Author-of-records.** The team (Institutional Platform Development)
  authored most of the records it will compare the account against. Every
  comparison outcome will carry the label "author-of-records."
- **Prior knowledge is inadmissible until obtained.** Everything the team
  knows about Huerta Group LLC from its own work is *prior knowledge*
  (Instrument 1 "obtained via: prior knowledge") and supports no finding. The
  stakeholder session is the method step that makes stakeholder-held facts
  admissible; the records-derived set (`records-derived-evidence-set.md`) is
  the method step that makes record-held facts admissible.
- **No answering on the stakeholder's behalf.** The team will not fill,
  complete, paraphrase from memory, or infer any response. An unanswered
  question is an Instrument 1 row with source "not supplied."

## 4. Interaction channel and conditions

- **Channel:** the execution interaction in which this program runs — the
  Founder replies in writing, in the Founder's own words. Asynchronous
  replies are acceptable; partial replies are acceptable.
- **Form:** free text. No template, no minimum length, no required order.
  Answering by reference ("see document X") is acceptable and is recorded as
  a pointer, with the document then treated under A2 rule 4 (existence
  Observed; asserted condition Client-Stated).
- **Permitted answers that are still evidence:** "I don't know," "no one,"
  "nowhere," "not applicable," "I'd rather not say," "that question doesn't
  fit how we work." Each is recorded as given. A declined question is
  recorded as declined, never filled.
- **No time pressure and no leading.** The team asks no follow-up designed
  to steer toward the method's model. Follow-ups are permitted only to
  clarify the meaning of the stakeholder's own words, and are recorded as
  follow-ups.
- **Language:** the questions use plain words and avoid the diagnostic's
  analytical vocabulary and Huerta Group's institutional vocabulary alike.
  Where the stakeholder uses an institutional term, that is the
  stakeholder's word and is captured as such.

## 5. Capture rules (Instrument 1 conventions for this session)

1. Each response is one or more rows `S-nn`, source "Founder Office
   (stakeholder session 1)," class **Client-Stated**, obtained via **method
   step**, provenance: the interaction and the date received.
2. Responses are captured **verbatim** where the wording carries meaning;
   formatting (line breaks, punctuation, obvious typos) may be normalized;
   **substantive meaning is never normalized** (`fd-0020` §2.11).
3. Every term the stakeholder uses for a concept the method has a neutral
   term for is recorded as an **organization's-terms row** (A1) with the
   question it arose in. No neutral term is substituted during capture.
4. Where a response asserts a condition and also points to a document, two
   rows are created (A2 rule 4).
5. Where a response conflicts with another response, both are kept and
   cross-referenced; the stakeholder is not asked to choose.
6. Where a response conflicts with the records-derived set, the conflict is
   recorded in the comparison (§7), not resolved in capture.
7. Nothing the team knows is added to a row as if the stakeholder had said
   it.

## 6. Question set

Thirty questions, bounded to what frozen Draft v2's stages A1 and A3–A16
require, each mapped to the stage and instrument it serves. The stakeholder
may answer any subset, in any order.

### Words and people (A1; A3 evidence requested)

- **Q1.** In your own words, what is this organization, and what do you call
  it and its parts — units, areas, roles, offices — when you talk about them
  day to day?
- **Q2.** Who works in or for the organization today, in whatever capacity
  — including part-time people, contractors, advisors, services, or tools
  that you treat as doing work? Please list them with the role you would give
  each.
- **Q3.** When you talk about your work, what words do you use for the
  things you are keeping track of, waiting on, deciding, or need to be told
  about?

### Access and what is not written down (A3 steps 8 and 10)

- **Q4.** Which records, systems, places, or people hold information about
  the organization that we have not been given access to, or that you would
  not want examined? Is there anything we have access to that you would
  rather we did not rely on?
- **Q5.** What about how the organization works exists only in your head or
  in conversations — nowhere written down — that someone would need to know
  to understand it?

### Purpose and identity (A4)

- **Q6.** What does the organization exist to do?
- **Q7.** What has it actually spent its time on over the last three months?
- **Q8.** Where, if anywhere, do you think what it says it does and what it
  actually does differ?

### Outputs and value (A5)

- **Q9.** What has the organization produced or delivered so far, and who
  received each thing? Who outside the organization, if anyone, depends on
  something it produces?
- **Q10.** Which recurring activities do you consider to create value, and
  which exist mainly because of habit, tooling, or process?

### Authority and decisions (A6)

- **Q11.** Who can decide what? For the decisions that come up repeatedly,
  who actually makes them, who has to be consulted first, and where does a
  disagreement go?
- **Q12.** Are there decisions you make that you do not think of as formally
  yours, or that someone or something else formally holds? Are there
  decisions that no one clearly holds?
- **Q13.** If you were unavailable for a month, which decisions could still
  be made, and by whom?

### Functions and responsibilities (A7)

- **Q14.** What areas of work or functions exist in the organization today,
  and what does each actually do? Which exist on paper only, and which exist
  in practice without being written down?
- **Q15.** What work happens only because you personally do it out of habit?
  What work do you think nobody owns?

### Recurring decisions (A8)

- **Q16.** Which decisions recur? What triggers each, what do you need in
  front of you to make it, and when or how often do you actually sit down to
  make decisions?
- **Q17.** What is waiting on you right now, where is that list kept, and
  how long do things typically wait?

### Information and definitive sources (A9)

- **Q18.** For the information you rely on to run the organization, where is
  the definitive version of each kept — the place you would go if two records
  disagreed? Which of those can you personally retrieve right now, without
  asking anyone and without searching a chat or session history?
- **Q19.** Is anything treated as authoritative in practice that you do not
  think is officially so — or the reverse?

### How work actually flows (A10)

- **Q20.** Walk through the last time a piece of work went from start to
  finish: what happened first, what was produced, who or what touched it,
  what it waited on, and how you knew it was done.
- **Q21.** Where does work stall, get handed off, or wait on something? When
  something is handed off, how do you know who acts next?

### Formality (A11)

- **Q22.** Which matters do you believe need a durable, formal decision or
  rule, and which are handled informally? Where does the organization have
  more formality than its work needs, and where less?

### Memory and loss (A12)

- **Q23.** What would be lost if you — or any single person, service, or tool
  — disappeared tomorrow? What has already been lost, and how did you find
  out?
- **Q24.** What knowledge about the organization exists only with people or
  services outside it — advisors, developers, vendors, hosting providers, AI
  tools?

### Systems and routine acts (A13)

- **Q25.** What systems and tools does the organization use to run? Which
  hold the definitive version of something? Which impose ways of working you
  would rather not have? What do you deliberately do by hand?
- **Q26.** Are there routine actions — clicking, pushing, paying, filing,
  sending — whose consequences cannot be undone or are visible to others? Does
  the way you authorize those actions carry that fact?

### Friction (A14)

- **Q27.** Where do you find yourself reconstructing information you already
  had, losing context, rediscovering deadlines, or keeping the same open item
  in more than one place? In how many places?

### Technology (A15)

- **Q28.** Is there a recurring problem you think software should solve? Is
  there one that people have suggested software for that you do not think is
  ready? Is anything already built or set up that is waiting on a decision of
  yours rather than on a build?

### Reserved to you (A16)

- **Q29.** Which questions about the organization's direction are yours alone
  to decide and are currently undecided?

### Closing

- **Q30.** Is there anything you expected to be asked and were not? Anything
  you would like to correct or withdraw from what you have said?

**Anti-leading check applied.** No question names a structure the method
expects to find; no question offers options to choose from; each asks for
the stakeholder's account, words, or list. Questions 3, 18, 24 and 26 were
rewritten from earlier drafts that named concepts the method uses.

## 7. Comparison procedure (after responses are received)

1. Freeze the Client-Stated evidence set exactly as captured.
2. Compare each material subject against the **records-derived evidence
   set** (`records-derived-evidence-set.md`), which was built and committed
   *before* any response existed, so that it cannot have been adjusted to
   the account.
3. Classify each comparison as one of: **Corroboration · Contradiction ·
   Difference in terminology · Missing records · Unsupported stakeholder
   assertion · Records that no longer describe actual operation · Unresolved
   condition requiring further evidence** (`fd-0020` §2.12).
4. Record, per comparison: subject; records-derived evidence and its
   provenance; Client-Stated evidence; classification; reason; additional
   evidence required if unresolved; effect on diagnostic advancement.
5. **No default preference.** Neither records nor account is preferred; a
   contradiction stays a contradiction. The team does not reconcile
   disagreements by its own judgment where further evidence or the
   organization's authority is required.
6. Identify **informal authority** and **habit-held responsibility** from
   the account and the comparison, as findings.
7. Apply A18 with the access statement, the records-only rule (no longer
   applicable once one account exists), the author-of-records label, and the
   evidence-classed judgement; record whether advancement is refused or
   conditioned.

## 9. Interaction dependency

This procedure cannot be executed by the diagnostic team alone. It depends
on the Founder Office answering, in its own words, some or all of Q1–Q30
through the execution interaction. Until that occurs, Stage 3 stakeholder
validation is **not complete** and is not represented as complete
(`fd-0020` §2.11, §2.13 completion rule).
