### PC-5 (from MF-5) — **ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION**

**Proposed:** split Part B's header requirement — (a) the advancement-state
declaration and the sufficiency marker with its reason at the head of every

**Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.** The six-question analysis `fd-0031`

- **Modified — a requirement the proposal does not carry, added because
  `fd-0031` §2.5's governing principle demands it:** where the access statement

## 3. The Part B analysis `fd-0031` §2.5 requires

**1. What does Draft v5 literally require?** At the head of every instrument,
before its first row: the access statement (A3 step 10), the advancement-state
declaration in the method's own words, and the evidence-sufficiency marker with
its reason in prose. A3 step 10 independently states the access statement is
*"carried into the header of every instrument."*

**2. What did the Stage 10 applier actually do?** Carried, at each of ten
instrument heads: a named reference to the statement in force (A3-O2), **plus
the access determinations material to that instrument**, plus the
advancement-state declaration in full, plus the marker and its reason in full.

**3. Why was literal compliance judged operationally defective?** In a
zero-session application the access statement is invariant — a two-pass,
four-condition table covering eleven identified items and thirteen access
determinations, roughly fifty lines. Ten identical repetitions is roughly five
hundred lines whose only function is to separate each marker from the next. It
buries the marker, which is the only element that varies between instruments and
the element Part B's own rationale says the reader must meet at the evidence.

**4. Does the requirement duplicate information held canonically elsewhere?**
**Yes, conditionally.** The statement is held canonically at A3-O2. **It is
invariant only where it has been re-derived zero times.** Where sessions occur it
is re-derived after each, and instruments produced at different points may fall
under different statements — in which case the repetition is not duplication and
carrying it in full is correct. **The duplication is a property of the
zero-session case, not of the requirement.** This is why PC-5 is accepted with a
re-derivation clause rather than as a flat exemption.

**5. Can the disclosure objective be met non-duplicatively?** **Yes — by
reference plus exception-only reporting**, which is what the applier did: cite
the statement in force by name and date, and carry the determinations material to
*this* instrument. Inheritance alone (a bare pointer) would not do: it satisfies
brevity and loses attribution.

**6. Does any proposed treatment preserve visibility where limitations
materially affect an instrument?** **The proposal as written does not guarantee
it; as modified it does.** The applier's headers named the material
determinations per instrument — *"none for the hosted database… none for any
participant"* — which is more conspicuous at the point of use than a fifty-line
table a reader skips. PC-5 is accepted **only** with that requirement written
into it.

**Governing principle applied.** `fd-0031` §2.5: optimize for conspicuous and
attributable evidence limitations, **not merely fewer lines**. The modification
exists because the proposal, read literally, optimized for the second.

## 4. Reserved questions returned to the Founder Office

**Two. Neither is drafted, and neither is answered here.**

**RQ-1 — the A15 preceding-question evidentiary standard.** *May Client-Stated
evidence that a capability exists, is built and is dormant fire the preceding
question and thereby terminate the Technology-Worthiness Test — and if the
standard is higher, what is it?* **Why Development cannot decide it:** it sets a
threshold governing a terminating step and would change methodology semantics
beyond any existing determination; `fd-0026` §2.4 settled the analogous question
for A18 element 4 and is silent here. **Blocking?** **No** — PC-7's recording
limb is severable and can be drafted without it.

**RQ-2 — whether the method should ever represent observer-independence
structurally.** *A2 rule 6 reaches for observer-independence ("account and record
share a source") and A2's model has no place to record it. Should the method be
able to represent it structurally, given that every structural route
Development can see runs into `fd-0031` §2.4's prohibitions on ordering,
ranking, strength and implicit hierarchy through terminology?* **Why Development
cannot decide it:** answering "yes" would require altering A2's non-ordering
architecture, which `fd-0031` §2.4 forecloses to Development absolutely. **Why
this is not the §2.4 stop rule firing:** the recurrence deficiency **has** been
dispositioned at the narrowest supported level — disclosure in A20 — without
touching the architecture. RQ-2 asks whether more should ever be done, not
whether the accepted treatment is possible. **Blocking?** **No.**
