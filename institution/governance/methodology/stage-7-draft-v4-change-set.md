# Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One: Minimum Coherent Draft v4 Change Set, and What Must Not Enter It

**Classification: Institutional Record** — proposal record (evidentiary,
non-governing). **PROPOSALS ONLY. NO DRAFT V4 EXISTS AND NONE IS CREATED
HERE.** Draft v3 is unchanged at
`04fd02c7ec80b457877f2de0363ce90e961bc370e019e945634d4720d379f89f`, one commit
on its path, verified before and after Stage 7. No edit was made to any frozen
draft in anticipation of a future one.

Drawn **only** from the ACCEPT and ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION dispositions at
[`stage-7-deficiency-dispositions.md`](stage-7-deficiency-dispositions.md), plus
coherence changes those force, plus one item the instrument itself commissioned.
Deferred, declined and speculative items are excluded and listed at §3.

## 1. The change set — seventeen items

Each carries: identifier · source deficiencies · Draft v3 location · the
required conceptual change · reason · interaction · wording-only or structural ·
whether a determination is required before drafting.

---

### CS-01 · An evidence-source kind, without a reliability ranking

- **Source:** D-1, MO-01. **Location:** A2; Instrument 1.
- **Change:** Client-Stated rows carry a **source-kind** attribute describing
  *what the source is* — a person, an organizational record, a supplied
  institutional source, or a subject's own systematic verification record. A2
  acquires **no ordering** and the attribute is never a strength.
- **Reason:** the method presently classifies a record of a hundred enumerated
  production probes identically to a stale sentence in a README, and both
  authors independently found the loss.
- **Interaction:** must not collide with CS-02's precedence rule; a subject's
  verification record is a *kind of source*, not an adopted evidence class.
- **Structural** — it changes the evidence model.
- **Determination required: YES** — the value set is methodology semantics, and
  the line between "kind" and "quality" is exactly where a ranking could enter.

### CS-02 · Adopting a subject's governed vocabulary, and A1/A2 precedence

- **Source:** D-4, MO-12. **Location:** A1; A2.
- **Change:** an A1 step for a subject that maintains an authoritative
  vocabulary — adopt it, record its governing status and enforcement mechanism
  as evidence, elicit only for concepts it does not cover. Plus an explicit
  **precedence rule** where a subject's apparatus reaches evidence
  classification: **classify by A2, record the subject's scheme as terminology,
  never adopt it.**
- **Reason:** the subject had performed A1 on itself more thoroughly than the
  diagnostic could, and its scheme reached evidence classification — where A1
  and A2 pull in opposite directions and the method does not acknowledge that
  they can.
- **Interaction:** CS-01 (kinds are not classes); A1's existing rule that a
  divergence between stated term and observed usage is a finding is retained as
  the guard against importing a subject's blind spots.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required: YES** — precedence between A1 and A2.

### CS-03 · One A18 disclosure block, and a definition of "the diagnostic team"

- **Source:** D-8, MO-03. **Location:** A18.
- **Change:** a single disclosure block that (a) **defines what "the diagnostic
  team" denotes** — the institution or the individual — and (b) adds
  **prior involvement**: where the team has previously designed, implemented,
  advised, or evaluated the subject, the outcome states it, states in what
  capacity, and states that the diagnosis is not independent. It sits beside
  *author-of-records* and *account and record share a source*, and is stated
  before any finding.
- **Reason:** the Stage 6 instrument had to demand externally what the method
  does not provide; and a second author reading A18 alone had to decide for
  itself what "the team" means, recording that a different author *"could
  defensibly have decided the opposite and dropped the label."*
- **Interaction:** CS-16's terminology pass settles one of the drifting terms
  here; CS-10 makes the team's own errors visible, which is the same concern
  from the evidence side.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required: YES** — how far "prior involvement" reaches, and
  whether the institutional reading is the default. The Founder Office has
  already determined D-8's *status*; adopting a disclosure into the method is a
  separate act.

### CS-04 · An evidence-boundary value, and naming the absent categories

- **Source:** D-2, MO-04. **Location:** A3 step 10; A17 rule 2; A18.
- **Change:** two linked parts. (a) A distinct value for **evidence the
  diagnostic was instructed not to obtain**, separate from *not supplied*, *not
  accessible*, and *not known to exist* — an imposed scope boundary is not an
  access failure. (b) A18's outcome **names which categories of evidence were
  absent** — accounts, observation of work as performed, the organization's
  operating systems — each with the constraint it triggers, instead of a second
  label beside *records-only*.
- **Reason:** two authors independently built the same missing vocabulary in
  prose; and both diagnoses were labelled for the absence of an account while
  the operating system the subject calls its permanent record went unlabelled.
- **Interaction:** CS-13's A20 additions; A18's existing access constraint is
  unchanged and still bars proceeding.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-05 · The A3 cluster — three small, certain repairs

- **Source:** MO-05, MO-06, MO-21. **Location:** A3 steps 1, 9, 10.
- **Change:** (a) step 9's citation check reads **"held in the authorized
  corpus"**, not "held by the organization"; (b) step 10 states that with **zero
  stakeholder sessions** the team's own inventory is the statement in force;
  (c) a step requiring the diagnostic to **verify that the state it inspected is
  the state it was told to inspect**, at open and at close.
- **Reason:** as written, step 9 has an application assert something about the
  organization it did not establish; step 10 leaves a records-only diagnosis
  with no statement in force; and both authors performed the baseline check
  because omitting it would invalidate everything, neither having been told to.
- **Interaction:** (b) depends on CS-04's absent-category naming being present
  in A18, or a records-only outcome will describe an access position no rule
  produced.
- **Wording-only** for (a) and (b); **structural** for (c), which adds a step.
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-06 · Source roles are participant-dependent, and A20 must say so

- **Source:** D-3, MO-22. **Location:** A9; Instrument 4; A20.
- **Change:** (a) A9 states that the first three source roles — where a matter
  is first worked through, clarified, and routed from — are **ordinarily
  obtainable only from participants**, and a records-only application records
  them **"not obtainable from records"** rather than *none evidenced*, because
  those mean different things. (b) A20 records that the Stage 4 widening of
  *source of resort* into *source roles* made Instrument 4 substantially more
  account-dependent.
- **Reason:** the clearest independent reproduction in the corpus — two authors,
  same field, same emptiness, same cause.
- **Interaction:** shares the A20 limb with CS-13.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-07 · A18's internal-consistency cluster

- **Source:** MO-02, MO-08, MO-15; and ambiguity AMB-2. **Location:** A18.
- **Change:** three repairs to a section that contradicts itself. (a) Define
  **identity-of-record statement** and name its producing stage and instrument —
  **or remove it** from the minimum outputs. (b) Disentangle *return unresolved
  questions to client authority* from Instrument 10: one is an outcome, the
  other is produced by every diagnosis, and they cannot be the same thing.
  (c) Give **explicit exclusions** a producing stage for each of its three
  limbs. Plus (d): settle whether element 4 asks for boundaries **identified**
  or **established**, since A9 distinguishes them and A18 uses the weaker word.
- **Reason:** three required outputs the method never sources, and one outcome
  that is also an output — a pattern in A18 rather than three accidents. MO-08's
  predicted author divergence **occurred** between the two applications.
- **Interaction:** CS-12 must not add a sixth outcome; CS-04 changes what the
  outcome states.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required: YES** — what the identity-of-record statement is.
  Development does not invent it.

### CS-08 · A15's dead end, stated rather than removed

- **Source:** MO-07. **Location:** A15; Instrument 9.
- **Change:** state that where no organizational authority is available the
  preceding question **terminates** the test for that problem; that answering
  the seven questions afterwards is permitted **for the record and cannot change
  the outcome**; and that this is a property of records-only diagnosis, not a
  finding about the problem.
- **Reason:** the conservatism is right — the second author recorded that
  without the preceding question *"I would have written a recommendation"* — and
  the method does not say that the test cannot be reached.
- **Interaction:** none. **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-09 · Instrument 1's two field repairs

- **Source:** MO-09, MO-10. **Location:** Instrument 1; A2 rule 5.
- **Change:** (a) *stated force* extends to **document qualifiers**, quoted
  verbatim under the same rule that the team assigns no category of its own.
  (b) The *opposing account* field remains **per-row by default**; a
  register-wide statement is permitted **only** where the value is genuinely
  uniform, must state the value and its reason once, and every exception is
  recorded inline.
- **Reason:** in a records-only diagnosis the document qualifier is the only
  form the field can take, and the second author reports it worked better there
  than in its intended use; and sixty identical fields bury the rows that differ.
- **Interaction:** (b) bears on the one-sided-evidence determination at §2.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-10 · Prior knowledge that conflicts with what the method returns

- **Source:** MO-11. **Location:** A17 rule 6.
- **Change:** where a prior-knowledge item **conflicts** with what a method step
  returns, both are recorded and so is the divergence. The prior item still
  supports no finding.
- **Reason:** *"a team whose prior knowledge is wrong and who silently drops it
  leaves no trace of having been wrong, while a team whose prior knowledge is
  right looks like it derived the answer. The rule polices admissibility and not
  calibration."* For a method whose independence defects are its largest
  weakness, this is worth more than most of the corpus.
- **Interaction:** CS-03 — the two together are the method's honest account of
  its own author.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No, but the change set records a **scope
  question**: a team holding hundreds of prior beliefs cannot check them all,
  and the rule needs a bound.

### CS-11 · A permitted value for a presupposition that does not hold

- **Source:** MO-13. **Location:** A1.
- **Change:** a value distinct from *no equivalent found* — **the question
  presupposes a structure this organization does not have** — for conditional
  terms that are malformed rather than negative for a given subject.
- **Reason:** *"'No coordinating level evidenced' is technically correct and
  misleading; the question presupposes a cross-unit structure whose absence is
  the actual fact."* Rewording the terms would overfit small organizations,
  which is what A1 exists to prevent.
- **Interaction:** the same device as CS-04, applied to terminology.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-12 · Closure semantics

- **Source:** MO-16; ambiguity AMB-5. **Location:** A17 rule 7; A18.
- **Change:** a diagnosis may be represented as closed with a mandatory closure
  condition unsatisfied **only** if it carries an **unresolved-closure
  statement** naming the condition, why it could not be satisfied, and who must
  satisfy it — carried beside the outcome, **never as a sixth outcome**. And the
  four states are distinguished in the method's own words: *application stage
  complete* · *subject diagnosis complete* · *advancement permitted* ·
  *closure condition unresolved*.
- **Reason:** a mandatory condition with no failure mode is not a condition.
  Both applications closed with A17 rule 7 unsatisfied; only one noticed.
- **Interaction:** CS-07 must not let this become an outcome. This is
  **diagnostic closure semantics only** — no universal project-management state
  is designed.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required: YES** — whether a diagnosis may close at all with
  A17 rule 7 unsatisfied. Development proposes the narrowest rule and does not
  decide it.

### CS-13 · A20's limitations, made non-selective

- **Source:** MO-18; the A20 limb of MO-22. **Location:** A20; A6.
- **Change:** A20 records that **A6's role-compression test is as
  observation-dependent as A7's habit test**, and that the Stage 4 source-roles
  widening increased Instrument 4's account-dependence.
- **Reason:** *"An application working from A20's limitation list would think A6
  was safer than A7, and it is not."* A limitations list that is selectively
  complete is misleading exactly where a careful reader trusts it — and this
  silence is part of why two authors read the same test differently.
- **Interaction:** shares its second limb with CS-06.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required:** No.

### CS-14 · The method's own cited-and-not-held dependency

- **Source:** MO-14. **Location:** A17 rule 5.
- **Change:** require the application to record **whether the cited handling

  applied.
- **Reason:** the method's confidentiality rule defers to a document that may
  not travel with it — *"cited and not held, the same defect I spent this

  governed material and risk drift.
- **Interaction:** none.
- **Wording-only.**
- **Determination required: YES** — whether Methodology Foundation v1 §7.5

### CS-15 · Zero-place rows

- **Reason:** the places count *"worked extremely well for records"* and has no

### CS-16 · A terminology pass over the method's own words

- **Source:** MO-19. **Location:** A3, A15, A16, A18 — wording throughout.
- **Change:** settle which of *client authority* · *the organization's

- **Reason:** A1 insists the subject's vocabulary be recorded precisely and the

- **Interaction:** CS-03 settles *the diagnostic team*; the pass must not

### CS-17 · A non-numeric evidentiary-sufficiency presentation

- **Source:** not a numbered deficiency — commissioned directly by `fd-0025`
  §2.10, on the accepted finding that a diagnostic artifact can be highly
  populated while evidence remains insufficient. **Location:** A18; Part B
  global instrument rules.
- **Change:** three parts, all non-numeric. (a) An **advancement-state
  declaration separate from instrument completeness**, stated in the method's
  existing words. (b) A per-instrument **evidence-sufficiency marker** limited to
  three values already in the method's vocabulary — *completed* · *completed
  with named gaps* · *blocked by insufficient evidence* — with the reason, never
  aggregated across instruments. (c) A **placement rule**: the declaration and
  the marker appear **at the head of each instrument**, not only in a report
  header.
- **Reason:** the second author's structural observation, which no prior stage
  produced: instruments filled from a corpus *"look complete… the labels that
  mark this are three lines in a header above several thousand words of filled
  table,"* and *"the method's weight of machinery is distributed almost
  inversely to where its evidential risk sits."*
- **Interaction:** CS-04 names the absent categories the declaration reports;
  CS-12's closure statement sits beside it.
- **Structural.**
- **Determination required:** No — but the **prohibition is absolute**: no
  percentage, no score, no maturity level, no traffic light, no weighted index,
  no aggregation. Three words per instrument and a reason.

## 2. The one-sided-evidence determination (`fd-0025` §2.10)

**Required answer, from the four permitted:**
**→ Descriptive treatment sufficient without methodology semantic change.**

- **What the evidence shows.** The first author met the condition and built a
  descriptive qualifier. The second author, **never told the qualifier existed**,
  met the same condition and **did not invent it** — it recorded per-row
  opposing-account values as A2 rule 5 requires and then criticized the
  *mechanism* for degenerating when the value is uniform (MO-09).
- **What follows.** The condition is real and a second author meets it
  unprompted. But a second author given a free hand did **not** reach for a new
  classification, which is the evidence that would justify making one-sided
  evidence a required methodological distinction. The seven comparison
  classifications remain fixed by determination; A2 rule 5 already forbids
  reading unopposed evidence as corroborated; and CS-09 fixes the mechanism the
  second author actually found wanting.
- **Architecture.** The descriptive layer coexists with comparison
  classification, as `fd-0025` §2.10 prefers. It adds no class and redefines
  none.
- **What it must never do**, and does not: infer credibility, speaker intent,
  knowledge state, truth, or reliability from silence. It records **which half
  is silent and what was done about the other** — acts and outcomes, not motives.
- **Status:** the qualifier stays **available reporting composition, not
  mandatory doctrine**, exactly where `fd-0024` §2.7 left it. Revisit if a
  future application reaches for a distinction the descriptive layer cannot
  carry.

## 4. Future replication-protocol requirements — separate from Draft v4

`fd-0025` §2.12: replication mechanics belong to methodology **validation
procedure**, not inside a client-facing diagnostic. Recorded here so they
survive, and kept out of §1.

1. **Freeze the author-independence disclosure before evidence inspection** —
   carried by commit history, not by assertion. Stage 6 did this in two turns
   and it worked.
2. **Identical permitted evidence and identical unavailable evidence** for every
   author. **Stage 6 failed this**, and the failure produced its largest
   divergence.
3. **The mechanism that prevents leakage must not change subject access.** The
   Stage 6 package withheld a directory to protect isolation and withheld the
   venture charter with it. Supplying the subject-governance records — which
   contain no prior-application material — while withholding the methodology
   directory was available and unseen.
4. **No first-author result exposure before the second result is sealed.**
5. **Explicit memory and context-leakage disclosure**, measured by quotation
   rather than estimated.
6. **Subject-positioning neutrality** — the harness must not place an author
   inside its subject.
7. **Comparison only after the seal**, and its author's own dependency
   disclosed.
8. **Supply enough of the program's context that the method's own mechanisms are
   visible** — MO-20 was declined because the package hid one.
9. **A different model family**, where available. Same-family agreement is
   correlated-error risk, not model-independent reproducibility.

## 5. Coherence

The seventeen items are implementable as **one internally coherent revision**,
not a collection of edits. They cluster:

- **The evidence model** — CS-01, CS-02, CS-04, CS-10, CS-11 — all describing
  *what a source is and what was done to obtain it*, none ranking reliability.
- **A18's self-consistency and honest self-report** — CS-03, CS-07, CS-12,
  CS-17 — the section that contradicts itself three times, discloses two of its
  three independence defects, and reports sufficiency in three lines above
  several thousand words of table.
- **A3's baseline discipline** — CS-05.
- **Small instrument repairs** — CS-06, CS-08, CS-09, CS-13, CS-14, CS-15,
  CS-16.

**Four require a determination before drafting**: CS-01 (the value set), CS-02
(A1/A2 precedence), CS-03 (what "the diagnostic team" denotes and how far prior
involvement reaches), CS-07 (what the identity-of-record statement is), and
CS-12 (whether a diagnosis may close with A17 rule 7 unsatisfied) — five
questions across four items. **Drafting cannot coherently begin until they are
settled**, because three of them change the evidence model and A18 simultaneously.
