# Organizational Diagnostic — Draft v4: Prepared Validation Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — proposal record (evidentiary,
non-governing). Prepared under `fd-0028` §2.10.

**Status: PREPARED — NOT AUTHORIZED, NOT BEGUN.** This package **authorizes
nothing**. It defines what a validation would have to look like to be worth
running; it does not commission one, does not select a subject as a decision,
does not name a validator, and does not begin any application. **No diagnostic
evidence was collected in preparing it, and no subject was inspected as a
subject.**

**Two prerequisites are unmet and both are the Founder Office's.**

1. **The A10 reconciliation is prepared and not applied**, pending a
   repository-versioning determination (`a10-determination-reconciliation-record.md`).
   Validating A10 Reading A against a draft that still carries the ambiguous
   wording would validate the wrong text.
2. **The validation itself is not authorized.** `fd-0028` §2.10 authorizes the
   package and forbids its execution.

## 1. The smallest meaningful objective

**One bounded internal application of Draft v4, read-only, by an authoring
context that did not draft Draft v4, against one internal subject, to establish
whether the consolidation behaves as intended.**

Not: whether the method is good. Not: whether the subject is well run. Not:
external validity, which one internal application cannot touch. The question is
narrower and answerable — **do the changed elements do what they were written to
do when someone else applies them to real evidence?**

**Why one application and not a programme.** Sixteen of the seventeen Stage 7
items require fresh validation and eight have never been exercised at all. A
single application against a subject with the right properties reaches thirteen
of the seventeen (§4). A second application adds little until the first has
shown which of the eight new treatments survive contact — and Stage 7 already
recorded that another application under *unchanged* conditions "would have low
marginal value."

## 2. Subject selection criteria, and the honest constraint

### 2.1 The constraint, stated first

**The institution contains exactly two candidate organizational subjects**, and
both have already been diagnosed: **Huerta Group LLC** (Stage 3, under Draft v2,
with the Founder Office as participant) and **Huerta Group Publishing** (Stage 5
and Stage 6, under Draft v3, both records-only). The venture registry records
one venture. There is **no unexamined internal subject**, and no internal
validation can supply one.

**This is a limitation of any internal validation, not a defect in the
package**, and it is stated here rather than discovered later. It also means the
validator must work with prior findings withheld, and that its independence is
**bounded non-authoring independence, never freedom from the subject's history**
(`fd-0028` §2.10).

A second constraint, equally structural: **every internal subject has one
available participant, the Founder Office.** Participant evidence is therefore
obtainable but single-source, and A2 rule 6 (unopposed is not corroborated) will
apply to nearly all of it. That is a property to be recorded by the validation,
not a reason to skip it — no application in this program has ever had participant
evidence for A10 at all.

### 2.2 What the subject must be able to exercise

| Required property | Why the changed elements need it |
| --- | --- |
| Its **own governed terminology** | CS-02's adoption step and A1/A2 precedence rule cannot be exercised by a subject with no vocabulary of its own |
| **Participant evidence**, obtainable | A10 Reading A's walkthrough category; CS-06's source roles; CS-08's preceding question; CS-13's observation-dependence |
| **Multiple source kinds**, genuinely different in kind | CS-01 needs systematic verification records *and* narrative records in the same corpus, or the attribute has nothing to distinguish |
| **Confidential or absolutely bounded material** | CS-14 has never met the condition it exists for |
| **Real authority and source-of-truth distinctions**, including unresolved ones | CS-07's element 4 (*sufficiently identified*, including contested authority) |
| **At least one workflow with actual-instance trail evidence** | A10 Reading A's four categories, and Instrument 5's three sufficiency states |
| **Genuine evidentiary gaps or access limitations** | CS-04's absent-category naming; CS-17's marker, which is uninformative if nothing is ever missing |
| **A real advancement/refusal question** | A18's outcome logic, the disclosure block, and CS-12's closure states |

### 2.3 Assessment of the two candidates

**Recommended: Huerta Group Publishing**, at a current baseline.

| Property | HGP | Huerta Group LLC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governed terminology | **Yes** — a ratified terminology canon, "one concept, one word… deviating copy is a bug," derived into code and **pinned by test** (Stage 5, H-06). The first and only subject whose vocabulary is enforced mechanically | Institutional vocabulary exists but is not a ratified, enforced canon |
| Subject scheme reaching **evidence classification** | **Yes** — an observation register with its own classification and evidence-strength vocabulary. This is the exact condition CS-02's second limb was written for | No comparable scheme |
| Multiple source kinds | **Yes** — 13 dated verification-log entries, one recording 35 SQL probes; production probes; drift-guard tests; alongside ordinary narrative records. **D-1 and MO-01 were both found here** | Predominantly narrative and governance records |
| Confidential / bounded material | **Yes, absolutely bounded** — FD-V1 §6.5: author manuscripts, memory and editorial records "never leave venture infrastructure absent explicit authorization" and "never qualif[y] as source material" for the parent. A real boundary the diagnostic must respect **and record without reproducing** | No comparable protected category |
| Actual-instance trail | **Yes** — `FVO-001-001`: a nine-step correction trail from production observation through a failed fix to closure, with timestamps and artifacts | Weaker; the parent's work is largely the corpus itself |
| Unresolved source-of-truth authority | **Yes** — Stage 6 recorded the permanent record's owner unresolved, applied-migrations disputed between two candidates, and capability status with **no authoritative source and no establishing act** | Fewer live contests |
| Prior-involvement severity (CS-03) | **Maximal, and therefore the right test** — Development designed, built and governs HGP's platform. Both disclosure layers are non-trivial and the institutional default bites | Also maximal, and less separable: the parent *is* the diagnosing institution |
| Prior diagnoses to withhold | **Two** (Stage 5, Stage 6) — the principal defect of this choice | One (Stage 3) |

**The trade-off, stated plainly.** Huerta Group LLC would be a *fresher* subject
for a third author but a *weaker* exerciser: it cannot test CS-14 at all, cannot
test CS-02's evidence-classification limb, and offers a thinner mix of source
kinds. HGP tests more of what changed, at the cost of being the subject two
prior applications already answered. **The package recommends HGP because the
changed elements are what is being validated**, and recommends that the
withholding controls in §5 carry the weight the freshness would otherwise carry.

**A subject may not be chosen for convenience.** If the Founder Office judges
that two prior diagnoses make HGP unusable, the honest consequence is that
**CS-14 and CS-02's second limb cannot be validated internally at all**, and
should be recorded as unvalidated rather than tested against a subject that
cannot exercise them.

## 3. Validator non-authoring and prior-involvement controls

**The requirement:** an authoring context that **did not draft Draft v4**.

**What that is, and what it is not.** This is **bounded non-authoring
validation**. It is not independence: the validator will share an institution, a
model family, and in all likelihood a subject history with the drafter.
`fd-0028` §2.10 forbids claiming evaluator independence "unless the actual
conditions support it," and the conditions do not support it. **The package
requires the limitation stated in the validator's own first output, not in a
footnote of the report about it.**

**Disclosure, frozen before any substantive subject evidence is inspected** —
carried by commit history, not by assertion, as Stage 6 established and
`stage-7-draft-v4-change-set.md` §4.1 records:

1. **Institutional prior involvement** — what the diagnosing institution
   designed, constructed, governed, implemented or materially evaluated in the
   subject.
2. **Individual/agent prior involvement** — what this authoring context
   specifically did, where it differs from the institutional layer.
3. **Prior familiarity with the chosen subject** — what it already believes it
   knows, enumerated before it reads anything, so A17 rule 6 has something to
   check against and CS-10's conflict-recording limb can actually fire.
4. **What methodology-development records are available to it** — and this must
   include enough of the program's context that the method's own mechanisms are
   visible. Stage 6 withheld too much and MO-20 was the artifact
   (`stage-7-draft-v4-change-set.md` §4.8).
5. **What prior diagnostic findings are withheld** — named as withheld, so the
   validator knows a withheld body exists and does not mistake its absence for
   nonexistence.

**Two-turn protocol.** Turn 1 produces the disclosure and nothing else, and is
committed. Turn 2 releases the evidence. Stage 6 did this and it worked.

**Leakage is measured, not estimated** (`stage-7-draft-v4-change-set.md` §4.5) —
by verbatim quotation, with any leaked identifier's downstream convergence
excluded from the evidence.

## 4. Changed Draft v4 elements the validation must exercise

Thirteen of seventeen are reachable from one application against a subject with
§2.2's properties. Each entry states **what would count as the element working**
— not as a score, but as an observable.

| Element | What must be exercised | What would count as working |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A10 Reading A** | The four categories treated as categories; the trail built from what exists | The validator performs A10 **without reverting to the conjunctive reading** and without treating "as available" as permission to stop early |
| **A10 recording fields** | existence · accessibility · obtention · reason-for-non-obtention, per materially relevant category | All four appear per category; a category that appears to exist but was not obtained carries a stated reason |
| **Instrument 5 sufficiency states** | *completed* / *completed with named gaps* / *blocked by insufficient evidence* | The three values discriminate across workflows rather than collapsing to one; an artifact-only trail is not marked *completed* by reflex |
| **A10 performed ≠ A18 satisfied** | Both judged, separately | The state *A10 performed + gaps + A18 not satisfied* is reachable and is actually recorded if it occurs |
| **CS-01 source kind** | Verification records and narrative records in one register | The attribute never carries weight in any finding; no ordering appears in prose |
| **CS-02 A1/A2 precedence** | The subject's terminology canon adopted; its observation-register scheme recorded as terminology | A2's classes are used for the diagnostic's own evidence while the subject's scheme is preserved untranslated, and neither displaces the other |
| **CS-03 prior-involvement disclosure** | Both layers, before any finding | The material/incidental line is applied by someone who did not write it, and the disclosure is neither omitted nor inflated |
| **CS-04 absent categories** | Named, each with the constraint it triggers | Categories genuinely absent are named; the imposed-boundary value is used only where a boundary was actually imposed |
| **CS-07 identity-of-record** | Produced by A4 from the subject's own authoritative evidence (FD-V1 is available to be that evidence) | An output exists — including, if that is the truth, one that records absence or conflict |
| **CS-12 closure states** | A17 rule 7's preservation agreement actually attempted with a participant present | Either the condition is satisfied, or the unresolved-closure statement is written and the diagnosis is not called complete |
| **CS-14 confidentiality** | FD-V1 §6.5 material encountered at its boundary | A claim is preserved without reproducing protected substance; whether the governing doctrine was held is recorded; the conservative default is named if it was applied |
| **One-sided evidence** | Participant evidence unopposed by records, or the reverse | Neither is read as corroborated; the per-row opposing-account field is used, and any register-wide statement meets CS-09's conditions |
| **CS-17 sufficiency presentation** | The marker and the advancement-state declaration at the head of every instrument | A populated instrument is distinguishable from a sufficient one **on sight**, without reading the whole table |

**Reachable only partially, and to be recorded as such rather than claimed:**
CS-05's baseline verification (exercised trivially by any competent application);
CS-06's source roles (needs the participant to actually answer them);
CS-08 (needs a technology-worthiness question to arise);
CS-15's zero-place rows (needs a matter recorded nowhere).

**Not reachable from one application, and to be recorded as not exercised:**
**CS-10** — needs a team whose prior knowledge is *wrong*, which cannot be
arranged, only noticed; **CS-11** — needs a subject that lacks a structure one of
A1's conditional terms presupposes; **CS-13** and **CS-16** — a disclosure and a
terminology pass, best read against the application's own record afterward.

**These are recorded as not exercised. They are not recorded as fine.**

## 5. Evidence-access and freeze controls

1. **Draft v4 freeze gate before any subject evidence is read.** The draft's
   digest is verified against its integrity record, and the verification is
   committed before the application begins. If the A10 reconciliation has been
   applied by then, it is that digest; if not, **the validation must not
   proceed**, because A10 Reading A is one of its principal targets.
2. **Subject baseline pinned and verified at open and at close** — Draft v4 A3
   step 1's own requirement, applied to its own validation.
3. **Identical permitted and identical unavailable evidence** for the validator
   as for any comparison author. Stage 6 failed this and it produced the
   largest divergence (`stage-7-draft-v4-change-set.md` §4.2).
4. **The withholding mechanism must not change subject access.** Stage 6
   withheld a directory to protect isolation and withheld the venture charter
   with it. Subject-governance records contain no prior-application material and
   are supplied (§4.3).
5. **Prior findings withheld and named as withheld** — Stage 5's report, Stage
   6's result, and the comparison. The validator is told a withheld body exists.
6. **Read-only.** No change to the subject of any kind, and no access beyond
   what the diagnostic requires. `fd-0028` §2.10: validation "must remain
   diagnostic/read-only unless separately authorized."
7. **Confidential material is bounded before it is met**, not after: the
   validator is told FD-V1 §6.5 governs, and whether it holds the doctrine —
   which is itself the CS-14 test.
8. **Nothing in this package pre-answers a diagnostic finding.** No expected
   conclusion about the subject appears anywhere in it, and the tables above
   describe **method behaviours**, never subject facts.

## 6. Proposed success criteria

**Non-numeric, per `fd-0028` §2.10.** No score, percentage, index, maturity
level or pass rate. Each criterion is answered in prose with its evidence, in
the method's own vocabulary.

The validation should determine:

1. Whether **A10 Reading A can be applied** without reverting to the conjunctive
   interpretation.
2. Whether materially relevant trail categories are **actually considered**
   rather than selectively ignored.
3. Whether **gaps remain visible** in the output, not just in the register.
4. Whether **procedure completion and evidence sufficiency stayed distinct**.
5. Whether **A18 retained independent advancement authority**.
6. Whether **source kind remained descriptive and non-hierarchical in use** —
   the test is whether it ever bore weight, not whether the rule was quoted.
7. Whether **subject terminology and A2 classification coexisted** without
   semantic substitution in either direction.
8. Whether **prior-involvement disclosure worked at both layers**, including
   whether the material/incidental line was applicable by someone else.
9. Whether **identity-of-record could be produced truthfully** where identity
   authority is clear, absent, conflicting, or unresolved.
10. Whether **unresolved closure was represented conspicuously** without
    becoming a sixth outcome.
11. Whether **confidentiality handling functioned** where the parent doctrine
    may not have been directly held.
12. Whether **incomplete evidence was distinguishable from a complete
    diagnosis**, visually and methodologically.
13. Whether the changed elements **introduced new ambiguity or application
    friction** — the Stage 6 lesson: a fresh reader finds what the author cannot.

**And one criterion about the validation itself:** whether any element could not
be exercised, and whether that was recorded as unexercised rather than passed
over.

## 7. What this validation would not establish

- **Not external validity.** No organization outside Huerta Group is involved,
  and none is authorized.
- **Not independence.** Bounded non-authoring validation is not independent
  validation, and the report must not describe it as such.
- **Not "validated through use."** That designation was withheld at `fd-0022`
  §2.1 and one internal application does not supply it.
- **Not established-method status, Method Library admission, standardization, or
  any commercial posture.** All remain unauthorized.
- **Not generalization from one subject.** A subject with a ratified vocabulary
  and a verification culture is unusual; elements that work there may fail on a
  subject with neither.
- **Not validation of the four unreachable elements** (§4).
- **Not, by itself, a tested baseline.** Draft v4 would remain a provisional
  draft with **one** application behind it — the position Draft v1 occupied
  after its dry run.

## 8. What this package does not do

It does not authorize the application; does not pre-answer any diagnostic
finding; does not seed any expected conclusion about any subject; does not
change Draft v4; does not create Draft v5; does not establish external validity;
and creates no methodology. Every requirement above is drawn from `fd-0028`, the
Stage 7 replication-protocol requirements, or the Stage 9 traceability record's
statement of what requires validation.
