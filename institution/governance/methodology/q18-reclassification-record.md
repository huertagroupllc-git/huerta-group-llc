## 2. The eight required analysis fields

- **Original Stage 3 evidence.** `S-29` (Client-Stated): "Founder Office
  conversation," given in answer to "[f]or the information you rely on to run
  the organization, where is the definitive version of each kept — the place
  you would go if two records disagreed?"
- **Original classification.** **Contradiction** (`C-46`), with material
  remaining uncertainty recorded and no resolution attempted.
- **Clarified Client-Stated meaning.** `S2-04`, `S2-05`, `S2-06`: not the final
  authoritative institutional record; a **sensemaking, clarification and
  routing** environment in current practice; the governed function or record
  remains the destination where durable authority is required.
- **Records-derived evidence.** Two bodies, both independent of the account:
  - *The sealed set* — R-18 names a definitive source **per domain** (the
    repository for governing records; custody for instruments and
    constitutional text; QuickBooks; the register for what it owns; the
    venture's systems; Supabase; Vercel). R-05 records the adopted principle
    that conversation and transient environments are never authoritative by
    existence.
  - *Routing evidence, retrieved post-response and declared as such*
    (§4, `X-13`…`X-16`) — the corpus does record a routing model: matters are
    determined or accepted at one place and proceed through a named function.
- **Record authority / status.** The per-domain source inventory is current;
  the memory principle is a current adopted principle; the routing statements
  are current governing text (`AGENTS.md`, Vision Foundation Guardrail 15,
  Methodology Foundation §6.1) and current evidentiary practice (the
  determinations register).
- **Revised classification.** **Difference in terminology** for the answer as
  given (`C-46-R1`), plus **Corroboration** for the clarified routing model
  (`C-46-R2`). Split under the standing rule that one answer may carry several
  claims with different classifications.
- **Why it changed.** The contradiction rested on the account asserting that
  the conversation *is* the authoritative source. The clarification withdraws
  that assertion. With it withdrawn, nothing in the account conflicts with the
  records: both now place durable authority in the governed record. What
  remains is that the stakeholder used the question's words — "the definitive
  version" — for an environment that is not one, which is a difference in
  terminology, not a disagreement about the organization.
- **Historical ambiguity.** Preserved in full (§1).
- **Effect on advancement.** The source-of-truth element of the A18
  minimum-evidence test improves but does **not** pass; advancement remains
  refused. See §5.

## 3. The revised comparisons

**C-46-R1 · Q18 · "the definitive version" as used by the account — Difference in terminology**

- **Stakeholder statement:** `S-29` "Founder Office conversation," clarified at
  `S2-04`–`S2-06`.
- **Stakeholder terminology:** "Founder Office conversation"; "room" in the
  institutional sense (`S2-T04`).
- **Records-derived evidence:** the records name a definitive source per
  information domain and hold no domain whose definitive source is a
  conversation; the adopted principle expressly excludes conversation from
  authority by existence.
- **Record source:** R-18; R-05; `fd-0019` §2.9; `CLAUDE.md`; `AGENTS.md`.
- **Record authority / status:** current governing rules and a current
  operational inventory.
- **Classification:** **Difference in terminology.**
- **Reason:** account and records describe substantially related organizational
  reality — where a matter is worked out, and where its truth is established —
  using overlapping words for different things. The account applied the
  question's phrase to the starting and routing environment; the records apply
  it to the authoritative record.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** which governed record is the destination for which
  information domain is still not stated (`S2-N02`). A more exact
  representation would be *the response addresses an adjacent question*, which
  is not one of the seven permitted classifications and is proposed rather than
  used (PT-04). Difference in terminology is the closest honest fit and is what
  is recorded.
- **Additional evidence needed:** the destination record per domain; and the
  unanswered retrievability limb (`S-N11`).
- **Effect on advancement:** removes the *contradiction* ground for refusing on
  source-of-truth; does not by itself establish the boundary (§5).

**C-46-R2 · Q18 · the sensemaking-and-routing model — Corroboration**

- **Stakeholder statement:** `S2-05`, `S2-06` — a matter begins and is thought
  through in one environment, is clarified and broken down there, is routed to
  the function or room through which it should proceed, and durable authority
  resides in the governed function or record.
- **Stakeholder terminology:** "begins," "thinks through," "clarified and
  broken down," "determines or identifies the appropriate institutional
  function / room," "destination."
- **Records-derived evidence:** the corpus records the same shape, in its own
  words and independently of the account — a five-level authority hierarchy
  ending in task instructions that "may not silently override higher
  authority"; the rule that "[w]hen technical work reveals a strategic or
  constitutional question, document it and return it to the appropriate room
  rather than resolving it through code"; Guardrail 15 separating who
  determines direction from who determines how; the Methodology Foundation's
  rule that an asset becomes authoritative only within "the legitimate
  authority of the Methodology room" and that "[p]resence in the Method Library
  records authority. It does not create authority"; twenty-two determination
  records each routing a matter from one office to a named function; and a
  deferred cost decision recorded as one that "returns to the Founder Office"
  on stated triggers. Independently, `C-41` already corroborated that ideas are
  formalized inside conversation.
- **Record source:** `X-13`…`X-16` (§4); `AGENTS.md:14–28`; Vision Foundation
  Guardrail 15; Methodology Foundation v1 §6.1; `fd-0015` §3; the
  determinations register; `C-41`.
- **Record authority / status:** current governing text and current
  evidentiary practice.
- **Classification:** **Corroboration.**
- **Reason:** the account's clarified model and the records' recorded model
  describe the same arrangement — a place where matters are worked through and
  routed, and a separate place where authority and the durable record reside.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** **[same-source]**. The governing text corroborating
  the account was approved by the same authority that gave the account. This is
  consistency, not independence — as with six corroborations in session 1. And
  the *conversational environment itself* remains at access = **none**; what
  happens inside it is corroborated in shape, not in content.
- **Additional evidence needed:** none for the shape; observation would be
  needed for the content, and is not available.
- **Effect on advancement:** none directly. A corroborated routing model does
  not supply the source-of-truth boundary the gate requires.
