# Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One: Correction Dispositions for Draft v2

**Classification: Institutional Record** — methodology refinement record
(evidentiary, non-governing). Records the individual disposition of all
seventeen corrections proposed by Stage 2
(`organizational-diagnostic-correction-package.md`) after frozen-draft
testing, decided under `fd-0020` §2.8 through the Methodology Foundation v1
§6.4 refinement process ("substantive refinement requires a new version"),
and the exact change each produced in
`organizational-diagnostic-generation-one-draft-v2.md`.

- **Draft v1 (tested baseline):** `organizational-diagnostic-generation-one-draft.md`,
  commit `076440c`, SHA-256
  `6fb2fb8448ca19577f1d0c53830ed52597ddf72e7a9b98a5ab2a10c02c4a9a73` —
  verified unchanged at Stage 3 start; **not modified** by any disposition.
- **Deciding office:** Institutional Platform Development, within the Stage 3
  authorization. Every disposition is reviewable and reversible by the
  Founder Office on review of the Stage 3 report (§38); none is a
  determination.
- **Dispositions used:** ACCEPTED (adopted as proposed; placement wording
  only) · MODIFIED (adopted with a substantive change, stated) · DEFERRED
  (not adopted in v2; returned to a named authority) · DECLINED (not adopted;
  reason stated). Where one correction contains separable elements, each
  element is dispositioned.
- **Rule honoured:** no correction was assumed adopted. Each was re-read
  against the dry-run evidence it cites and against the twelve priority
  tests of `fd-0020` §2.9, which sometimes required more than the correction
  proposed and once required less.

## 2. Changes in Draft v2 not traceable to a Stage 2 correction

The twelve priority tests of `fd-0020` §2.9 required four changes that no
Stage 2 correction proposed. They are recorded here so that traceability is
exact rather than flattering:

| Ref | Priority | Draft v2 change | Section |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P-1 | 1 (partial access) | An **access statement** — full / partial / none, per corpus, system, and person — is a required A3 output and is carried into every instrument header and the A18 outcome | A3 step 10; A18 |
| P-8 | 8 (workflow discovery without prior knowledge) | A10 gains a **trail-first procedure**: discover work from what was produced (artifacts, records, timestamps, correspondence) and from a stakeholder walking through the *last actual instance*, before any described process is recorded; described and observed flow are recorded as two rows | A10 |
| P-7 | 7 (source-authority verification) | A9 separates three facts that must never be collapsed: **a source exists**; **a stakeholder is confident in it**; **the organization treats it as authoritative (by what act)** — each recorded with its own evidence class | A9 rules |
| P-5 | 5 (incomplete records) | A17 rule 2 gains the operational form: a missing item is a register row with source "not supplied" / "not accessible" / "not known to exist," and every finding that would have used it names the gap | A17 rule 2; Instrument 1 |

## 3. What did not change

As Stage 2 recorded, no change is made to the ten instruments' global rules
(no scoring, no maturity levels, no weighted formulas, no automated
conclusions, no standardized questionnaire, no software), to the five
permitted Instrument 9 outcomes, to the thirteen non-prescription rules
(A19), or to the mechanism-neutral institutional-memory principle (A12).
Instruments 8 and 10 are unchanged in fields and rules.
