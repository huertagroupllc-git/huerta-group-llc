## 10. CS-02 A1/A2 precedence implementation

Implemented as two A1 blocks, and nothing in A2:

- **"An organization that governs its own vocabulary"** — adopt an authoritative
  vocabulary as the organization's terms; record its governing status and
  enforcement mechanism as evidence; elicit only for uncovered concepts; and
  **adoption is not endorsement** — A1's existing divergence-is-a-finding rule
  applies to a governed vocabulary exactly as to a spoken one. That retained
  rule is the guard against importing a subject's blind spots, as Stage 7
  required.
- **"Where the organization's apparatus reaches evidence itself"** — the
  subject's evidence taxonomy is recorded faithfully and used **when describing
  how the subject classifies its own evidence**; it "does not replace, redefine,
  or silently modify A2"; a scheme that appears stronger is preserved as
  evidence for later methodology evolution and **not substituted during the
  engagement**; and the section closes on the determination's own formulation —
  "A1 preserves the organization's language; A2 preserves the diagnostic's
  evidence semantics; neither erases the other."

**Consistency check required by the instrument** — *no Draft v4 wording causes
A1 and A2 to compete for authority*: A2 is not amended by CS-02 at all, and A1's
new text states the boundary in A2's favour for evidence classification and in
A1's favour for terminology. The two do not overlap in the drafted text.

## 12. CS-07 identity-of-record and A18 implementation

Four repairs, three in A18 and one in A4:

1. **Identity-of-record defined and sourced.** A4 gains its first output. It is
   "a **sourced** statement of what the organization's **own authoritative or
   governing evidence** establishes" about what the organization is, what it
   exists to do, and the obligations or purposes that define it, with explicit
   notation where no authoritative record exists, where sources conflict, or
   where identity authority is unresolved. The four excluded forms are listed —
   consultant-written mission statement, public copy by default, a summary
   inferred from observed activity, a judgement about what the organization
   ought to become. **A3 supplies evidence; A4 owns the output.** Where the
   evidence is absent or conflicted, "the output is still required and states
   that truthfully — it does not disappear because the organization has no
   settled answer."
2. **Exclusions given sources.** A new A18 paragraph, *Where the exclusions come
   from*: each limb is produced where the domain that discovers it sits, "and no
   limb is assigned to a single universal stage"; A18 **aggregates** and "does
   not originate them."
3. **Outcome disentangled from instrument.** A new A18 paragraph, *The outcome
   is not the instrument*: the advancement action is one of five states a
   diagnosis can end in; **Instrument 10 is a required output of every
   diagnosis** whatever the outcome; "a diagnosis that proceeds still produces
   Instrument 10; a diagnosis that returns questions still had to produce it
   first."
4. **Element 4 settled (AMB-2).** A18's minimum-evidence element now reads
   "source-of-truth boundaries are **sufficiently identified** for diagnosis —
   including unresolved or contested authority where that is the truthful
   finding," with the express statement that this "is not a requirement that
   every source authority be institutionally established before diagnosis may
   proceed."

**Stage 8 Deficiency 3 is discharged**: the ambiguity `fd-0026` settled without
naming it is now settled in the text and recorded as settled.

## 14. CS-14 confidentiality implementation against `fd-0026`

A17 rule 5 is rewritten, and it now states its own minimum rather than pointing
at a document that may not travel with it.

| `fd-0026` §2.6 requirement | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| State the minimum binding handling principle | The rule opens with it, "which governs whether or not any parent doctrine is available": do not reproduce, transfer, abstract into another institutional corpus, or disclose client-specific or confidential material beyond what is necessary to perform and evidence the authorized diagnostic |
| Cite the governing doctrine where available | "Where a governing confidentiality doctrine applies to the engagement, it is cited and it governs" |
| Record whether it was actually held | "the diagnosis records whether that doctrine was actually held by the team" |
| Conservative default where it is not held or cannot govern | "the minimum above is the conservative default and the diagnosis says which it applied" |
| Claim preservation without reproducing substance | preserve "that the evidence exists, its provenance, the conclusion it supports within the authorized boundary, and any necessary limitation — without copying the substance itself" |
| Beyond the default | "requires confidentiality terms or the organization's authority established **before** proceeding" |
| Do not duplicate the full doctrine | The rule states a principle and a default; it reproduces no part of MF v1 §7.5, and the sentence about never converting client material into general methodology without review, abstraction, protection and approval is carried over from Draft v3 |

**This is the item where `fd-0026` is broadest against its Stage 7 text.** The
change set expressly declined to propose a minimum principle on the ground that
copying doctrine would create a second governed source; the determination
required a minimum principle anyway, distinguishing *stating a minimum* from
*duplicating a doctrine*. Draft v4 implements the determination. Stage 8
recorded this expansion as Deficiency 2, and it is repeated here so the scope of
the drafted rule is measured against `fd-0026` and not against the change set.

## 30. Recommendation for the smallest meaningful Draft v4 validation stage

**Recommendation, and recommendation only** (`fd-0027` §2.10): *recommendation
is not authorization.*

**Recommended: one application of Draft v4 to a subject where participant
evidence is actually obtainable, run by an author who did not draft it, on a
subject with confidential material and a governed vocabulary.**

That is one application, not a programme. It is the smallest thing that can
exercise the changed elements, and here is why nothing smaller will do:

- **Participant access is not optional this time.** CS-06, CS-08 and CS-13 all
  concern what happens when participants are unavailable — and all three were
  written from two applications that had no participants. A third records-only
  application would re-exercise the empty case and validate none of them.
- **A second author is required by what CS-03 changed.** The material/incidental
  prior-involvement test was written by the office it governs. Its applicability
  by anyone else is the open question, and only a different author can close it.
- **Confidential material is required by CS-14.** The rule has never met the
  condition it exists for.
- **A governed vocabulary is required by CS-02.** The precedence rule was
  derived from one subject that had one; it needs a second before it can be
  said to generalize even internally.

**What one such application would exercise:** CS-01 (source kinds against real
mixed evidence), CS-02, CS-03, CS-04 (absent categories where some are genuinely
absent and some are boundaried), CS-05, CS-06, CS-07 (whether A4 can produce
identity-of-record), CS-08, CS-09, CS-12 (whether an application that fails
closure actually writes the statement), CS-14, CS-15 and CS-17 (whether the
three-value marker discriminates). That is thirteen of seventeen from one
application.

**What it would not exercise, and what to do about it:** CS-10's conflict
recording needs a team whose prior knowledge is *wrong*, which cannot be
arranged; CS-11 needs a subject whose structure the question presupposes and
that does not have it; CS-13 and CS-16 are disclosure and wording and are best
read against the application's own record afterwards. **Record these as not
exercised rather than declaring them fine.**

**What is expressly not recommended:** external application. `fd-0027` §2.10
forbids recommending it merely because Draft v4 exists, and the reason is
substantive as well as procedural — a version with eight untested structural
treatments is not what should meet a real client first. The internal
application above should come first, and its result should decide what comes
after it.

**A10 must be answered before or during that application**, since its reading
determines whether the trail-first procedure was performed. That answer is the
Founder Office's, not the diagnostic team's.
