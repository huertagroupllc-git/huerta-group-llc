## 27. Proposed future diagnostic closure mechanism

**CS-12, and it is deliberately the narrowest thing that works.**

A diagnosis may be represented as closed with a mandatory closure condition
unsatisfied **only** if it carries an **unresolved-closure statement** naming
the condition, why it could not be satisfied, and who must satisfy it — carried
**beside** the outcome and **never as a sixth outcome**. And the method
distinguishes four states in its own words: *application stage complete* ·
*subject diagnosis complete* · *advancement permitted* · *closure condition
unresolved*.

**No universal project-management closure state is designed.** This is
diagnostic closure semantics only. **Whether a diagnosis may close at all with
A17 rule 7 unsatisfied is a determination** and is returned, not taken.

## 29. Proposed non-numeric sufficiency presentation mechanism

**CS-17 — three parts, none numeric:**

1. An **advancement-state declaration separate from instrument completeness**,
   in the method's existing words.
2. A per-instrument **evidence-sufficiency marker** with exactly three values
   already in the method's vocabulary — *completed* · *completed with named
   gaps* · *blocked by insufficient evidence* — with the reason, and **never
   aggregated across instruments**.
3. A **placement rule**: both appear at the **head of each instrument**, not
   only in a report header. This is the part that answers the observation; a
   label a reader passes once at the top of a document does not travel with the
   table.

**Prohibited and absent:** percentage complete, diagnostic score, maturity
score, traffic light, weighted evidence index, numerical confidence, any
aggregation. Three words per instrument and a reason — bounded that tightly
precisely so it cannot slide into a score.

## 49. Would another internal application add meaningful evidence?

**Answer, from the three permitted:**
**→ ANOTHER INTERNAL APPLICATION WOULD HAVE LOW MARGINAL EVIDENTIARY VALUE —
with one specifically named exception that is not a change of subject.**

**Why low.** The internal subjects available — the parent, the venture, the
operations runtime — share one steward, one implementer, and one records author.
A third would supply a third corpus and no new independence, and would re-enter
the same conditions already observed twice: single-actor authority, no
contested authority, records-only evidence, and a diagnostic team that helped
build the subject. The elements that failed in both applications — **purpose and
outputs sufficiently understood**, and **critical workflows sufficiently
understood** — failed for want of **accounts and observation of work**, and a
new subject does not supply either.

**The named exception, which is an access change rather than a subject change.**
An application in which **stakeholder accounts and observation of work as
performed are actually authorized** would exercise the two elements no
application has ever met, and would exercise A10's observation limb, which
**A20 records has never been exercised at all**. That is available internally,
on any of the three subjects. It would narrow nothing in §48 and should not be
represented as doing so.

**Not conducted under Stage 7**, and not recommended at §52 in preference to
settling the determinations that currently block a coherent revision.
