# Generation One — Draft v6 Bounded Correction Construction — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0032`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument **answers RQ-1 — Alternative B** — and authorizes construction of
Draft v6 from Draft v5, bounded to the seven Stage 11 items and that
determination. It carries its own guard against the answer's principal risk:
the Observed threshold applies **only** to A15's terminating question, **A2 is
not redefined**, and no hierarchy, ranking, weighting or general preference for
Observed evidence may follow from it. It authorizes **no validation, no
application, and no Draft v7.**
