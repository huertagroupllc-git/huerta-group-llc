# Generation One — Stage 10: Draft v5 Internal Validation — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0030`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument authorizes **one bounded internal validation application** of
frozen Draft v5 against **Huerta Group Publishing**, read-only, by an authoring
context that did not draft Draft v5 — and states in its own objective what such
an application **cannot** test: fresh-subject discovery, external validity,
client validity, evaluator independence, or general applicability. It
authorizes **no Draft v5 modification, no Draft v6, no external application,
and no maturity elevation**.
