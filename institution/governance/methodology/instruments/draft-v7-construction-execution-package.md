# Generation One — Post-Draft-v6 Semantic Determinations & Draft v7 Construction — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0036`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

**This is the instrument that answered the questions the method could not
answer for itself.** It carries Founder Office determinations on **all five**
method defects recorded by the Draft v6 internal participant validation
(**MD-1 … MD-5**) and the **RQ-G** custody determination, and it authorizes
**Draft v7** — the first new version in this program produced from a *validated
application's* findings rather than from a review or a reconciliation. Its
governing discipline is **boundedness**: a preconstruction traceability matrix
is required *before* drafting, the permitted authority sources are enumerated
and closed, and no change lacking one of them may proceed unless it is a
strictly necessary consistency consequence. It preserves A2 absolutely —
**"Do not add: evidence ranking; weighting; quality; independence scoring;
maturity scoring; new evidence axis; preferential evidence classes"** — and it
forbids encoding Huerta Group's own custody arrangement as client doctrine. It
authorizes **no validation application, no external application, and no Draft
v8**, and it requires the versioning question answered on evidence rather than
on drafting momentum.
