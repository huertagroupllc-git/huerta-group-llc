digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

of the seventeen Stage 7 change-set items, their dispositions, and the six
Founder Office determinations at `fd-0026`. It authorizes **no validation of
Draft v4, no external application, no further replication, and no elevation of
the method's maturity posture.**
