# Generation One — Draft v6 Clarification Reconciliation — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0035`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument carries **two things at once**: the Founder's answers to the
five clarification-only follow-ups returned at the close of the Draft v6
participant validation, and the **Founder Office determination of RQ-H —
Alternative C, with Alternative B expressly declined.** It authorizes a
**bounded additive reconciliation** of the closed validation record and
nothing wider: reassessment is confined, provision by provision, to what the
new evidence actually reaches, and each `<reassess_only>` block names its own
limit. Its governing discipline is **chronology** — every affected item must
preserve the original response, the original interpretation, the later
clarification, and the reconciled result, and **"Do not rewrite earlier
evidence as though the clarifications had existed at the time."** It carries
its own anti-inflation rule for the versioning question: another version is
**not** justified merely because answers became clearer, findings remain
unresolved, external validation is still absent, or conditions were not
internally exercisable. It authorizes **no Draft v6 modification, no Draft v7,
no further application, and no subject repair.**
