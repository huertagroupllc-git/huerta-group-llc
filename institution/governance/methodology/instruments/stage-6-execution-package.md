# Generation One — Stage 6: Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0024`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument authorizes a reproducibility test: the same frozen Draft v3,
the same subject at the same baseline, applied by a **second authoring
context** isolated from the first author's conclusions, with the two results
compared only after the second is sealed. It authorizes no change to Draft v3,
no Draft v4, and no change to the venture.
