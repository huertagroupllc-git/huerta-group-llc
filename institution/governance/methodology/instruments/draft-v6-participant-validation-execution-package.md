# Generation One — Draft v6 Internal Participant Validation — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0033`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument authorizes the **first application in this program's history to
require authentic participant evidence** — frozen Draft v6 applied to **Huerta
Group LLC**, by a non-authoring context, with the Founder as participant. It
names A15/RQ-1 as the **highest-priority** target and, unusually, requires the
validation to test **whether the method's own new threshold has caused drift**
in A2. It carries an explicit anti-fabrication rule: **if participant responses
are not available, return the minimum neutral question set rather than a
completed report.** It authorizes **no Draft v6 modification, no Draft v7, and
no external application.**
