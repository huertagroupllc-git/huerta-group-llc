# Generation One — Stage 11: Draft v5 Validation Finding & Correction Disposition — Founder Office Execution Package

**Classification: Institutional Record** — preserved Founder Office
instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0031`. **The instrument below is the source.**
This header is outside the source and alters nothing in it; provenance,
digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

The instrument authorizes a **disposition stage only**: retrieve the
authoritative Stage 10 corpus, reconcile the seventeen-item exercise matrix
under four Founder Office classification definitions, and disposition every
finding and every proposed correction **individually and separately**. It
authorizes **no Draft v5 modification, no Draft v6, no applied correction, and
no further application** — and it carries two constraints that bound the work:
**A2's non-ordering architecture is preserved absolutely**, and **an unexercised
provision is not a defect merely because Stage 10 did not meet its condition.**
