instrument (evidentiary, non-governing). Preserved under the determination
transcribed at `fd-0020` §2.4 through the canonical-source preservation
standard, and recorded at `fd-0025`. **The instrument below is the source.**

digests, and deficiencies are recorded in
[`founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md`](../founder-office-instrument-preservation-record.md).

methodology-deficiency corpus — D-1…D-8 and MO-01…MO-22 — **without creating
