# Stage 10 — Evidence-Access and Prior-Result Isolation Record

**Classification: Institutional Record** — validation evidence (evidentiary,
non-governing). Produced under `fd-0030` §2.5.

This is the **orchestrating office's** record of what the validating author was
given, what it was denied, and what leaked anyway. It is written by Institutional
Platform Development — **the office that drafted Draft v5** — and that dependence
is recorded at `fd-0030` §4 Deficiency 4 rather than left implicit. The
validating author's own account of its position is separate and preserved
verbatim at `stage-10-validator-independence-statement.md`.

## 4. Leakage, measured rather than estimated

### 4.1 Leakage the briefing itself created

The briefing is not neutral and could not be. It necessarily told the author:

1. that the method's own institution built the subject and authored most of its
   records;
2. that two prior applications of earlier versions exist on this subject, and
   that their results are withheld;
3. **the twelve treatments the validation exists to exercise**, which is a map of
   where the method recently changed — and therefore, indirectly, of where it was
   previously found wanting;
4. **the seventeen item identifiers and their Draft v5 locations**, needed as the
   controlling validation map under `fd-0030` §2.7;
5. that four items were identified in advance as unlikely to be exercisable
   internally;
6. that findings adverse to the method have previously been adopted.

**Items 3 and 4 are the substantive leak.** A validator told to look hardest at
A10, Instrument 5's sufficiency marker, source kind, A1/A2 precedence,
confidentiality, closure and identity-of-record has been told where this method
has been weak. That cannot be avoided while still executing `fd-0030` §2.7's
requirement to classify all seventeen items — the instrument requires the map —
but it means **any convergence between this application's method findings and the
withheld prior findings is at least partly attributable to the briefing, not to
independent reproduction.**

### 4.2 What was deliberately redacted from the map

The seventeen-item map supplied to the author was reduced to **identifier and
Draft v5 location only**. The deficiency narratives, the prior authors' quoted
reasoning, and — critically — the prior HGP-specific findings recorded in the
validation package's subject assessment were **not** supplied. The author was
told what to look at. It was not told what a previous application found there.

### 4.3 Leakage the author disclosed itself

The author's frozen statement §2 and §5.8 record that **its operating context
named Huerta ventures as subjects of prior sessions**. This is the same
contributor-memory vector recorded at Stage 6. The author disclosed it before
evidence, on its own initiative, and stated that it had not opened the memory
material and that anything obtained from it would be institutional
self-description rather than corroboration.

### 4.4 What was not withheld and should be understood as shared context

**The subject has not moved.** HGP is at `e28f12a` — the same commit Stage 5 and
Stage 6 both read. Stage 10 therefore applies a new method version to **the same
corpus**, not merely the same organization. Recorded at `fd-0030` §4 Deficiency 2
before the application began, because it bounds what any resemblance between
applications can mean.

## 5. What this isolation is, and is not

- It is **instructed, not enforced.** The validating author operates in the same
  filesystem as the withheld records. Nothing technically prevented it from
  opening one. Isolation rests on the instruction plus the author's own
  disclosure of what it read, exactly as at Stage 6 (`fd-0030` §4 Deficiency 3).
- It is **not independence.** The author is an agent instance inside the same
  development function that wrote the method and built the subject. Its own
  statement calls this "a separation of hands, not of interest," and that
  formulation is adopted here.
- It is **not blindness.** The author was told the institutional structure, the
  authorship of the evidence, the existence of prior applications, and the
  treatments under test.

**The honest description of what this control achieves:** the application's
substantive findings about HGP were not copied from or steered by the prior
applications' findings, because the author did not hold them. It achieves nothing
beyond that, and nothing in the Stage 10 record may claim otherwise.
