## 13. Priority correction — one-sided-subject classification

**MODIFIED; classification element DEFERRED.**

The Founder Office's scrutiny reframed the proposal, and the reframing is
better than the original: the question is whether *the evidence model*
represents a one-sided matter "without treating that evidence as corroborated
merely because no opposing account exists." That is the dangerous half, and it
is what Draft v3 fixes.

**A2 rule 5** now reads, in part: *"Unopposed is not corroborated… Absence of
disagreement is recorded as the absence of a second source, never as
agreement,"* and Instrument 1 carries **opposing account (sought / not sought /
sought and unavailable, with the reason)**, filled for every row a finding
relies on.

**Deferred to the Founder Office:** whether the comparison procedure gains
sub-classifications *not addressed by the account* / *not addressed by the
records*. The seven classifications are fixed by determination, and Development
does not amend a determined vocabulary through a draft.

**Consequence, stated plainly:** at least five Stage 3 comparisons will keep
being reported as *Unresolved* when they are one-sided, until that deferral is
decided. §24 names them.

## 14. Priority correction — limb-level capture

**ACCEPTED.**

The Founder Office's scrutiny asked whether multi-claim answers must be
decomposed "so a single response can contain corroborated, unsupported,
contradictory, and unresolved elements simultaneously." The Stage 3 evidence
says yes and shows it already happened informally: thirty answers produced
forty-eight claim rows and nineteen not-supplied rows, and three answers landed
beside the question asked.

Draft v3 makes it a rule rather than a practice. **A3 step 8:** *"Each question
is decomposed into its limbs before it is put,"* every limb recorded *answered*
or *not supplied*, and a response addressing a different question recorded as
such. **Instrument 1:** each limb is its own row, and **"response does not
address the question asked"** is a row type recording what was asked, what was
answered, and whether a follow-up was taken.

**The limbs are enumerated in the record, not in the asking** — the global
instrument rules forbid the method becoming a questionnaire, and that
constraint is unchanged.

## 15. Priority correction — two-pass access statement

**MODIFIED — reframed by the Founder Office's own scrutiny, and improved by it.**

The Stage 3 proposal was: the team's inventory first, then re-derivation from
what the account names. The Founder Office's framing is *what is stated to
exist* against *what is actually obtained or verified*. That covers a condition
the Stage 3 version missed entirely — a participant states that evidence exists
and it never arrives — which in a real engagement is more common than a holding
the team failed to imagine. Both are now carried by the same field.

**A3 step 10** records, separately and never merged: **(a) stated to exist or
identified** — by whom, when, on what basis; and **(b) access actually
obtained** — full / partial / none, how verified, what "partial" excluded. The
statement is built from the team's inventory and **re-derived after every
stakeholder session**, adding what the account names with the question it came
from. *"The statement in force is the re-derived one,"* and A18 evaluates that
one.

**Why this matters more than any other change:** without it, a diagnosis can
pass A18 on an access statement the participant has already falsified. In
Stage 3 the re-derivation added three holdings at access = none, two of which
carry material findings.

## 16. Priority correction — source-of-resort field

**MODIFIED — widened to source roles.**

The Founder Office directed that the clarified sensemaking-and-routing
behaviour be considered as evidence for this correction, and it changes the
correction's shape. "Source of resort" alone would have recorded that people go
somewhere, and still not shown *why* — that it is where matters are worked
through and routed, not where truth is kept.

**The Draft v2 test required by `fd-0022` §2.6 was performed and failed.**
Draft v2's A9 separates three facts (a source exists · someone is confident in
it · the organization treats it as authoritative) and Instrument 4 carries
authoritative source, owner, retrievability, representations, conflicts and
freshness. Neither allows *where a matter is worked through*, *where it is
clarified*, and *where it is routed from* to differ from *where its truth is
established*. That deficiency is treated through this disposition, as the
instrument directs.

**Draft v3's A9** adds **source roles, recorded separately** — six of them,
each with its own evidence class and "none evidenced" permitted — under the
rule that *"none of the first three roles makes a source authoritative."* It
adds **source of resort**, per participant, Client-Stated, *"recorded beside
the authoritative source and never as it,"* with divergence recorded under
conflicts. Instrument 4 carries both.

**Neutrality held.** No office, room, environment, or mechanism of this
organization entered the method. What is generalized is only the distinction
(`fd-0022` §2.6 critical rule).

## 17. Sensemaking-versus-authority treatment

The instrument asked the diagnostic to distinguish five things. Draft v3
distinguishes six, in two places.

| The question | Where Draft v3 answers it |
| --- | --- |
| Where does sensemaking occur? | A9 source roles — *where a matter is first worked through* |
| Where is a matter clarified? | A9 source roles — *where it is clarified* |
| Where is it routed? | A9 source roles — *where it is routed from* |
| Who has authority to decide it? | A9 source roles — *who decides it*, cross-referenced to A6 |
| Where does the durable authoritative record reside? | A9 source roles — *where the fact is established* **and** *where the durable record of the decision resides*, kept apart because a fact can be established in one place and retrievable from another |

**The governing rule.** *"None of the first three roles makes a source
authoritative. An environment used consistently — even exclusively — for
sensemaking, clarification, or routing is recorded in those roles and is not an
authoritative source unless the organization has established it as one by act,
instrument, or practice."*

**A6 carries the same distinction into authority.** Its chain extends from five
terms to eight — authority ≠ responsibility ≠ representation ≠ execution ≠
custody ≠ sensemaking ≠ clarification ≠ routing — with the statement that the
last three *"confer no authority whatever, however consistently or exclusively
they are used."*

**Where the roles differ, that is a finding and not a defect.** Draft v3 says
so explicitly, because the evidence that produced this change came from an
organization where they differ and the Stage 3 method read the difference as a
contradiction.

gives. It is recorded in this program's findings, not in the method. Draft v3
does not encode a single apex individual as structurally required, and A1 now
non-assumes authority concentrated in any one actor.

## 21. Material differences from Draft v2

1. **Unopposed is not corroborated** (A2 rule 5) — single-source evidence never
   becomes corroborated by the absence of a contrary account, and whether a
   second account was sought is recorded.
2. **A participant who authored the records is disclosed** (A18, A2 rule 5,
   Instrument 1) — the mirror of the author-of-records label the team already
   carried, with the count of affected corroborations stated.
3. **Source roles are separated from authority** (A9, A6, Instrument 4) — where
   a matter is worked through, clarified, routed from, decided, established as
   fact, and durably recorded may be six different places, and the first three
   confer nothing.
4. **Source of resort is recorded per participant** (A9, Instrument 4) —
   separately from the authoritative source, with divergence a finding.
5. **The access statement is two-pass and re-derived after every session**
   (A3 step 10, A18) — stated-to-exist against actually-obtained.
6. **The organization's boundary is established before anything is asked about
   what lies outside it** (A3 step 11, A12, Instrument 6).
7. **Questions are recorded by limb**, and an answer that addresses a different
   question is recorded as such (A3 step 8, Instrument 1).
8. **A clarification-only follow-up is required** for under-determined answers,
   with three named triggers, anti-leading rules, and the original preserved
   (A3 step 8, Instrument 1).
9. **Habit-held responsibility is established by observation, not by asking**
   (A7, Instrument 3, A20) — and where observation is unavailable the
   capability is recorded unexercised rather than reported empty.
10. **Contradiction within one account** is preserved and recorded as a finding
    (A17 rule 3).
11. **Authority gains contested, informal, and anticipated-but-not-established**
    (A6, Instrument 2), with concentration recorded as a dated condition rather
    than a model.
