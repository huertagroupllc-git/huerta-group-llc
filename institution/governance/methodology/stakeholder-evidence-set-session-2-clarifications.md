## 1. What a clarification is, and is not, in this record

1. **Additive, never substitutive.** A clarification is a **new row** with its
   own date. It does not edit, replace, annotate, or supersede the session-1
   row. Both stand, and the session-1 rows `S-04`, `S-21`, `S-22`, `S-29`,
   `S-47` are unchanged at their captured wording.
2. **The original ambiguity is preserved as a fact of the record**
   (`fd-0022` §2.2 chronology rule and prohibition). That an answer was
   under-determined, and that Development read it a particular way at Stage 3,
   are themselves evidence about the method and are not erased.
3. **Prospective, not retroactive.** The clarification changes what is known
   from 2026-08-25 (session 2) forward. It does not make the Stage 3 reading
   wrong at the time it was made, and the Stage 3 record is not rewritten.
4. **Clarification-only.** No clarification was elicited by offering candidate
   words, structures, or answers. Each was supplied by the Founder Office in
   its own words inside the instrument.
5. **Still Client-Stated.** A clarification from the accountable authority is
   not corroboration. It is a second statement by the same source, and where
   it agrees with a record that source authored, the agreement is marked
   **[same-source]**, exactly as in session 1.
