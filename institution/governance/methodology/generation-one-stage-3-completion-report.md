## 25. New assumptions exposed by the stakeholder application

Predicted at the pre-response report §33, now **realized in use**: question
authorship is itself a leading act; the team's records can be sealed and its
memory cannot; **a stakeholder may be the author of the records too** (six
same-source corroborations); "records-only" has an unlabelled mirror.

Newly exposed by the application itself:

1. **The account may answer a different question than the one asked**, without
   either party noticing (Q14, Q16, Q21). The method classifies evidence; it
   never checks responsiveness.
2. **A one-word answer is both evidence and a hole.** "Process." satisfies every
   capture rule and under-determines A1's entire concept set, and nothing says
   what happens next.
3. **The stakeholder can contradict the stakeholder** — five times here. Capture
   rule 5 keeps both; the classification vocabulary describes only
   account-versus-records.
4. **Records may address a subject the account never reaches, and the reverse.**
   Neither corroboration nor contradiction; *Unresolved* is carrying at least
   five such cases and misstating them.
5. **Corroboration by the author of a record is not corroboration.**
6. **Totality answers can be neither verified nor falsified** — "All of them,"
   "all of it," "None" — and the method has no rule for them.
7. **A perception and an assertion look identical in capture.** The comparison
   then does work the capture should have marked.
8. **The access statement can be falsified by the stakeholder** and the method
   does not require re-deriving it (PT-08).
