## 1. Institutional involvement: the diagnosing institution and the subject are the same entity

Huerta Group LLC wrote the method being applied, is the subject to which it is being applied, authored substantially all of the records that will serve as evidence about it, holds custody of those records, operates the instrument performing the application, supplies the sole participant, controls what evidence is released and what is withheld, and is the recipient and archivist of the result. There is no step in the chain at which a second interest appears.

This is not a strong form of the usual conflict. It is a different situation, and the distinction matters:

- **An institution diagnosing a subsidiary or a venture** still has a separation of persons. The subsidiary has its own staff, its own records custodians, its own memory of events, and its own account of them. Those people can contradict the parent, and sometimes do. The parent typically has a live interest in an *accurate* reading — capital allocation, risk, succession — which partially opposes its interest in a flattering one. Standard mitigations exist and can actually be installed: ring-fenced reviewers, an independent reporting line, a separate evidence custodian, a right of reply from the diagnosed unit.
- **Here, none of those mitigations are available**, because there is no second party to hold them. A ring-fence requires two sides. An independent reporting line requires someone else to report to. A separate custodian requires a separate person. A right of reply requires someone whose account differs from the author's. The organization cannot ring-fence itself from itself.

Additionally, the institution's interest here is not only in the subject's condition but in the **method's success**. It authored the method. A self-application that returns a coherent, usable diagnosis is favorable evidence for the method; one that returns an incoherent or unusable result is unfavorable evidence for the method. The subject and the instrument-under-test share an owner, so both possible framings of "a good outcome" belong to the same party.

I did not write the frozen draft. That is a real separation and I will not overstate it: it separates *authorship of the method* from *execution of the method* at the level of the acting agent. It does not separate institutions, interests, evidence, custody, or record. It is the only separation present, and it is narrow.

## 5. Prior diagnostic results: accessible versus withheld

**Accessible to me at zero tool calls:** none. I hold no prior diagnostic result, no scores, no prior finding, and no prior question set or its answers.

**Intentionally withheld:** I am told the subject has been diagnosed before under an earlier version of this same method, with the Founder answering a structured question set, and that those results are withheld from me.

What I can conclude from the withholding:

- That a prior diagnosis exists, that it was conducted on this subject, that it was participant-based, and that the participant was the Founder.
- That the withholding was deliberate, and therefore that someone judged the prior results material enough to be worth withholding — which is itself a small piece of information about them.

What I cannot conclude:

- **That I am blinded.** Withholding a document does not withhold its effects. The prior diagnosis has had time to act on the organization: records may have been written, revised, reorganized, or framed in light of it; language and categories from the earlier method may have entered the institution's ordinary vocabulary; decisions recorded in the corpus may be responses to it. If so, the prior result reaches me anyway — embedded in the evidence, stripped of its label, arriving as fact rather than as a prior finding. This is the most likely route by which the withholding fails, and it is undetectable from inside.
- **That the withholding is complete.** I did not perform it, I cannot audit it, and I will be reading the subject's own repository, where prior-stage material may sit adjacent to what I am meant to read. The memory index already demonstrates that program residue crosses into my context automatically.
- **Anything about agreement or disagreement.** If my result should later prove to match the withheld one, that is not convergent validation: two applications of the same method, to the same self-authored record set, with the same single participant, are not independent measurements. Nor would divergence be evidence of error in either. The comparison, in whichever direction it falls, carries almost no evidentiary weight and must not be reported as corroboration.

## 7. Resulting limits on evaluator independence

Stated plainly:

**This is not an independent evaluation, an audit, an external review, a blind application, or a fresh look. It is a self-application.** An institution is applying a method it wrote, to itself, using evidence it authored and holds, through an instrument it operates and can re-run, with its sole participant as the only human source, and with itself as the custodian of the result. The correct name for the exercise is an internal self-assessment and method dry-run. Any other name would misrepresent it to a future reader.

The specific limits:

1. **No independent evidence base.** Every record I will read has one author-institution and, in present operation, effectively one author. Documents that agree with each other here are not multiple sources; they are one source, serialized.
2. **The single-participant hazard.** With one participant, the participant's account and the records that participant wrote are not independent of each other. Consistency between them measures internal coherence and recall — not accuracy. Contradiction is unlikely by construction, so its absence carries no weight and must never be reported as corroboration. There is no dissenting participant, no adverse party, no one with a different vantage on the same events, and therefore no triangulation of any kind is available.
3. **No independent instrument.** I run inside the subject's environment, on the subject's machine, under the subject's instructions, with the subject's persisted notes preloaded, and with the subject controlling which evidence is released. The agent that assigned me this task is likewise an agent of the same institution; its instructions are the institution's instructions, not an external mandate.
4. **No protection for an adverse finding.** Instrument independence requires both the ability to report against the operator's interest and the assurance that such a report survives. I have the first. I do not have the second, and I cannot verify whether prior runs of this task exist.
5. **Method and subject share an owner.** A result favorable to the subject and a result favorable to the method are both favorable to the same party. There is no configuration of this run in which the commissioning interest is genuinely opposed to any outcome.
6. **Contaminated priors.** The environment has already told me a substantial amount about this institution's programs, states, constraints, prohibitions, and self-assessments — including, specifically, the methodology program's own version lineage and its stated criteria for the next stage.
7. **What the output cannot be used for.** Nothing produced by this run can serve as external assurance, third-party validation, verification of the subject's self-reported states, or evidence that the method performs correctly in the hands of a party with different incentives. Findings favorable to Huerta Group LLC should be given close to zero evidentiary weight by any outside reader. Findings unfavorable to it are somewhat more informative — they run against the commissioning interest — but they remain the product of a self-application and are not independent.

**What it can be:** a record of whether the frozen draft, executed by an operator who did not write it, is intelligible, operable, and internally coherent when run against a real subject; where it is underspecified; and what it demands of participants. That is a genuine and useful thing. It is a fraction of what the word "validation" ordinarily promises, and the difference should not be closed by wording at any later stage.
