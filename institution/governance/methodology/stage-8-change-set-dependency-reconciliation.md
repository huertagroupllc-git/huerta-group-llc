## 1. CS-01 — An evidence-source kind, without a reliability ranking

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage 7 question** | "the value set is methodology semantics, and the line between 'kind' and 'quality' is exactly where a ranking could enter." |
| **Stage 7 status** | Determination required: YES |
| **Stage 8 determination** | Approved as a **descriptive** attribute answering only *"What kind of thing supplied this claim?"* and never *"How good… is this evidence?"* Values must be "categorical; descriptive; organization-neutral; non-ordinal." Minimum capability: person/stakeholder account · organizational or institutional record · supplied institutional source · systematic verification/validation record · other kinds genuinely required by application evidence. Evidence quality is expressed only through limitations, contradictions, provenance, freshness, coverage and contextual explanation. "A2's existing non-ordering rule remains controlling." |
| **Resulting bounded Draft v4 requirement** | A2 gains a source-kind attribute on Client-Stated rows with **at least** the five listed values, carried as a field in Instrument 1; the attribute is never a strength and A2 acquires no ordering. |
| **Remaining implementation ambiguity** | Exact labels, and whether the open-ended fifth value needs a recording convention. **Future drafting jurisdiction** — the determination grants normalization authority "provided the categories remain descriptive and non-hierarchical." |
| **Founder Office authority still required?** | **No.** |

**Scope note.** The determination is **broader** than the Stage 7 item: the
change set proposed four categories, the determination requires five plus an

## 3. CS-03 — One A18 disclosure block, and a definition of "the diagnostic team"

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage 7 question** | "how far 'prior involvement' reaches, and whether the institutional reading is the default." |
| **Stage 7 status** | Determination required: YES |
| **Stage 8 determination** | "'Diagnostic team' means the actual institutional or individual actor materially performing the diagnostic," and the method must disclose **both** the institutional relationship and the human/agent relationship where they differ. **Material** prior involvement is enumerated — designing, constructing, governing, implementing, materially evaluating, authoring substantial portions of the evidence later diagnosed, or repeatedly advising on the same structures. **Non-material:** "Mere incidental familiarity, one-off exposure, or ordinary awareness does not automatically make a diagnosis non-independent." Institutional involvement is the **default structural reading**, "because replacing an individual or agent does not eliminate prior involvement by the institution." Scope: disclosure within the Organizational Diagnostic only, not a universal independence doctrine. |
| **Resulting bounded Draft v4 requirement** | A single A18 disclosure block defining the term, requiring **both** layers, applying the material/non-material test, and stating the five required disclosure elements — beside *author-of-records* and *account and record share a source*, before any finding. |
| **Remaining implementation ambiguity** | How the two layers are laid out in a single block. **Future drafting jurisdiction.** |
| **Founder Office authority still required?** | **No.** |

**Scope note.** The Stage 7 item offered the denotation as an either/or — "the
institution or the individual." The determination requires **both**, disclosed
separately where they differ. An expansion, recorded at `fd-0026` §4
Deficiency 2.

## 5. CS-12 — Closure semantics

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage 7 question** | "whether a diagnosis may close at all with A17 rule 7 unsatisfied." |
| **Stage 7 status** | Determination required: YES |
| **Stage 8 determination** | **Yes, qualified.** "A subject diagnosis may close as an application record with an unresolved mandatory closure condition, but it may not be represented as an unqualified completed diagnosis." Four states distinguished: application/stage work completed · subject diagnosis complete · advancement permitted · closure condition unresolved. Where a mandatory condition is unsatisfied the application may stop, its work be preserved and its outcome returned, but the diagnosis "must carry a conspicuous unresolved-closure statement" identifying the condition, why it is unsatisfied, who must satisfy it, and "what consequence the unresolved condition has for use of the diagnosis." **Not a sixth outcome** — "a condition on the status and interpretation of the diagnostic record." **Language rule:** "closed", "complete" and equivalents may not be used unqualified. |
| **Resulting bounded Draft v4 requirement** | The unresolved-closure statement at A17 rule 7 and A18, the four distinguished states, and the language rule — carried beside the outcome, never inside the outcome set. |
| **Remaining implementation ambiguity** | Where the statement physically sits relative to the CS-17 sufficiency declaration. **Future drafting jurisdiction**; CS-17's placement rule already governs the head of each instrument. |
| **Founder Office authority still required?** | **No.** |

**One thing the determination adds:** a **language rule** the Stage 7 item did
not propose. It bears directly on the Stage 5 defect — a diagnosis that closed
unagreed and was described without qualification.

## 6. CS-14 — The method's own cited-and-not-held dependency

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage 7 question** | "whether Methodology Foundation v1 §7.5 travels with the method." |
| **Stage 7 status** | Determination required: YES |
| **Stage 8 determination** | **It does not travel by implication.** A client-facing or independently usable diagnostic "may not depend on an inaccessible parent governance document for a rule necessary to handle confidential material safely," **and** may not duplicate the full doctrine, which "would create a second governed source and drift risk." A17 rule 5 must therefore: state the **minimum binding handling principle** necessary for safe operation; cite the governing doctrine where available; record whether it was actually held; and require a **conservative default** where it is not. The default is specified: "Do not reproduce, transfer, abstract into another institutional corpus, or disclose client-specific/confidential material beyond what is necessary to perform and evidence the authorized diagnostic," while preserving the existence of the evidence, its provenance, the conclusion it supports and any limitation "without copying the confidential substance itself." Handling beyond the default requires confidentiality terms or client authority established first. |
| **Resulting bounded Draft v4 requirement** | A17 rule 5 rewritten with four elements: a minimum binding principle stated in the method; a citation to the governing doctrine; a record of whether it was held; and the conservative default with its claim-preservation rule. |
| **Remaining implementation ambiguity** | The exact wording of the "minimum binding handling principle" — it must be sufficient for safe operation without duplicating the doctrine, and the line between the two is a drafting judgement. **Future drafting jurisdiction**, and the narrowest of the six. |
| **Founder Office authority still required?** | **No.** |

**Scope note.** This is the largest expansion of the six. The Stage 7 item was
**wording-only** and expressly declined to propose stating any principle inside
the method; the determination requires one. Drafting scope for CS-14 is
therefore **structural, not wording-only**, and is measured against the
determination. Recorded at `fd-0026` §4 Deficiency 2.
