# Draft v6 Validation — Participant Questions Returned Unanswered

**Classification: Institutional Record** — validation evidence (evidentiary,
non-governing). Produced under `fd-0033` §2.4.

**Eight questions, returned and not answered.** `fd-0033` §2.4 makes authentic
Founder participant evidence **mandatory** for this application and forbids
Development answering on the Founder's behalf — from the records, by inference,
from prior conversations, or otherwise. **None was answered. None was put.**
Each is reproduced verbatim from the validating author, who wrote it, named the
gap and the provision creating it, stated why the existing evidence is
insufficient, and **stopped that line of work.**

**Why this record exists rather than a completion report.** `fd-0033` carries an
express anti-fabrication rule: *"do not fabricate a completed report if
participant responses have not yet been obtained… Return only the minimum
neutral participant-evidence request necessary for the current diagnostic
progression."* The application proceeded as far as records permit and stopped
here. **The fifty-nine-section completion report is not due and has not been
written.**

**Who they are for:** the **Founder Office**, in its Huerta Group LLC capacity —
the subject's sole participant.

**How answers would be treated.** Each becomes **Client-Stated** participant
evidence under Draft v6, captured verbatim where wording carries meaning, with
each limb recorded *answered* or *not supplied*. **A direct answer, a partial
answer, "I don't know", and declining are all evidence** and would be recorded
as given. `fd-0033` §2.10 then requires the application resumed **without
restarting or altering the diagnostic boundary.**

**One of them governs the diagnosis's status.** **PQ-V6-6** is the A17 rule 7
custody agreement: until it is answered, the closure condition is unsatisfied
and this application may not be represented as an unqualified completed
diagnosis.

**Neutrality.** Each was checked by its author against A3 step 8's anti-leading
rules: no expected answer embedded, no structure named that the method hopes to
find, no options offered. On **PQ-V6-6** the author notes it deliberately did
*not* offer A17's three dispositions, because offering options is forbidden.
**Development did not edit them.**

**PQ-V6-1 — A10 walkthrough**

- *Why existing evidence is insufficient:* the artifact trail shows outputs, not sequence; the timestamp trail shows three commits 61 and 59 seconds apart, which records the commit ceremony rather than the work — demonstrated by an earlier instance where a genuinely long step *does* appear as a 37-minute gap. No records evidence exists for the transmission step at all.

## What these questions are

is the correct output of an application that requires participant evidence and
was not given a session. Seven of the eight reach provisions that have **never
been exercised in any version of this method** — A10's walkthrough limb, A7's
habit test, A6's role compression, A9's first three source roles — which is
precisely why `fd-0033` made participant evidence mandatory.

**They are not a request, and answering them is not authorized by this record.**
If the Founder Office answers, `fd-0033` §2.10 governs what follows: resume the
same application with the responses as participant evidence, without restarting
or altering the boundary.
