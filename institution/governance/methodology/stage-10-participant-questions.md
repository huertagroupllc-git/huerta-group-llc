non-governing). Produced under `fd-0030` §2.6.

**These eight questions are returned, not answered.** `fd-0030` §2.6 forbids

author, a venture steward, an imprint, Publishing Operations, or any other HGP
authority — from the records, by inference, or otherwise. **None was answered.
None was put.** Each is reproduced verbatim from the validating author, who

**Who they are for:** the **Founder Office, in its HGP capacity** — HGP's sole

## What these questions are, and are not

**They are the method working as designed**, not a shortfall. A16 and Instrument
10 exist precisely so that decisions belonging to the organization are separated
from the diagnostic's findings and returned rather than answered. Eight
questions returned unanswered is the correct output of a diagnosis conducted
without participant access.

**They are neutral.** Each was checked by its author against A3 step 8's
anti-leading rules: no expected answer is embedded, none names a structure the
method expects to find, and none offers options to choose from. Development did
not edit them.

**They are not a request.** Nothing in Stage 10 depends on their being answered,
and no subsequent stage is authorized by this record. If the Founder Office
answers any of them, the answers become **Client-Stated** participant evidence
under Draft v5 and would require a separately authorized application to be used.

**Full context:** `stage-10-draft-v5-validation-application.md` §9, and
Instrument 10 at §5 of the same record, which carries the same matters as
questions reserved to the organization's authority with their dependencies and
consequences.
