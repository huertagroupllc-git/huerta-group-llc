**Classification: Institutional Record** — admission package (evidentiary,
non-governing). **This package proposes; it admits nothing.** Prepared by
Institutional Platform Development under `fd-0019` §2.8 for Founder Office
determination.

- **Governing boundary:** FD-V1 Charter §14 — venture operating knowledge "is a
  legitimate future source for parent institutional knowledge **only** through
  governed admission — abstracted, reviewed, and approved; author-confidential
  material never qualifies as source material." Corporate Venture Governance
  Blueprint §11: "Venture operating experience is a legitimate evidence source;
  evidence does not self-admit." FD-V2: venture operational records are
  "authoritative only within their delegated plane," never parallel
  institutional authority.
- **Evidence source:** `huerta-group/huerta-group-publishing` at `e28f12a`
  (branch `broadsheet-design-adoption`, identical to `main`), read as evidence
  under the Generation One program authorization. Paths below are relative to
  that repository. **No author-confidential material was read or used** —
  only governance, blueprint, operations, validation and globalization records.
- **Prohibitions honoured:** HGP operational doctrine is not copied into
  Huerta Group methodology; the Author Operating System is not treated as a
  generalized organizational-systems method. What is proposed is the
  *discipline* each source evidences, abstracted from its publishing objects.
- **Prepared:** 2026-08-25. **Parent admission status of every lesson:**
  **PROPOSED — pending a Founder Office admission act.** Until such an act, the
  Stage 2 draft's reliance on these lessons is reliance on *evidence*, not on
  admitted parent knowledge, and the draft says so.

status line "proposed, awaiting Founder Office approval" and its constitutions

## Lesson 1 — Blueprint before code; as-built deviations recorded with reasons

- **HGP source identifiers.** `docs/blueprints/print-production.md:3`,
  `docs/blueprints/cover-production.md:3`, `docs/blueprints/publication-metadata.md:3`
  ("Status: proposed, awaiting Founder Office approval. Blueprint only — no…");
  `docs/blueprints/milestone-2-book-memory-system.md:504` ("Blueprint →
  approval → amendments → vertical slices → acceptance + …");
  `docs/constitution/engineering-constitution.md:7` ("deviations require
  amending it first"), `:20` (§2 Vertical slices), `:118` ("If it mattered
  enough to decide, it matters enough to commit");
  `docs/design/broadsheet-implementation.md:99` ("## Deliberate deviations from
  the design source"); `docs/blueprints/production-bridge.md:670` ("Ships as
  vertical slices with a blueprint-governed…").
- **Source authority / status.** Venture-plane blueprints (status "proposed,
  awaiting Founder Office approval"), venture constitution (status "proposed,
  awaiting approval"), venture as-built record.
- **Exact lesson proposed for parent admission.** *Design is written and
  approved before construction; construction proceeds in bounded slices;
  deviations between the design and the as-built are recorded with a stated
  reason; both the design and the as-built are preserved.*
- **Why Stage 2 relies upon it.** The draft's advancement logic (A18:
  "Diagnosis does not advance to design merely because data collection is
  complete") and the Design/Construct stages of the proposed refinement rest
  on the same discipline; the dry-run method itself ("freeze the draft before
  testing; record corrections separately") is this lesson applied to a method.
- **Case-specific vs reusable.** *Reusable:* the discipline. *Case-specific:*
  the Phase 1 → Phase 2 → as-built → baseline program shape, the Founder
  Office as sole approver, the "(existing)/(new)" marking convention.
- **Limitations.** Single operator; some blueprint-vs-as-built deviations
  occurred silently within a phase (e.g., print embedding strategy); the
  blueprints' approval status lines were never updated after approval, so the
  record of *approval* lives in acts, not in the documents. Cross-case
  support exists in the corporate corpus (Gen One blueprint → ADRs →
  construction), so this lesson is not HGP-only.
- **Proposed parent knowledge classification.** Candidate *implementation
  guidance* (Methodology Foundation v1 §4.1); development status *working
  concept* (§5.1); disclosure *internal*; IP referral *not referred*.

## Lesson 2 — Deterministic records of truth; AI holds no authority over any record of truth or approval act

- **HGP source identifiers.** `docs/blueprints/production-bridge.md:99` ("Keep
  human judgment sovereign…"), `:103` ("…participates in any approval act"),
  `:394` ("AI holds no publication authority of any kind");
  `docs/operations/publication-candidates.md:116` ("AI holds no publication
  authority"); `docs/constitution/terminology.md:135–136` (Publication
  Artifact "A reproducible derivative"; Exported File "Files are derivable;
  the artifact identity is the fact"); `docs/blueprints/cover-production.md:195`
  ("one checksum, forever"); `docs/operations/deterministic-export.md:18`
  ("Existence means success — failed attempts never appear here");
  `docs/blueprints/manuscript-import.md:4` and `README.md:39` ("a
  deterministic, no-AI pipeline").
- **Source authority / status.** Venture blueprints and operations records;
  ratified venture terminology (venture plane).
- **Exact lesson proposed for parent admission.** *A record of truth is
  produced deterministically and verifiably, so that its identity is the fact
  and its files are derivable; AI may inform judgment but holds no authority
  over any record of truth or any approval act.*
- **Why Stage 2 relies upon it.** The draft's non-prescription rule against
  AI-generated diagnostic conclusions, the technology-worthiness question "Is
  technical representation likely to preserve institutional meaning?", and the
  evidence-class rule that inference never becomes fact.
- **Case-specific vs reusable.** *Reusable:* the principle. *Case-specific:*
  EPUB/print/cover determinism mechanics, fingerprints, serializer versioning.
- **Limitations.** Publishing-artifact context; however the corporate corpus
  carries the parallel doctrine (Vision Foundation §8; Founder Attention never
  derived; Material Change never produced by a trigger), so cross-case
  support exists.
- **Proposed parent knowledge classification.** Candidate *principle* (§4.1);
  development status *working concept*; disclosure *internal*; IP referral
  *not referred*.

## Lesson 3 — Gold standard before any run; controls; one variable; independent copies; human sign-off

- **HGP source identifiers.**
  `docs/globalization/spanish-editorial-pilot/pilot-design.md:3–4` ("authored
  BEFORE any review run… expected results are documented first and never
  tailored to model output");
  `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-control/control-design.md:3,6`
  ("authored BEFORE any review run"; "a structural mirror of the Spanish
  pilot, not a translation");
  `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-engineering/diagnosis.md:34` ("S6/S7
  recall was zero by construction");
  `docs/globalization/editorial-model-comparison/results.md:42` ("gpt-5.5
  found the seeded chronology contradiction in 8 of 8 runs; gpt-4o found it in
  0 of 8"); `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-engineering/results.md:6,135`
  (human sign-offs "remain separately required" / recorded pending).
- **Source authority / status.** Venture globalization program records
  (venture-plane experimental records).
- **Exact lesson proposed for parent admission.** *Validation of a
  judgment-producing system fixes its expected results and decision rules
  before any run; isolates the variable under test with a control; evaluates
  on independent copies; never amends the standard to accept non-detection;
  and reserves acceptance to a human sign-off.*
- **Why Stage 2 relies upon it.** The self-application dry-run method (freeze
  first; test against the frozen text; record without repairing; corrections
  afterward) is this discipline applied to a method; the evidence classes
  (observed / client-stated / inferred) implement "grading informs, it does not
  approve."
- **Case-specific vs reusable.** *Reusable:* the discipline. *Case-specific:*
  editorial-AI review, Spanish/English register, model identities, seeded
  manuscripts.
- **Limitations.** HGP-only; the single-run-unreliability finding is
  model-specific; the human sign-offs the program requires are recorded as
  pending in every July record, and no results document exists for the six-run
  hybrid matrix — the discipline is evidenced more completely than its
  closure.
- **Proposed parent knowledge classification.** Candidate *verification
  procedure* (§4.1); development status *working concept*; disclosure
  *internal*; IP referral *not referred*.

## Lesson 4 — Applied state is verified against the live system, never assumed from the repository (the HGP portion of "backup/restore validation")

- **HGP source identifiers.**
  `docs/operations/production-migration-baseline.md:57` ("Ledger state:
  `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations` is EMPTY"), `:71` ("`supabase db
  push` is the single sanctioned…"), `:163` ("Re-verified: 42/42 in exact local
  = remote agreement").
- **Source authority / status.** Venture operations record (operational
  baseline).
- **Exact lesson proposed for parent admission.** *The state a repository says
  is applied is verified against the live system; a single sanctioned
  application path is named; discrepancies are recorded and repaired forward,
  never assumed away.*
- **Correction to the Discovery Report.** The report classified "backup as a
  hypothesis until restored and re-tested for refusals" as supported across
  both cases (P40). The HGP evidence supports **live-state verification and
  ledger integrity**, not restore rehearsal; the tested-restore lesson is
  corporate-case evidence only (Production Readiness §7–8). The dry run and
  the Stage 2 report carry this correction; the admitted Discovery Report is
  not edited.
- **Why Stage 2 relies upon it.** The draft's systems-discovery rule "current
  software is not proof of actual workflow… where a tool is used outside its
  sanctioned path, record the path actually used" (A13) and the baseline
  procedure's "record the state of every corpus, system, or record set
  inspected" (A3).
- **Case-specific vs reusable.** *Reusable:* verify-against-live and the
  single-path rule. *Case-specific:* Supabase CLI mechanics, migration count.
- **Limitations.** One repository, one operator; cross-case support exists in
  the corporate corpus ("applied state is confirmed against the live project
  rather than assumed from the repository," Development Standards / Gen One
  completion report).
- **Proposed parent knowledge classification.** Candidate *verification
  procedure*; development status *working concept*; disclosure *internal*; IP
  referral *not referred*.

## Lesson 5 — A validation ledger slower than direct authorization is bypassed (finding, not principle)

- **HGP source identifiers.** `docs/validation/founder-validation-program.md`
  §1–§12 (the designed method: observation model §3, closed classifications
  §4, qualitative evidence strength §5, lifecycle §6, themes §7, authority and
  escalation §8 — including `:179` "Routine refinement — bounded implementation
  improvements within…" proceeding under standing implementation authority);
  `docs/validation/observation-register.md:17` (the single row, FVO-001-001,
  2026-08-11, "Implementation Defect", "Closed");
  `docs/validation/theme-register.md:17` ("No themes opened yet");
  `docs/validation/decision-history.md:27` ("No Validation Decisions recorded
  yet."); `docs/blueprints/capability-3-writing-workspace.md:277` ("Amendment
  8, August 2026 — Founder Validation Cycle 001…");
  `docs/constitution/terminology.md:206` (terminology ratified "from Founder
  Validation Cycle 001"); `docs/blueprints/edition-architecture.md:487`.
- **Source authority / status.** Venture operational standard (the program)
  and venture registers; venture-plane.
- **Exact lesson proposed for parent admission (as evidence).** *A validation
  ledger designed as the required path from observation to decision was
  bypassed in practice: the register holds one engineering-found observation,
  no theme and no decision, while the real corrections attributed to the same
  validation cycle reached the record through direct Founder authorization.
  A learning record that is slower than the principal's direct authorization
  will be bypassed unless the path of least resistance passes through it.*
- **Why Stage 2 relies upon it.** The draft's governance-excess test ("bypassed
  in practice by faster direct authorization" — A11), the friction question
  "Where does work routinely bypass intended controls?" (A14), and the Learn &
  Evolve failure mode in the proposed refinement.
- **Case-specific vs reusable.** *Reusable:* the finding as a named risk.
  *Case-specific:* the FVP's structure and identifiers; the fact that FVP §8
  itself licenses "routine refinement" outside the ledger, so the bypass is
  partly by design.
- **Limitations.** One cycle, fourteen days of record; the program was
  instituted 2026-08-11 and the bypassed corrections date from 2026-08-15/16 —
  a young ledger. Classified as *evidence* (Methodology Foundation §2.1 Layer
  5), not as a principle.
- **Proposed parent knowledge classification.** Candidate *evidence from use*
  (Layer 5); development status *working concept*; disclosure *internal*; IP
  referral *not referred*.
