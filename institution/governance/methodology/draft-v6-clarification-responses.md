## What the instrument fixed about each, before any of it was applied

Recorded because the constraints are part of the evidence's meaning.
`fd-0035` §2.3 states them:

- **FU-1** is *clarification of referent* — it distinguishes present
  institution-building from actual external/business operation. **"Do not
  infer that no organizational activity has occurred."**
- **FU-2** is recorded at its stated breadth. **"Do not normalize this into a
  precise methodology taxonomy unless independent evidence warrants that
  taxonomy."**
- **FU-3** clarifies preservation intent — durable preservation *"comparable
  to keeping something in a library"*, and *"for the record"* as **official
  institutional standing**. It **does not satisfy A17 rule 7**: custodian,
  storage location or mechanism, retention rule and the specific custody
  disposition all remain absent, and **the closure condition is preserved as
  unresolved**.
- **FU-4** supplies only what it explicitly supplies — that the Founder points
  to current methodology and governance work, that `.md` files are being
  created, and that the work concerns governance and accessibility within the
  system being created. **"Do not manufacture missing sequence steps from
  repository chronology. Do not convert nearby commits into
  participant-described actions. Do not treat repository facts as though the
  Founder personally supplied them in the walkthrough."**
- **FU-5** is Client-Stated evidence for A10's correspondence/source-location
  fact. **ChatGPT Projects are not authoritative merely because work is
  discussed there** — which is A9's own rule and A12's principle, applied
  independently of where the boundary places them. **Nothing is inferred about
  whether any particular conversation exists, is accessible, or was
  obtained.**
