| Requirement | How it is met |
| --- | --- |
| Does not imply corroboration | Its entire content is that one half is silent; A2 rule 5 independently forbids the move. The qualifier is that rule's reporting expression |

| Descriptive, not semantic | It states which half of an already-classified comparison holds evidence. Remove it and you lose information about the diagnosis, not about the subject |

## 9. Prior-familiarity and evaluator-independence limitation

**This was not blind, independent, external, or unfamiliar-subject validation,
and no part of this program may be read as any of those.**

Development previously read HGP records, supported its architecture and
implementation, evaluated its design and validation evidence, and used admitted
HGP evidence in earlier methodology work. For this subject the team is
author-of-records in part, prior evaluator, **and prior implementer**.

**How it was handled:** prior familiarity supported no finding. Every one of
the 35 evidence rows was obtained by reading the venture's records in this
session and carries its path. Where the team's memory held an answer the
obtained evidence did not, the row reads *not obtained* — the same rule that
produced nineteen such rows at Stage 3.

**Recorded execution deviation.** `fd-0023` §2.6 required the limitation to be
recorded *before* examining HGP. It was known from the outset and governed the
work throughout, but the **written record was produced after** the startup
inspection had already obtained the README, terminology canon, constitutions
and validation registers. The deviation is recorded rather than concealed; its
material consequence is assessed as none, because the limitation's content does
not depend on what was read. **The Founder Office may disagree with that
assessment**, which is why the fact is here.

**What it costs.** Any claim of the form "the method worked on an unfamiliar
subject" is unavailable. The available claim is narrower: *the method worked on
a subject whose records the team had seen before, when the team was required to
obtain everything again.* Draft v3 has no disclosure for this condition at all —
recorded as deficiency **D-8**, the most consequential of the eight.
