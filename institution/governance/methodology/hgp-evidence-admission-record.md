# HGP Evidence Admission Record — Provisional Evidentiary Methodology Support

**Classification: Institutional Record** — admission record (evidentiary,
non-governing). Records the Founder Office act admitting HGP-derived lessons
as **provisional evidentiary methodology support** under `fd-0020` §2.3,
through the FD-V1 §14 parent knowledge-admission boundary.

- **Admitting authority:** Founder Office (`fd-0020`, 2026-08-25).
- **Package admitted from:** `hgp-evidence-admission-package.md` (Stage 2;
  preserved unaltered — it proposed and did not self-admit).
- **Source corpus:** `huerta-group/huerta-group-publishing` at `e28f12a`
  (venture custody; read as evidence under program authorization; no
  author-confidential material used).
- **What admission is:** evidence admission. **What it is not:** doctrine
  adoption; HGP doctrine as Huerta Group doctrine; external validity;
  universal applicability; HGP product semantics as organizational
  methodology; Method Library admission.

## 1. The mechanism used

FD-V1 Charter §14: venture operating knowledge enters parent institutional
knowledge "only through governed admission — abstracted, reviewed, and
approved." Blueprint §11: "candidate → review → admission." Mechanically:
the Stage 2 package abstracted and proposed (candidate); the Founder Office
reviewed and admitted (`fd-0020`); this record preserves the admission with
its limits. The Method Library architecture reserves "abstract evidence
references" for exactly this class of client- or venture-derived learning;
no Method Library record is created or modified, and the admitted lessons are
citable by a methodology asset only as **evidence** at working-concept status
(Methodology Foundation v1 §5.1), never as authority.

## 2. Lessons admitted

Each entry preserves the package's source identity, source authority,
evidence basis, precise lesson, limitations, and case-specific vs reusable
distinction, and records the admitted status. Nothing is broadened beyond
what the cited evidence supports.

### Lesson 3 — Gold standard before any run; controls; one variable; independent copies; human sign-off

- **Source identity:**
  `docs/globalization/spanish-editorial-pilot/pilot-design.md:3–4`;
  `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-control/control-design.md:3,6`;
  `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-engineering/diagnosis.md:34`;
  `docs/globalization/editorial-model-comparison/results.md:42`;
  `docs/globalization/editorial-recall-engineering/results.md:6,135`.
- **Source authority / classification:** venture globalization program
  records (venture-plane experimental records).
- **Evidence basis:** "authored BEFORE any review run… never tailored to model
  output"; "a structural mirror… not a translation"; "S6/S7 recall was zero by
  construction"; "8 of 8 runs… 0 of 8"; human sign-offs "remain separately
  required."
- **Precise lesson admitted:** *Validation of a judgment-producing system
  fixes its expected results and decision rules before any run; isolates the
  variable under test with a control; evaluates on independent copies; never
  amends the standard to accept non-detection; and reserves acceptance to a
  human sign-off.*
- **Limitations:** HGP-only; the single-run-unreliability finding is
  model-specific; required sign-offs recorded as pending; no results document
  for the six-run hybrid matrix — the discipline is evidenced more completely
  than its closure.
- **Case-specific vs reusable:** reusable — the discipline; case-specific —
  editorial-AI review, registers, model identities, seeded manuscripts.
- **Admitted status:** provisional evidentiary methodology support; candidate
  *verification procedure*; working concept; internal; not referred. **No
  cross-case support** — HGP-only evidence.

### Lesson 4 — Applied state is verified against the live system, never assumed from the repository

- **Source identity:**
  `docs/operations/production-migration-baseline.md:57,71,163`.
- **Source authority / classification:** venture operations record
  (operational baseline).
- **Evidence basis:** "Ledger state: `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations`
  is EMPTY"; "`supabase db push` is the single sanctioned…"; "42/42 in exact

  The Discovery Report's P40 classification is corrected by this record, not
  by editing the Discovery Report.
- **Limitations:** one repository, one operator.
- **Case-specific vs reusable:** reusable — verify-against-live and the
  single-path rule; case-specific — Supabase CLI mechanics.
- **Admitted status:** provisional evidentiary methodology support; candidate
  *verification procedure*; working concept; internal; not referred.
  Cross-case support exists (Development Standards; Gen One completion

## 4. Effect

Draft v2 may cite Lessons 1–6 as **evidence** at working-concept status. No
lesson is doctrine, principle-in-force, standard, or Method Library record.
Lessons 3 and 5 carry no cross-case support and are marked HGP-only wherever
Draft v2 relies on them. The external-validation gap is unaffected: admitted
evidence from a related internal case is still internal evidence.
