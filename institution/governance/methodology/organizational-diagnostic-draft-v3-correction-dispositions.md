# Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One: Correction Dispositions for Draft v3

**Classification: Institutional Record** — disposition record (evidentiary,
non-governing). The individual disposition of the **ten Stage 3 correction
proposals** and of **three narrowly derived Stage 4 clarification
corrections**, authorized by `fd-0022` §2.5–§2.6, together with the Draft v3
integrity and version record (§4).

**Governing rule applied** (`fd-0022` §2.5): *"The Stage 3 report's
characterization of a correction as 'necessary before external validation' is
evidence/recommendation, not Founder adoption."* Four proposals were so
characterized. None was adopted on that ground; each was tested on its own
evidence, and two of the four were modified rather than accepted.

**Outcome: 5 ACCEPTED · 5 MODIFIED (one carrying a DEFERRED element, one
carrying a DECLINED element) · 0 DEFERRED in whole · 0 DECLINED in whole.**

## 2. The ten, individually

### PT-01 — One-sided-subject classification · **MODIFIED**, one element **DEFERRED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** Sixteen of seventy-two comparisons classified
  *Unresolved*; at least five of them (`C-45`, `C-55`, `C-59`, `C-63`, `C-64`)
  are not unresolved at all — the relationship is determinate and the two
  halves are about different subjects. The comparison record says so against
  each of them.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, narrowed.** The Q14 clarification supplies
  a function inventory that partly reaches `C-35`, but `C-45`, `C-55`, `C-59`,
  `C-63` and `C-64` are untouched by any clarification.
- **Disposition.** **MODIFIED.** The Founder Office's own scrutiny reframes the
  proposal: the question is whether *the evidence model* represents a
  one-sided matter "without treating that evidence as corroborated merely
  because no opposing account exists." That is a better statement of the defect
  than the sub-classification the Stage 3 report proposed, and it is what
  Draft v3 implements.
- **Reason.** The evidentiary error is the dangerous half: one-sided evidence
  silently reading as corroborated. The classification-vocabulary half is a
  presentational improvement, and the seven classifications are fixed by
  determination (`fd-0020` §2.12, restated at `fd-0022` §2.4). Development
  does not amend a determined vocabulary through a draft.
- **Affected Draft section.** A2 (evidence classification).
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** **A2 rule 5** added: *"Unopposed is not
  corroborated. Evidence available from only one participant, source, or side
  does not become corroborated because no contrary account exists or could be
  obtained… Absence of disagreement is recorded as the absence of a second
  source, never as agreement."* Instrument 1 gains the field **opposing
  account (sought / not sought / sought and unavailable, with the reason)**,
  filled for every row a finding relies on.
- **DEFERRED element.** Whether the comparison procedure gains
  sub-classifications *not addressed by the account* / *not addressed by the
  records* — for the Founder Office, since it alone can extend the
  classification set.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether an evidence-model rule is enough without
  the classification, or whether comparison outputs will keep misreporting
  one-sided subjects as unresolved. One application is thin evidence.

### PT-02 — Within-account contradiction · **ACCEPTED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** Five conflicts inside one account (evidence set §6,
  pairs A–E), four of which had to be recorded inside comparison blocks whose
  vocabulary is defined for account-versus-records.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes.** Pair B (Q19 against Q18) is resolved by
  the Q18 clarification and preserved as having existed; pair D is narrowed;
  **A, C and E stand unresolved.**
- **Disposition.** **ACCEPTED.**
- **Reason.** The method could hold two conflicting statements and had nowhere
  to record the conflict as a finding or to say what it does to the weight of
  either. In this application those conflicts were among the most
  diagnostically significant material produced. With multiple participants they
  become routine.
- **Affected Draft section.** A17 rule 3.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1 (conflict cross-references, unchanged
  in form).
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A17 rule 3 extended: contradiction within a
  single account is preserved, both statements verbatim and cross-referenced,
  recorded as a finding against the account — *"never merged, never reconciled
  by asking the participant to choose, and never resolved by the team
  preferring the statement that fits its other evidence"* — and any
  different-objects reading is marked *Inferred* and attributed to the team,
  with what would settle it.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether within-account and between-account
  contradiction want the same treatment. Only the first has been tested.

### PT-03 — Stakeholder-author-of-records disclosure · **ACCEPTED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** Six of twenty-three corroborations are one source
  agreeing with itself (`C-03`, `C-13`, `C-49`, `C-51`, `C-57`, part of
  `C-17`). Predicted as an unexercised assumption at the Stage 3 pre-response
  report §33.3; realized in use.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, and reinforced** — the new `C-46-R2`
  corroboration is itself [same-source], since the routing model is
  corroborated by governing text the same authority approved.
- **Disposition.** **ACCEPTED.**
- **Reason.** Draft v2 discloses when the *team* authored the records it
  judges and has no equivalent for a *participant* who did. Without the
  disclosure, a reader counts corroborations without seeing that a quarter of
  them are consistency rather than independence.
- **Affected Draft section.** A18; A2 rule 5.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A18: each such comparison is marked **"account
  and record share a source,"** and the outcome states how many corroborations
  carry the mark. A2 rule 5 closes with the same rule. Instrument 1 gains
  **shares a source with**. Corroboration is **labelled, not downgraded**.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** How far "same source" reaches — the same person,
  the same office, or the same approval chain. Draft v3 says "the same party"
  and does not resolve the boundary.

### PT-04 — Limb-level capture · **ACCEPTED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** Nineteen unanswered limbs across fourteen of thirty
  questions; three answers addressing an adjacent question (Q14, Q16, Q21).
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, reinforced.** Six limbs remain unsupplied
  after the clarification round, and the Q14 clarification answers the *scope*
  limb while leaving `S-N07` — what each area does — still open.
- **Disposition.** **ACCEPTED.**
- **Reason.** Recording a multi-limb question as "answered" overstates the
  evidence position, always in the flattering direction; and an answer landing
  beside the question was recorded as evidence with no marker. Both are cheap
  to fix and change what a diagnosis can honestly claim to have asked.
- **Affected Draft section.** A3 step 8.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A3 step 8: *"Each question is decomposed into
  its limbs before it is put,"* every limb recorded *answered* or *not
  supplied*, and a response addressing a different question recorded as such.
  Instrument 1: each limb is its own row, and **"response does not address the
  question asked"** is a row type recording what was asked, what was answered,
  and whether a follow-up was taken.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether limb enumeration makes questions feel like
  a questionnaire in delivery — which the global instrument rules forbid the
  method becoming. Draft v3 enumerates limbs in the *record*, not in the asking.

### PT-05 — Follow-up trigger for under-determined answers · **ACCEPTED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** Three of the most diagnostically loaded answers stayed
  ambiguous for want of one question: "Process." (Q3), "All of them." (Q29),
  "Founder Office conversation." (Q18) — the last of which alone carried a
  contradiction and a refusal ground.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes — Stage 4 is the proposal's own evidence.**
  A single clarification round reclassified `C-46` from Contradiction to
  Difference in terminology, discharged one of two grounds for the
  source-of-truth failure, and clarified three further answers, at the cost of
  one exchange.
- **Disposition.** **ACCEPTED.**
- **Reason.** The strongest evidence any of the ten proposals has. The method
  recorded under-determined answers faithfully and had no rule about what
  happens next; the consequence was a preserved contradiction that a single
  question dissolved.
- **Affected Draft section.** A3 step 8.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A3 step 8 states three triggers — fewer terms
  than the concept set asked about; a totality that can be neither verified nor
  falsified; a reference the team would have to guess at — and requires a
  **clarification-only** follow-up before the answer supports a finding: *"It
  asks the participant to say more about their own words; it never offers
  candidate words, structures, or answers; it is recorded as a follow-up with
  its own date; and the original answer, together with the fact that it was
  under-determined, is preserved beside it unchanged."* Where no follow-up is
  available, findings carry **"under-determined answer; no follow-up
  available."** Instrument 1 records a clarification as a new dated row that
  never replaces the original.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** The anti-leading boundary. A clarification that
  offers candidate words leads; one that merely asks for more may not clarify.
  The stakeholder procedure had to rewrite four of thirty questions for leading
  language, so this is a live risk. Stage 4's own round was supplied by the
  participant unprompted, which is the easy case.

### PT-06 — Establish the organization's boundary · **MODIFIED** (placement)

- **Stage 3 evidence.** `C-60` — a preserved contradiction (Q24 "None" against
  five recorded forms of externally-held knowledge and the account's own Q5 and
  Q14) that may be a **boundary disagreement rather than a factual one**, and
  the method gave no way to tell. Also `C-11` and `C-62`.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, untouched.** No clarification addresses
  where the organization ends; pair A remains unresolved.
- **Disposition.** **MODIFIED** — accepted in substance, placed at **A3**
  rather than A4 as the proposal suggested.
- **Reason.** The boundary was needed *before* Q24 was asked, which makes it
  part of the evidentiary baseline rather than of identity discovery. The
  Stage 3 proposal itself recorded the placement as uncertain and argued for
  A3.
- **Affected Draft section.** A3 step 11 and the A3 evidence-requested list;
  A12's question.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 6.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A3 step 11 requires the organization's own
  statement of what it counts as itself — people, units, tools, environments,
  vendors, services — as Client-Stated, with the team's differing working
  boundary recorded; where unresolved, *"no question depending on it… is
  answered as though it were settled."* A12's "outside" question is qualified
  accordingly, and Instrument 6 records the holding and the unresolved boundary
  rather than a judgement about inside or outside.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether asking an organization to state its own
  boundary at the baseline stage is answerable early, before the diagnostic has
  shown it what its edges look like.

### PT-07 — Habit-held responsibility by observation · **ACCEPTED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** `C-38` — the one capability `fd-0020` §2.11 required
  to be tested that produced **no finding**. Asked at Q15, answered by
  generalization; records cannot show habit; three matters proved *unowned*,
  which is a different finding.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, untouched.** The Q14 clarification names
  areas of work and still does not distinguish habit from role; the Q29
  clarification confirms the concentration that makes them indistinguishable.
- **Disposition.** **ACCEPTED.**
- **Reason.** Instrument 3 detects habit by comparing observed responsibility
  with formal assignment. Where one actor holds every assignment the comparison
  has nothing to bite on, and the account cannot supply the distinction because
  from inside it there is none. Reporting "no habit-held work found" would have
  been false.
- **Affected Draft section.** A7; A20.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 3.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A7: *"Habit is established by observation…
  Where only accounts are available, the finding is recorded as 'habit-held
  responsibility: capability not exercised — observation unavailable,' never as
  'no habit-held work found.'"* Where every assignment resolves to one actor,
  the distinction *"may be unobtainable in principle"* and that is recorded as
  the reason. A20 carries the limit; Instrument 3 carries the rule.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether observation of work as performed is
  available in a real engagement at all. A10's observation preference has never
  been exercised in this program, which Draft v3's A20 now states.

### PT-08 — Two-pass access statement · **MODIFIED** (reframed)

- **Stage 3 evidence.** The access statement gained **three** rows at access =
  none purely because the participant named holdings the team did not know to
  look for (`C-11`, `C-46`, `C-08`); two of the three carry material findings
  and together they are a principal ground of the refusal to advance.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, untouched.** The Q18 clarification
  characterizes one of those holdings better and grants no access to it.
- **Disposition.** **MODIFIED.** The Founder Office's scrutiny frames the two
  passes differently and better: *what is stated to exist* against *what is
  actually obtained or verified*. That is adopted in place of the Stage 3
  framing (team's inventory, then re-derivation), which is kept as the
  mechanism inside it.
- **Reason.** The Founder Office's framing covers a condition the Stage 3
  version missed entirely: a participant states that evidence exists and it
  never arrives. In an engagement that is more common than a holding the team
  failed to imagine, and the same field carries both.
- **Affected Draft section.** A3 step 10; A18.
- **Affected instrument.** every instrument header (the access statement is
  carried into all of them).
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A3 step 10 rewritten: record **(a) stated to
  exist or identified** — by whom, when, on what basis — and **(b) access
  actually obtained** — full / partial / none, how verified, what "partial"
  excluded — *"separately and never merged."* The statement is built from the
  team's inventory and **re-derived after every stakeholder session**, adding
  what the account names with the question it came from; *"the statement in
  force is the re-derived one,"* and A18 evaluates that one.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** None about the need. Untested: how a client reacts
  to being told access is worse after talking than before.

### PT-09 — Judgement versus assertion · **MODIFIED**, taxonomy **DECLINED**

- **Stage 3 evidence.** `C-56` (Q22 "I feel like everything is pretty
  governed," compared against ten recorded governance gaps); `C-69` (an
  expectation); `C-25`; `C-01` ("I'd say…").
- **Survives clarification?** **Partly.** The Q3 clarification shows the risk
  from the other side: "Process." was ordinary usage, neither an assertion
  about the organization nor a perception of it, and a three-value taxonomy
  would have had to force it into one.
- **Disposition.** **MODIFIED** — the mechanic is adopted, the **taxonomy is
  DECLINED**.
- **Reason.** The Stage 3 proposal asked the team to categorize a participant's
  statement as *asserted condition* / *judgement* / *expectation*. That
  requires the team to classify a speaker's state of mind, which is the kind of
  interpretation the method forbids everywhere else, and the proposal itself
  flagged the risk that marking a statement a "perception" reads as dismissal.
  Quoting the speaker's own qualifier achieves the purpose and assigns nothing.
- **Affected Draft section.** none — the change is at the instrument.
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 1.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** Instrument 1 gains **stated force, in the
  speaker's own words** — *"the qualifier the speaker used, quoted, or
  'none'"* — with the rule that *"the team assigns no category of its own to
  the speaker's state of mind."* No three-value taxonomy exists in Draft v3.
- **Remaining uncertainty.** Whether a quoted qualifier is enough for a
  downstream reader who has not read the transcript, or whether the comparison
  will keep doing work the capture should have marked.

### PT-10 — Source-of-resort field · **MODIFIED** (widened to source roles)

- **Stage 3 evidence.** `C-46` and comparison §7 — the first time in the
  program that *a source exists*, *it is trusted*, and *it is treated as
  authoritative* pointed at different places. In the dry run they collapsed
  because the team held all three.
- **Survives clarification?** **Yes, and the Q18 clarification widens it.** The
  clarified account distinguishes four things the proposal collapsed into one:
  where a matter begins, where it is clarified, where it is routed from, and
  where durable authority resides.
- **Disposition.** **MODIFIED** — widened from one field to a set of roles, as
  `fd-0022` §2.6 requires.
- **Reason.** "Source of resort" alone would have recorded the conversation as
  a place people go and would still not have shown *why* — that it is where
  matters are worked through and routed, not where truth is kept. The Draft v2
  test required by `fd-0022` §2.6 found that neither A9's three facts nor
  Instrument 4's fields allow those locations to differ; this is the
  disposition through which that deficiency is treated.
- **Affected Draft section.** A9 (source roles; source of resort); A6
  (distinctions extended).
- **Affected instrument.** Instrument 4.
- **Exact Draft v3 treatment.** A9 gains **source roles, recorded
  separately** — where a matter is first worked through · where it is clarified
  · where it is routed from · who decides it · where the fact is established ·
  where the durable record resides — each with its own evidence class and
  "none evidenced" permitted, under the rule that *"none of the first three
  roles makes a source authoritative."* A9 also gains **source of resort**, per
  participant, Client-Stated, recorded beside the authoritative source and
  never as it, with divergence recorded under conflicts. A6's distinction chain
  extends to eight terms and states that sensemaking, clarification and routing
  *"confer no authority whatever, however consistently or exclusively they are
  used."* Instrument 4 carries both as fields.
- **Neutrality.** Nothing about any particular office, room, or environment
  enters the method. What is generalized is only the **distinction**
  (`fd-0022` §2.6 critical rule).
- **Remaining uncertainty.** With several participants there will be several
  sources of resort; whether that is one row with N values or N rows is
  untested. Whether six roles is the right decomposition, or too fine for
  organizations that keep them together, is also untested.

## 5. What was deliberately not changed

- **No contradiction was removed from any record.** The eight remaining Stage 3
  contradictions stand; nothing in Draft v3 exists to make them disappear, and
  two dispositions (PT-01, PT-02) exist precisely because contradictions and
  asymmetries could not be represented cleanly.
- **No classification vocabulary was amended.** The seven comparison
  classifications are as determined.
- **No advancement rule was relaxed.** Nothing in Draft v3 would have let the
  Stage 3 diagnosis proceed to design; A18's constraints are unchanged apart
  from disclosures that make refusal *more* likely, not less.
- **No new instrument, register, numbering system, or software.** Every change
  is a rule, a field, or a step inside instruments that already existed.
- **No Huerta mechanism entered the method.** No office, room, environment,
  tool, or governance structure of this organization appears in Draft v3 except
  in A1's inherited non-assumption list, whose function is to forbid assuming
  them.
- **Nothing was changed because it would be tidier.** Each change traces to a
  recorded failure in use.
