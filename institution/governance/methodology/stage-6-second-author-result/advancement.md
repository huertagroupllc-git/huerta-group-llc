## 3. The minimum-evidence test before design

A18: *"Diagnosis does not advance to design merely because data collection is

*Evidence:* `evidence-register.md` in full.

### Element 2 — Organizational purpose and outputs are sufficiently understood

**NOT MET. Class: Client-Stated as to purpose; Inferred and unverified as to
outputs.**
Purpose is stated consistently across four independent surfaces (F-04) and is
**Client-Stated** — a document proves the document asserts it (A2 rule 4), and

author-confidential material was not sought (NA-02, NA-04). The corpus's own
figure — one book, one author — is itself Client-Stated (EV-011).
"Sufficiently understood" cannot be claimed for an organization whose outputs the
diagnosis never saw.

### Element 3 — Authority/decision-rights ambiguity relevant to the problem is visible

**MET, in the narrow sense the element asks. Class: Observed.**

whether it is **resolved**. It is visible. It is not resolved, and resolving it

## 4. Outcome

A18 permits five outcomes: *Proceed to Establish Meaning & Authority · Request
additional evidence · Return unresolved questions to client authority · Narrow
the engagement problem · Conclude that no material intervention is presently
warranted.*

**A records-only diagnosis may produce only the middle three.** *Proceed* and
*conclude no material intervention is warranted* are both unavailable to this
diagnosis by rule, and would be unavailable on the evidence in any case:
elements 2, 5 and 8 are not met, and the access statement records "none" for
systems and persons that material findings depend on.

### **DETERMINATION: REQUEST ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE.**

That is the outcome, stated as one of the outcomes the method permits.

**What is requested, in priority order, each tied to the element it would
repair:**

1. **Stakeholder accounts** — the whole of `stakeholder-questions.md`. Repairs
   elements 2, 4 (fact (b)), 5, and materially improves 8. Without it the
   diagnosis is permanently records-only and can never advance, at any level of
   documentary thoroughness.
2. **Observation of work as performed** (A10). Repairs element 5, and is the one
   limb of this method that has never been exercised in any application (A20).
3. **The Founder Office instruments** (AS-04) — the determinations, directives,
   and authorizations cited across 36 files and held nowhere. Repairs elements 3 and 4
   and answers RQ-02, RQ-03, RQ-05.
4. **Production access, or a substitute admissible under the organization's own
   §1 standard** (AS-02, AS-03). Repairs element 8 and converts a large body of
   Client-Stated conditions into Observed ones.

**Also carried, and not instead of the above:** the nine reserved questions
stand returned to the organization's authority as Instrument 10, which is a
minimum output rather than an alternative outcome. Narrowing the engagement
problem is available and I do not recommend it, because the narrowing that the
evidence would support — confining the diagnosis to the repository — is what
already happened, and it produced this determination.

### What this outcome does not do

It does not authorize anything. It does not find that the organization is well or
badly governed. It does not find that authority is concentrated or distributed.
It does not find that the platform works or does not work. Each of those would
require evidence this diagnosis did not have, and A19's rules against inferring
missing organizational facts *"merely to make the diagnostic complete"* apply
with full force to all of them.
