## 2. The minimum question set

These are **my own** questions, derived from the requirements above. They are
stated in the subject's own terminology wherever I have learned it. Each is
decomposed into its limbs **before it is put**, as A3 step 8 requires, so that
each limb can be recorded *answered* or *not supplied*.

Rules I have applied to my own wording, from A3 step 8: a clarification-only
follow-up "asks the participant to say more about their own words; it never
offers candidate words, structures, or answers." I have therefore avoided
offering candidate answers inside the questions, including where I have a
strong expectation about the answer.

**I do not seek answers to these, and I supply none.**

---

### SQ-01 — Authority: who holds what
*Serves: A6; RQ-01; Instrument 2 (observed authority, 14 of 18 rows).*
**Recipient: whoever the organization says may speak for authority.**

The records name the Founder Office, the imprint, standing implementation
authority (also called Publishing Development authority), the author, staff, the
publisher, and a designation written "(Opus 4.8)".

- **L1.** For each of those seven names, is it a person, an office, a body, or
  something else?
- **L2.** Which of the seven, if any, are the same as each other?
- **L3.** For each, who currently occupies it?
- **L4.** Where is that recorded, if anywhere?
- **L5.** Has any of them ever been occupied by someone else?

### SQ-03 — The Broadsheet status line
*Serves: A17 rule 3 (a preserved contradiction); RQ-06; CX-04; F-12.*

The as-built record at commit `e28f12a` reads: *"work packages 1–6 complete on
the `broadsheet-design-adoption` branch, unpushed. Pre-production only —
production replacement is not authorized and has not been performed."* At that
same commit, this working copy's `refs/remotes/origin/main` points at `e28f12a`.

- **L1.** In your own words, what is the current state of that work?
- **L2.** What does "unpushed" refer to in that sentence?
- **L3.** What does "production replacement" refer to?
- **L4.** Was there an authorization for the work to reach `main`? If so, where

### SQ-08 — Drift, and the guards against it
*Serves: A14; A15 (2) and (6); RQ-08; Instrument 9.*

- **L2.** Has anything else been considered for the same treatment?
- **L3.** Has anything ever gone wrong because a record was out of date? What

  rather than being checked mechanically — and why?

judgement and is recorded as undetermined without it.)*

### SQ-09 — The permanent record's durability
*Serves: A12; F-29; CX-08.*

The Product Constitution states that losing work must be structurally

years without the codebase.
