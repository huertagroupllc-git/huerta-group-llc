non-governing). The individual adjudication of the complete standing
methodology-deficiency corpus — **D-1…D-8 and MO-01…MO-22, exactly thirty
records** — authorized by `fd-0025`.

- **No Draft v4 exists**, and none is created here. **No frozen draft was
  modified.** A disposition recorded here changes nothing in the method: it

## 3. Sequence 1 — method ambiguities analysed first

These affect how other records are read, so they are settled or returned before
any disposition below depends on them.

### AMB-1 · Is A10's trail-first procedure satisfiable from artifacts alone?

A10: work is discovered from *"the artifacts produced, the records and
timestamps they left, the correspondence around them, **and** a stakeholder
walking the team through the last actual instance."* The list is conjunctive.
The first author completed a trail from artifacts and answered element 5
*partially*; the second author read the walkthrough as required and answered
*not met*.

**Resolved? No — returned.** Nothing elsewhere in the frozen method settles it,
and it changes an element answer and an instrument's completeness. **Founder
Office / Methodology room determination required.** It bears on MO-18 and on the
reading of D-3 and MO-22.

### AMB-2 · Does "identified" satisfy A18 element 4 where the boundaries are unresolved as to authority?

Element 4 asks whether *"key source-of-truth boundaries are identified."* A9
separates three facts, the third being *treated as authoritative by what act*.
The first author answered *yes*; the second *partial*, because no establishing
act exists for several domains and fact (b) is unobtainable without accounts.

**Resolved? Partially, and by the method's own text.** A9's three-facts rule
already distinguishes *identified* from *established*, and A18's element uses
the weaker word. **Development's reading: both answers are faithful, and the
element's wording is the defect.** Returned as a wording determination and
carried into the change set with MO-08 and MO-02 as an A18 internal-consistency
cluster.

### AMB-3 · Evidence state versus comparison classification

**Already answered by the method and preserved.** A2 classifies *evidence
items*; the seven comparison classifications (fixed by `fd-0020` §2.12)
classify *the relationship between two evidence halves*. They are different
layers. This is cited, not changed, and it is the ground on which §4's
one-sided-evidence determination rests.

### AMB-4 · Representable versus validated behaviour

**Already answered and preserved** — `fd-0023` §2.3 requires the distinction,
and Stage 5 and Stage 6 both applied it to contested authority. No change.

### AMB-5 · Diagnostic closure versus stage completion

**Not answered by the method.** Stage 5's report had to invent the distinction
between a complete *validation* and an incomplete *diagnosis*, and the Founder
Office accepted it at `fd-0024` §2.1 — as a determination about that stage, not
as method doctrine. MO-16 is the corpus record covering it. **Returned as part
of the closure determination** (§4, MO-16; change set CS-12).

### AMB-6 · Evidence sufficiency versus instrument completion

**Not answered by the method.** `fd-0025` §2.10 states the distinction —
*"Instrument population is not evidence sufficiency"* — and asks for a
mechanism. **Returned with a proposed mechanism** (change set CS-17); no corpus
record covers it, and it is sourced to the instrument rather than to a
deficiency.

### AMB-7 · Author behaviour versus method defect

**Answered by the instrument, not by the method** (`fd-0025` §2.11). Applied
throughout §4 and summarized at §9. It is not itself proposed as a method
change: a method that adjudicates its own users' mistakes would exceed what a
diagnostic is for.

### AMB-8 · Access limitations versus substantive diagnosis

**Answered by the method** — A18's access constraint bars *proceeding* on a
finding that depends on a corpus, system or person recorded at access = none.
Both applications applied it. Cited, preserved, unchanged. MO-04 concerns the
*vocabulary* of access, not this rule.

### AMB-9 · Descriptive reporting qualifiers versus governed evidence classifications

**Answered and preserved.** The seven comparison classifications are fixed by
determination; a descriptive qualifier that adds none and redefines none is
reporting composition. `fd-0024` §2.7 left the single-source qualifier available
and not mandatory. §4's determination on one-sided evidence rests on this.

### Sequence 2 — D-8 and independence / authorship

**D-8 · The method has no disclosure for a team that built the subject**

- **Source:** Stage 5, first author, `stage-5-method-deficiencies-and-proposed-corrections.md`.
- **Evidence that produced it:** `fd-0023` §2.6 had to *demand* the
  prior-familiarity limitation because Draft v3 supplies no place for it;
  Draft v3 discloses *author-of-records* and *account and record share a source*
  and nothing for a team that designed, implemented, advised or evaluated the
  subject.
- **Reproduced?** **Partially** — MO-03 reaches the same underspecified label
  from the other direction (a team whose composition changed).
- **Contradicted?** No. **Weakened by later clarification?** No.
  **Strengthened?** **Yes, twice:** the Founder Office determined it a
  continuing evidence qualification (`fd-0024` §2.4), and Stage 6 demonstrated
  that a second author, reading A18 alone, had to *decide for itself* what "the
  diagnostic team" denotes.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18 ("how the judgement is recorded").
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** the deficiency is supported by both authors and by an instrument
  that had to supply externally what the method lacks. The remedy stands
  substantially as proposed — a third disclosure beside the existing two.
- **Relationship:** paired with MO-03 in change-set item **CS-03**; neither
  subsumes the other (§7).
- **Remaining uncertainty:** how far "prior involvement" reaches — advice, a
  single review, or construction. The change set records this as a determination
  required before drafting.

**MO-03 · "Author-of-records" has no rule for a diagnostic team that changes composition**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author, sealed result.
- **Evidence:** *"I authored none of the subject's records. The diagnosing
  institution's principal authored 202 of 203 of them."* It decided the
  condition holds **institutionally** and stated both halves — and recorded that
  *"a different author could defensibly have decided the opposite and dropped
  the label."*
- **Reproduced?** **Partially** — D-8 is the same label from the other side.
- **Strengthened?** Yes — it is a named reproducibility divergence source with
  nothing to do with the subject.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18.
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.**
- **Reason:** the problem is supported; the remedy should not be a separate rule
  about team composition. Both this and D-8 are symptoms of one gap — **A18
  never defines what "the diagnostic team" denotes.** Modify to a single A18
  disclosure block that defines the term (institution or individual) and adds
  prior involvement, rather than two disclosures answering two halves of one
  question.
- **Relationship:** CS-03 with D-8.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether "institutionally" is the right default. The
  second author's reasoning — the mark warns of a structural dependency, not of
  who typed — is persuasive and is not adopted here as doctrine.

### Sequence 3 — evidence access and replication design

**D-2 · "Records-only" names the wrong absence**

- **Source:** Stage 5, first author.
- **Evidence:** both applications were labelled *records-only* for want of an
  account, while the more consequential absence in each was the operating
  system the subject itself calls its permanent record.
- **Reproduced?** **First-author only** as a deficiency — though the second
  author carried the label correctly and independently recorded that production
  access was none and that this was the governing limitation (its F-01).
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18; A3 step 8.
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.**
- **Reason:** the underlying problem — one absence labelled, another equally
  constraining absence unlabelled — is supported by both applications. The
  proposed remedy (a second label, *artifacts-only*) is **rejected**: it invites
  a third and a fourth, and multiplying labels is how a vocabulary stops being
  read. Modify: **A18's outcome states which categories of evidence were absent**
  — accounts, observation of work as performed, the organization's operating
  systems — each with the constraint it triggers. One mechanism, extensible,
  no new labels.
- **Relationship:** CS-04 with MO-04, which supplies the vocabulary this needs.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether three categories are the right set. Two
  applications is thin evidence for a taxonomy.

**MO-04 · The method conflates "we could not obtain it" with "we were forbidden to obtain it"**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** A3 step 10's *full / partial / none* and A17 rule 2's *not
  supplied / not accessible / not known to exist* — five values, none of which
  distinguishes evidence the diagnostic **was instructed not to touch**. *"The
  fourth case was the dominant condition of this entire application."*
- **Reproduced?** **Functionally equivalent issue independently observed.** The
  first author's Stage 5 §11 had already invented a four-way prose distinction —
  *known to exist, not obtained* / *deliberately not sought — boundary* /
  *deliberately not sought — instruction* / *no record located*. **Two authors
  independently built the same missing vocabulary in prose.**
- **Strengthened?** Yes, by that convergence — and it is not a leaked-identifier
  convergence.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A3 step 10; A17 rule 2.
- **Type:** EVIDENCE RULES.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** the strongest evidence in the corpus for any single record. An
  imposed scope boundary says something entirely different about a diagnosis
  than an access failure, and flattening them misstates the diagnosis's own
  position.
- **Relationship:** CS-04 with D-2.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** none material on the need. The precise value set is
  a drafting question.

**MO-05 · A3 step 9's citation check assumes the diagnostic's access is coextensive with the organization**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** step 9 requires each cited authority to be *"checked for being
  held by the organization"*; a diagnostic can only check whether the
  **authorized corpus** holds it. Eight-plus instruments cited and absent; a
  corpus at a named path it was forbidden to open.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only.** The first author recorded the same
  general condition and did not notice the wording.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A3 step 9; Instrument 1's "cited, not held" row type.
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** a precise textual defect with a one-line fix. As written the
  method has an application assert something about the organization it did not
  establish — which A17 rule 4 forbids everywhere else.
- **Relationship:** CS-05, the A3 cluster.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** none.

**MO-06 · A3 step 10 is silent on zero sessions**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author. **This lands on a change the first author
  introduced at Stage 4 (PT-08).**
- **Evidence:** the two-pass statement is *"re-derived after every stakeholder
  session"* and *"the statement in force is the re-derived one."* With zero
  sessions there is no statement in force.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only.** The first author wrote the rule and
  did not notice the hole; in Stage 5 it silently carried its own inventory.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A3 step 10.
- **Type:** WORKFLOW.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** a hole in text the adjudicating author wrote, found by the first
  outside reader it met. One sentence closes it.
- **Relationship:** CS-05.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** none.

**MO-21 · No stage verifies that the diagnostic is looking at the state it was told to look at**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** A3 step 1 requires *recording* the state of every corpus
  inspected; nothing requires *verifying* it against the baseline the engagement
  specified. The second author verified anyway, at open and at close, *"because
  getting it wrong would have invalidated everything."*
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only** as a deficiency. The first author also
  verified the baseline in Stage 5 and likewise treated it as obvious rather
  than as a method requirement — which is corroboration of the *behaviour* and
  of the gap.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A3 step 1.
- **Type:** WORKFLOW.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** both authors did it and neither was told to. A step every
  application performs and no stage owns is a gap, and the cost of omitting it
  is total.
- **Relationship:** CS-05.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether this belongs to the diagnostic or to
  engagement setup. Kept in the method: any engagement naming a state needs it,
  not only a replication.

### Sequence 4 — advancement, closure, evidentiary sufficiency

**MO-16 · The method has no way to record a defect in the diagnosis's own closure**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** A17 rule 7 requires the custody disposition *"agreed with the
  organization before the diagnosis closes."* No party was available. *"The
  diagnosis closed anyway, with custody unagreed. There is no field for this."*
  It recorded the defect in three places rather than burying it in one.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only** — and it applies to the first author's
  Stage 5, which closed unagreed **without saying so**. The Founder Office has
  determined that a method-application defect (`fd-0025` §2.9).
- **Strengthened?** Yes — by being true of both applications, and noticed by
  only one.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A17 rule 7; A18.
- **Type:** CLOSURE.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** a mandatory condition with no failure mode is not a condition. The
  method requires something, provides no way to record that it was not done, and
  the predictable result occurred twice — once undetected.
- **Relationship:** CS-12; interacts with AMB-5.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether a diagnosis may close at all with A17 rule
  7 unsatisfied. That is a determination, not Development's to make; the change
  set states it as such.

**MO-08 · One of the five "possible outcomes" is also a mandatory minimum output**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** *Return unresolved questions to client authority* is one of five
  A18 outcomes; *Questions Reserved to Client Authority* is a minimum output
  every diagnosis produces. *"Choosing it as the outcome would say nothing; not
  choosing it does not mean the questions are not returned."* And: *"For a
  reproducibility test, this is a direct source of divergence between authors
  that has nothing to do with the subject."*
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only** — but the divergence it predicts
  **occurred**: the first author's Stage 5 stated the outcome as *request
  additional evidence, **and** return unresolved questions*; the second stated
  *request additional evidence* with Instrument 10 as a standing output. Two
  faithful readings, one text.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18 (outcomes and minimum outputs).
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** an internal inconsistency in the frozen text, demonstrated to
  produce author divergence, and cheap to settle. It is not a coverage gap; it
  is the method contradicting itself.
- **Relationship:** CS-07 with MO-02 and MO-15 — the A18 internal-consistency
  cluster; interacts with AMB-2.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether the outcome is removed or the output
  renamed. A drafting choice, but the change set records that one of the two
  must happen.

**MO-02 · "Identity-of-record statement" is a required minimum output that the method never defines**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** *"The phrase appears exactly once in the entire method."* Not
  defined in A0–A17, A19–A20, any instrument, or Parts C or D. No stage produces
  it. The second author read it against A9's one-matter rule, produced statements
  in that shape, and recorded that **if a different thing is meant the output is
  explicitly absent** — which is the honest handling of an undefined
  requirement.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only.** The first author produced Stage 5's
  minimum outputs without noticing that one of them is undefined — which is
  itself evidence of the defect: an author can fail a required output without
  knowing the output exists.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18 minimum outputs.
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** *"an application can be told it failed to produce a required
  output it had no way to identify."* That is not a gap in coverage; it is an
  unusable requirement.
- **Relationship:** CS-07.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** **what the output is.** Development does not invent
  it. The change set records the exact determination required: define it and
  name its producing stage and instrument, or remove it from the minimum
  outputs.

**MO-15 · A18's "explicit exclusions" minimum output has no producing stage and no instrument**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** *"explicit exclusions (what must not enter any system; what
  should remain manual; non-goals)"* — three distinct things, one bullet, no
  home. *What should remain manual* needs A15's accountable-authority judgement,
  unavailable to a records-only diagnosis; *what must not enter any system* is
  produced by nothing; non-goals were produced by the **subject**.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only.** The first author's Stage 5 produced
  exclusions from the subject's own list without noticing the output had no
  producing stage.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A18 minimum outputs; A15.
- **Type:** SEMANTICS.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** the same class of defect as MO-02 and MO-08 — a required output
  the method never sources. Three instances is a pattern in A18, not three
  accidents.
- **Relationship:** CS-07.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether the three limbs should be split across
  stages or one stage should own all three.

**MO-07 · A15's preceding question creates a dead end that records-only diagnoses cannot leave**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author.
- **Evidence:** a "yes" routes to *existing system should be improved* or to
  Instrument 10, *"and the test proceeds only if the organization's authority
  has decided otherwise."* In a records-only diagnosis **no authority is ever
  available**, so the test can never proceed past its own preceding question —
  *"regardless of how well the problem is understood."* The second author
  answered the seven questions *"for the record, not to reach a different
  outcome"* and recorded that the method gave no guidance on whether that was
  permitted.
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only** as a deficiency — and the first author
  hit the same structure in Stage 5 and read it differently, reaching
  *insufficient evidence* rather than the preceding question's outcome.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A15; Instrument 9.
- **Type:** WORKFLOW.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.**
- **Reason:** the dead end is real. The remedy is **not** to remove the
  conservatism, which the second author itself called *"probably the right
  conservatism"* and which stopped it writing a recommendation. Modify: state
  in A15 that where no authority is available the preceding question
  **terminates** the test for that problem, that answering the seven questions
  afterwards is permitted **for the record and cannot change the outcome**, and
  that this is a property of records-only diagnosis rather than a finding about
  the problem.
- **Relationship:** CS-08; interacts with D-6, which is declined below on
  related evidence.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** whether "for the record" answers help a reader or
  merely lengthen the output.

### Sequence 5 — instrument and reporting defects

**D-1 · "Client-Stated" presumes a person is speaking**

- **Source:** Stage 5, first author.
- **Evidence:** the subject asserted conditions about itself through documents;
  no person spoke at all. A2's second limb ("or supplied institutional source")
  covers it in substance while the **name** does not.
- **Reproduced?** **Functionally equivalent issue independently observed** —
  MO-01 reaches A2's coarseness at the same point from a different angle.
- **Draft v3 affected:** A2; Instrument 1.
- **Type:** EVIDENCE RULES.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.**
- **Reason:** the underlying problem — A2 discards information about
  organizational-record evidence — is supported by both authors. The proposed
  remedy (a *source-kind* attribute: person / organizational record / supplied
  institutional source) is sound but **incomplete**: MO-01 shows the sharper
  loss is between two organizational records of radically different rigour.
  Modify to one attribute serving both, and adopt MO-01's case as the driver.
- **Relationship:** CS-01 with MO-01; neither subsumes the other (§7).
- **Remaining uncertainty:** the value set must not become a strength ranking.
  A2 has no ordering and must not acquire one by the back door.

**MO-01 · The three evidence classes are too coarse in exactly one place, and it is the place that mattered most**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author. Marked by it **"Severity: highest."**
- **Evidence:** A2 rule 4 forced it to classify a record of *"well over a
  hundred enumerated production probes, each with its refusal code, its fixture
  lifecycle, and its residue count"* **identically to the README's sentence
  "Nine runtime dependencies."** It did not invent a fourth class; it put the
  distinction in the prose confidence field and recorded that *"prose in a field
  is not classification."*
- **Reproduced?** **Functionally equivalent issue independently observed**
  (D-1).
- **Draft v3 affected:** A2; Instrument 1.
- **Type:** EVIDENCE RULES.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.**
- **Reason:** supported, and the strongest single case in the corpus for an
  evidence-model change. Modified only in that it is merged with D-1 into one
  attribute rather than two devices, and that the attribute must describe **kind
  of source**, not **quality of evidence** — a systematic verification record is
  distinguishable by *what it is*, and the method must not be made to rank
  reliability.
- **Relationship:** CS-01.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** the second author notes the nearest existing device
  — A2 rule 5's shared-source marking — *"addresses a different problem."*
  Agreed. Whether a subject's own verification record deserves a named kind is
  the determination the change set records.

**MO-10 · Instrument 1's "stated force, in the speaker's own words" has no provision for documents**

- **Source:** Stage 6, second author. **Lands on a Stage 4 first-author change
  (PT-09).**
- **Evidence:** the field is defined for speakers. In a records-only corpus the
  most diagnostically loaded strings are document qualifiers — *"proposed,
  awaiting approval"*, *"canon in force"*, *"Ratified"*, *"Blueprint only"*.
  The second author extended the field to quote them and reports it *"worked
  well — arguably better than in its intended use, because a document's
  qualifier is fixed text rather than a recollection."*
- **Reproduced?** **Second-author only.** The first author designed the field
  against accounts and never applied it to documents.
- **Draft v3 affected:** Instrument 1.
- **Type:** EVIDENCE RULES.
- **Disposition: ACCEPT.**
- **Reason:** evidenced by use, cheap, and it makes the field usable at all in a
  records-only diagnosis — which is the condition both applications were in. The
  anti-categorization rule carries over unchanged.

## 7. Duplicate / subsumption map

**No record is dispositioned DUPLICATE / SUBSUMED.** `fd-0025` §2.4 permits it
only where another specifically identified deficiency **fully** captures the
same required change, and forbids using it to reduce the corpus cosmetically.

Four pairs were tested against that standard and **all four failed it**:
