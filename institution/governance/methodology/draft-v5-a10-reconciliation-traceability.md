### 3.1 A10 — *Trail-first procedure* — **rewritten**

- **Clause-level change.** The single paragraph becomes six: the four items
  named **source categories** with the express statement that they are "not
  four limbs that must all exist and all be obtained before the procedure
  counts as performed"; *Pursued, not picked* (the materially-relevant and
  reasonably-accessible standard, the four per-category recording facts, the
  no-convenience-omission rule, and prior familiarity excluded via A17 rule 6);
  *The procedure is performed when* (the five conditions, and performance
  permitted with gaps remaining); *A missing category does not void the
  procedure* (sufficiency, visibility, and the anti-inference rule); *Performed
  is not sufficient* (A18 decides, and the permitted state named); and *The
  walkthrough is a category, not a gate* (load-bearing obtain-or-record, and
  other stakeholder-evidence provisions untouched).
- **Necessity.** A10 is the provision `fd-0028` construes. The determination has
  no other home.
- **Prior evidence.** The Draft v4 paragraph was exercised in **three**
  applications and produced **two different procedural outcomes on the same
  subject at the same baseline** — evidence that it was ambiguous, not evidence
  for either reading.
- **Fresh validation required: YES.**

  `a10-determination-reconciliation-record.md` §3.1, applied **verbatim**
