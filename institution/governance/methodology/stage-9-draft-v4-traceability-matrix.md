# Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One: Draft v4 Seventeen-Item Traceability Matrix

**Classification: Institutional Record** — traceability record (evidentiary,
non-governing). Produced at Stage 9 under `fd-0027`. It traces every difference
between **Draft v3** (`04fd02c7…d379f89f`, frozen at `d738d7e`) and **Draft v4**
to an authorized source, and states for each element **what validation it
actually carries**. It is not a validation record: **Draft v4 has been applied
to nothing.**

## 1. How to read the classifications

| Class | Meaning | What it licenses a later reader to claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UNCHANGED_WITH_PRIOR_INTERNAL_EVIDENCE** | the element is materially unchanged and was exercised in earlier internal testing | the evidence of the applications that exercised it — one organization and one of its units, two authors, and nothing beyond |
| **MODIFIED_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION** | prior evidence and rationale informed the change, but the requirement or behaviour changed materially | the **rationale**, not the validation. The predecessor's evidence attaches to the predecessor's wording |
| **NEW_STRUCTURAL_TREATMENT_REQUIRES_VALIDATION** | a treatment directed by determination that no tested version exercised | **nothing.** The evidence that something was missing is not evidence that this is the right thing to put there |
| **UNRESOLVED_LIMITATION** | an ambiguity or unvalidated condition Stage 9 expressly preserves | that the limitation is **disclosed**, and only that |

**Change types** used below: *definition clarification · evidence-rule change ·
required-output change · instrument-field change · closure-rule change ·
presentation/sufficiency change · confidentiality-handling change ·
cross-section consistency correction.*
