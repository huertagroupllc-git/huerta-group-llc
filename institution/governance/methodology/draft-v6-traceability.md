## 2. The chain of events, in order

1. **Draft v5 constructed** and frozen (`fd-0029`).
2. **Draft v5 applied once**, read-only, to Huerta Group Publishing by an
   authoring context that did not draft it (`fd-0030`) — producing seven method
   findings and seven proposed corrections.
3. **Stage 11 dispositioned** all seven findings (ACCEPT) and all seven
   proposals separately (two ACCEPT, five ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION), and
   assembled the unapplied change set V6-01 … V6-07 (`fd-0031`).
4. **RQ-1 reserved** — the evidentiary standard for A15's preceding question —
   and returned to the Founder Office rather than drafted.
5. **RQ-1 determined: Alternative B** (`fd-0032` §2.2).
6. **Draft v6 constructed** from Draft v5 by applying those two authorities and
   nothing else.

**Draft v5 is not retrospectively corrected.** It remains the version Stage 10
actually applied, and the text whose application produced every correction in
this version.

### 3.7 A15 — the preceding question — **RQ-1 and V6-07**

  2. **What answers it affirmatively** — Observed (A2); *already exists* means

     class like any other evidence; the terminating condition recorded as **not

  6. **Where existence is affirmatively established** — the Draft v5 routing and

     **a records-only diagnosis may be unable to use the shortcut at all, and
