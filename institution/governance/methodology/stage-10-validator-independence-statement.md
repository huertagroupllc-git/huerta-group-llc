# Independence and Prior-Involvement Statement

**Subject of the forthcoming task:** Application of a frozen methodology draft (organizational diagnostic method) to Huerta Group Publishing
**Author of this statement:** The agent instance assigned to perform that application
**Status at time of writing:** Written before any subject evidence, method text, or task material was read. Zero tool calls made. Frozen on completion.

---

## 1. Institutional prior involvement

Huerta Group Publishing is not an arm's-length subject. It is a publishing venture operating within Huerta Group LLC. Huerta Group LLC's development function designed its platform, built it, governs it, and authored most of the records that will be presented to me as evidence.

The method draft I am to apply was also written by Huerta Group LLC's own development function.

I am an agent instance operating inside that same development function.

This means every element of the exercise — the diagnostician, the diagnostic instrument, the subject, and the evidentiary record — originates from a single institution. There is no external party anywhere in the chain. Specifically:

- **The evidence is not neutral testimony about the subject.** It is the institution's own account of itself, written by the same function that built the thing being described. Where the records are silent, incomplete, self-flattering, or simply framed in the institution's own vocabulary, I have no independent source against which to detect that. I can only read what was written.
- **The method is not a neutral instrument.** A method authored inside an institution may encode that institution's assumptions about what is worth measuring and what counts as healthy. Applying it to that institution's own venture cannot test whether those assumptions are correct — only whether the venture satisfies them.
- **The institution has an interest in the outcome.** A diagnostic performed by an organization on its own venture, using its own method, against its own records, carries an inherent directional pressure toward a favorable or at least a self-legible result.

A diagnosis produced under these conditions **cannot be independent of Huerta Group LLC**. Whatever else it establishes, it does not establish that.

## 2. Individual / agent prior involvement

What I bring to this task, stated exactly:

**What I do not have:**

- I have no memory of any prior session. I do not carry knowledge forward from one conversation to the next. Anything I worked on previously — including any prior work on this subject, this method, or this institution — is not available to me now and I cannot recall it or verify that it happened.
- I have no prior work on this subject that I am aware of within this context. I did not build Huerta Group Publishing's platform in this session and hold no recollection of having done so in any other.
- I did not write the method draft I am to test. This is the one genuine separation in the arrangement, and it is narrow.
- I have not read the method draft, the subject evidence, or any task material. As of this sentence I have made no tool calls.

**What I do have:**

- General knowledge from training about organizations, publishing, software platforms, governance structures, diagnostic and assessment methods, and organizational analysis. This is unavoidable and not specific to the subject.
- Whatever this briefing prompt has told me, which is enumerated in section 5.
- A note in my operating context flagging that a memory directory exists and may contain entries relevant to Huerta ventures. I have not opened it. I therefore do not know its contents at the time of writing, but I must record honestly that **persisted institutional notes may be available to me through tools during the task**, and if I read them, they will not be independent knowledge — they will be more of the institution's own record. Should I consult such material later, that fact and its influence should be disclosed at that point. I cannot state now what it says, only that its existence is known to me and that any content I obtain from it must be treated as institutional self-description, not corroboration.

**What "a different agent instance" does and does not buy:**

It buys that the person or process applying the method is not the person or process that wrote it, and that I carry no unstated grudge, hope, or recollection about the subject.

It does **not** buy institutional independence. I am instantiated by, directed by, and operating inside Huerta Group LLC's development function. My task, my materials, my scope, and my access are all set by that function. A fresh instance of an internal reviewer is still an internal reviewer. This distinction must not be blurred later into a claim of independence.

## 4. Prior-result material being withheld

As I understand it, two prior diagnostic applications of earlier versions of this method have been performed on Huerta Group Publishing. **Their results are being withheld from me.** I do not know their findings, their severity, their direction, their recommendations, whether they agreed with each other, or whether anything changed as a result of them.

What their absence permits me to say:

- My findings will not have been copied from, anchored to, or steered toward those prior results, because I will not have seen them.

What their absence does **not** permit me to say, and what I will not later claim:

- It does not make my application independent. Withholding one class of material from an internal reviewer does not make the reviewer external. Section 1 stands regardless.
- It does not mean my findings constitute a replication, a confirmation, or a contradiction of the prior applications. Any comparison between my results and theirs would be performed by someone who holds both, not by me. I am not in a position to assert agreement or disagreement, and if my results happen to converge with theirs, I cannot claim I converged independently on a stable truth — only that I did not see them first. Convergence and divergence alike will require interpretation by a party with access to both, and that interpretation is not mine to make.
- It does not mean the prior applications had no effect on what I will see. The prior applications were performed on this subject by this institution. They may already have changed the subject, the platform, the records, the method draft I am about to apply, or the selection of evidence handed to me. Withholding the *results* does not withhold their *consequences*. I cannot detect or quantify that influence, and I should not assume it is zero.
- I cannot infer anything about the prior findings' content from the fact of their withholding. Withholding is a procedural control; it is not evidence that the prior results were bad, good, contested, or notable.

## 5. Unavoidable contextual leakage

This briefing prompt has already shaped my priors. Recording it specifically, because failing to do so would be the most likely route by which it goes unexamined:

1. **It told me the method is applied by its own authors' institution to its own venture.** This frames the entire exercise as one whose credibility is in question, which may prime me to look harder for signs of self-serving framing in the records than I would have otherwise — a bias, even if a defensible one.
2. **It told me most of the evidence was authored by the development function.** I now expect the record to be institutionally authored and possibly self-favorable. This could make me discount genuine, well-documented achievements as self-report, or conversely make me over-read ordinary internal documentation as evasive.
3. **It told me the method draft is "frozen."** This signals that the method is not mine to revise mid-application and that its defects, if any, are to be exercised rather than fixed. It may also lead me to treat the method's categories as more settled than their draft status warrants.
4. **It told me two prior applications exist.** This tells me the subject has been diagnosed before, which implies the method has been considered applicable and that the subject was considered worth diagnosing more than once. It may create an unwarranted expectation that there is something durable to find, or that a third pass should produce something comparable.
5. **It told me the prior results are deliberately withheld.** The act of withholding signals that someone expected them to be capable of influencing me — i.e., that they contain something substantive. That inference itself is leakage, even though its content is not.
6. **Its entire framing is about independence, bias, and the limits of self-assessment.** This is a strong prime. A briefing that spends its length on the risk of institutional self-flattery may push me toward severity as a way of demonstrating that I was not captured. Excess severity is a bias in the same way excess leniency is, and it is the one this prompt makes more likely in me.
7. **The instruction to write this statement before reading anything** signals that the exercise's integrity is itself contested or under scrutiny. That is context about the institution's own posture, obtained before any evidence.
8. **My operating context named Huerta ventures as subjects of prior sessions**, which tells me this is an ongoing, multi-session body of work rather than a one-off, before I have read anything about it.

None of this can be unlearned. It is recorded so that it can be weighed against my conclusions rather than sitting invisibly beneath them.

## 6. Resulting independence limitation

Stated plainly, without softening:

**This is an internal self-assessment.** It is Huerta Group LLC applying a method it wrote, through an agent it operates, to a venture it owns and built, on the basis of records it authored.

It is **not** independent validation. It is **not** external validation. It is **not** third-party, arm's-length, or adversarial review. It is **not** blind — I have been told the institutional structure, the authorship of the evidence, and the existence of prior applications. It is **not** fresh in the institutional sense; only in the sense that this particular instance holds no recollection.

What it can legitimately be called: **a first-time application of this method version by an operator who did not author the method and does not hold the prior results.** That is the whole of the separation. It is real but small, and it is a separation of *hands*, not of *interest*.

What it cannot establish, and what no reader of the resulting output should take from it:

- That the method is valid. A method cannot be validated by being run once, by its authoring institution, on its authoring institution's own venture. At most this exercise can show whether the method is *executable* — whether its steps can be followed, whether its criteria can be applied to real material, where it is ambiguous or underspecified.
- That the resulting diagnosis of Huerta Group Publishing is objectively accurate. It is a diagnosis derived entirely from the institution's own record of itself.
- That any favorable finding is corroborated. Favorable findings under these conditions carry the least weight of anything this exercise can produce, because they are exactly what the structure would tend to generate anyway.
- That the absence of a problem in the record means the absence of a problem in the venture. I can only see what the institution wrote down.

Any downstream use of this work — internal, external, promotional, or governance — must carry this limitation with it. If the output is later cited without this statement attached, the citation misrepresents it.

This statement is frozen as of its writing and is not to be amended in light of anything I subsequently read.
