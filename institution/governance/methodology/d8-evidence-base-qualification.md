# Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One: D-8 Evidence-Base Qualification

**Classification: Institutional Record** — qualification record (evidentiary,
non-governing). Records the Founder Office determination at `fd-0024` §2.4
that every Generation One application to date provides **internal testing
evidence but not evaluator independence**, and applies that qualification
across the program's evidence base.

**Two rules govern this record, and both cut against the easy reading:**

- **"Do not rewrite prior reports."** No Stage 2, 3, 4 or 5 record is edited,
  annotated in place, withdrawn, or restated. Every one stands exactly as
  returned. This is a **later qualification**, recorded once, here.
- **"Do not retroactively invalidate prior evidence merely because it lacks
  evaluator independence."** Internal testing may still be substantive. The
  qualification narrows what the evidence *supports*; it does not delete it.

**And one that cuts the other way:** *"Do not amend Draft v3 merely because
D-8 exists."* The method is not corrected because a defect was named. Whether
D-8 warrants a method change is a question for replication evidence, not for
this record.

## 1. The determination, verbatim

> "Previous Generation One methodology applications were performed by a
> Development function that materially participated in construction of: the
> methodology; Huerta Group systems and governance; and/or prior work involving
> the diagnostic subject. **Those applications provide internal testing
> evidence but not evaluator independence.**"

## 2. Where D-8 came from

D-8 was **found by the diagnostic team against itself**, in the Stage 5
deficiency record, and it was recorded there as one of two proposals that make
the method's own conclusions weaker. Its Stage 5 statement: Draft v3 discloses
two independence defects — *author-of-records* (the team wrote the records it
judges, adopted at Stage 2) and *account and record share a source* (the
participant wrote them, adopted at Stage 4) — and has nothing for the third and
largest, that **the team previously designed, built, or advised the subject
itself**.

The Founder Office has now determined that third defect to be a fact about the
whole program rather than an observation about one stage.

## 3. The three independence defects, distinguished

They are different conditions and are repeatedly confused, so they are
separated here once:

| Defect | What it means | Where it is disclosed | Adopted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Author-of-records** | The diagnostic team wrote or approved the records it is now judging | Draft v3 A18; every comparison outcome in Stages 3–5 | Stage 2 (correction C-17) |
| **Account and record share a source** | The *participant* wrote or approved the records their account is being compared against — so agreement is consistency, not corroboration | Draft v3 A18 and A2 rule 5; six Stage 3 corroborations marked | Stage 4 (PT-03) |
| **Prior involvement (D-8)** | The diagnostic team previously **designed, implemented, advised, or evaluated the subject organization itself** | **Nowhere in the method.** Recorded only because the Stage 5 instrument demanded it | **Not adopted** — deferred by `fd-0024` §2.4 |

The first two are about **records**. D-8 is about the **subject**. A team can
be clean of the first two and still have built the organization it is
diagnosing.

## 5. What Stage 6 does and does not do to D-8

**What it changes.** The second author did not build the subject, did not
write the method, did not participate in any prior application, and inherited
no working context from the first. On the *prior-involvement* limb specifically,
it is materially cleaner than any author this program has had.

**What it does not change, and must not be read as changing:**

1. **Isolation was partial and is measured.** The second author's context
   carried a user-memory index it quoted verbatim before starting, containing
   subject product-status claims and Stage 3-era program facts. Stage 5's
   findings, deficiencies and advancement outcome did not leak. Convergence on
   any leaked identifier is excluded from the reproducibility evidence.
2. **Both authors are the same model family.** The second author raised this
   and the first did not: *"Correlated errors will look like reproducibility."*
   No internal arrangement removes it.
3. **The subject was not chosen by the second author.** It was placed in it.
4. **The comparison is performed by the first author.** Stage 6's comparison
   and this qualification are written by the office whose work is being
   compared. That is a fourth independence defect, is not D-8, and is recorded
   here so that it is on the record rather than discovered later.

**The ceiling, restated from `fd-0024` §2.10:** Stage 6 can establish, at most,
*stronger internal reproducibility evidence that more than one authoring
context can produce materially similar findings from the same frozen method and
subject evidence.* It cannot establish evaluator independence, and nothing in
this program yet does.

## 6. What the Generation One evidence base is, after D-8

Stated once, plainly, so that no later record has to reconstruct it:

> Six stages of **internal testing** of a provisional organizational diagnostic,
> performed on two subjects inside one institution, by a diagnostic office that
> helped build both the method and the subjects, with one participant who
> authored most of the records, and one partially-isolated second author on the
> same model family.
>
> Within those limits the testing is **substantive**: it produced seventeen
> corrections, ten more, eight more, four Founder clarifications, two frozen
> baselines and a third draft, seventy-two classified comparisons, and — on
> every occasion the method required it — a refusal to advance.
>
> It establishes nothing about external validity, and it never has.

## 7. What this record does not do

- It does not rewrite, annotate, withdraw, or restate any prior report.
- It does not invalidate any prior finding.
- It does not amend Draft v3, and no Draft v4 exists.
- It does not adopt the prior-involvement disclosure into the method. That
  remains a Stage 5 proposal (**D-8**), deferred by `fd-0024` §2.4 pending
  replication evidence, and is decided by the Founder Office and the
  Methodology room — not here.
- It does not claim that Stage 6 repairs the defect. It reduces one limb of it
  and adds a fourth defect of its own (§5.4).
