## What the instrument forbade being inferred from them

Recorded here because the prohibitions are part of the evidence's meaning, not
commentary on it. `fd-0034` §2.3 enumerates them:

- From PQ-V6-6 — **no storage system, no repository location, no custodian, no
  retention rule, no transfer mechanism, and no formal custody policy.** A
  general preference to preserve is not a disposition, and A17 rule 7's closure
  condition is not satisfied by one.
- From PQ-V6-5 — **no legal or institutional non-existence determination.** The
  Founder's colloquial *"doesn't actually exist yet"* is preserved against
  governing records that establish Huerta Group LLC as a constituted entity, and
  the tension is kept rather than resolved.
- From PQ-V6-7 — **no organizational actorhood for a named tool.** Tools are not
  actors merely because a boundary answer names them.
- From PQ-V6-4 — **no answer to whether a built, standby or dormant capability
  exists.** *"Website hasn't gone live"* bears on recurrence; the A15 existence
  rule applies independently of it.
- From PQ-V6-1 — **no manufactured walkthrough.** If no qualifying actual
  instance exists, the affected target is classified under the existing exercise
  semantics rather than supplied with one.
- From PQ-V6-8 — **no conversion to Observed** because the Founder stated it.
