## 1. Founder Office Venture Determination (recitals)

**Whereas** the Corporate Venture Governance — Phase 1 Blueprint establishes that venture status is created exclusively by Founder Office determination and never by existence, repository, platform, brand, or operating history;

**Whereas** repository evidence establishes that Huerta Group Publishing operates as a production system under Huerta Group LLC — repository `huerta-group/huerta-group-publishing`, `main` @ `ab0dac8` (2026-08-11), clean and synchronized; 170 commits since 2026-07-02; 42 applied migrations verified against the named hosted production database (project `huerta-group-publishing`, ref `jlsvwqfptjbhbioolonh`); 62 routes across a two-locale public site, an authenticated author workspace, and a staff administration area; a deterministic EPUB, print-PDF, and cover production chain with evidence-gated releases; an ISBN provenance registry; CI on every push; and a founder-validation program with a live observation register;

**Whereas** the venture's own records identify it as "Huerta Group Publishing," "the digital platform of the Huerta Group Publishing imprint, and the home of its Author Operating System" (venture `README.md`), publishing under the legal entity constant `PUBLISHER_LEGAL_ENTITY = "Huerta Group LLC"` with imprint `"Huerta Group Publishing"` (`lib/publication/publisher.ts`, test-enforced), values established by prior Founder Office Phase 2 authorization;

**Whereas** the parent institutional record contains no venture concept, rendering the relationship real but ungoverned (Corporate Platform Alignment Audit D-1/D-2; fd-0006 FO-02/FO-03), and this admission is a **regularization** under Blueprint §23.6, using retrospective evidence honestly marked as such;

**Now, therefore, the Founder Office determines (FD-V1):**

1. **Huerta Group Publishing is admitted as the first formal venture of Huerta Group LLC**, effective upon approval of this instrument, with the Charter set out herein as its governing document.
2. Huerta Group Publishing is an **operational venture of Huerta Group LLC. It is not an independent company**, not a separate legal entity, not a subsidiary, and not an independent brand owner.
3. Huerta Group LLC retains in full: **constitutional authority; institutional governance; legal ownership; strategic direction; capital allocation; and portfolio governance.**
4. Huerta Group Publishing receives **delegated operational authority only within this approved Charter** (§7). Everything not expressly delegated is retained (§8).
5. Shared institutional capabilities exist **only through explicit institutional grant** recorded in this Charter or a subsequent determination (§20).
6. Repository and infrastructure independence follows the approved **isolation-by-default** principle (§13, §17) unless governance explicitly provides otherwise.
7. The **Venture Graduation Principle (FD-V9) applies** to this venture as to all ventures, and **implies no expectation** that Huerta Group Publishing will progress beyond its current institutional role (§25).

This determination records existing facts and establishes governance over them. It rewrites no history, creates no new capability, and authorizes no new operations beyond those the Charter delegates.

## 2. Purpose of the Venture

Huerta Group Publishing exists to publish books under the Huerta Group Publishing imprint of Huerta Group LLC, and to build and operate the software through which that publishing is done — an Author Operating System whose stated purpose is preserving authorship: "Huerta Group Publishing exists to help authors create books that sound more like themselves, not more like AI" (venture `README.md`). Its institutional character, per its own catalog copy, is "a publishing house operating through software: a membership workshop where authors develop a book with careful editorial review."

## 3. Mission

To develop books, not just manuscripts: to take an author's work from manuscript through disciplined editorial review to deterministic, evidence-gated publication artifacts (EPUB, print, covers, releases), under bibliographic and provenance records worthy of an institution — with authorship preserved, editorial judgment human, and software in service of both.

The mission statement above is charter framing drawn from the venture's own recorded language ("Develop books, not just manuscripts"). It creates no public claim; public copy remains governed by §18 of the Blueprint and this Charter's disclosure posture (§24).

## 4. Scope

**In scope (the venture's field of work):**

- Editorial development of books: manuscript intake (including the deterministic PDF import pipeline), editorial review, findings, deliberation, and author collaboration.
- Publication production: EPUB and print-PDF generation, cover production, edition/manifestation records, evidence-gated releases, bibliographic metadata, and ISBN **provenance recording**.
- Operation of its own platform: the authenticated author workspace, staff administration area, and its public editorial site (two locales; Spanish as unlaunched preview).
- Membership and support operations **without payment collection** (publisher-provisioned access; no billing).
- Venture-internal governance: its founder-validation program, observation register, and operational documentation.

**Out of scope (not part of this venture, regardless of capability):**

- Distribution: ONIX feeds, retailer integration, barcode graphics, and manufacturing (the venture's own records mark these explicitly out of scope).
- Commercial transactions of any kind: billing, payment collection, pricing commitments, storefront sales (§11, §22).
- Legally operative acts: contracts, rights assignments, ISBN block acquisition or agency registration, trademark or copyright filings (§8, §19).
- The parent practice's services: organizational systems consulting, education, managed services — the venture neither offers nor represents them.
- Any other venture's subject matter.

## 6. Institutional Responsibilities

The venture is responsible, through its steward (§21), for:

1. Conformance to this Charter and to the truth discipline (Guardrail 8) on every surface it operates, human- and machine-readable.
2. Maintenance of its own records: operational documentation, validation program, observation register, migration baselines, and production-verification records — in the disciplined form it already evidences.
3. Timely reporting of material lifecycle changes to the parent for register recording (upon Phase 3 implementation of the venture registry).
4. Preservation of its history: no deletion of venture records, forward-only correction (the venture's fix-forward migration practice is the required pattern).
5. ## 7. Delegated Operational Authority

Within this Charter, Huerta Group Publishing holds delegated authority to:

1. Make all **editorial decisions**: acceptance of manuscripts into the workshop (within publisher-provisioned access), editorial review and deliberation, revision guidance, and readiness judgments for production.
2. Operate the **publication production chain**: generate and regenerate deterministic EPUB/print/cover artifacts; create and manage edition and manifestation records; execute evidence-gated releases; maintain bibliographic metadata and the ISBN provenance registry (recording only — see §8.4).
3. **Provision authors and staff** on its platform within its access model (publisher-provisioned; no self-serve).
4. Operate and evolve its **own technical platform**: application code, schema migrations, CI, deployments, and locale development — under its technical custody (§23) and the Blueprint's isolation defaults.
5. Use **AI within its implemented editorial tooling** as presently evidenced (review assistance under human editorial judgment), subject to Vision Foundation §8 and the venture's own AI-usage administration surface.
6. Maintain **venture-internal governance records** and escalate to the Founder Office per its validation program.
7. Conduct **membership and support operations without payment collection**.

Delegation is exhaustive: operational authority not enumerated here or subsequently granted by determination is not delegated.

## 25. Venture Graduation Principle (FD-V9)

As approved by the Founder Office and applied here:

1. **Definition.** A venture's institutional role may change over time — toward greater operational, financial, brand, or legal independence (up to and including a separate legal entity), or toward reduced scope, dormancy, or reintegration — **only through explicit, staged Founder Office determinations**, each with its own evidence, conditions, and recorded decision.
2. **No automatic graduation.** Maturity, revenue, scale, public success, or operating history never graduate a venture by themselves. No status dimension, singly or in combination, effects graduation. Graduation is an institutional act, not a milestone consequence.
3. **No implied expectation.** The Principle's availability creates no expectation, plan, or trajectory. **Specifically: nothing in this Charter implies any expectation that Huerta Group Publishing will progress beyond its current institutional role.** Remaining a chartered operational venture indefinitely is a fully legitimate steady state.
4. **Graduation stages are not pre-enumerated.** Each proposed graduation defines its own target state, conditions, and consequences at determination time, within the Blueprint's architecture; legal-form changes additionally require Corporate Headquarters action (FD-V6 roadmap remains open).
5. **Reversibility and history.** Graduation determinations, like all venture acts, preserve history and are superseded rather than erased.
