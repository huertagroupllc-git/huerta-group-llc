# Corporate Operations & Command Management — Institutional Function Record

**Classification: Approved Methodology, Governance, and Policy.**

- **Governing authority:** Founder Office.
- **Established:** by the Founder Office in the Corporate Headquarters
  project, prior to and independently of this repository record.
- **Repository status:** institutionalized as durable repository
  authority under `fd-0010` (Governance & Methodology Readiness Phase 1).
- **Disclosure:** repository-internal.

## 1. What this record is

This record makes an **already-established** institutional function
repository-native (determination D-6: institutionally significant
governance shall become repository-native). It preserves authority that
already exists; it does not create, enlarge, or diminish it.

Two things follow, and both are load-bearing:

- **Repository absence was never non-existence.** Corporate Operations &
  Command Management existed as an established Founder Office function
  before this record. Its prior absence from the governed corpus
  evidenced a repository institutionalization gap and nothing more
  (`fd-0010` execution principle 4).
- **This record does not expand jurisdiction.** Only the authority
  enumerated in §3 is preserved. Where this record and the establishing
  Founder Office authority could be read differently, the establishing
  authority governs.

**Provenance deficiency.** The establishing instrument itself was not
transmitted to the implementing office and is not present in this
repository. This record therefore preserves the authority substance
enumerated by the `fd-0010` instrument — preserved verbatim in that
record so the two can be compared — and nothing beyond it. The absent
establishing instrument is recorded as a deficiency (`fd-0010`
Deficiency 3), never completed by inference. A reader who needs to know
what the Founder Office actually transmitted, as distinct from how this
record renders it, should read `fd-0010` first.

## 2. Position in the institution

Corporate Operations & Command Management sits within **Corporate
Headquarters**, which the governed corpus establishes as governing
legally, financially, contractually, administratively, and commercially
operative matters. It is a function of Headquarters, not a peer of it,
and not a replacement for any other established authority.

It is distinct from **Operations (OSPA)**, which governs the conceptual
architecture and institutional semantics of operational work. Corporate
Operations & Command Management operates a Headquarters *representation*;
OSPA governs what operational concepts *mean*. Neither absorbs the other.

## 3. Authority preserved

The following, and only the following, is preserved as established. Each
item corresponds to the enumeration transmitted by the `fd-0010`
instrument and preserved verbatim in that record under "Transmitted
substance preserved verbatim" — that record, not this one, is where the
authority's wording is auditable against its source.

1. **Corporate Headquarters role.** The function operates as a Corporate
   Headquarters function.
2. **Jurisdiction over Headquarters operational representation.** It owns
   the Headquarters-level operational representation of what is
   happening across the Company.
3. **Headquarters representation is distinct from source-function
   authority.** Source functions retain authority over their own
   authoritative facts. Headquarters may represent a fact; it does not
   thereby own, restate, override, or become able to change that fact.
4. **System-of-truth principle.** Each fact has one authoritative source.
   Headquarters representation references and summarizes; it does not
   become a competing source of truth.
5. **Requirements-before-software discipline.** Business and operational
   requirements are established before software is built to serve them.
6. **Long-term Corporate Command Center intent.** A Corporate Command
   Center is recognized as long-term institutional intent.
7. **That intent is bounded.** Recognition of long-term intent is not
   authorization, scope, or a present requirement.
8. **Technical architecture and implementation remain separately
   governed.** Nothing in this function's authority authorizes technical
   architecture, implementation, or production behavior.

## 4. Authority boundary

Institutionalization confers no substantive authority belonging to
another function, venture, governance domain, accounting authority,
administrative authority, or technical authority. Specifically, this
record does **not** grant Corporate Operations & Command Management:

- authority over any source function's authoritative facts;
- venture authority (venture facts remain venture-authoritative under the
  Corporate Venture Governance Phase 1 Blueprint, FD-V1 and FD-V2);
- operational-semantic authority (that is OSPA's, and is separately
  constrained by OSPA's admission status — see
  `fd-v7-evidence-package.md`);
- accounting, legal, or administrative authority reserved to Corporate
  Headquarters generally or to legal guidance;
- technical, architectural, data-topology, identity, or environment
  authority (reserved under ADR-0003 through ADR-0006 and to the Founder
  Office);
- authority to create operational vocabulary absent from governing
  institutional authority.

## 5. What this record does not do

It creates no register, no schema, no application, no runtime behavior,
and no data. It does not authorize the Internal Operations & Command
Platform, whose technical architecture and implementation remain paused
under `fd-0010`. It does not amend the Constitution, the Vision
Foundation, any foundation, ADR, or determination.
