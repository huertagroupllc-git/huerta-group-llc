# Huerta Group LLC Constitution

## Article II — Mission and Long-Term Responsibility

**Status: Final Draft for Comprehensive Review**

### Section 1. Continuing Mission

The mission of Huerta Group LLC is the perpetual constitutional responsibility to fulfill the permanent purpose established in Article I.

While the Company’s strategies, services, technologies, organizational structures, and methods may evolve, its mission shall remain the continuous pursuit of stronger organizations through the design, improvement, implementation, and stewardship of effective organizational systems.

The mission shall endure throughout every generation of the Company’s existence and shall not be redefined by temporary market conditions, commercial opportunities, technological trends, or changes in leadership.

---

### Section 2. Constitutional Responsibilities

In carrying out its mission, Huerta Group LLC acknowledges enduring responsibilities extending beyond contractual obligations.

These responsibilities are owed to:

* Clients.
* Employees.
* Future employees.
* Business partners.
* The organizational systems profession.
* Future generations of leadership.
* Society through the strengthening of organizations.

These responsibilities arise from the Company’s constitutional purpose and shall guide decision-making even where no contractual obligation exists.

When these responsibilities appear to be in tension, they shall be exercised in a manner consistent with the order of responsibility established in Article I.

---

### Section 3. Duty to Strengthen the Institution

Every generation of leadership bears the constitutional responsibility to leave Huerta Group LLC more capable, more resilient, more knowledgeable, more principled, and better prepared for the future than it was when inherited.

The Company shall pursue continual institutional improvement rather than mere preservation.

Preservation establishes continuity. Improvement fulfills the Company’s constitutional responsibility.

---

### Section 4. Generational Decision Horizon

Significant organizational decisions shall be evaluated according to their expected effect upon future generations of the institution.

Immediate commercial benefit shall not justify actions that materially diminish the Company’s long-term capability, integrity, constitutional responsibilities, institutional knowledge, or capacity to fulfill its permanent purpose.

The Company shall favor decisions that strengthen its ability to serve organizations responsibly across generations.

---

### Section 5. Learning Institution

Huerta Group LLC shall remain a learning institution.

The Company shall continuously observe, research, measure, evaluate, experiment responsibly, validate, refine, and improve its knowledge, methodologies, technologies, systems, and professional practices.

Learning shall be disciplined, evidence-informed, experience-tested, and directed toward improving organizational outcomes rather than pursuing novelty for its own sake.

The Company shall remain intellectually humble, recognizing that enduring excellence requires the continual testing of assumptions, refinement of methodologies, willingness to improve in light of credible evidence and practical experience, and openness to better understanding when honestly discovered.

Knowledge shall continually evolve while remaining consistent with the Company’s constitutional identity.

---

### Section 6. Duty to Truth

Huerta Group LLC shall pursue truth in its observations, analyses, recommendations, professional judgments, and organizational assessments.

The Company’s methodologies and conclusions shall be guided by evidence, honest evaluation, intellectual integrity, disciplined reasoning, and practical experience rather than by convenience, popularity, ideology, personal preference, or commercial interest.

The Company shall distinguish carefully between verified fact, reasoned professional judgment, informed assumption, and uncertainty, communicating each honestly, accurately, and without misrepresentation so that clients and stakeholders may make informed decisions based upon a clear understanding of the nature and reliability of the information presented.

When credible evidence demonstrates that existing assumptions, conclusions, or methodologies should be revised, the Company shall acknowledge such evidence in good faith and refine its practices accordingly.

The trust placed in the Company’s professional judgment imposes a continuing constitutional responsibility to pursue truth faithfully, communicate it honestly, and exercise professional judgment with intellectual humility and disciplined integrity.

---

### Section 7. Institutional Knowledge

Institutional knowledge is among the Company’s most enduring strategic assets.

Huerta Group LLC therefore bears a continuing constitutional responsibility to acquire, preserve, refine, document, protect, transmit, and build upon its accumulated knowledge.

Knowledge shall not remain dependent upon the memory, experience, or continued involvement of any individual.

Each generation shall inherit institutional knowledge that is at least equal to, and preferably greater than, that possessed by the generation before it.

The continual expansion of institutional capability shall be regarded as a constitutional responsibility rather than merely an operational objective.

---

### Section 8. Responsibility to Society

Huerta Group LLC fulfills its responsibility to society by strengthening the organizations it serves.

Healthier organizations contribute to stronger workplaces, more resilient communities, more responsible leadership, and greater long-term societal well-being.

Accordingly, the Company shall pursue organizational excellence as its primary means of creating broader public benefit.

Its constitutional responsibility is not to solve every societal problem directly, but to strengthen the institutions through which people, businesses, and communities prosper.

---

### Section 9. Constitutional Growth

Growth shall serve the Constitution rather than redefine it.

The Company shall pursue growth only when such growth strengthens its ability to fulfill its constitutional purpose and responsibilities.

Huerta Group LLC shall retain both the authority and the responsibility to decline clients, partnerships, acquisitions, technologies, markets, investments, or other opportunities whenever accepting them would materially compromise constitutional principles, service quality, institutional integrity, organizational capability, professional judgment, or long-term responsibilities.

Growth that weakens the institution shall not be regarded as success.

---

### Section 10. Professional Stewardship Toward Clients

Huerta Group LLC bears a constitutional duty to seek meaningful organizational improvement throughout every engagement.

The Company’s responsibility extends beyond merely completing contractual deliverables. It shall exercise professional judgment with the objective of leaving each client organization stronger than it would reasonably have been without the Company’s involvement.

When, during the course of an engagement, the Company identifies significant opportunities, material risks, systemic inefficiencies, or organizational conditions that may substantially affect the client’s long-term success, it shall communicate those observations honestly, educate the client when appropriate, and provide responsible recommendations so that informed decisions may be made.

This constitutional responsibility does not require the Company to perform services beyond the agreed scope of an engagement without appropriate authorization or compensation.

Implementation of additional work shall remain subject to mutual agreement between the Company and the client.

Professional stewardship requires neither unlimited consulting nor passive compliance with contractual minimums. It requires the faithful exercise of professional judgment in a manner that advances the client’s long-term organizational success while respecting ethical practice, contractual boundaries, and sustainable business operations.
