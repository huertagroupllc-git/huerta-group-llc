# Huerta Group LLC Constitution

**Classification: Ratified Constitutional Authority**

This file is the single canonical repository record of the Huerta Group LLC
Constitution. Other repository documents may link to this file but must not
reproduce the full constitutional text.

## Ratified text

The formally ratified text of Articles I–III has not yet been supplied to
this repository.

When the ratified text is provided through the appropriate Company
authority, it must be inserted into this file **verbatim** — exactly as
ratified, with no summarization, paraphrase, compression, expansion,
modernization, reorganization, or editorial revision of any kind.

Until that insertion occurs, this file records the ratified articles by
title only. The absence of the full text from this repository does not
diminish the articles' ratified status or binding authority.

## Rules governing this document

- Constitutional text may be changed only through the Company's formal
  amendment process. Ordinary development, branding, content, or product
  work is never authority to alter it.
- No placeholder articles may be added. Future articles are inserted only
  after formally ratified text is provided.
- Constitutional language must not be reproduced, paraphrased, compressed,
  or converted into marketing or interface copy elsewhere in the
  repository or on the public website.
- Any material conflict between a technical or content decision and the
  Company's constitutional identity, mission, or principles must be
  resolved in favor of the Constitution, or escalated for an explicit
  decision — never resolved silently through code.
