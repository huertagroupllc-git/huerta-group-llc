# Huerta Group LLC Constitution

## Article I — Purpose and Identity

**Status: Ratified**

### Section 1. Permanent Purpose

Huerta Group LLC exists to strengthen organizations by designing systems that improve operational performance, clarity, accountability, adaptability, and long-term sustainability, enabling people and technology to work together more effectively in service of their customers, employees, and communities.

This purpose is permanent. It shall not be altered merely in response to market trends, technological developments, temporary commercial opportunities, changes in leadership, or short-term financial pressures.

The methods through which the Company fulfills its purpose may evolve. The purpose itself shall remain constant.

---

### Section 2. Organizational Identity

Huerta Group LLC is an organizational systems company whose central discipline is operations and organizational systems design.

The Company shall not be limited to any single service model, delivery method, industry, technology, or form of commercial activity. It may fulfill its purpose through consulting, technology, education, intellectual property, software, managed services, strategic partnerships, publishing, research, or other present or future solutions, provided that such activities remain consistent with this Constitution.

The Company may evolve in structure, capability, and scope without abandoning its foundational identity.

---

### Section 3. Order of Responsibility

When duties, interests, opportunities, or competing considerations conflict, Huerta Group LLC shall evaluate its decisions according to the following order of responsibility:

1. Ethical and legal integrity.
2. Long-term organizational health.
3. The interests and well-being of the people affected by the Company’s work, including clients, employees, partners, and communities.
4. Quality, effectiveness, and excellence of execution.
5. Sustainable profitability.
6. Short-term growth, convenience, market pressure, or technological trends.

No lower responsibility shall be permitted to override a higher responsibility.

Profitability is necessary to preserve the Company’s independence, continuity, capability, and long-term service. It shall therefore be pursued responsibly and deliberately, but it shall never override ethical integrity or long-term organizational health.

---

### Section 4. Human Purpose of Systems

Huerta Group LLC shall never regard people as obstacles to efficiency.

Technology, artificial intelligence, automation, data, processes, and organizational systems shall be treated as instruments for strengthening human capability, improving judgment, reducing unnecessary complexity, increasing accountability, and enabling people to perform their highest-value work.

The Company may use technology to eliminate waste, reduce repetitive burdens, improve accuracy, and redesign work. It shall not pursue efficiency without regard for the dignity, development, judgment, and legitimate interests of the people affected.

Technology shall remain a servant of the Company’s purpose and shall never become its purpose.

People remain the reason the Company exists.

---

### Section 5. Industry Neutrality and Ethical Eligibility

Huerta Group LLC shall remain industry-neutral and may serve organizations across industries, sectors, and institutional forms.

Eligibility for engagement shall require both lawful operation and ethical compatibility with the principles established by this Constitution.

Lawfulness alone shall neither obligate nor justify the Company’s participation in any engagement.

Prospective clients, partners, projects, and opportunities shall be evaluated according to constitutional principles rather than solely according to industry classification, revenue potential, prestige, market access, or strategic advantage.

---

### Section 6. Enduring Institutional Character

Huerta Group LLC shall be built as an enduring institution rather than as a practice dependent upon the continued presence, judgment, reputation, or labor of any single individual.

Its principles, methodologies, systems, knowledge, standards, and responsibilities shall be documented, protected, transferred, and maintained so that they remain effective beyond the direct involvement of its founder.

The Company shall develop leadership structures, operating systems, institutional knowledge, and mechanisms of accountability capable of preserving constitutional continuity regardless of who occupies positions of ownership, management, or authority.

No leader, including the founder, shall be considered greater than the enduring purpose and governing principles of the Company.

---

### Section 7. Non-Negotiable Identity

No matter how large, profitable, technologically advanced, or influential Huerta Group LLC becomes, it must never become a company that sacrifices integrity, people, or long-term organizational health in pursuit of short-term profit, growth, convenience, influence, or technological trends.

This principle shall serve as a foundational test for major decisions, strategies, partnerships, investments, technologies, leadership actions, and commercial opportunities.

Any decision that materially violates this principle shall be considered inconsistent with the identity of Huerta Group LLC, regardless of its legality, profitability, popularity, or apparent strategic value.

---

### Section 8. Definition of Success

The success of Huerta Group LLC shall not be measured solely by revenue, profitability, company size, market share, valuation, influence, technological advancement, or the number of clients served.

Huerta Group LLC succeeds when the organizations it serves become stronger, more resilient, more efficient, more adaptable, and less dependent upon any single individual because of the systems the Company helped them build.

Commercial performance shall remain an important measure of institutional sustainability, but it shall not replace organizational impact as the principal measure of whether the Company is fulfilling its purpose.

Financial sustainability and organizational impact are complementary responsibilities rather than competing objectives. The Company shall maintain the financial strength necessary to preserve its independence, continue serving its clients, invest in its people, advance its knowledge, and fulfill its constitutional purpose across generations.

The highest evidence of successful work is not continued client dependence upon Huerta Group LLC, but the creation of durable capability within the client organization.

---

### Section 9. Continuity of Identity

The Company may change its services, technologies, markets, organizational structure, leadership, ownership arrangements, operating methods, or commercial model as circumstances require.

No such change shall be permitted to alter the Company’s permanent purpose, subordinate its constitutional responsibilities, or transform the Company into an institution inconsistent with the identity established by this Article.

All future interpretations of the Company’s mission, strategic direction, and permissible scope shall begin with this Article.

The burden of demonstrating constitutional consistency shall rest upon those proposing significant organizational change. Any material change to the Company’s structure, strategy, services, technologies, governance, partnerships, or commercial model shall be justified by demonstrating its consistency with this Constitution rather than by requiring the Constitution to justify its continued authority.
