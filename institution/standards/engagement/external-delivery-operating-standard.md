# External Delivery Operating Standard — Generation One

**Classification: Institutional Record** — operating standard (governing
within its subject, subordinate to the Constitution, the Vision Foundation, and
approved Company-wide governance). Produced under `fd-0041`, which records the
Founder Office's acceptance of the External Delivery operating model, its
Generation One product definition, and its validated technical architecture.

**What this standard is for.** So that Huerta Group LLC can know, engage and
learn from external delivery capability **without improvising** qualification,
authority, commercial commitment, acceptance, or accounting truth — and so that
the Founder's possession of a capability never becomes a requirement that the
Founder personally execute it.

**Where it sits.** External Delivery is a Headquarters capability within the
existing client-delivery and operations architecture of the Company. It creates
no organizational function and no institutional domain. It operates **beneath**
the [Client Engagement Operating Standard](client-engagement-operating-standard.md):
every act it governs is subordinate to an engagement, a client decision and an
authorization that standard establishes, and nothing here may satisfy, weaken
or substitute for any of them.

**What it is not.** Not a procurement system, not a marketplace, not a
directory, not a referral network, not a contract or its doctrine, not a
project-management method, not an accounting record, not a rating scheme, and
not authority to perform client work. **Authority to perform work for a client
comes from the client, in writing, under the Client Engagement Operating
Standard — never from a provider record, a sourcing determination or an
assignment made under this standard.**

---

## 1. The distinctions this standard exists to hold

1. **Discovery ≠ qualification.** How Huerta Group learned of a provider —
   referral, personal relationship, Tucson Digital Deals participation,
   marketplace presence, research, prior relationship, direct outreach — is a
   fact about provenance. It establishes nothing about capability, maturity,
   preference or approval.
2. **Evidence ≠ evaluation.** Evidence is what was observed or received.
   Evaluation is Huerta Group's dated interpretation of evidence. Evidence
   accumulates; evaluation concludes; neither rewrites the other.
3. **Provider ≠ universally proven.** Maturity belongs to one provider *for one
   capability*. The same provider may be proven for one capability, tested for
   another and merely discovered for a third.
4. **Tested ≠ proven.** A bounded test is Huerta Group's own exercise. Proven
   rests only on accepted actual external client delivery.
5. **Actual use ≠ automatic advancement.** Performance from an assignment is
   evidence. Only a subsequent explicit evaluation citing it may change
   maturity or posture.
6. **Candidate consideration ≠ assignment.** Considering a provider for a
   requirement qualifies nothing, selects nothing and assigns nothing.
7. **Commercial readiness ≠ Commercial Proposal.** A sourcing determination
   that enough is known for a commercial decision is not the offer, not Founder
   approval, not client acceptance and not implementation authorization.
8. **External assignment ≠ Authorized Work.** An assignment executes a bounded
   component of work the client has already authorized. It creates no
   engagement, client authority, client decision, authorized work, work
   product, deliverable, custody, acknowledgement or closure.
9. **Provider completion ≠ Huerta Group acceptance ≠ Work Product ≠
   Deliverable ≠ client acknowledgement.** Each is a separate recorded act by
   a different party, and none follows from the one before it.
10. **Planning economics ≠ accounting truth.** Quotations and expected costs
    are planning facts for a commercial decision. Bills, expenses,
    liabilities, payments and invoices are QuickBooks' and are never held here.
11. **External execution = ordinary option, not mandate.** External delivery is
    a first-class way to perform work, chosen case by case; it is neither an
    exception nor a default, and it is never selected automatically.
12. **Founder possession of capability ≠ Founder execution requirement.**

## 2. The four layers

**Capability Network → Implementation Scoping & Sourcing → External Delivery
Assignment → Provider Performance Learning.** Provider knowledge informs
sourcing; sourcing informs delivery architecture; delivery architecture may
inform a commercial decision; authorized work permits bounded execution; an
assignment connects a provider to that work; actual execution produces evidence
that may improve provider knowledge. **No transition creates the next state.**
Every arrow is an operator's recorded act.

## 3. Capability Network

**3.1 Providers.** A provider is an external individual or organization that
may supply delivery capability: an individual freelancer or independent
specialist, a business, an agency, a professional firm, or another legitimate
external delivery organization. Provider type is descriptive and establishes no
quality, preference, qualification, maturity or approval.

**3.2 Identity is separate from client identity.** A provider is Huerta
Group's counterparty, not a client-world party. Where one real-world party is
also represented in client records, the correspondence may be recorded as an
identity fact only; it transfers no authority, access, qualification or data in
either direction, and the two records must share the same authentic or
synthetic classification.

**3.3 Incomplete records are legitimate.** A provider may be recorded with a
name and a discovery source and nothing else, and is useful before pricing,
capabilities, evaluation, testing or client work exist. Unknown remains
representable and is never rendered as absence, negation or certainty.

**3.4 Discovery provenance** is preserved with every provider and is kept
distinct — in record and in presentation — from capability and maturity.
Tucson Digital Deals participation, marketplace presence, personal relationship
and referral are provenance, never qualification. No Tucson Digital Deals
integration exists.

**3.5 Capabilities** are named entries in one flat, operator-curated vocabulary.
The capability a contemplated implementation needs, the capability a provider
claims or holds, and the capability evidence establishes are three different
facts joined by that one vocabulary.

**3.6 Evidence** is durable and append-only: provenance, work samples,
references, credentials or licences, bounded Huerta Group tests, quotation
interactions, prior observation, assignment performance, security or access
behaviour, and other capability-relevant observation — each with its provider,
its capability where relevant, its kind, context, source, observation or
reference, relevant date, and who recorded it and when. A mistaken record is
corrected by a later record that names it; nothing is edited or deleted.

**3.7 Evaluation** is a deliberate, dated, attributed interpretation of cited
evidence for one provider capability: maturity, sourcing posture, strengths,
limitations, material unknowns, risk considerations and rationale. Evaluations
are append-only. A later evaluation may lower maturity or change posture; it
never destroys the prior one.

**3.8 Maturity** per provider capability is **Discovered → Evaluated → Tested →
Proven**, with Unknown for a capability not yet characterised. Maturity is
evidence-based, contextual, reversible, and **derived from the authoritative
evaluation history** — never a separately held current value.
- *Discovered*: the capability is claimed, observed or inferred; no evaluation
  exists.
- *Evaluated*: an evaluation exists resting on evidence other than a test or an
  assignment.
- *Tested*: an evaluation cites a bounded Huerta Group test of that capability.
- *Proven*: an evaluation cites first-party performance evidence from an
  assignment that Huerta Group accepted for that same capability, **and** that
  evaluation is a subsequent explicit act. Performance evidence alone never
  makes a capability Proven.

**3.9 No grades.** No universal provider score, star rating, ranking,
approved-vendor badge, or permanent grade exists. Any provider-level summary is
derived from capability-specific state and may not obscure it. "Would consider
again" is a dated posture, not a grade.

**3.10 Network development** proceeds as Discover → Record → Characterize →
Gather Evidence → Evaluate → Optional Test → Actual Use → Learn. This is an
operating progression, not an enforced lifecycle; steps may be skipped or
repeated, and the only ordering rules are the evidential ones in §3.8.

**3.11 Bounded non-client tests** are capability-specific exercises with a
stated purpose, a bounded requirement and an observed result. A test is not
client work, is not an assignment, creates no client authority, and can
support Tested but never Proven.

## 4. Implementation Scoping & Sourcing

**4.1 Origin.** Scoping attaches to Possible Work, and where legitimate to an
engagement that originated from that Possible Work. An Organizational Systems
Assessment is never a prerequisite. More than one contemplated implementation
may be scoped for one Possible Work.

**4.2 The bounded workflow** — contemplated implementation → defined outcome →
decomposed requirements → required capabilities → candidate execution methods
→ material unknowns → sourcing and evaluation where necessary → costs,
dependencies and risks → delivery architecture → commercial readiness → Founder
commercial decision through the existing Commercial Proposal path, or do not
pursue — is an operating reading, not a workflow engine and not a sales
pipeline.

**4.3 Requirements** decompose the implementation into bounded components, each
with its outcome, constraints, dependencies, required capabilities, candidate
and selected execution methods, external-resource need, sourcing status,
expected economics where known, and risk and control considerations.

**4.4 Execution method** — the architecture by which Huerta Group contemplates
or performs work — is one of: Founder or internal; AI- or software-assisted
internal; external subcontractor; hybrid; client-retained provider
coordination; independently retained licensed or specialised professional;
unresolved. It confers no qualification, creates no assignment and asserts no
contractual sufficiency. No method is a default, none is inferred, and none is
selected automatically. Execution method is distinct from the method by which
an existing deliverable reaches a client.

**4.5 Material unknowns are first-class.** Information not yet known is
recorded as unknown, with what would resolve it, and is never converted into
false certainty. *Unknown ≠ none. Unresolved ≠ negative. Not yet obtained ≠
does not exist.*

**4.6 Candidate consideration** associates a provider capability with one
requirement: reason considered, suitability, unknowns, quotation where known
with its date, validity and source, risks and dependencies, and selection
posture with its rationale. It qualifies, selects and assigns nothing.

**4.7 Planning economics.** Provider quotations, expected external cost,
expected direct or software cost and related figures are operational planning
facts, labelled as such, preserved with the basis and date they rest on, and
snapshotted with each readiness disposition so a later reader can reconstruct
what informed a commercial decision. They are never bills, expenses,
liabilities, payments or invoices; QuickBooks remains authoritative for all
accounting truth, and nothing here duplicates or synchronises with it.

**4.8 Ordinary proposal work versus paid scoping — the substance test.**
Ordinary proposal development may include reasonable research, straightforward
provider quotations, software-price checking and similar work principally
necessary to price understandable implementation. Paid scoping is indicated
when reliable pricing requires material professional analysis necessary to
determine what the solution itself should be. **No fixed hour, fee, dollar
threshold, complexity score or formula exists or may be introduced.**

**4.9 Dispositions.** A scoping is *being scoped*, *unresolved*, *ready for a
commercial decision*, *paid scoping indicated*, or *do not pursue*. Dispositions
are recorded as append-only history with who, when and why; the current
disposition is derived from that history. *Paid scoping indicated* records that
the current scoping cannot responsibly proceed without separately authorized
professional scoping; it authorizes nothing, creates nothing, and **does not
close the scoping**. Only *do not pursue* closes it. No disposition creates,
pre-populates or alters a Commercial Proposal; readiness offers deliberate
navigation to the existing Commercial Proposal path and nothing more.

**4.10 No turnaround.** Timestamps are preserved so that elapsed time can be
learned from authentic use. No standard duration, target or service level
exists.

## 5. External Delivery Assignment

**5.1 Subordination.** An assignment connects one provider capability to one
bounded component of Authorized Work inside an engagement that is presently
eligible for execution under the Client Engagement Operating Standard. It
cannot exist against Possible Work, a proposal, or work not so authorized, and
it creates or modifies no client authority.

**5.2 Content.** The bounded scope, execution method, expectations,
dependencies and constraints, and a proportionate acceptance basis explicit
enough for Huerta Group to decide whether the output meets the requirement —
without enterprise quality bureaucracy.

**5.3 Readiness.** Actual external client execution remains gated. Nine
concerns are visible for every assignment: underlying client authorization;
client-side authority for the delivery arrangement; provider-side contractual
basis; confidentiality and data handling; intellectual-property and
work-product chain; security, system or physical access controls; licensing or
professional restrictions; assignment-specific scope; acceptance basis. The
first, the eighth and the ninth are structural facts of the assignment and are
derived from it; the other six are recorded, each as unresolved, established,
not applicable, or blocked, with a reference to where the basis lives.
**Unresolved never means satisfied. Readiness is derived, never stored, and
the software records an operator's asserted basis; it establishes no legal
sufficiency**, which remains with counsel, Administration & Compliance and the
Founder Office.

**5.4 Execution.** Preparation → readiness → execution → provider completion →
Huerta Group review → *accepted*, *rework required*, or *rejected or
escalated*; cancellation where closure semantics permit. Every transition is an
explicit act; none follows from an unrelated record. Execution, completion and
review history is append-only, so a rework cycle preserves the earlier
completion, the review, the deficiency, the resubmission and the final outcome.

**5.5 Completion and acceptance.** Provider completion means only that the
provider has represented the assigned output as complete for review. Huerta
Group acceptance is a separate explicit disposition. Acceptance creates no work
product, deliverable, custody, acknowledgement, client decision, authorized
work or closure. Accepted output may afterwards be associated with a Work
Product created through the existing authoritative act, within the same
authorized work; linking is not creating.

## 6. Provider Performance Learning

After a meaningful assignment review, Huerta Group may record performance
evidence for the provider capability used: requirement satisfaction, quality and
rework, timeliness, communication, security and access compliance, integration
burden, problems, the acceptance outcome, and whether Huerta Group would
consider the provider again. It is historical evidence and nothing else: it
changes no maturity, no posture, no preference. A later explicit evaluation may
cite it and establish current maturity and posture. Current sourcing posture
may change while historical evidence remains.

## 7. Authority boundaries

- **Client Engagement** remains authoritative for engagement, client authority,
  client decisions, authorized work, work product, deliverable, acknowledgement
  and closure. Nothing here satisfies an activation prerequisite.
- **Corporate Operations & Command Management** owns the operating requirement
  this standard serves.
- **Administration & Compliance** owns the administrative and compliance
  records readiness references point to.
- **Accounting / QuickBooks** owns all bills, expenses, liabilities, payments
  and invoices.
- **Counsel** owns legal sufficiency; this standard invents no subcontract,
  intellectual-property, confidentiality, licensing or professional-restriction
  doctrine.
- **Founder Office** retains the commercial decision, paid-scoping
  authorization and every reserved determination; the capability prepares
  information and records outcomes.

Access is Headquarters-only. No client, no provider and no public party reads
any record made under this standard, and a client-side operator's engagement
access reveals only the minimum provider identity an assignment requires.

## 8. How these rules are held

Held structurally rather than by convention: history that is append-only;
current state that is derived from history rather than held beside it;
readiness that is computed from recorded concerns; transitions that occur only
as explicit recorded acts; attribution and time set by the system, never
supplied by the caller; deletion absent everywhere; and authentic and synthetic
records that can never be mixed. Evidence and evaluations, dispositions and
assignment events are never rewritten; a correction is a later record that
names the earlier one.

## 9. Generation One exclusions

Not designed, not implemented: enterprise procurement; public provider
directory; provider marketplace; referral network; Tucson Digital Deals
integration; RFQ or RFP platform; purchase orders; accounts payable; provider
invoicing or payment processing; contract management; project management;
workforce scheduling; time tracking; utilization planning; automatic provider
selection; AI provider matching or ranking; provider portal; changes to the
Client Platform, the public website or the Organizational Systems Assessment;
generalized ratings; generalized skills taxonomy; workflow engine; document
management; accounting integration; any sourcing service level.

## 10. What this standard does not decide

- **Subcontract, confidentiality, intellectual-property and licensing
  instruments** — Administration & Compliance and counsel.
- **Whether any particular external arrangement is legally sufficient** —
  counsel; the readiness record asserts, it does not establish.
- **Commercial terms and Founder approval** — Founder Office, through the
  Commercial Proposal path the Client Engagement Operating Standard governs.
- **Accounting treatment of any external cost** — QuickBooks.
- **Any later generation** — provider access, client visibility, cross-matter
  analytics or automation of any kind require their own authorization.
