# Client Engagement Operating Standard — Generation One P0

**Classification: Institutional Record** — operating standard (governing
within its subject, subordinate to the Constitution, the Vision Foundation, and
approved Company-wide governance). Produced under `fd-0037`.

**What this standard is for.** So that Huerta Group LLC can accept its first
outside client **without improvising** authority, scope, confidentiality,
custody, provenance, or institutional rules.

**Scope, extended under `fd-0038`.** It governed the path from an outside
organization becoming known through to engagement activation. It now governs the
**whole operating chain**: *opportunity → Founder disposition → engagement
authorization → engagement operation → evidence and work → decisions →
deliverables → closure.* **The activation gate at §4 remains the hinge**, and
nothing downstream of it may be recorded for an engagement that has not passed
it.

**What it is not.** Not a consulting lifecycle, not a CRM, not a
project-management system, not a contract or its doctrine, not a pricing or
commercial policy, not a diagnostic method, and not authority to perform client
work. **Authorization to act for a client comes from the client, in writing, per
§4 — never from this standard and never from a record made under it.**

---

## 1. The nine distinctions, which this standard exists to hold

These are held **structurally**, not by convention. §8 records how each is
mechanically enforced.

1. **Organization ≠ Engagement.** An organization exists whether or not any
   engagement does. An engagement is a bounded, authorized body of work for one
   organization. An organization may have none, one, or several over time.
2. **Person ≠ Organization.** People are recorded as people. Their connection
   to an organization is a separate, dated relationship that can change without
   either the person or the organization changing.
3. **Opportunity ≠ Engagement.** An opportunity is possible work. **Accepting
   an opportunity does not create an engagement** (§4).
4. **Diagnostic ≠ Engagement.** A diagnostic is something that may be performed
   *within* an engagement. An engagement is not a diagnostic and does not become
   one.
5. **Evidence ≠ Finding.** Registering evidence records that material exists,
   where it came from, and under what conditions it is held. **It asserts
   nothing about what the evidence shows** (§6).
6. **Recommendation ≠ Authorization.** Nothing Huerta Group produces authorizes
   its own implementation. Authorization is the client's separate act.
7. **Client Question ≠ Client Decision.** A question reserved to the client's
   authority is not answered by asking it, and is not answered by Huerta Group.
8. **Work Product ≠ Deliverable.** Internal working material is not a
   deliverable, and does not become one by being useful.
9. **Client-specific learning ≠ Huerta Group institutional knowledge.** §7.

**Three further distinctions the operating chain requires**, added under
`fd-0038` and held the same way:

10. **Authorized Work ≠ Recommendation.** Work traces to a **client decision**,
    never to a recommendation. A recommendation carries a field whose only
    permitted value is that it authorizes nothing.
11. **Metadata ≠ raw confidential evidence.** A registration records that
    material exists and how it is held. It never carries the material.
12. **A reference to confidential material ≠ custody of it.** The reference may
    live in a governed record; the material may not.

**And one rule that makes the chain honest end to end.** A client decision may
authorize only what **its own authority supports**: the capacity it relies on
must be *established*, and the person deciding must be the person who holds it.
Authority is not conferred by deciding.

## 2. Objects, and the narrowest representation of each

**Five collections carry independent identity.** Everything else is a property
of an engagement and has no life apart from it — recorded within it rather than
as a separate object, because a separate object would assert an independence
that does not exist.

| Object | Representation |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | independent record |
| **Person** | independent record |
| **Organization–Person Relationship** | independent record — a person may relate to more than one organization, and a relationship may end while both parties persist |
| **Opportunity** | independent record, carrying its **Founder disposition** |
| **Engagement** | independent record, carrying its **scope and boundaries**, **client and Huerta Group authority**, **confidentiality and access boundary**, **custody references**, **evidence registrations**, **chronology**, and **seams** |

**No object is created because a concept exists.** Founder disposition, scope,
authority, confidentiality, custody reference, evidence registration and
chronology are all recorded **inside** the record whose existence gives them
meaning.

## 3. The flow

> Organization → Person and Organization–Person Relationship → Opportunity →
> Founder disposition → **acceptance prerequisites** → Engagement

Each step is durable and recorded before the next may rely on it. **A step
skipped is not a step performed.**

## 4. The engagement activation gate

**This is the load-bearing rule of the standard.**

**A Founder disposition of *accept* does not create an engagement.** It records
that the Company is willing. Willingness is not authorization.

**An engagement may become `active` only when durable written authorization
establishes all eight of the following**, each recorded with the evidence that
establishes it:

1. **the parties;**
2. **the authorized body of work, or the scope it incorporates by reference;**
3. **material exclusions and boundaries** — what is *not* authorized;
4. **the person or authority acting for the client;**
5. **the commercial basis, or an authoritative reference to it;**
6. **authorization for Huerta Group to perform the agreed work;**
7. **the applicable confidentiality and access obligations, or an authoritative
   reference to them;**
8. **evidence that the authorization was accepted or executed** — that it was
   agreed, not merely sent.

**Where any prerequisite is absent, the engagement does not activate, and the
record preserves which prerequisite is missing and why.** The Company does not
proceed and then reconcile; it stops and says what is missing. An engagement
that cannot activate is a truthful record of a real condition, not a failure of
the system.

**This standard does not establish the instrument** that carries the
authorization — agreement, statement of work, contract or otherwise. **That is
Administration & Compliance's to establish**, and until it exists this limb is
not satisfiable in practice however complete the record structure is.

## 5. Authority

**Client authority is never inferred from participation.** That a person spoke
to Huerta Group, supplied material, attended, or is senior establishes nothing
about what they may authorize. Authority is recorded **per capacity**, and each
capacity is recorded as established or **unresolved** — never assumed:

- who may **authorize the engagement**;
- who may **provide access**;
- who may make the **organizational decisions** the work depends on;
- who may **approve changes** to scope;
- who may **receive and approve deliverables**;
- who may **authorize implementation**.

**Unresolved is a valid and often correct value.** It is a finding about the
engagement, not a gap to be filled by assumption.

**Huerta Group authority is recorded as current assignment, never as
structure.** The Founder may presently hold several responsibilities at once;
that is recorded as **who currently holds them**, and the record distinguishes
the responsibilities themselves — engagement lead, analyst, consultant,
contractor, administrator, other authorized operator — so that a future holder
can be recorded without the model changing. **A one-person Company is a present
condition and is never encoded as the Company's shape.**

## 6. Evidence, custody, and isolation

**Registering evidence asserts only that the material exists and how it is
held.** Every registration carries: the organization; the engagement; the
source identity and type; who or what supplied it, where relevant; date and
time context where known; the custody reference; the access and confidentiality
condition; and the authority or ownership context where relevant.

**Registering evidence creates no finding, and evidence existing authorizes no
inference beyond it.**

**Five custody classes**, and the rule attaching to each:

| Class | What it is | Where it may be held |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HG_ENGAGEMENT_RECORD` | Huerta Group-authored governed structured engagement records | **repository-native permitted** |
| `CLIENT_SOURCE` | raw client-supplied source material | **outside the institutional corpus — always** |
| `INTERNAL_WORKING` | Huerta Group internal working material about the client | **outside the institutional corpus** |
| `DELIVERABLE` | material transferred or intended for transfer to the client | **outside the institutional corpus** |
| `LEARNING_CANDIDATE` | a proposed abstracted institutional-learning candidate | **outside the corpus until admitted** (§7) |

**Raw confidential client material never enters the ordinary institutional
corpus.** Not as an attachment, not as a quotation of substance, not
"temporarily," and not because it would be convenient. **A governed engagement
record references it** — with provenance sufficient to establish organization,
engagement, source identity, custody location, access boundary, confidentiality
condition, and date context — **and the material itself stays where it is
held.**

**Isolation requirements**, which any custody mechanism must satisfy before
client material is placed in it: client material attributable to the correct
organization and engagement; **private by default**; an explicit access
boundary; **no cross-client retrieval by default**; operator access **scopable
rather than universally granted**; **no automatic inclusion in institution-wide
search, indexing, or AI context**; and **no automatic admission to institutional
knowledge or methodology.**

## 7. The institutional-learning boundary

**No client engagement material becomes Huerta Group institutional knowledge or
methodology automatically. Ever.**

> Client engagement → **candidate abstracted learning** → governance review →
> institutional admission

**Only the boundary exists at P0.** A learning candidate may be recorded as a
candidate. **Nothing admits it**, and no path exists by which it admits itself.
Admission is a separate governed act that this standard does not create.

## 8. How these rules are enforced rather than merely stated

`institution/validation/validate-engagements.mjs`, in the repository's
verification gate, mechanically enforces: the five collections and their
disjoint identifiers; that every reference resolves; that an engagement carries
an organization and an originating opportunity; **that no engagement is `active`
unless all eight §4 prerequisites are recorded as established**; that an
engagement which cannot activate records what blocks it; that every authority
capacity carries a value including `unresolved`; that every custody reference
carries the §6 provenance fields; **that no `CLIENT_SOURCE`, `INTERNAL_WORKING`
or `DELIVERABLE` custody reference points inside the repository**; that evidence
registrations carry no findings or conclusions; that learning candidates are
candidates only; and **that no file in the governed corpus is marked as carrying
client-confidential material.**

**The operating chain is enforced the same way.** Engagement work may not be
recorded against an engagement that has not activated; a finding must cite
registered evidence; a recommendation must rest on findings and authorizes
nothing; a client decision must rely on an established capacity held by the
decider; authorized work must trace to a decision and is refused if it cites a
recommendation; a work product must trace to authorized work and cannot be
marked a deliverable; a deliverable must derive from a work product;
participant-evidence records carrying content fields are refused; contradictions
have no resolved state; and closure may not be *complete* while any condition
remains unresolved.

**A rule that is not enforced is a rule the first busy day removes.**

## 9. What this standard does not decide

- **The authorization instrument** — Administration & Compliance (§4).
- **Official Huerta Group business email identity** — Administration &
  Compliance; a first-engagement prerequisite.
- **Where raw client material is actually held.** Decided in principle by
  `adr-0021` and placed by `adr-0022`: a separate application and repository
  with its own Supabase project, local while it is being built and hosted when
  authorized. **The current Supabase project owns inquiry records only**, and
  `adr-0003` holds that client-confidential data never shares a runtime or
  repository with the public website. **No client custody store exists yet**,
  and none may be improvised.
- **Scoped operator access.** `adr-0006` introduces authentication only when a
  real capability requires it. Until then, access to any store is by shared
  credential and **cannot be scoped per operator** — an isolation requirement
  in §6 that is presently unmet.
- **Retention, deletion, and disclosure doctrine** — owner and legal authority,
  not architectural.
