## Prior state

Huerta Group had no governed operating architecture for outside client
work. Every institutional program to that point concerned the institution
itself — its repository, its governance, its methodology, its own
diagnostic. `fd-0037` records the instrument as *"the first instrument
outside the Organizational Systems Methodology program and the first
concerning outside clients."* Taking Client #1 would have meant
improvising authority, scope, confidentiality, custody, provenance and
institutional rules for each engagement.

## Change or development

A minimum governed client-engagement operating backbone was constructed
and admitted to the repository: a governing operating standard, a schema,
a registry, and mechanical validators. Nine distinctions were fixed
structurally rather than by convention — Organization not Engagement,
Person not Organization, Opportunity not Engagement, Diagnostic not
Engagement, Evidence not Finding, Recommendation not Authorization,
Client Question not Client Decision, Work Product not Deliverable, and
client-specific learning not Huerta Group institutional knowledge.

Two rules of that architecture are load-bearing and were mechanically
enforced from the outset: **opportunity acceptance does not create an
active engagement**, and an engagement may activate only where durable
written authorization establishes all eight prerequisites — parties,
authorized work or incorporated scope, material exclusions, the person
acting for the client, commercial basis, authorization to perform,
confidentiality and access obligations, and evidence that authorization
was accepted.

## Implications

The institution can describe a client engagement in governed terms
before it has one. The readiness standard fixed here — that READY may
not be declared merely because the software works, and that NOT READY
must be reported while any external administrative dependency remains
unresolved — governs every later readiness assessment of this
capability. Two deficiencies were returned rather than approximated
(CUSTODY-01, ISO-01) and became the subject of `adr-0021`; see
`evo-0008`.

## Dependencies

Depended on the repository governance architecture (`evo-0001`) and on
the Organizational Systems Methodology program having reached a frozen
diagnostic (`evo-0006`), which freed the Founder Office to open a
program outside it. Everything in the client-engagement domain that
follows depends on this.

## Historical notes

The instrument required the mechanics to be exercised with hypothetical
structural fixtures only, and expressly forbade fabricating stakeholder
testimony, client authority, executed acceptance, confidential source
material, findings, recommendations, decisions or deliverables. The
capability was therefore built and proven without any client existing —
which is why its first authentic client remained a separate,
administratively gated question.
