# evo-0004 — First Venture Formally Admitted: Huerta Group Publishing (FD-V1)

- **Date:** 2026-08-12
- **Institutional domain:** governance
- **Type of evolution:** governance_determination

## Prior state

Huerta Group LLC had no venture concept in its governed corpus: no
definition, admission process, lifecycle, registry, or recorded
parent–venture relationship (Corporate Platform Alignment Audit
D-1/D-2; fd-0006 FO-02/FO-03). Huerta Group Publishing operated as a
de facto initiative under Founder authorization — an operational
production platform in its own repository (history from 2026-07-02),
publishing under the legal entity "Huerta Group LLC" with the
relationship evidenced only by code constants and venture-internal
records. The capability registry carried `publishing-platform` as
`conceptual` with no governing authority.

## Change or development

The Founder Office approved the Corporate Venture Governance — Phase 1
Blueprint and, under it, FD-V1: Huerta Group Publishing was admitted
as the **first formally chartered venture** of Huerta Group LLC —an
operational venture, not an independent company — with an approved
Charter (v1.0), an exhaustive two-grant capability register
(publisher-of-record presentation; venture name usage), delegated
operational authority scoped by the Charter, the FD-V1 reserved
parent-authority set, and a per-dimension lifecycle status. FD-V9
(Venture Graduation Principle: never automatic) and FD-V10 (Venture
Success Principle: fulfillment of chartered purpose, not
organizational form) were determined alongside. The repository gained
the venture governance area (`institution/governance/ventures/`), the
venture registry/schema/validator wired into the canonical gate, and
the reconciled `publishing-platform` capability entry.

## Reason or evidence

The accepted Corporate Platform Alignment Audit identified the
ungoverned parent–venture relationship as the institution's largest
truth gap; fd-0006 reserved it to this program. Evidence: the Phase 3
instrument (approval transmission), fd-0007 (verbatim determinations),
the admitted blueprint and charter, the venture repository's
production evidence (baseline `ab0dac8`, 2026-08-11), and the passing
verification gate on the admitting change.

## Implications

Future ventures inherit a complete admission architecture (blueprint
§23) without governance redesign; the admitted-venture set is
validator-enforced, so venture records cannot appear without their
authorizing determination. Parent-platform representation (FD-V4),
register topology (FD-V2), general name-use policy (FD-V3), and the
remaining FD-V5–FD-V8 matters stay open. No commercial, launch, or
distribution authorization exists.
