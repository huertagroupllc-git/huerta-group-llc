# evo-0010 — Huerta Group Acquired a Governed Operating Model for Client Work, and Froze It

- **Institutional domain:** operations

## Prior state

The institution had the *architecture* for client work and not yet the
*operating model*. `evo-0007` records the governed client-engagement
architecture established under `fd-0037` — nine structural distinctions,
eight activation prerequisites, a readiness standard. `evo-0008` records
the confidentiality, custody and isolation architecture; `evo-0009`
records the pre-launch footing it runs on. Generation One had been
constructed and validated as the first authenticated institutional
system (`evo-0005`).

What had not been settled was how an operator would actually work an
engagement. The workspace carried **eight peer destinations** until
2026-08-31, so reading an engagement meant answering a question about
Huerta Group's filing before one could be asked about the client's
matter. No end-to-end exercise of the whole lifecycle — opportunity
through closure — had been carried out and accepted.

## Change or development

Headquarters Client Engagement **Generation Two** is established,
validated, accepted and frozen as Huerta Group's governed operating
baseline for client-engagement work.

**Its role.** Generation Two is the operating model. It does not replace
repository governance authority, Corporate Operations custody,
QuickBooks accounting authority, or the governed domain model beneath
it.

**How it was validated.** The client-engagement lifecycle was exercised
end to end under governed synthetic validation. The system distinguishes
what a body of engagement history *is* — `record_class` is `authentic`
or `synthetic_exercise`, and synthetic history is *"never to be
represented as authentic client history."* No authentic client
information has been admitted.

**Source-of-truth boundaries carried into the baseline**, as Headquarters
itself states them: the **Corporate Operations register** answers what
the company is carrying and what needs the Founder; **QuickBooks**
answers what has been billed, paid or is owed; the **institutional
repository** answers what governs the company and what the Founder
Office has determined; Headquarters holds governed client-engagement
operating records within its own domain. Raw confidential client source
material remains under the custody and isolation architecture of
`evo-0008` rather than ordinary institutional corpus treatment.

**Semantic boundaries held structurally, not by convention.** Each
distinction is carried by a separate governed record, which is why none
of them can quietly collapse into another: organization apart from
engagement; opportunity apart from engagement; held material apart from
registered evidence apart from finding; recommendation apart from client
decision apart from authorized work; work product apart from deliverable;
and the engagement grant that confers access held apart from the client
authority that confers the power to decide — access is not authority.

**Operator principles.** Plain business language; obvious primary
actions; compact status; reduced standing explanation; progressive
disclosure; strong information hierarchy; governance precision preserved
beneath the ordinary operator layer rather than removed from it; and
complexity absorbed by the product rather than transferred to the
operator. The accepted navigation is **Today · Clients · Company ·
Guides**, and within an engagement **Overview · Files & Findings ·
Decisions & Work · Reports · Closure**.

## Implications

Huerta Group can operate a client engagement to a governed standard
rather than improvise one, and the standard is now a thing an operator
uses rather than a document they consult. **Discretionary Generation Two
development is frozen**: further change requires concrete operational
evidence, bounded launch hardening, or another independently governed
authorization. Routine fixes and bounded hardening do not themselves
become evolution.

What remains outstanding is not in the product. Admitting an authentic
first client is gated administratively, and by the extraction obligation
`evo-0009` scheduled — the baseline is validated, and it is validated
synthetically.

## Dependencies

Depends on `evo-0007` for the architecture it implements, on `evo-0008`
for the custody and isolation guarantees it operates under, and on
`evo-0009` for the infrastructure footing it currently runs on. The
extraction obligation recorded in `evo-0009` is unaffected by this
acceptance and remains due before authentic client information.
