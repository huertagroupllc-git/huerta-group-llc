## Prior state

Huerta Group LLC held an approved Methodology Foundation v1 with an approved
principle-level work progression (mr-0004) and eight Method Library records,
and recorded its own incompleteness: "Detailed organizational-discovery
methodology" and "Organizational assessment methodology" were listed among
missing methodology substance (§10.1), and no diagnostic method, instrument,
or worked example of diagnosing an unfamiliar organization existed. The
institution's organizational-systems practice was evidenced only in its work
on itself (the Repository Foundation Project, venture governance, the
Corporate Command Center) and on its first venture, with the three audits
closest to diagnostic instruments unpreserved. Evidence: Methodology
Foundation v1 §2.2, §10.1; the Generation One Discovery Report §1.5.

## Change or development

Under two Founder Office instruments (Generation One — Discovery; Stage 2),
the institution moved from evidence discovery about its own practice to a
**frozen provisional Organizational Diagnostic**:

- a repository-first Discovery Report across the corporate corpus, the
  Command Center runtime and Huerta Group Publishing, returned as workbench
  working material and then admitted as an evidentiary Institutional Record
  (`fd-0019`);
- four of six accepted historical instruments recovered as authentic
  originals and preserved canonically; Deliverables A and A.1 recorded as
  unavailable and not reconstructed;
- a bounded HGP evidence-admission package that proposed and did not
  self-admit;
- the Organizational Diagnostic — Generation One draft with ten provisional
  working instruments, frozen by commit `076440c` (SHA-256
  `6fb2fb84…c4a9a73`);
- a self-application dry run on Huerta Group LLC against the frozen text,
  recording hidden-knowledge, access, terminology and structural-assumption
  findings, fourteen held behaviours and thirteen deficiencies, with the
  advancement logic refusing to advance to design on documents alone;
- seventeen corrections proposed separately from the tested draft, none
  applied.

Stage 2 was accepted as complete by the Founder Office (`fd-0020`).

## Implications

established method, not externally validated, not a Method Library record**

automatically adopted, and any adoption occurs only through Methodology
Foundation v1 §6.4. The proposed progression refinement remains a proposal to
mr-0004, which is unamended. The external-validation gap is total and must
remain explicit in every use of the draft.
