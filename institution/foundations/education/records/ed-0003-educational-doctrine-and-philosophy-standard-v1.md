## Purpose and scope

Establishes the enduring principles governing Huerta Group education —
capability, competency, application, systems thinking, judgment,
evidence, governance, AI responsibility, knowledge transfer, and
continuous improvement. The Foundation source (§3) is authoritative;
this record summarizes it and is corrected by it wherever they differ.

## Approved content

**Governing doctrine (§3.1)** — the fourteen enduring principles:
practical capability over passive consumption; demonstrable competency
over simple completion (completion documents participation, never
establishes competency by itself); application in real organizational
contexts; approved knowledge as the instructional basis (content
derived from methodology, IP, constitutional doctrine, or
institutional knowledge stays faithful and traceable to approved
sources); enduring principles before changing tools; structured
thinking; systems thinking; responsible judgment; governance
awareness; evidence and verification (claims supported by evidence
proportionate to consequence); knowledge transfer (reducing avoidable
dependence on individual instructors, consultants, tools, or
undocumented experience); continuous improvement; human responsibility
in technology and AI use (humans remain responsible for consequential
interpretation, judgment, approval, and action); and sustainable
capability (strengthening the ability to continue learning, adapting,
governing, and improving after an intervention ends).

**Doctrine versus program design (§3.2).** Doctrine establishes
enduring institutional principles; individual program design applies
them to a particular audience, capability, context, duration, delivery
model, and assessment strategy. Programs may vary substantially while
remaining compliant with the same doctrine, and no single
instructional sequence, delivery format, course length, assessment
type, or technology is universally required.

## Limitations and open questions

- Summary record: Foundation §3 is authoritative and corrects this
  record wherever they differ.
- Public adaptation of doctrine requires approved brand and disclosure
  review (§18.3); no public adaptation exists.

## Technical implementation

Approved as governed content and metadata only. Doctrine authorizes no
program, offering, or runtime behavior.
