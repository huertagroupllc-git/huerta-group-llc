## Purpose and scope

Defines education, workforce development, capability development,
competency-based learning, organizational education, professional
education, and knowledge transfer — terminology, institutional
relationships, exclusions, and authority boundaries. The Foundation
source (§2) is authoritative; this record summarizes it and is
corrected by it wherever they differ.

## Approved content

**Definitions (§§2.1–2.7).** Education is the governed process through
which approved knowledge, methodology, judgment, skills, practices,
and institutional principles are transformed into structured
experiences intended to develop demonstrable capability — broader than
a course, tied to no particular delivery format. Workforce development
is the systematic development of individual and collective
capabilities required to perform, improve, govern, and sustain work in
modern organizations, and is not limited to employment placement,
vocational instruction, or job-specific software training. Capability
development improves the ability of an individual, team, or
organization to produce desired outcomes under relevant conditions —
capability requires more than knowledge. Competency-based learning
bases advancement, recognition, or verification on demonstrated
evidence of defined competency rather than exposure, attendance,
elapsed time, or completion alone. Organizational education develops
shared capability within an organization; professional education
develops professional capability without implying licensure,
accreditation, statutory recognition, or regulated-practice
qualification unless separately and expressly authorized. Knowledge
transfer is the governed movement of usable knowledge from an
authoritative source to another individual, team, organization,
system, or generation — requiring more than access to information.

**Institutional relationships (§§2.8–2.14).** Education determines how
approved methodology is taught, practiced, assessed, reinforced, and
transferred — it may adapt methodology for instruction but never
redefines its substantive meaning (§2.8). Education translates
approved IP into learning experiences without determining legal or
institutional ownership (§2.9); consumes institutional knowledge
through traceable adaptations that never replace their sources
(§2.10); reinforces and is reinforced by consulting without
subordination either way (§2.11); uses technology that never defines
doctrine, competency, assessment validity, or quality (§2.12);
complements managed services where knowledge transfer or
organizational independence is an approved objective (§2.13); and
never treats participation or completion as evidence that an
organizational outcome occurred (§2.14).

**Exclusions (§2.15).** Education & Workforce Development is not any
of the thirteen listed things — from a substitute for Methodology
authority or IP governance through an accreditation body, a licensing
authority, a guarantee of employment or performance, or authority to
collect learner data without approved operational, privacy, and legal
governance.
