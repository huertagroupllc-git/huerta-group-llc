contents and reserved structure of this directory only. It creates no
institutional authority and states no operational doctrine.

**Authority boundary.** OSPA is authority for the surviving text of those
six sections and nothing more. Sections **S05–S08 and S11–S15 are
permanently unavailable** and must never be reconstructed, inferred, or
substituted by professional convention. The foundation is never
represented as complete. The derived `RS05-EV01` evidence packet is not
canonical authority and remains outside the corpus at
`workbench/ospa-reconstruction/`.

## Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation, Version 1 — preserved

This directory holds the Foundation at `CORP-OPS-SEM-FND-V1.md`. It was
formerly a reserved-but-empty location; that reservation is now filled.

- **Title:** Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation — Version 1
- **Authority:** Corporate Operations & Command Management, subject to
  Founder Office governance
- **Nature:** **prospective** operational doctrine — new authority
  adopted going forward, not recovered material
- **Relationship to OSPA:** it must be compatible with the surviving
  recovered canonical OSPA authority in this directory, and **must never
  be represented as recovered OSPA**

**Adopted and preserved.** The Founder Office adopted the Foundation on
2026-08-18 (`fd-0012`); the complete source was supplied and preserved
byte-identical on the same date (`fd-0013`). Digest and custody chronology
are in the adoption record, deliberately **not** in this directory's
`SHA256SUMS`, which covers the six admitted OSPA sections only — keeping
prospective doctrine and recovered OSPA separately verifiable is the point.

**Proposal-era text is preserved as transmitted.** The source still reads
"Prospective Operational Doctrine Proposal — Founder Office Review
Required" and "PENDING FOUNDER OFFICE ADOPTION." That is the document as
it was written, not its current authority. The adoption is a subsequent
institutional act recorded at `fd-0012`; the source was not rewritten to
absorb it. Cite the Foundation together with its adoption record.

**What it was asked to resolve** was recorded, as questions rather than
answers, in
`institution/governance/operations/corporate-operations-semantics-definition-package.md`.
That package has since been **answered and closed** (`fd-0013`), and its
concept-by-concept reconciliation against the Foundation is recorded
there. It is retained as history and states no current doctrine.
