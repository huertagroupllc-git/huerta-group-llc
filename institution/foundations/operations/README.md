# Operations Foundation — Directory Guide

**Classification: Repository Standard scope** — this guide describes the
contents and reserved structure of this directory only. It creates no
institutional authority and states no operational doctrine.

**Authority boundary.** OSPA is authority for the surviving text of those
six sections and nothing more. Sections **S05–S08 and S11–S15 are
permanently unavailable** and must never be reconstructed, inferred, or
substituted by professional convention. The foundation is never
represented as complete. The derived `RS05-EV01` evidence packet is not
canonical authority and remains outside the corpus at
`workbench/ospa-reconstruction/`.

## Reserved location — Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation, Version 1

This directory is the reserved repository location for a future
instrument:

- **Title:** Corporate Operations Semantics Foundation — Version 1
- **Authority:** Corporate Operations & Command Management, subject to
  Founder Office governance
- **Nature:** **prospective** operational doctrine — new authority
  adopted going forward, not recovered material
- **Relationship to OSPA:** it must be compatible with the surviving
  recovered canonical OSPA authority in this directory, and **must never
  be represented as recovered OSPA**

**Adopted, but not held here.** The Founder Office adopted the Foundation
on 2026-08-18 (`fd-0012`). Its **text was not transmitted to the
implementing office**, so this location remains empty and the repository
holds no Foundation (`fd-0012` Deficiency 1).

No draft, outline, placeholder, or partial definition of it exists in this
repository, and none should be created to make the structure look
complete — least of all from the adopting instrument's abstract of the
Foundation's invariants and semantic tests, which is evidence that
adoption occurred and is not the doctrine. Platform & Systems Development
is not authorized to draft its substance; doing so would place Development
in the position of defining institutional operational meaning, which OSPA
S02 Principle 1 reserves to institutional authority.

**What it is expected to resolve** is recorded, as questions rather than
answers, in
`institution/governance/operations/corporate-operations-semantics-definition-package.md`.
That package is the handoff to Corporate Operations & Command Management;
it deliberately contains no Development-authored operational definitions.
