## Purpose and scope

Defines institutional methodology and establishes its approved scope and
exclusions, governing all Huerta Group methodological development and
application.

## Approved definition (§1.1)

Institutional methodology is Huerta Group's governed body of doctrine,
principles, methods, standards, frameworks, models, assessment
approaches, implementation guidance, verification practices, sustainment
practices, and related methodological assets used to understand, assess,
improve, implement, verify, and sustain organizational systems. It
governs how Huerta Group approaches organizational improvement, provides
a consistent basis for reasoning, analysis, decision-making, service
delivery, implementation support, knowledge transfer, and continuous
improvement across industries, technologies, consultants, engagements,
and future institutional applications — and it is an enduring
institutional authority, authoritative independently of any particular
client engagement, service line, employee, technology platform,
educational program, publication, or software product.

## Approved scope domains (§1.2)

Ten approved scope domains (authoritative definitions in the source):
**organizational understanding** · **assessment** · **analysis** ·
**improvement** · **implementation** · **verification** ·
**sustainment** · **knowledge transfer** · **continuous improvement** ·
**client engagements**.

## Approved exclusions (§1.3)

Methodology is not: the institutional Vision; the Constitution; a
current strategic plan; Corporate Headquarters operations; a specific
client's operating model; a catalogue of consulting services; a
collection of software features; a technology architecture; a training
curriculum; a publication; a marketing description; a set of templates
without governing reasoning; a collection of consultant preferences; an
undocumented customary practice; a client-specific recommendation
automatically generalized into institutional doctrine; intellectual
property merely because it was created internally; nor public
methodology merely because a general principle has been disclosed
publicly.

**Technology-support boundary:** technology, AI, automation, software,
documentation, dashboards, assessments, and analytical tools may support
the methodology; they do not independently constitute the methodology
and do not determine its objectives.
