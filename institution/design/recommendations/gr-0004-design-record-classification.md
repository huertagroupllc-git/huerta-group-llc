# gr-0004 — Dedicated Manifest Classification for Design Records

**Classification: Institutional Record** — Governance Recommendation
(non-authoritative; fd-0005). Date: 2026-08-08.

DDRs and GRs are manifested under the general "Institutional Record"
classification. This is accurate but coarse: design decisions may
warrant their own taxonomy value (and eventually a registry/validator
pair mirroring the determinations pattern) once volume justifies it.
**Recommendation:** revisit when the design-record count or a
Design System Foundation admission makes a dedicated classification
worthwhile.
