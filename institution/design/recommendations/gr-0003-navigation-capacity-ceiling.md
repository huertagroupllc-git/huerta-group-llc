# gr-0003 — Navigation Capacity Ceiling

**Classification: Institutional Record** — Governance Recommendation
(non-authoritative; fd-0005). Date: 2026-08-08.

The desktop navigation renders only at the xl breakpoint with a
measured ~20px of slack at the container cap; the in-code record states
any additional nav item will overflow. Phase 1 deliberately made no
navigation-architecture change. **Recommendation:** a Design System
navigation-architecture decision should precede any future public page
or nav addition.
