# Authority and Governing References

Canonical governing documents (linked, never duplicated here — see
`docs/README.md` for the full index and document classifications):

1. **Huerta Group LLC Constitution** — `docs/governance/huerta-group-constitution.md` — the highest internal authority.
2. **Vision Foundation for Development** — `docs/governance/vision-foundation-for-development.md` — translates the Company's identity and long-term direction into strategic guidance for technology.
3. **Other formally approved Company-wide strategy, methodology, policy, and governance documents** — govern their subjects, consistent with 1–2.
4. **Development Standards** — `docs/development/development-standards.md` — and approved architectural decisions govern implementation methods and technical quality.
5. **Individual task instructions** — govern the immediate assignment but may not silently override higher authority.

Rules:

- Read the Vision Foundation before any consequential decision involving architecture, products, platforms, databases, AI, automation, client portals, internal operational systems, knowledge systems, proprietary software, or major interface or content direction.
- The Vision Room determines strategic direction. Development determines how approved objectives are implemented; it may not independently redefine Company identity, purpose, mission, constitutional principles, institutional character, business model, technology/AI/growth philosophy, or governance authority.
- Do not infer broader strategic authority from an immediate task.
- Keep current implementation, planned capability, and long-term vision distinct — public content must never present aspirations as existing capabilities.
- Preserve proportionate flexibility; premature complexity is prohibited.
- When documents at different levels appear to conflict, pause the conflicting portion and request an explicit decision rather than choosing whichever instruction is easiest to implement.
- A task may authorize a clearly stated exception to a lower-level development standard; it may not amend constitutional text or Company-wide strategic authority. Ordinary code changes are never authority to alter governing documents.
- When technical work reveals a strategic or constitutional question, document it and return it to the appropriate room rather than resolving it through code.
