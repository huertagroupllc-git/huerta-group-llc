# Development governance

Before any consequential decision involving architecture, products, platforms, databases, AI, automation, client portals, internal operational systems, knowledge systems, proprietary software, or major interface or content direction, read:

`docs/governance/vision-foundation-for-development.md`

That document is the authoritative strategic reference for development. It is subordinate only to the formally ratified Huerta Group LLC Constitution, and authoritative over website specifications, product requirements, technical designs, and implementation decisions.

Rules it establishes:

- The Vision Room determines strategic direction. Development determines how approved objectives are implemented.
- Development may not independently redefine Company identity, mission, business model, or institutional vision.
- Do not infer broader strategic authority from an immediate task.
- Keep current implementation, planned capability, and long-term vision distinct — public content must never present aspirations as existing capabilities.
- Preserve proportionate flexibility; premature complexity is prohibited.

The document describes long-term possibilities; it does not itself authorize building them.
