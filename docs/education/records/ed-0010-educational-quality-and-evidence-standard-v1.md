## Purpose and scope

Defines the initial quality dimensions and metadata required for
governed educational development — accuracy, fidelity, coherence,
accessibility, relevance, application, validity, feedback, evidence,
improvement, version control, and limitations. The Foundation source
(§13) is authoritative; this record summarizes it and is corrected by
it wherever they differ.

## Approved content

**The thirteen quality dimensions (§13.1):** accuracy (content
accurately represents its approved source material and relevant
verified knowledge); methodological fidelity (adaptations preserve the
meaning, boundaries, and intended use of the governing methodology);
instructional coherence (objectives, experiences, practice,
assessments, and outcomes logically aligned); accessibility (approved
accessibility requirements respected — no complete accessibility
standard established); relevance; practical application (application
appropriate to the capability where practical demonstration is
necessary); assessment validity (evidence and methods support the
conclusion being made — a knowledge quiz is never proof of complex
applied competency without justification); learner feedback (collected
and reviewed where authorized; satisfaction stays distinct from
competency and outcome evidence); instructor or facilitator review;
outcome evidence (claims supported by evidence proportionate to the
claim); continuous improvement (documented review, evidence-based
refinement, versioning, controlled change); version control
(participants and systems can identify the applicable version of
consequential assets); and known limitations (material limitations,
assumptions, unresolved questions, and boundaries recorded where
relevant).

**Minimum quality metadata (§13.2).** Records may carry quality-review
status, review type, reviewer authority or role, review date, accuracy
status, methodological-fidelity status, instructional-coherence
status, accessibility-review status, assessment-readiness status,
evidence basis, known limitations, required corrective actions, next
review condition or date, and related issue or decision records.

**No unsupported effectiveness claims (§13.3).** No educational asset,
program, or public statement may claim demonstrated effectiveness,
competency improvement, employment outcomes, organizational
improvement, or return on investment without appropriate evidence and
approval.
