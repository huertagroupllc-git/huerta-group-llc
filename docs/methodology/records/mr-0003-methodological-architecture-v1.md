## Purpose and scope

Establishes the approved layers and high-level domains through which
Huerta Group methodology is organized, institution-wide — deliberately
without asserting a complete proprietary consulting framework.

## Approved content

Methodology Foundation v1 §2 approves a **five-layer methodological
architecture**, version 1.0, explicitly incomplete by design: layers
and high-level domains are approved as the organizing structure;
detailed methods, assessments, instruments, and domain content are not
yet developed and are not implied to exist. **The layer definitions
themselves were not transmitted with the implementing authorization and
await verbatim insertion into the source record through Methodology
authority.** Until then, no layer may be named, described, or filled by
inference.

## Limitations and open questions
