## Purpose and scope

Establishes the approved principle-level progression for high-level
organizational-improvement work.

## Approved content

Huerta Group's organizational-improvement work moves through the
approved principle-level progression, exactly as transmitted: Huerta
Group **understands**, **defines**, **examines**, **improves**,
**implements**, **verifies**, **sustains**, and **learns**. What each
movement takes as its object, and how the movements relate in
practice, awaits further approved methodology substance and is not
elaborated here.

This is deliberately a **non-numbered, non-automated reference
framework**: the movements are not phases, are not sequential gates,
carry no deliverables, thresholds, or scoring, and must not be rendered
as a numbered lifecycle, workflow engine, or automated process. It is
not a finalized proprietary phase model.

## Limitations and open questions

- No phase definitions, entry/exit criteria, deliverables, or
  verification thresholds exist or are implied; creating any requires
  new approved methodology substance.
- Relationship of the movements to the five-layer architecture
  (mr-0003) awaits the pending §2 text.

## Technical implementation

Approved only as this non-numbered, non-automated reference framework.
