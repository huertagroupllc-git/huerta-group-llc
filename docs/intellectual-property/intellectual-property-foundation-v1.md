  Property room, with the determination "INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

## 1. Definition, admission, relationships, and exclusions (§§1.1–1.13)

Approved subject: the definition of institutional intellectual
property; admission requirements; relationships to institutional
knowledge, methodology, software, publications, education, branding,
technology, services, operations, research, and future capabilities;
and exclusions. **Full approved text: pending verbatim insertion.**
Derived record: `ip-0002`.

Transmitted admission principle: repository presence and IP admission
are separate; any future substantive asset requires an identifiable
source, approved substantive authority, provenance, an
institutional-value rationale, admission review, an admission decision,
a governance status, known ownership and rights status, disclosure, and
stewardship. Nothing is institutional IP merely because it exists
internally, was created by Huerta Group personnel, appears in this
repository, is a Method Library record, an ADR, an architecture
document, a source file, a brand expression, a publication reference,
an educational concept, or an internal process.

## 5. Authority and admission (§5)

- Admission requires substantive-source authority (the source room
  retains authority over the underlying asset), Intellectual Property
  admission authority, and recorded decision context. **Admission does
  not create legal ownership**, and repository presence does not create
  admission.
- **Approval authority is configurable**: no immutable office, no named
  individual embedded as permanent authority, no technical role treated
  as institutional authority; the authority basis is stored as data,
  subordinate to the Constitution and future decision-rights
  architecture.
- Boundaries: substantive authority (source rooms) · IP authority
  (admission, classification, stewardship) · Institutional Knowledge
  Architecture (metadata/taxonomy/identifiers/versioning conventions) ·
  Education (instructional substance) · Branding (brand substance) ·
  Platform & Systems Development (technical implementation and custody)
  · Corporate Headquarters (legally, financially, administratively, and
  commercially operative matters).

Derived record: `ip-0005`.

## 6. Disclosure, origin, adaptation, ownership, and rights (§6)

**Disclosure classifications:** `public` · `internal` · `confidential`
· `proprietary` · `restricted` · `unresolved`. Institutional
proprietary status is never legally enforceable protection; public
disclosure never implies ownership or relicensing.

**Origin designations** (separate from disclosure; never imply
ownership): `original_institutional` · `client_derived` ·
`third_party_derived` · `jointly_developed` · `acquired` · `unknown` ·
`not_applicable`. Client-derived assets require explicit review-state
metadata.

**Adaptation designations** (separate): `educational_adaptation` ·
`publishing_adaptation` · `software_implementation` ·
`brand_implementation` · `method_adaptation` · `none` · `unresolved`.

**Ownership statuses** (conservative factual states, never legal
conclusions): `not_evaluated` · `claimed_institutional_interest` ·
`pending_review` · `third_party_interest_known` ·
`mixed_or_joint_interest_possible` ·
`legally_confirmed_institutional_ownership` ·
`legally_confirmed_third_party_ownership` · `unresolved`. Legally
confirmed values require authoritative Corporate Headquarters or legal
evidence.

**Rights statuses** (evidence status, not rights conclusions):
`not_evaluated` · `rights_information_incomplete` ·
`agreement_reference_recorded` · `registration_reference_recorded` ·
`restrictions_known` · `legally_reviewed` · `unresolved`.

Derived record: `ip-0006`.

## 8. Technical implementation boundary

Platform & Systems Development may implement the governed, file-based
repository: the six initial records, metadata, status dimensions,
provenance and evidence references, relationship and traceability
structures, versioning/supersession/archival preservation, configurable
approval authority, and deterministic validation. It must **not**
implement or determine: legal ownership or rights conclusions
(copyright, patentability, trademark rights, trade-secret status,
inventorship, authorship, work-for-hire, assignment validity, chain of
title), licensing, royalties, valuation, commercialization,
registration, enforcement, automated legal decisions, automatic
admission of any existing repository asset, a runtime IP application,
database, API, search interface, portal, or administrative system.
Search and retrieval are authorized only as future architectural
capability metadata.
