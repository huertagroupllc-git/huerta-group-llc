## Purpose and scope

Defines institutional intellectual property and establishes its
admission criteria, scope, relationships, and exclusions — governing
all Huerta Group institutional intellectual-property identification and
governance.

## Approved content

The approved definition, admission requirements, relationships (to
institutional knowledge, methodology, software, publications,
education, branding, technology, services, operations, research, and
future capabilities), and exclusions reside in Intellectual Property
Foundation v1 §§1.1–1.13. **The full approved text of those sections
was not transmitted with the implementing authorization and awaits
verbatim insertion into the source record through Intellectual Property
authority.** Until then, this record governs by reference and asserts
no definition content of its own beyond the transmitted admission
principle:

Repository presence and IP admission are separate. Any future
substantive asset requires an identifiable source, approved substantive
authority, provenance, an institutional-value rationale, admission
review, an admission decision, a governance status, known ownership and
rights status, disclosure, and stewardship. Nothing is institutional IP
merely because it exists internally or was created by Huerta Group
personnel — and admission, when granted, is an institutional governance
act that creates no legal ownership or protection.
